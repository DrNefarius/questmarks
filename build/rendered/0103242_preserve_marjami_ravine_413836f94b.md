## Walkthrough
[1] Participate in one Lair Reive in Marjami Ravine.
    - You may join the reive in the middle of the battle as long as you are present for the final evaluation.

[2] Lair Reives can be found at the following locations:
    - (C-7) - 3 Gorge Vultures and 2 Undaunted Colibri - SW of Bivouac #4
    - (E-7) - 3 Foraging Apkallu and 2 Trembling Tulfaires - Bivouac #4 - Exit NE side and take the "Scaleable Area" at E-6. Head South when you exit the cave and hold the left wall
    - (I-8) - 3 Undaunted Colibri and 2 Trembling Tulfaires - Bivouac #2 - Exit SE Side and hold the left river bank. You'll come to the Reive eventually.
    - (J/K-12) - 3 Gorge Vultures and 2 Trembling Tulfaires - Bivouac #1 - Exit SW Side and head South past the ramp and enter the Southern path. Hold the right wall until you run into the Lair Reive
    - (E-10) - 5 Trembling Tulfaires - Bivouac #3 - Exit SE side and head to the "Scalable Area" at E-11. Take this down and exit to the NW. The Lair Reive should be directly ahead.
