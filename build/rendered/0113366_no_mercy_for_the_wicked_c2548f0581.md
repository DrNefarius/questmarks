[1] Talk to Rigobertine in Eastern Adoulin (at (J-8) outside Order of Weatherspoon.)
    - Waypoint Eastern Adoulin Castle Adoulin gates.
[2] Approach Oscairn, outside the Peacekeepers' Coalition, for a cutscene.
[3] Head to Yorcia Weald via Bivouac #1 (and head south) or #2 (and head north).
[4] Examine the Ergon Locus ???, in the grove of Numbing Blossoms south part of (I-8) (come from (H-8) for getting access) for a cutscene with Ingrid and Morimar.
    - Make sure to not come from (I-7), and should not be confused with the regular Ergon Locus.
    - Previously, the patch could be blocked by a bush, except between 21:00 and 03:00 in game time. This bush appears to have been removed in the August 2025 version update. [unverified]
