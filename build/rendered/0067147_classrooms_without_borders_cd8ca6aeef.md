## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Moreno-Toeno (A) at (K-5) near conflux #1 to begin the quest.
[2] Go to Conflux #7 and defeat Manigordo for three Manigordo Tusks, or acquire them by any other means such as the Auction House.
[3] Trade the tusks to Moreno-Toeno (A) to complete the quest.
    - 50 quest completions will max fame in this zone.

Zoning is not required to repeat this quest.
