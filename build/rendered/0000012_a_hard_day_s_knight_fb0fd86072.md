## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Quelveuiat. (I-10, Middle Floor)
[2] Zone into Lufaise Meadows by taking the south exit at (I-6).
[3] Head to (H-10) afterwards.
    - Clear any nearby Orcs as they will link.
[4] Click the ??? at the top of the platform to spawn Splinterspine Grukjuk, a Ranger Orc NM.
    - Defeat Splinterspine Grukjuk.
[5] Return to Tavnazian Safehold and speak with Quelveuiat to complete the quest.

## After the walkthrough: Optional

[1] After completing the quest you may trade Quelveuiat a  Sealion Crest Key and a   Coral Crest Key to obtain a [KI] Temple Knight key.
    - This allows you to open the large door to the inner part of Sacrarium at (H-7) solo.
