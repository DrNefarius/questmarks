[1] Speak to Tosuka-Porika again for a cutscene. Zoning is not required after the previous mission.
[2] Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo in Port Windurst (G-5) (HP #1) behind Warehouse 1 for a cutscene.
    - (Optional): Talking to Kohlo-Lakolo again will give you a hint.
    - (Optional): Speaking to Shanruru gives an additional cutscene.
[3] Open up your Temporary Key Item menu and examine the [KI] Piece of evidence in your Temporary Key Items to see a cipher.
[4] Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo for another cutscene. He will ask if you deciphered the evidence, say yup.
    - Even though you probably didn't, it seems your character did.
        - For anyone curious, the encrypted message uses a simple caesar cipher and says: "I require the special egg and a very strong individual. First comes the egg."
[5] Travel to the The Boyahda Tree and visit any of the three Muggy Air sites at (D-5), (H-6), and (H-9) Map 1, bottom floor.
    - The best option is to take the Proto-Waypoint to The Boyahda Tree, which places you on Map 1 in (D-4).
    - You may instead take the Unity Warp (Level 125) to The Boyahda Tree. Once there, turn around to zone to Map 1.
    - May need to travel to all 3 spots as one activates the cutscene.
[6] Touching the active site will immediately spawn the Templar Crawler NM. The active site will have a white glowing animation. Not all sites will be active, so keep looking at the different positions.
    - Deals enhanced damage via Poison Breath.
    - Appears to gain an additional Protect buff after using Cocoon.
[7] Each party member will receive a [KI] Sublime lucky egg upon defeating the NM.
[8] Return to Kohlo-Lakolo for a cutscene and your reward.
