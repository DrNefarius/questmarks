## Walkthrough

[1] Trade Mhebi Juhbily (I-10) a Sandfish. When asked, tell her you give it to her. Then check the shed behind her for a cutscene.
[2] Speak to Vah Keshura (H-9).
[3] Obtain a Tonberry Board.
    - Tonberry Board's drop off Tonberry Pursuers in the Temple of Uggalepih, Tonberry Shadowers in Yhoator Jungle, and from Tonberry Trailer in Den of Rancor.
[4] Use a Unity Concord NPC warp to Temple of Uggalepih (lv125), or follow the directions below.
    - Head to (J-12) Yhoator Jungle to enter the Temple of Uggalepih.
    - Once inside, follow the path and go South at (J-7), then North at (H-9), then North once more at (G-7).  This will lead you back outside to the Yhoator Jungle.
    - Head left and re-enter the Temple at (H-11).
        - Be careful not to fall off the ledge here.
[5] Back inside, head west, then south until you reach (H-10).
[6] Turn west once more and pass the Granite Door to reach the Tonberry Kitchen.
[7] Speak to Chef Nonberry, then trade him the Tonberry Board.
[8] He will give you the (Key Item) Nonberry's knife.
[9] Return to Mhebi Juhbily for your reward.
