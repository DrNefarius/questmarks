## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Ailbeche to start the quest for a cutscene.
[2] Talk to Ailbeche's father, Exoroche, who can be found in Southern San d'Oria at (K-7) in Helbort's Blade.
[3] Return to Ailbeche and talk to him again for a cutscene.  You'll receive the willow fishing rod as reward.
    - In order to active the Paladin AF1 quest, Sharpening the Sword, it's necessary to trade back the fishing rod to Ailbeche.  Doing so will also change your title to Family Counselor.
