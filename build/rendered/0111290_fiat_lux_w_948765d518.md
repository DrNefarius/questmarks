## Walkthrough

Note: This special campaign ops is only available when Beaucedine Glacier (S), Xarcabard (S), Castle Zvahl Baileys (S), and Castle Zvahl Keep (S) are Allied control. Throne Room (S) does not need to be Allied control.
[1] Head to the Throne Room (S) through Castle Zvahl Baileys (S).
[2] Defeat the Shadow Lord.
    - All party members must have this Campaign Op active to enter.
    - Having this Campaign Op is NOT required to receive loot from the loot pool.
    - See Shadow Lord for more information.
[3] Once the Shadow Lord is defeated an armory crate will appear.
[4] Speak with Emhi Tchaoryo to complete this mission.
