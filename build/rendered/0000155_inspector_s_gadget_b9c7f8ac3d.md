## Walkthrough

[1] Zone after finishing the previous quest.
[2] Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo for a cut-scene, then to Pichichi near him, who directs you to her mother.
[3] Go to Windurst Waters and speak to Chamama at (F-10) (use Home Point #4), who requests four balls of Saruta Cotton to make a Fake moustache.
[4] Give Chamama the four Saruta Cotton, then return to Kohlo-Lakolo for a cut-scene.
