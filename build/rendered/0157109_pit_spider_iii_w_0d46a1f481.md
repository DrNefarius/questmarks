## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo.
[2] Travel to the Beastmen Ensign at (H-8) in Fort Karugo-Narugo (S).
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ghoyu's Reverie.
[4] Two parties of Yagudos (4x Yagudo Guard, 1x Yagudo Carrier) will start moving slowly in different directions. The objective of this mission is to kill all 10 Yagudos before they reach the end of their route.
    - The Carrier will run off as soon as he's engaged, so you'll need to kill him quickly.
    - Should a Carrier make it to the end of the area, which is quite large, the mission is failed.
[5] Once all ten Yagudos are defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
[6] You must exit the area by using the Twilight Aureola for Emhi Tchaoryo to successfully complete the mission!
    - You cannot warp out.
[7] Return and talk to Emhi Tchaoryo for your reward.
