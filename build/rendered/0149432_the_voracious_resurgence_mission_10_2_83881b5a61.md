[1] Before heading out, get an Acidic Humus Acidic Humus and a Watermelon Watermelon for each member of your party.
    - Acidic Humus Acidic Humus can be farmed in Abyssea - Tahrongi.
        - Warp to conflux #2 and walk north.
        - Fight Pachypodiums until you get an Alkaline Humus Alkaline Humus .
        - Trade the Alkaline Humus Alkaline Humus to the ??? spot on the eastern side of (H-8) to spawn Gancanagh, who drops the Acidic Humus Acidic Humus .
    - You may also buy the Acidic Humus Acidic Humus from the auction house (Others → Misc. 2).
    - WatermelonWatermelon can be bought from Scamplix near HP#2 in Rabao (F-7).
[2] Talk to the Mandragora Warden in The Boyahda Tree, Map 1 at (F-4), and ask about Magh Bihu.
    - There are two spots at (F-4) with Mandragora Warden. The correct one has an entrance from E-4.
    - There is only a single Mandragora Warden at the correct location.
    - The Proto-Waypoint is closest.
    - The next fastest way to reach it is by using the Home Point near the Cloister and walking towards exit F to the north.
    - Unity Warp level 135 to The Boyahda Tree is another way very close to it.
[3] Trade an Acidic Humus Acidic Humus for a cutscene.
[4] After the cutscene, speak to Mandragora Warden again and select "Where did Magh Bihu go?".
[5] Trade a Watermelon Watermelon for the final cutscene and your reward.
