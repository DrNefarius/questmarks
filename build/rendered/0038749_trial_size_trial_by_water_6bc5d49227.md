# Trial-Size Trial by Water

The game knows this quest as: Trial-Size Trial by Water   (quest/outlands #148)

## Quest Header
Start:      Verctissa, Norg (H-9)
Description: The Rhinostery has developed a new "mini" tuning fork of water. Ringing it upon the protocrystal in the Den of Rancor will open the path to a one-on-one battle with the beast that awaits inside. However, only summoners are allowed to take up this challenge.

## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Verctissa in Norg (H-9) to begin quest and obtain a Mini Tuning Fork of Water.
[2] You may prepare for the upcoming battle and whenever you are ready, trade Verctissa the tuning fork to be transported to the arena.
[3] Trade the fork to the Water Protocrystal to enter the fighting arena.
[4] Defeat Leviathan Prime to complete quest.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Only Carbuncle may be used for this battle.
    - Siren can also be used but it seems unintended.
[2] Use pet food (e.g. Grape Daifuku) and bring along a few Dawn Mulsum and juices if you don't want to use Astral Flow
[3] You must face this challenge alone.
[4] You may buff outside of the fight. Level cap will not dispel them.
[5] There is a 15 minutes time limit on the arena.
[6] Warning You may re-obtain the Mini Tuning Fork from the starting NPC at any point if you desire and repeat the fight, but the NPC will no longer teleport you to the Cloister.
    - These were historically prized for their teleportation abilities, but Home Point warps have largely supplanted them.
