## Walkthrough

[1] With the three key item petrifacts in possession from the previous quest, examine the Provenance Protocrystal in Provenance to engage and defeat Provenance Watcher.
    - All members of the alliance will need all three of the key item petrifacts to participate, and they are lost on entry.
    - This battle can be repeated by reobtaining all three key items, but the quest can only be completed once.
[2] Check the Regal Pawprints upon victory for a cutscene which ends this quest and begins the next quest automatically.
