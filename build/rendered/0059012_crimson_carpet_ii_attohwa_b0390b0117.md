## Walkthrough

Only one Ward Warden / Desert Rain / Crimson Carpet quest can be completed per Vana'dielian day, regardless of Abyssea area.
[1] Speak to the Resistance Sapper (Attohwa) at (F-9) near Veridical Conflux #00 to activate the quest
    - You will receive [KI] Weakening trap fluid.
[2] Travel to the nearest Fore Trap.
    - A nearby one is at Veridical Conflux #04. From there, head north through the Chasm Gnats to (E-6) to find it.
    - The other trap is at (F-10), but further from a conflux.
[3] Check the Fore Trap to receive information on where to stand before beginning infusion.
    - 1 yalm is just inside the fringe of the trap.
    - 2 yalm is just next to the trap without touching it.
    - 5 yalm is max distance from the trap.
[4] Once in place, check the Fore Trap again to begin the infusion process.
    - If done correctly, you will be told to report back to the Resistance Sapper. You will also obtain a [KI] Weakening trap fluid bottle, but you will have no indication of this.
[5] Return to the Resistance Sapper to complete the quest and receive reward.
    - The amount of Cruor and Resistance Credits obtained depends on your accuracy on distance from the trap.
