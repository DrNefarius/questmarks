## Walkthrough

[1] This quest is flagged upon completion of the previous quest, Re-Drafted by the Duchy.
[2] Engage in and complete the three tier IV Voidwatch battles in field areas around the three nations:
    - Lancing Lamorak in West Ronfaure. (H-6), (H-8), (H-10).
    - Bhishani in South Gustaberg. (J-10), (K-7), (L-9).
    - Rw Nw Prt M Hrw in East Sarutabaruta, (G-11), (H-6), (J-5).
[3] Return to any Atmacite Refiner to have your White stratum abyssite IV examined and upgraded to White stratum abyssite V.
    - You do not need to return to a refiner after each battle, only once all three are completed.
[4] Engage in and complete the three tier V Voidwatch battles in the three crag areas:
    - Stachysaurus in La Theine Plateau. (E-5), (I-11), (L-8).
    - Gwynn Ap Nudd in Konschtat Highlands. (G-4), (I-11), (J-8).
    - Smierc in Tahrongi Canyon. (I-9), (I-12), (J-6).
[5] Speak with any present-day Voidwatch Officer to receive a Voidwatch alarum.
[6] Return to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene and to receive 50,000 gil.
