## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Phara in Bastok Mines at (J-9).
[2] Talk to Deidogg (H-6) Bastok Mines twice.
    - He is at the bottom of the ramp that leads to the guild.
    - After this point the quest can be completed on any job.
[3] Go Palborough Mines and work your way to the top floor. You will find a ??? at (G-10) in a dead end room.
    - Home Point #1 is very close. If you don't have the Home Point, Domenic can also teleport you to Waughroon Shrine which is right next to it.
[4] Examine it to spawn Ni'Ghu Nestfender. Defeat the NM who will drop a Mottled Quadav Egg.
    - 3 minute re-spawn.
[5] Return to Bastok and trade the Mottled Quadav Egg to Deidogg.
    - You can speak to Deidogg again for an additional cutscene.
[6] Go to Castle Oztroja and kill the Yagudo Parasite in the pond on the 3rd floor (H-9) for a Parasite Skin.
[7] Return to Bastok again and trade the Parasite Skin to Deidogg.
[8] Wait a game day (after clock rolls around past 00:00).  Talk to Deidogg for your reward.
