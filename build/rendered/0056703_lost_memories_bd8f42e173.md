## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Halver (A) at (L-11), near Veridical Conflux #06.
[2] He will request two  Lambent Scales.
[3] Lambent Scales drop from Farfadets, a Thief with a high level of Treasure Hunter is recommended as the drop rate is not superb.
[4] Trade the Lambent Scales to Halver (A).
    - You will complete the quest, and receive [KI] Vial of lambent potion. This is used for the next quest.
[5] Completing this quest 8 more times gives enough fame to open up Rank 6 fame quest with Glenne (A) across from Halver (A)
Zoning is not required to repeat this quest.
