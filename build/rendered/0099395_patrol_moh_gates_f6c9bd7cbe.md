## Walkthrough
[1] Defeat 5 Ruby Raptors in Moh Gates. You do not need to be in EXP range as long as you are in Moh Gates when your party/alliance defeats the monsters.
    - Easiest way to find some is to travel from Morimar Basalt Fields Frontier Station and zone into Moh Gates
    - The Ruby Raptors located at the Reive do not count.

5/27/26 **Note** Raptors must be killed one at a time. I AoE a group of 4 after killing my first one and only got credit for 1 more.
