## Walkthrough

This quest automatically is flagged after completing the previous quest, The Truth Beckons.
[1] Before progressing, you must obtain a new [KI] Traverser stone from Joachim. You will need to wait until his restock timer is up.
[2] Examine any of the following Cavernous Maws to begin the corresponding quests.
    - This can be done if you have the 1 hour lockout from the last quest but still have a Traverser Stone.
    - After flagging one of these quests, you unlock the ability to teleport to the corresponding maw for a fee of 200 Cruor.
    - Teleportation can be done via these abyssea maw teleportation NPCs.
[3] You must complete any three of these sub-quests to proceed with the next main Abyssea quest.

Vision of Abyssea
[4] Examine the Cavernous Maw in Konschtat Highlands at (I-12) to start the quest To Paste a Peiste.
    - Survival Guide in North Gustaberg is close.
[5] Examine the Cavernous Maw in Tahrongi Canyon at (H-12) for the quest Megadrile Menace.
    - Unity Wanted Battle lv.99 to Tahrongi Canyon is close.
[6] Examine the Cavernous Maw in La Theine Plateau at (E-4) for the quest A Goldstruck Gigas.
    - Survival Guide in West Ronfaure is close.
Scars of Abyssea
[7] Examine the Cavernous Maw in Jugner Forest at (J-8) to start the quest the quest The Beast of Bastore.
    - Survival Guide in Jugner Forest is close.
[8] Examine the Cavernous Maw in Buburimu Peninsula at (F-7) for the quest A Fluttery Fiend.
    - Survival Guide in Buburimu Peninsula is close.
[9] Examine the Cavernous Maw in Valkurm Dunes at (I-9) for the quest A Delectable Demon.
    - Survival Guide in Valkurm Dunes is close.
Heroes of Abyssea

    - Survival Guide in Xarcabard is close.
[11] Examine the Cavernous Maw in North Gustaberg at (G-6) for the quest An Ulcerous Uragnite.
    - Survival Guide in Oldton Movalpolos is close.
[12] Examine the Cavernous Maw in South Gustaberg at (J-10) for the quest A Beaked Blusterer.
    - Home Point #1 in Bastok Mines is close.
