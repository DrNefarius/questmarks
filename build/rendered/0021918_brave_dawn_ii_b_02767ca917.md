# Brave Dawn II (B)

The game knows this mission as: Brave Dawn II (B)   (mission/campaign #232)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Military training
Description: Travel to Ruhotz Silvermines via North Gustaberg (K-9) and assist in the supervision of troop training exercies. Do what is necessary to prevent any accidents.
Orders:      For this operation, you'll have to head over to the training grounds and teach a nugget what side to the axe to hold.
NPC Name:    Hieronymus
Zone:        Bastok Markets (S)
pos:         E-8
Campaign Rank: Allied Ribbon of Glory §§§§
Time:        30 min
Level:       Any
Rewards:     * 210 Experience Points
            * 820 Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus
[2] Exit to North Gustaberg (S), follow the wall to the east-(K8) to find the Allied Ensign.
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ruhotz Silvermines.
[4] You will come across a Soldier, who immediately runs towards a Trained Bat and begins attacking.
    - During this time, you will see messages that display how much attention he is giving to the fight:
        - The soldier seems unfocused...
        - The soldier has begun to train harder!
        - The soldier is fighting with vigor!
[5] Once the bat defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
    - It is probably a good strategy to let the soldier tank until he is fighting with vigor, then attack the bat.
    - If the soldier never gains focus, you lose the mission.
[6] Return and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] There seems to be no time limit to how long you can take to get to Ruhotz Silvermines.
[2] The soldier will die if left alone, however can cure himself.
[3] The bat lasts a long while, and seems to shift hate very often.
