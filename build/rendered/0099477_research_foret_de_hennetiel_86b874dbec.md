## Walkthrough
[1] Fight Craklaws in Foret de Hennetiel or Sih Gates until the Sundering Snip special attack is used.
    - There are 4 Craklaws directly west of Bivouac #4 in Foret de Hennetiel
    - After being hit with Sundering Snip, the assignment will be complete.
    - Defense Down effect is not required to complete assignment.
    - This move appears to be a short-ranged beam (less than 7 yalms).
        - Stand directly behind whoever is holding hate if clearing for multiple characters.
[2] Note: Reive mobs do NOT count towards completion of this objective.
