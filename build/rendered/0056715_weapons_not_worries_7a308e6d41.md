## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Peshi Yohnts (A) at (F-9) in the Western Encampment. Veridical Conflux #03 is nearby.
    - You'll need to speak to Kupipi (A) at (H-12) beforehand if you've never done so before.
[2] She'll give you a  Pickaxe and asks you to excavate a  Hardened Bone from Excavation Points, or purchased on the Auction House.
    - She'll only give you one so any extras from failure or repeating the quest will require your to bring own.
[3] Excavation Points are located at (E-5), scattered around the dragon skeleton. This is a little walk from Veridical Conflux #06.
    - Mine until you find a  Hardened Bone; multiple bones can be mined in a single session.
    - Various other junk can be obtained rather than the Hardened Bone. There is a respawn timer on the Excavation Points.
[4] Trade Peshi Yohnts (A) a  Hardened Bone to complete the quest.
    - Note as soon as you engage her to repeat the quest a second time, it automatically is flagged in the quest log.
    - 50 quest completions will max fame in this zone.
Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
