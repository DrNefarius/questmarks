## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Tsetseroon to begin this quest.
    - There is no cutscene to start this quest.
[2] Head to Wajaom Woodlands (D-9) and zone into Aydeewa Subterrane.
    - Reach (G-10) on map 3 for a cutscene.
[3] Return to Tsetseroon to offically begin this quest.
[4] Trade Tsetseroon a Tin Ore and a Cobalt Jellyfish.  He will give you a Pot of Tsetseroon's stew.
[5] Return to Aydeewa Subterrane (G-10 Map 3) and check the ??? at the top of the damaged stairwell for a cutscene.
    - Choose to look into Wawaroon's bag for a Map of Aydeewa Subterrane.
[6] Return to Tsetseroon for an Imperial Bronze Piece and to complete the quest.

Note:
[7] If you chose not to look into Wawaroon's bag, you will receive the Map of Aydeewa Subterrane when you return to Tsetseroon instead; you will not get an Imperial Bronze Piece.
