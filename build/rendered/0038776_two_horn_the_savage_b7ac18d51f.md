## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Milazahn for a cutscene.
[2] Speak to Cacaroon (G-11) for a cutscene.
[3] Trade Cacaroon 1,000 gil for another cutscene.
[4] Zone into Mamook from the Mamool Ja Staging Point exit.
[5] Head northwest to (E-8) and check the Viscous Liquid for a cutscene.
[6] Examine the Viscous Liquid again to spawn Mamool Ja (NM).
[7] Examine the Viscous Liquid after the NM is defeated for a cutscene.
[8] Return to Milazahn for your reward.
