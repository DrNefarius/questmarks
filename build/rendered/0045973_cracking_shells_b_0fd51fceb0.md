## Walkthrough

Note: This special campaign ops is only available when Beadeaux (S) is under the control of Bastok.
[1] Head to Beadeaux (S) after selecting the mission.
[2] Obtain a Diamond Seal (Key Item).
    - The key item drops off any of the Quadav's in Beadeaux (S) (excluding NM's).
    - It can be obtained when the campaign op isn't flagged, so check that you don't have it before you begin farming it.
    - Every member of the party currently in the zone will receive a stone when it drops.
[3] You will also need a Quadav Charm to get to the Qulun Dome area of the zone.
[4] Make your way to the Qulun Dome area (L-8) on Map 2 and examine the Ironbound Gate to enter the battlefield.

## After the walkthrough: Cracking Shells

[1] Trusts can be summoned and there is no level cap for this battlefield.
[2] Inside the battlefield, you will face four Quadav NMs.
    - Za'Dha Adamantking (RNG)
    - Di'Mho Platekeeper (WHM)
    - Zi'Bhe Gauntletkeeper (BLM)
    - Zo'Bha Helmkeeper (RDM)
[3] Only Za'Dha Adamantking has to be defeated to win the fight.
    - Za'Dha Adamantking will use Eagle Eye Shot.
    - Za'Dha Adamantking is immune to Sleep, but nothing else.
[4] His helpers are much weaker, with no known immunities. They will respawn shortly after they are defeated. The helpers share hate with Za'Dha Adamantking.
[5] Once Za'Dha Adamantking is defeated, an Armoury Crate will spawn.
[6] Return to Hieronymus to complete the mission.
