## Walkthrough
[1] Fight Matamata in Morimar Basalt Fields or Moh Gates until the Tail Thwack special attack is used.
    - After being hit with Defense Down effect from Tail Thwack, the assignment will be complete.
    - This move appears to be a short-ranged beam (less than 7 yalms).
        - If Tail Thwack misses, the effect won't apply and the assignment won't complete.  Recommend using a non-evasive job and wearing no +evasion equipment.
            - Stand directly behind whoever is holding hate if clearing for multiple characters.
[2] Matamata can be found in quantity near the Morimar Basalt Fields Home Point.
[3] Note: Reive mobs do NOT count towards completion of this objective.
