## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Hari Pakhroib (I-11), who wants Wild Pamamas.
    - Wild Pamamas drop off Young Opo-opo in Yuhtunga Jungle.
    - They can also sometimes be found on the Auction House.
    - Despite what the NPC says, you don't need to bring Ice Clusters, as there are no Flame Spouts on the path we'll be taking.
[2] Head to Ifrit's Cauldron using the entrance from (G-6) in Yhoator Jungle.
    - The Survival Guide can also bring you there.
[3] Once inside Ifrit's Cauldron, take the left path at the fork and continue on until you find the Altar of Ashes located at (I-9) on map 2. It's a black pillar near the edge of the path.
    - Alternatively, utilize Unity 125 teleport to travel quickly to Map 3, taking exit G > A > B to quickly reach (I-9) of Map 2.
[4] Trade the Wild Pamamas to the Altar.
    - The NPC mentions needing to shout your name, but that's not necessary.
[5] Return to Hari Pakhroib for your reward.
