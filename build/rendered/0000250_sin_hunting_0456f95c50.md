## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Perih Vashai in Windurst Woods (K-7) and recieve temporary key item Chieftainness's twinstone earring.
[2] She will send you to Ranguemont Pass to look for Semih Lafihna.
[3] After arriving, you will not find Semih Lafihna there. Talk to Perchond at (F-10).
[4] Perchond will tell you about Semih Lafihna coming to him and questioning him about a creature he encountered in Ranguemont Pass. She was searching for a Glittersand around the pond (E-5).
[5] Go to the pond at (E-5) and kill Evil Weapons until you get the drop Glittersand.
    - There are several high level (88+) Goblins along the way which lower level Rangers will need to either navigate around or use Invisible/Prism Powder to avoid detection.
[6] Trade the Pinch of Glittersand to Perchond, he will give you another temporary key item, Perchond's envelope and explain there is a merchant in Jugner Forest who has also seen the creature and that this is probably where Semih Lafihna is.
[7] Head to Jugner Forest at the very top of (I-6) near the lake and speak with Alexius. He will tell you that he did speak with Semih Lafihna about the creature he saw and that she would be looking for it at the south end of the lake during a full moon.
    - A full moon is not required to get the cutscene.
[8] Go to (H-6) in Jugner Forest and find the ??? on a tree. You should get a cutscene.
[9] After the cutscene, return to Perih Vashai and receive the Sniping Bow.
