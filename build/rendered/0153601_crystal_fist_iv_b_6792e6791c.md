## Walkthrough

[1] Gain your mission from Hieronymus. Select Supply Manufacture, and mission Crystal Fist IV.
[2] Speak with Adjutant (J-9) for details. He will ask you which craft you want to assist him in. You must be skill level 61 or higher in the selected craft in order to participate in this op.
[3] Once you've picked a craft, he'll test you on three synths made with said craft.
    - You will have to recite the ingredients for 3 different recipes in the craft you have chosen.
    - Time is limited when selecting crystals and ingredients, however it is important to note that time only counts against you while the selection screen is up. This means that you may pause as long as you like after selecting a crystal or ingredient and before bringing up the next selection screen so that you can verify your recipe before proceeding.
    - You will fail the mission if you take too long to select the crystals and ingredients while the selection screen is up or if you choose the wrong ones.
    - There is a time limit for choosing the correct ingredients.
[4] Once you've completed your three tests, go back and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.
