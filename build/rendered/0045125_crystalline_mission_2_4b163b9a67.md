## Walkthrough

[1] Collect the following 3 items:
    - Seedspall Lux Seedspall Lux from Orcs in Jugner Forest around (G-11).
        - Closest Survival Guide is Davoi.
    - Seedspall Luna Seedspall Luna from Quadavs in Pashhow Marshlands around (K-10).
        - Closest Survival Guide is Beadeaux.
    - Seedspall Astrum Seedspall Astrum from Yagudos in Meriphataud Mountains around (K-8).
        - Closest Survival Guide is Castle Oztroja.
[2] Trade the 3 Seedspalls to the ??? at (G-6) in Qufim Island for a cutscene.
    - Mid-east section of (G-6), near 2 rock columns.

    - The key can be received once per day.
