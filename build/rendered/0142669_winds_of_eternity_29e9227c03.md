## Walkthrough

- Note: You must zone and wait until after JP midnight after completing The Silent Forest before you may begin this quest.
[1] Examine the Drifting Feather (H-7) in Leafallia for a short cutscene to begin the quest.
[2] Venture to the crag closest to your home nation and click the Ergon Locus ??? located beneath the stairs leading up to the Telepoint to obtain [KI] Fistful of familiar soil.
    - Cannot be obtained while possessing [KI] Holla crystal of gales, [KI] Dem crystal of dunes, or [KI] Mea crystal of flames from Treasures of the Earth of the same type.
        - e.g. having the Holla crystal prevents obtaining the Fistful of familiar soil from the Crag of Holla Ergon Locus ???.
[3] Return to the Drifting Feather again to enter a BCNM.
    - Your opponent will be Siren Prime.
        - The fight is Content Level 119.
        - Trusts may be used in the fight.
[4] Upon defeating Siren, a cutscene will begin where you will be able to choose your reward.
    - You may repeat this quest once per Earth day, resetting at midnight in Japan.
