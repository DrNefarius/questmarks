## Walkthrough
[1] Travel to the frontier station in Foret de Hennetiel or Morimar Basalt Fields and speak with the Station Worker.
[2] You will be presented with a list of various emotes from which you must choose one to cheer up the worker.
[3] To complete the assignment, the correct emote must be chosen while facing the Station Worker and the direction listed below. (If the directions say East, you need to be both facing the worker and facing east, not standing east of the Worker)
    - It is far easier to line up facing the correct direction using first-person view. (V Key by default on Keyboard.) Locking onto the NPC can also help.
    - After each emote attempt, a dialogue will inform you whether the direction or emote was correct.
    - Directions are approximate; take a few steps to the left or right if the angle is incorrect.
[4] The correct emote/direction changes approximately every 3 game hours, so it is possible to warp back and forth from the Mummers' Coalition and rapidly repeat this quest once you figure out the proper emote/direction.
