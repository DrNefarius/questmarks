## Walkthrough

You will want to bring some sort of warp (Warp Ring or Instant Warp). After finishing the next Mission, you'll be left with no fast way to get back into town.
If you have at least completed Wings of the Goddess Mission 45, Time Slips Away:
[1] Enter Walk of Echoes from Pashhow Marshlands (S) (J-9) or Grauberg (S) (F-5) and check the Ornate Door.
    Otherwise, as long as you have at least triggered Wings of the Goddess Mission 8, In the Name of the Father:
[2] Enter Walk of Echoes by touching the glowing ??? on the ground (You will be asked to use your [KI] Lightsworm) next to one of the Cavernous Maws outside Jeuno:
    - Batallia Downs (H-5) Note: Survival Guide to Rolanberry Fields is very close.
    - Rolanberry Fields (H-6) Note: Survival Guide to Rolanberry Fields is closer.
    - Sauromugue Champaign (K-9)
[3] After you zone in run straight ahead, up the stairs, and click on the Ornate Door.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This mission may be continued from within after entering for Ganged Up On.
    - If you leave, you must re-enter Walk of Echoes from Grauberg (S).
[2] The earliest you can do this mission is when you are on Wings of the Goddess mission 8 In the Name of the Father.
[3] From previous posters comments it is possible to get stuck on this mission. Appears they were on a different mission in Wings of the Goddess and unable to resume it until after completing A Dreamy Interlude.
