## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Chelidoine who is NE of the Coronal Esplanade waypoint to accept the quest.
[2] He asks for your help with completing Coalition tasks.
[3] Pioneers' Coalition, Any tips for Colonization Reives?
    - Choose: Assist other pioneers.
[4] Couriers' Coalition, Any tips for supply shipping?
    - Choose: Using waypoints is acceptable.
[5] Inventors' Coalition, Any tips for material procurement?
    - Choose: Buy from Auction House or other pioneers.
[6] Scouts' Coalition, Any tips for ergon locus surveys?
    - Choose: Find a good angle of approach
[7] Peacekeepers' Coalition, Any tips for Lair Reives?
    - Choose: assist other pioneers.
[8] Mummers' Coalition, Any tips for morale boosting?
    - Choose: Check what the target wants.
[9] Wait til JP midnight and return to Chelidoine
