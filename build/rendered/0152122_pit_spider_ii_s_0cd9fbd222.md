## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Rasdinice.
[2] Travel to the Beastmen Stand at (J-7) in Vunkerl Inlet.
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Everbloom Hollow.
[4] Two groups of three Orcs (six total) will immediately start slowly running towards their designated ends.
    - Orcish Carrier - Will Occasionally run away while fighting. Susceptible to Sleep and Gravity
    - Orcish Grunt x2 - Links with the Orcish Carrier, does not run. Susceptible to Sleep and Gravity
    - Should the Orcish Carrier make it to the end of the area (which is smaller than the area in Pit Spider I (S)) the mission is failed.
[5] Once all six Orcs are defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
[6] Exit at the Twilight Aureole to complete the mission.
[7] Return and talk to Rasdinice for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] There seems to be no time limit to how long you can take to get to Everbloom Hollow.
[2] All 3 of the Orc are Susceptible to Sleep and Gravity.
[3] Repetative stun/sleep/bind is helpful in this battle.
[4] The Orcs are very weak.
    - Approximately 2500 HP each.
[5] The Orca will warp out of you fail to kill them quickly enough.
[6] Be sure to exit at the Twilight Aureole to complete the mission.
[7] The following actions will cause you to fail this mission:
    - Leaving before killing the Orcs.
    - Warping out directly after killing the Orcs.
