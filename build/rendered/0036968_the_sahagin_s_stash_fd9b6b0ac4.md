## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Laisrean in Norg at (H-7)  to begin this quest.
[2] Exit Norg into Sea Serpent Grotto.
[3] Head South until you reach the fork at (H-6), head East.
[4] Turn South at (J-5).
[5] Head East at (J-10).
[6] Turn East at (K-12)
[7] Go past the Silver Door at (N-14).
    - Examine the Door three times and trade it a Silver Beastcoin to get past it. You will keep the Silver Beastcoin.
[8] Fall off the path to the one below at (H-8) and continue going North.
[9] Follow this path until you find the ??? at (H-3). Interacting with it will give a [KI] Sea serpent statue.
[10] Return to Laisrean for your reward.
