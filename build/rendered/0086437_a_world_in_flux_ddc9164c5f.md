## Walkthrough

[1] This quest is flagged upon completion of the previous quest, No Rest for the Weary.
[2] Engage in and complete the three tier VI Voidwatch battles in the following past areas:
    - Gaunab in Vunkerl Inlet (S). (F-5), (G-8), (G-13)
        - Both the Atmacite Refiner teleport (available in shadowreign areas only) and the Survival Guide  to Vunkerl Inlet (S) take you (E-7), not very far south of the (F-5) planar rift.
        - The (F-5) location can also be reached by taking Survival Guide to Batallia Downs (S), zoning into nearby Jugner Forest (S), then hugging left and zoning into Vunkerl Inlet (S) at (L-6). There, it is just a short trip south-west.
        - Campaign teleport to Vunkerl Inlet (S) takes you to (H-10), roughly in the middle between the (G-8) and (G-13) locations.
    - Ocythoe in Grauberg (S). (D-14), (G-7), (G-11)
        - The (G-7) planar rift is not too far from the campaign teleport to Grauberg (S).
    - Kalasutrax in Fort Karugo-Narugo (S). (D-12), (I-11), (K-8)
        - The (I-11) planar rift is not far south from where the campaign teleport to Fort Karugo-Narugo (S) places you.
        - The (D-12) planar rift can be reached by taking Survival Guide to West Sarutabaruta (S) and zoning into Fort Karugo-Narugo (S).
[3] Speak to any Shadowreign (past) era Voidwatch Officer to receive a Voidwatch alarum.
[4] Return to the Bulwark Gate in Sauromugue Champaign (S) for a cutscene that ends this quest and starts the next quest, and for your reward.
