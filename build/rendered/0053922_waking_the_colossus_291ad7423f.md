## Walkthrough

Warning: Trust Magic cannot be used in this BCNM, but the fight is targeted at level 75.

[1] Approach Naja Salaheem in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-10) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
[2] Examine Imperial Whitegate at Aht Urhgan Whitegate (L-8/9) to receive the [KI] Imperial missive.
    - The quest now appears under Aht Urhgan: Current Quests.
[3] Examine the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens at (H-6).
[4] You must speak to representatives from the main nations, these can be done in any order.
    - Speak to Halver in Chateau d'Oraguille (I-9) to receive the [KI] San d'Orian approval letter.
    - Speak to Iron Eater in Metalworks (J-8) to receive the [KI] Bastokan approval letter.
    - Speak to Kupipi in Heavens Tower to receive the [KI] Windurstian approval letter.
[5] Return to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens at (H-6) for the [KI] Jeunoan approval letter.
[6] Examine Imperial Whitegate at Aht Urhgan Whitegate (L-8/9) to receive the [KI] present for Megomak.
    - To save time buy 3 Slabs of Plumbago from the AH or you can choose to farm them from Troll enemies in Mount Zhayolm or through mining.
[7] Examine the Acid-eaten Door in Mount Zhayolm at (I-9) to receive the [KI] Megomak's shopping list.
    - The door is past the Gates of Halvung near Halvung Staging Point, not the other one.
        - Requires a [KI]Cast metal plate to open the Gates of Halvung.
[8] Trade 3 Slabs of Plumbago to the Acid-eaten Door to receive the [KI] lightning cell.
[9] Examine the blank target on the single dark lamp at the Nyzul Isle Staging Point in Alzadaal Undersea Ruins (J-8/9) for a cutscene.
[10] Examine the Runic Seal in Alzadaal Undersea Ruins (I-8/9) to start the BCNM fight.
    - In this fight, you will be against Alexander.
    - Alexander can spawn up to three Alexander Images.
    - At low HP, Alexander will use Divine Judgment dealing high damage to everyone in range.
[11] Upon defeating the BCNM you will receive the [KI]Whisper of radiance.
[12] Examine Imperial Whitegate at Aht Urhgan Whitegate (L-8/9) for a cutscene.
[13] Speak to Naja Salaheem to choose your reward.
