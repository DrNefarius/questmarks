## Walkthrough

[1] For Selbina path:
    - Talk to Pacomart at (H-10) in Selbina to be brought to Norg.
        - If for some reason you do not complete the next step, he will take you back to Norg again.
    - Talk to the Oaken Door at (K-8) in Norg to Gilgamesh's room.
[2] For Mhaura path:
    - Talk to Tonasav at (H-9) in Mhaura to be brought to Norg.
        - If for some reason you do not complete the next step, he will take you back to Norg again.
    - Talk to the Oaken Door at (K-8) in Norg to Gilgamesh's room.
    New players should make sure they elect to unlock the Survival Guide, Proto-Waypoint and Home Point before leaving Norg.
