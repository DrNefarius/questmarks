# Cut and Cauterize I (B)

The game knows this mission as: Cut and Cauterize I (B)   (mission/campaign #241)

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus
[2] Exit to North Gustaberg (S), follow the wall to the east to find the Allied Ensign at (K-8).
    - Party of 3 or greater required.
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ruhotz Silvermines.
[4] At the start, two Cadet Woundpatchers will pop. Speak to them to have them follow you to a room to the north with 5 wounded solders.
[5] Speaking to the Woundpatchers will show the following menu options:
    - Give the physician orders.
        - Commence healing, stat!
        - Specify healing methods.
            - Prescribe what type of treatment?
                - Cures and physical healing.
                - Status ailment recovery.
        - Offer moral support.
        - Rest and recover.
        - Issue no orders.
[6] When you are close to a solder who is injured, use the "Commence healing, stat!" command to order the Woundpatcher to use cure spells. (You must issue the command each time you wish her to cure.)
    - If a solder says "P-p-poisoned... C-c-can't b-b-breathe..." or "You must help me! I can't move my arms or legs!", use the "Prescribe what type of treatment?" option and chose "Status ailment recovery" to switch the Woundpatcher to use the appropriate status spells. (Be sure to issue the "Commence healing, stat!" command after, and use the "Prescribe what type of treatment?" option to change back to "Cures and physical healing" mode.)
    - When a solder is sufficiently cured, his/her name will turn green and will say "I owe you my life. Many thanks!".
    - Woundpatchers can and will run out of MP meaning that they will have to rest their MP before they can continue.
    - If the Woundpatcher says "I'm not sure I'm cut out for this...", use the "Offer moral support" option. (But do not use it too often, as it seems to annoy the Woundpatchers.)
[7] When four solders are cured, you will get the message that the operation is complete. (But you are still able to cure the final wounder solder.) Return to the Dawn Aureola to receive your XP and finish the Campaign Op.
    - You will not receive XP if you are too far from the Dawn Aureola.
