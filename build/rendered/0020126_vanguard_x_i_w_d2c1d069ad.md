## Walkthrough

[1] Easily soloable mission.
[2] After confirming the mission with Emhi Tchaoryo, go to a Windurstian controlled Campaign area and talk to the Gate Sentry.

## After the walkthrough: List of Gate Sentries

[1] West Sarutabaruta (S): (I-4)
[2] Sauromugue Champaign (S): (K-11)
[3] Fort Karugo-Narugo (S): (E-11)
[4] Your mission is to escort an NPC named "Reinforcement" to the Campaign NPC of the zone.
    - If you get too far away from the NPC, she will say a line of dialogue and proceed to run on her own back towards the Gate Sentry.
    - You can regain control of the NPC by talking to her again.

## After the walkthrough: List of Campaign NPCs

[1] West Sarutabaruta (S): (H-6)
[2] Fort Karugo-Narugo (S): Lamurara, C.C. at (H-8) inside Fivespires (Not on the main map.)
[3] Once you've arrived at the Campaign NPC, the mission will end, you will receive the experience points and you will be told to report back to Emhi Tchaoryo.
    - If a campaign battle is currently in progress, the NPC will stand there, but not despawn. You will have to wait for it to finish for the mission to be counted as a success.
[4] After talking to Emhi Tchaoryo, you gain the Allied Notes and the mission is complete.
