## After the walkthrough: Yorcia Weald

This route may only be taken if you have cleared the Wildskeeper Reive for Yorcia Weald and thus have the [KI] Aged Ligneous Naakual crest Key Item.
[1] The shortest path starts at Yorcia Weald (I-11).
    - Head to the Enigmatic Device (Alluvion Skirmish entrance) or Waypoint 2 and head south to the crystal structure ??? (I-11) and warp to Cirdas Caverns (Map 2).
    - Head north and examine the crystal structure at (H-6) to be warped to Ra'Kaznar Inner Court (Map 2).

## After the walkthrough: Marjami Ravine

This route may only be taken if you have cleared the Wildskeeper Reive for Marjami Ravine and thus have the [KI] Aged Booming Naakual crest Key Item.
[1] You may have to break a Colonization Reive in Ra'Kaznar Inner Court (Map 1).
[2] This route starts at Marjami Ravine (F-11).
    - Proceed to Waypoint 3 and head east the crystal structure ??? to warp to Woh Gates (Map 2).
        - Head south east and climb down the Scalable Area.
        - Hug the right wall towards Lapiniontraps and Lapinions around (E-12), yes it is odd they coexist.
        - On your left will be another Scalable Area, climb up it and examine the crystal structure ??? to be warped to Woh Gates.
    - At (H-9) in Woh Gates, warp to Ra'Kaznar Inner Court.
    - From (D-7) on Map 1, head southeast to (L-10/11), and you will be warped to (L/M-12) on Map 2.
        - Head southwest then north to (K-10). You may have to break a Colonization Reive here.
        - Head north to (K-8), and you will be at the same place as if you had taken the Yorcia Weald Route.
[3] Follow the left wall and use the Vertical Transit Device to enter Ra'Kaznar Turris.
[4] There is a Home Point next to the entrance (yay!).
[5] Head to the bottom and examine the Ominous Postern for a very long cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: Last Route

If you do not have either of the above Naakual crests for their respective zone. Then you'll have to take the "long" (trying to obtain the Naakual crests without a group can and will take longer than choosing this path) route, traversing all of Outer Ra'Kaznar and Ra'Kaznar Inner Court.
[1] You will have to pass through 2 Colonization Reives in Outer Ra'Kaznar, and 2 Colonization Reives in Ra'Kaznar Inner Court to get to Ra'Kaznar Turris.
    - This requires [KI] "Demolishing".
    - You will need a [KI] Silvery plate, obtained within Outer Ra'Kaznar from the ??? at (C-7) (Map 1) in order to access Ra'Kaznar Turris via this route if you did not already obtain it earlier.
[2] Follow the arrows on the Outer Ra'Kaznar maps to the right. You must reach the eastern side of the central Elevator, on Map 2, (I-8).
    - The starting point is either through Kamihr Drifts or more conveniently the Enigmatic Warp to the Augural Conveyor via an Adoulin Waypoint.
[3] Upon completing your run through Outer Ra'Kaznar, you will arrive at the Inner Court, Map 1 (I-7).
[4] Using the marked map connections for Ra'Kaznar Inner Court, proceed to Warp A on Map 1 to reach A on Map 2.
    - Then to D on Map 2, sending you to D Map 1.
    - From D on Map 1 proceed to H.
    - From H on Map 2 to I.
    - From I on Map 1 to J.
[5] Finally, after using the J warp you will be right around the corner from the Home Point and zone. Good job surviving this nightmare.
[6] Head to the bottom and examine the Ominous Postern for a very long cutscene. (Down through the door by the Home Point, then further down, as down as you can go.)
