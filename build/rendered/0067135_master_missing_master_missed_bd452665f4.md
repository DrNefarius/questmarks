## Walkthrough

You do not need to zone after completing The Mysterious Head Patrol to start this quest.
[1] Talk to Eight of Clubs (A) at (H-8), southeast of Conflux #5, to begin this quest.
[2] Interact with the Faint Glister targetable location at the northwest corner of (J-8) to obtain an [KI] Elegant gemstone.
    - This is south of Conflux #8, or a short walk from Eight of Clubs (A).
[3] Return to Eight of Clubs (A) to complete the quest.
