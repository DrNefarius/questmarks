[1] Speak with Rodeupansat to begin quest
[2] You will receive [KI] Evil warding seal
[3] Travel to (D-11) of Vunkerl Inlet (S) to find a ??? behind the tower (not the irridescent markings). Be very careful here, there are a lot of Djinns (bomb types) floating around, if you need to recast invisible, just use a powder or tonko.
