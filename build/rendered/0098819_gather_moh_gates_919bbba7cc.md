## Walkthrough
[1] NPC asks you to obtain 3 Matamata Shells.
    - The NPC specifically notes it does not matter how the player obtains it. It can be bought, traded, or farmed.
    - There are a few Matamatas at the north end of Moh Gates map #1, but there are significantly more in Morimar Basalt Fields near Bivouac #3.
[2] Trade these to the NPC and then Report on the quest outcome.
