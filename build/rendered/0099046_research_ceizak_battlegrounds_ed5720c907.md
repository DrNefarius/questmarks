## Walkthrough
[1] Fight Fernfelling Chapuli in Ceizak Battlegrounds or Calfcleaving Chapuli in Yahse Hunting Grounds until the Sanguinary Slash special attack is used.
    - Fernfelling Chapuli can be found just south of Waypoint #1 in Ceizak Battlegrounds.
[2] After being hit with Sanguinary Slash, the assignment will be complete.
    - Players may need to remove any equipped shield as has been reported to prevent completion of this assignment despite the move landing and doing damage.
    - Sanguinary Slash is at least conal, possibly an AOE move. To complete multiple character at once, it succeeded with the characters stacked behind each other.
