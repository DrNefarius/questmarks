## After the walkthrough: Traveling to Norg

}|Notes|
[1] Norg is located on Elshimo Island, which can be reached one of the following ways:
    - [KI] Kazham Airship Pass to travel from Port Jeuno to Kazham.
    - If you are on or past Rhapsodies of Vanadiel Mission 1-5, Pacomart and Tonasav can transport you to Norg.
[2] Additional methods become available once either of the above two conditions are met:
    - Waypoints in Adoulin after purchasing the [KI] Norg warp rune or [KI] Kazham warp rune.
    - Unity Warp after completion of 10 Records of Eminence objectives and joining a Unity.
        - Sea Serpent Grotto in the CL 125 category is closest.
[3] NOTE: You must be minimum rank 6 in your home nation in order to continue any further from here.
