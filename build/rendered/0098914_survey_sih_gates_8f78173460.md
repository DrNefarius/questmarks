## Walkthrough
[1] Travel to Sih Gates or Moh Gates and locate an Ergon Locus

[TABLE]
    + Ergon Loci
    Name | Zone | Position | XP | Notes
    Prominence of the Gales | Sih Gates | J-11 | 1000 | Requires Key Item "Watercrafting". Use the Frontier Station Waypoint to go to Foret de Hennetiel, then take the nearest entrance into Sih Gates. Hug the right wall until you come to a castoff point.
    Lake of Light | Sih Gates | K-7 | 1250 | There is a Colonization Reive on the way. Bring Silent Oil and Prism Powder, as the area has a very large amount of Apex mobs.
    Sweltering Spring | Moh Gates | K-6 | 1250 | Enter from Morimar Basalt Fields (Frontier Station Warp). There is a Colonization Reive on the way.
    Prominence of the Flames | Moh Gates | K-10 | 1500 | Requires Key Item "Climbing". Enter from Yahse Hunting Grounds (H-5) (Bivouac #2 Warp). Beware of True Hearing Cave Panopts on the way.
[/TABLE]
