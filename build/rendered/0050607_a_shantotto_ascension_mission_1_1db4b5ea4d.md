## Walkthrough

[1] Zone into Windurst Walls from Windurst Woods or Windurst Waters while level 10 or above.
    - Entering via any other means (including the Mog House) will not trigger the starting cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: Postponing

[1] If you chose to postpone the starting scenario, the mission "To Start ASA..." will appear in your mission log.
[2] Use the Tales' Beginning book at Windurst Walls (C-6/D-6) to start the Mission.
    - The Tales' Beginning book can only be seen visually in-game or on the map by the player(s) who set their story on hold.
