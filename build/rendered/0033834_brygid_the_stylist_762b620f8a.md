## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Brygid the Fashion Police in Bastok Markets at (K-9), and she'll tell you how much your fashion sense sucks!
[2] Improve her view of you by acquiring a Robe and a Bronze Subligar both of which are easy enough to find either on the Auction House or at some armor merchant.
[3] Wear the two pieces of equipment. Keep in mind that only the following jobs can wear both these items at the same time:
        - Starter Jobs: Monk, Red Mage, Thief
        - Advanced/Expansion Jobs: Bard, Dark, Blue Mage
[4] Complete this quest to later unlock Brygid the Stylist Returns.
