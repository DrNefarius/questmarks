## Walkthrough

[1] Enter the Outer Horutoto Ruins from West Sarutabaruta (F-11).
[2] Kill Cardians in the area to get one (or more) of the following Key Items:
    - [KI] Orb of Swords
    - [KI] Orb of Cups
    - [KI] Orb of Batons
    - [KI] Orb of Coins
[3] After receiving the Orbs you want, zone into Outer Horutoto Ruins from East Sarutabaruta (H-3).
[4] Examine the ??? on top of the platform at (H-6) (it'll be right in front of you when you enter the zone) to begin a battlefield.
    - Trust Magic can not be used in this battlefield.
    - Up to 15 Custom Cardians will spawn and begin to attack.
    - The player starting the battle will lose all of their possessed Orbs.
    - The number and types of Orb key items in the possession of the player starting the battle will affect the number of Custom Cardians that spawn and damage type immunity:
        - [KI] Orb of Swords removes slashing damage immunity.
        - [KI] Orb of Batons removes blunt damage immunity
        - [KI] Orb of Cups removes piercing damage immunity.
        - [KI] Orb of Coins removes magic damage immunity.
        - With 1 or 2 Orbs, 15 Cardians will appear.
        - With 3 Orbs, 10 Cardians will appear.
        - With all 4 Orbs, 5 Cardians will appear.
    - Anyone else in the party with different Orbs, will lose them and not have their bonus granted.
        - You receive 500 experience points, a [KI] Ripe starfruit, and a [KI] Peach coral key upon winning.
[5] Return to Shantotto. She will require you give her 5000 gil.
[6] Return to the Inconspicuous Door in Upper Jeuno (H-8) (across from Durable Shields) for a cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: "A Moogle Kupo d'Etat" Key

[1] Use the [KI] Peach coral key (Level 50) to open the red treasure coffer at Lower Jeuno (J-8).
    - Rewards are listed on the Add-on Scenario Rewards page.
[2] Repeat the mission to obtain another [KI] Peach coral key.
    - Alternatively, the set of 4 Mana Orbs ([KI] Orb of Swords, [KI] Orb of Cups, [KI] Orb of Batons, [KI] Orb of Coins) can be purchased from Squintrox Dryeyes for 500g and 5 Beastmen's Seals after all Kupo Missions are completed.
