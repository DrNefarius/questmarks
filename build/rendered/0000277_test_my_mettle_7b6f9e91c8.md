## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Devean who will ask you to bet on how long it takes you to get a pair of Power Sandals from Davoi.
    - The quicker you return, the more money you get back.
    - The time is measured in game hours, not earth hours.
[2] Once in Davoi you need to locate a Jar that contains the Power Sandals.
    - The jar can spawn at (I-8), (K-9), (J-8), (H-8), (F-6), (J-9), (F-9), (G-9), (H-9), and (F-7).
[3] Return the Power Sandals to Devean before time runs out.
[4] You can also choose to keep the Power Sandals for yourself.  This however means that you will fail the quest.
