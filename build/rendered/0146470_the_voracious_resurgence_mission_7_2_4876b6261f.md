## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Dancing Wolf in Rabao (G-6) HP #2 for a cutscene to begin the mission.
[2] Zone into Quicksand Caves from the Altepa Gate for a cutscene. The entrance is in Western Altepa Desert at (I-6/7).
    - See the Altepa Gate page for instructions on how to open the Altepa Gate.
[3] The fight with Uran-Mafran (BLM/RDM) will immediately begin once you touch the Journey's End at (K-10).
    - Has approximately 185k HP.
    - Trusts may be used in this fight.
    - Uran-Mafran can use Manafont multiple times and has a very high degree of Fast Cast
        - Manafont will remove all applied debuffs.
    - Possesses a substantial resistance to silence (though not immunity). Other enfeebles landed normally.
        - Seen using BLM -jas, Cure VI, Enspells, Protect V, Shell V, Meteor, Impact, Comet, and AoE enfeebles.
        - Manafont will make short work of any trusts. Recommended to kill quickly or attempt to land silence.
    - Will attempt to self-skillchain with Club Weapon Skills and magic burst accordingly.
        - Black Halo and tier VI magic bursts hit hard. Damage taken gear is highly recommended.
        - Possesses large amounts of regain and can complete skillchains even if unable to land regular hits.
        - Trust tanks will not survive magic bursts. They can deal over 10k damage.
    - You will be joined by Oggbi (MNK), who must be kept alive.
        - Can use Hundred Fists multiple times and various Hand-to-Hand weaponskills.
[4] Touch the Journey's End afterwards for a cutscene.
        - Credit for beating Uran-Mafran is retained even if your character is defeated.
[5] Speak with Gumbah in Bastok Mines (J-7) HP #1 for the final cutscene and your reward.
