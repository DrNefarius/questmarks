[1] Speak with Ghatsad in the Automaton Workshop (I-7) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate HP #1 for a cutscene.
    - You will receive a [KI] Gift for Megomak.
[2] Head to Mount Zhayolm (I-9) via the Halvung Staging Point.
[3] For the next step, you will need the [KI] Cast metal plate permanent Key Item.
[4] Head to the Gates of Halvung near the Halvung Staging Point at (J-7) and open it with the [KI] Cast metal plate.
[5] Go through the gate you just opened and head southwest. Find the ramp at the central part of (I-8) and go up. Head west then south to reach the Acid-eaten Door at (I-9 NW corner). Click it for a cutscene.
    - You will lose the [KI] Gift for Megomak.
[6] Proceed to Hazhalm Testing Grounds in Caedarva Mire (Einherjar's location) and examine the Entry Gate for a cutscene.
    - Fastest route is Caedarva Mire Home Point #1.
[7] Head back outside to Caedarva Mire and examine the Vexing Sniffles (E-10) (Graveyard, outside of Hazhalm zone) for a cutscene.
[8] Examine the Vexing Sniffles once more to begin a confrontation against Gloom Phantom (WAR/DRK), Magh Bihu (MNK), and Dazbog (PLD).
    - Approximately 60k HP each.
    - Mnejing will assist and must be kept alive.
        - You will receive a [KI] Pure white ampoule after the fight.
[9] Examine the Vexing Sniffles a final time for a cutscene.
[10] Return to the Acid-eaten Door in Mount Zhayolm at (I-9) for a cutscene.
    - You will lose the [KI] Pure white ampoule.
[11] Return to Ghatsad (I-7) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate for the final cutscene and your reward.
