## Walkthrough

Note: To unlock this you need Rank 2 fame in Abyssea - Attohwa. Completing about 6 quests should get you to Rank 2. Commonly doing Something in the Air once, Wayward Wares once, and then repeating Crimson Carpet II (Attohwa) 4 times should get you Rank 2.
[1] Speak to Harith (A) at (G-10) to accept the quest and obtain [KI] Miasmal counteragent recipe.
[2] Craft, or otherwise obtain, one or more M. Counteragent Miasmal Counteragents.
    - The item can only be crafted with the recipe in possession. The major ingredients are purchasable from NPCs:
    - Salinator: Upih Khachla in Windurst Waters North - (H-9) for 4,048 Gil
    - 2x  Distilled Water: Buy just about anywhere. 3 places in Windurst Waters.
    - Seashell: Shih Tayuun in Windurst Woods - (H-13) for 90 Gil.
        - From Boneworkers' Guild, open from 8:00 to 23:00. Use HP #4 or #5.
[3] Search the area for any targetable, path-blocking "Dark Miasma," and trade the counteragent to extinguish the miasma. Dark Miasma are most often found in the rocky, narrow path areas around Parradamo Tor. They dissappear occasionally, so you may need to check the same areas more than once.
    - One is located close to Veridical Conflux #03.
    - Another is located right next to Veridical Conflux #07.
    - You will not receive a message when you trade the M. Counteragent, but you know you were sucessful if the Miasma vanishes and lets you walk through.
[4] Return to Harith (A) to complete the quest.
