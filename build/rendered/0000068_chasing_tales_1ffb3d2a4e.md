## Walkthrough

NOTE: You must zone after completing the previous quest before you can start this quest.
[1] Talk to Tosuka-Porika.
[2] Talk to Furakku-Norakku behind the counter in the same room.
    - Furakku-Norakku tells you that Hae Jakkya at the Windurst Woods Auction House has an overdue romance novel called "A Song of Love". He provides you with an Overdue book notification
[3] Talk to Hae Jakkya at I-12 in Windurst Woods to deliver the notification.
[4] Return to Furakku-Norakku.
[5] Travel to Southern San d'Oria and talk to Hae Jakhya (G-9) and receive the overdue library book, "A Song of Love".
    - She is on top of the wall, so you will need to take the passage at F-8 to get up there.
[6] Return the book to Furakku-Norakku for your reward.
