## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Wazyih at (F-11) to begin the quest.
[2] Talk to Saliyahf at (G-7).
[3] Return to Wazyih.
[4] Walk towards the crates at (F-9).
[5] Return to Saliyahf.
[6] Speak to Wazyih again.

You must now find a targetable location in Al Zahbi to find Munahda's package. The following are the potential locations:
[7] (G-7) in the water
[8] (G-8) entrance to the south tunnel
[9] (I-10) Chocobo alley
[10] (J-7) Behind Kahah Hobichai
[11] (I-5) Flameserpent Square
[12] (H-9) Tunnel to Chocobo alley from Galeserpent Square
[13] (J-9) Galeserpent Square
    - The target moves after another player finds it.
[14] Zone back in to Aht Urhgan Whitegate from Al Zahbi for a cutscene.
[15] Return to Wazyih at (F-11) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate.
[16] Finally, speak to Saliyahf at (G-7) for your reward and to finish the quest.
