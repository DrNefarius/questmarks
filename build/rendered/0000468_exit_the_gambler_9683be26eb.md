## Walkthrough

[1] Start the quest by talking to Aurege (F-3) near Northern San d'Oria Home Point #4, outside of the Carpenter's Guild.
[2] (Optional): Talk to Nonterene (I-10) at the Parade Grounds near Victory Arch.
[3] Varchet is located in Southern San d'Oria at (L-6) right next to the fountains.
[4] You'll need to gamble against him.  Each attempt against him costs 5 gil.  Keep trading until you win.
    - Gambling against him is still possible after this quest is complete.
[5] After winning, return and talk to Aurege for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This quest does not appear in your log until it is completed.
[2] Exit the Gambler was taken a bit literally when Varchet was temporarily removed from the game in an emergency maintenance in September 2007.
    - A Moogle was put in his spot, who would inform adventurers that Varchet fell into financial ruin [unverified] or had the flu [unverified] or something and couldn't gamble.
    - This action was taken due to an exploit where his gambling mini-game could be manipulated by 3rd-party tools, causing an infinite gil glitch.
    - Varchet returned to his usual spot to try to win his fortune back a few weeks later.
