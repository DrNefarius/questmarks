## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Keshab-Menjab at the lower level of (H-9) in Mhaura.
    - (Optional) You can speak to Keshab-Menjab again for some additional dialogue.
[2] Head to Bibiki Bay - Purgonorgo Isle and fish up a Lancet Jagil.
    - Lancet Jagil seems to like Minnows as bait, but other baits may work as well.
[3] Defeat Lancet Jagils to obtain a Cleanly Snapped Rod.
[4] Return the rod to Keshab-Menjab to complete the quest.
