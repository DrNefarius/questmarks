## Walkthrough

[1] Solo mission
[2] Speak with the Quartermaster in front of the Tavern, if you're facing the West Sarutabaruta exit arch, he'll be behind you across the bridge from the tents.
[3] He will provide different players with different types of 'supplies' to bring. The following are possible options:
    :*Fresh Marjoram x1
    :*Saruta Cotton x1
    :*Moko Grass x1
[4] Specifically, they are items harvested in Giddeus/West Sarutabaruta. (Note that harvesting is not actually required as trading an item obtained from the Auction House or store NPCs does count for completion of the quest).
[5] Despite the title/quest giver, this is a Windurstian quest.
