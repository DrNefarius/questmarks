## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Michea to start quest
[2] Obtain a Copper Ingot from either the Auction House, Goldsmithing guild shops or by crafting it.
    - (Recommended method) To buy the Copper Ore to craft the Ingot from a guild shop, visit the following NPCs:
        - Teerth  Bastok Markets (H-8)
        - Visala  Bastok Markets (H-8)
        - Yabby Tanmikey  Mhaura (G-8)
        - Celestina  Mhaura (G-8)
    - To craft the ingot:
        - Use a Fire Crystal with 4 Copper Ores
[3] Trade the Ingot to Michea to complete the quest.
[4] May be repeated by zoning out of the area. No longer repeatable once you attain Bastok fame level 2.
