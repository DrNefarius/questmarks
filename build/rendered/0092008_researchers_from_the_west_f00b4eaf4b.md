## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Anastase in Ru'Lude Gardens and choose to partake in his quest to receive the Prototype attuner.
[2] At this point, you must discover four additional Proto-Waypoints hidden throughout Vana'diel by using the hints provided upon asking Anastase about his prognostications.
[3] After discovering each Proto-Waypoints, speak to the NPC standing nearby to activate teleportation:
    - <Port>: A geomagnetic fount located in a port named after a young woman. Pure white beaches... Clay... Disembowelment...
        - Selbina (G-10) - Talk to Jillia.
    - <Dwelling>: A geomagnetic fount located in a dwelling located in plains dotted by craggy outcroppings. A long-necked beast... A chef in search of meat from the beast... "The Old Lady"...
        - Mhaura (I-8) -  Talk to Zurko-Bazurko.
        - A geomagnetic fount located in a gushing oasis bursting forth from the surrounding dunes. A windmill... A large lake... An island where sand stretches to the sky...
        - Rabao (G-8) -  Talk to Quwi Orihbhe.
    - <Fort>: A geomagnetic fount located in a hard-to-find locale carved out of rock. Samurai and ninjas... Pirates... Contraband...
        - Norg (G-7) -  Talk to Wistful Bison.
[4] Once you have spoken to all four NPCs, return to Anastase.
