## Walkthrough

[1] Place a Bronze Bed in your Mog House.
[2] Wait one earth minute, then speak to the Moogle.
    - If you have enough fame, you will receive a cutscene.
    - If you get the cutscene for A Moogle Kupo d'Etat you need to speak again.
[3] Trade your Moogle a Power Bow and a Beetle Ring.
    - You can purchase a Power Bow from a Records of Eminence NPC using Sparks (Equ. Lv.10 - 19)
    - Beetle Ring +1 does NOT count
[4] When your Moogle returns from his trip after one earth minute, (but somehow never leaving your Mog House), he will give you a Mog Safe that holds 60 items.
