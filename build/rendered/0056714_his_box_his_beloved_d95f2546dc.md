## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Apururu (A) at (H-12).
    - You'll need to speak to Kupipi (A) at (H-12) beforehand if you've never done so before.
[2] Next speak with Kopuro-Popuro (A) at (F-9) in the Western Encampment near Veridical Conflux #03.
[3] You'll be tasked with retrieving a [KI] Ripe starfruit from the Bottomless Box next to him, in order to do this however you must gain its affection.
    - You're suggested to use various emotes such /cheer, /clap, /praise, /smile or /joy to gain the affection of the Bottomless Box.
        - Perform the suggested emotes from Kopuro-Popuro a few times over on the box in order to increase the chances of obtainment.
            - It is possible however, to receive the item without the use of emotes.
[4] Return to Apururu (A) to complete the quest.
