## Walkthrough

If you just completed the previous quest, Overnight Delivery, zone before continuing.
[1] Speak to Ohbiru-Dohbiru to receive a Rhinost. Canteen Rhinostery Canteen.
[2] Trade the Rhinost. Canteen Rhinostery Canteen to the Giddeus Spring at (K-12) or (E-12) in Giddeus to receive a  Canteen of Giddeus water.
[3] Return to Ohbiru-Dohbiru and trade him the Giddeus Water Canteen for your reward.
