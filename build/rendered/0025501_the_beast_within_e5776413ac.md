# The Beast Within

The game knows this quest as: The Beast Within   (quest/toau #40)

## Quest Header
Start:      Waoud/Raubahn, Aht Urhgan Whitegate - (J-10)
Description: No questions. No refusals. Obtain a "blue mage's testimony" and present yourself at the Imperial Whitegate.
Fame:       Treasures of Aht Urhgan
FLevel:     (empty)
Quest Reqs: Blue Mage's Testimony
Item Reqs:  (empty)
Level:      66+ Blue Mage
Repeatable: No
Previous:   Transformations
Next:       New Worlds Await
Title:      Master of Ambition
Reward:     Raises level cap to 75.
Expansion:  Treasures of Aht Urhgan

## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Waoud (J-10) Aht Urhgan Whitegate to flag the quest.
    - Remember to set the Records of Eminence quest under RoE → Objective → Tutorial → Level Cap Increase → Level Cap Increase: 75 (BLU)
[2] Obtain a Blue Mage's Testimony from Mamool Ja Mimickers.
[3] Trade the testimony to the Imperial Whitegate and you will be teleported to Jade Sepulcher.
[4] Trade the testimony to the Ornamental Door to initiate a battle.
    - Buffs wear upon entry.
    - If you have the "Rhapsody in Umber" Key Item, you are able to summon Trust Magic to aid you.
[5] Defeat Raubahn to complete the quest and raise your level cap to 80.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Does not need to be completed (for level limit increasing) if the player has already completed New Worlds Await.
