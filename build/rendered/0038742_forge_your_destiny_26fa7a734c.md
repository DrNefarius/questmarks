# Forge Your Destiny

The game knows this quest as: Forge Your Destiny   (quest/outlands #129)

## Quest Header
Start:      Jaucribaix, Norg (K-8)
Description: A lump of bomb steel and a sacred branch are required to make a new katana. You will have to ask Jaucribaix's pupils to find out where you can get these items.

## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Jaucribaix in Norg (K-8), near the Captain's Chamber, to begin the quest.
[2] Speak to Aeka (I-8) to receive Oriental Steel.
[3] Speak to Ranemaud (I-7) to receive a Sacred Sprig.
[4] Travel to Konschtat Highlands (D-8), trade the Oriental Steel to the ??? inside the cave to spawn a Bomb Notorious Monster named Forger, approximately level 33.
    - The level 99 Unity warp (Sleepy Mabel) will place you close to this point at (G-7).
[5] Once the Forger is defeated, a Bomb Steel will drop.
    - If you are defeated while fighting the Forger or somehow lose the Bomb Steel before the next part, Aeka can give you another Oriental Steel in exchange for a Darksteel Ore.
    - If you are doing the quest for multiple people, you must wait 4 minutes to re-spawn the Forger for another Bomb Steel.
[6] Purchase a Hatchet and travel to The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah. You will find an area off of the map in the northern part of (K-10) that will take you to a block at (L-9).  There you will find a ??? spot you may reach by climbing up a nearby rock. Trade the Hatchet to the ??? spot to spawn a Treant Notorious Monster named Guardian Treant.
    - Trbl. Hatchet does not work. It must be a regular Hatchet.
    - The level 122 Unity warp (Keeper of Heiligtum) will place you close to this point at (K-12).
    - If you are doing the quest for multiple people, you only need to kill the Treant once.
        - The Treant has a minimum respawn time of 10 minutes, so make sure everyone has the Sacred Sprig before proceeding.
[7] Once the Treant is defeated, trade the Sacred Sprig to the ??? to receive a Sacred Branch.
[8] Trade the Bomb Steel and the Sacred Branch to Jaucribaix.
[9] Return in 72 in-game hours (3 real-life hours) to receive your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] The Mumeito is required to do the AF1 quest: The Sacred Katana.  If you plan on levelling Samurai, it would be wise to hold on to it, otherwise you will have to pay Ranemaud 30,000 gil for a replacement.
