## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Talib to start quest. After the cutscene, 'accept' the quest to proceed to the next portion.
    - If you happened to decline the quest the first time, you can still restart it by speaking with Parraggoh in Bastok Mines (H-5).
        - He is on the lower level.
[2] Trade a Zinc Ore to Talib and he'll give you a [KI] Palborough Mines logs
[3] Speak to Parraggoh in Bastok Mines (H-5) to complete quest.
    - You may need to speak with him twice to complete this quest.
    - In the House on the bottom level

## After the walkthrough: Notes

This quest rewards Tenshodo fame rather than Bastok fame.
