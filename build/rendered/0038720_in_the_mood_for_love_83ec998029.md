## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Odasel outside Gems by Kshama (H-9) to begin this quest.
[2] Travel to Lufaise Meadows and defeat the Gigas there until they drop a Chameleon Dia. Chameleon Diamond.
[3] Trade the Chameleon Diamond to Odasel to complete this quest.
[4] Speak to Matoaka inside the shop for an additional cutscene.
