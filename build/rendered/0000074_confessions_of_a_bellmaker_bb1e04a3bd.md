## Walkthrough

[1] Travel to Riverne - Site #A01 and check the Stone Monument at C-7
[2] You will receive a cutscene and the Ornamented scroll.
[3] Speak to Reinberta in I-8 Bastok Markets.

[5] Head back to and check the Stone Monument in Riverne - Site #A01 for a cutscene.
[6] Examine it again to spawn Arcane Phantasm.
[7] After defeating the NM, click the Stone Monument once more to complete the quest.
