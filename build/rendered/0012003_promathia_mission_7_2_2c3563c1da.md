# Promathia Mission 7-2

The game knows this mission as: Flames in the Darkness   (mission/cop #718)

## Mission Header
Mission Name: Flames in the Darkness
Start:       Misareaux Coast - Dilapidated Gate - (F-7)
Description: Tenzen is nowhere to be found. You must locate him before returning to Jeuno to meet with Esha'ntarl.
Level:       None
Repeatable:  No
Previous:    Promathia Mission 7-1 | Chains and Bonds
Next:        Promathia Mission 7-3 | Fire in the Eyes of Men
Title:       Esha'ntarl's Comrade-in-Arms
Reward:      None
Expansion:   Chains of Promathia

## Walkthrough

[1] Make your way toward the north Dilapidated Gate in Misareaux Coast (F-7) and check it for a cutscene.
    - The Level 122 Unity Warp to Misareaux Coast will put you just south of the the gate.
    - Otherwise warp to Tavnazian Safehold (Home Point #1), exit to the Misareaux, and proceed to (F-7).
[2] Return to Sealion's Den and speak with Sueleen
[3] Travel to Ru'Lude Gardens and head toward the Palace for a cutscene.
    - The Ducal Guard's Ring you obtained previously can warp you right in front of the Palace.
[4] Travel to Upper Jeuno and check the door of the Marble Bridge Tavern for a final cutscene.
    - Optional: Talk to Mojuro-Nojuro out front before and after interacting with Marble Bridge. He'll jest about a hypothetical 33rd Marble Bridge day when talking to him afterwards: Next thing you know, they'll be having "Goblin's Day."
    - If you are on RoV missions, you might need to click the door twice for this cutscene to trigger after the RoV cutscene.
