## Walkthrough

[1] As a White Mage, talk to Narcheral (M-6) upstairs in the cathedral in Northern San d'Oria to get the quest.
    - Note: You can do the rest of the quest on any job.
[2] Head to (B-8) (Secret Beach) in Valkurm Dunes.
    - The Survival Guide teleport to Gustav Tunnel, then walking out will bring you directly next to the spawn point.
[3] Between 18:00 and 5:00 a ??? will appear near the Song Runes. Click on it to spawn Marchelute.
[4] Marchelute drops a  Tavnazia Pass.
[5] Take the Tavnazia Pass back to Narcheral to complete the quest and receive your  Blessed Hammer.
