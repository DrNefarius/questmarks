## Walkthrough

[1] Start the quest by speaking to Rondipur (A) at the base camp (Conflux 1) (G-11).
[2] Warp to Conflux #7, and head north past the golems.
[3] Cannau (A) is walking around (G-6).
    - Trade her a  Pea Soup or  Emerald Soup.
[4] Head to Conflux #6, then go west to (I-5).
[5] Search for Wanzo-Unzozo (A) in the area.
    - Speak with him then trade him a  Flint Stone.
[6] Travel to Conflux #4, then find Olavia (A) wandering back and forth between the tigers and buffalo. Speak to her.
    - Walk to (J-11) and examine the Strewn Carrion targetable location near the wall by the tigers.
    - After checking the location, return to Olavia (A) and talk to her.
[7] Return to Rondipur (A) for your reward.
Zoning is required in order to repeat this quest.
