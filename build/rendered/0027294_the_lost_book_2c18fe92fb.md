## Walkthrough

[1] Head to the Rhinostery in Windurst Waters (S) southern map (I-8) and examine the southern door for a cutscene.
    - There are two Rhinostery doors. The one on the right (south) starts this quest.
    - (Optional): Check the door a second time for a reminder of where to go.
[2] With a Mythril Beastcoin in hand, head to Giddeus and locate Quu Bokye in the lower levels.
    - The Mythril Beastcoin can be farmed or purchased on the Auction House. ( ➞ Others ➞ Beast-made)
[3] Trade Quu Bokye the Mythril Beastcoin to receive [KI] Leather-bound book.
    - (Optional): Talk to Quu Bokye before trading for some text.
[4] Return to the Rhinostery door in Windurst Waters (S).
    - (Optional): Check the door a second time for a reminder of where to go.
[5] Head to the Optistery door in Windurst Waters (S) (F-8 on the first map) for the next cutscene. They request a sheet of Vellum and a [KI] Lynx pelt.
    - The Vellum can be crafted or purchased on the Auction House. ( ➞ Materials ➞ Leathercraft)
[6] Travel to Castle Oztroja (S) and find the ??? at (G-8) on the first floor. Examine it to receive the [KI] Lynx pelt.
[7] Return to Optistery in Windurst Waters (S) and trade the Vellum to the door for a cutscene.
[8] Wait one game day then return to the Optistery to receive the Scroll of Retrace.
