## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Cid as a Dark Knight to begin quest.
You can be on any job from here on.
[2] Obtain a Darksteel Ingot from the Auction House or Crafting.
    - One is enough for multiple people doing the quest.
[3] Zone into Ordelle's Caves for a cutscene.
    - Survival guide and voidwatch warp bring you to the same entrance.
[4] Trade the Darksteel Ingot to the ??? at (I-8) on map 1 to spawn Gerwitz's Axe (NM).
    - If you zoned in from La Theine Plateau or used Survival Guide, you should take the east path (B on the wiki maps).
[5] Defeat Gerwitz's Axe and it will drop Gerwitz's Axe.
    - If you are defeated you will need another Darksteel Ingot to respawn the NM.
[6] Head South up the ramp (D on the wiki maps). At (H-11/12) drop down the hole (the southeast hole). Go east to reach map 3 (E on the wiki maps).
[7] Trade Gerwitz's Axe to the ??? at (H-8) on map 3 to spawn Gerwitz's Sword (NM).
[8] Defeat Gerwitz's Sword and it will drop Gerwitz's Sword.
[9] Head South to enter a large square room.
[10] Trade Gerwitz's Sword to the ??? at (H-10) and it will spawn Gerwitz's Soul.
[11] Defeat Gerwitz's Soul then head for La Theine Plateau at (G-10) on the same map. You will receive your reward after the cutscene.
    - All party members in zone receive credit for Gerwitz's Soul's defeat for quest progression even if they are too far to gain exp.
