# Shady Business

The game knows this quest as: Shady Business   (quest/bastok #8)

## Quest Header
Start:      Talib - Port Bastok (F-6)
Description: Talib of the Tenshodo is buying zinc. Bring him four chunks of the ore to receive gil.
Fame:       Norg

Item Reqs:  Zinc Ore x4
Level:      (empty)
Repeatable: Yes
Previous:   Beauty and the Galka
Next:       (empty)
Title:      (empty)
Reward:     350g
Expansion:  (empty)

## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Talib to start quest.
    - Homepoint #3 is the closest
[2] Obtain 4 Zinc Ore via:
    - Teerth in Bastok Markets for 200 Gil (HP #4)
    - Celestina in Mhaura for 200 Gil
    - The Auction House
    - By Mining
    - Mineral Vein
[3] Trade 4 Zinc Ore to Talib to complete the quest.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This is one of the best and fastest way to earn Tenshodo Fame. Zoning is not required to repeat the quest.
[2] After the February 2014 version update, 20 quest completions are required to max Tenshodo Fame from 1.
