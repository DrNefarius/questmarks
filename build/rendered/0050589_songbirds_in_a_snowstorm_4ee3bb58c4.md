## Walkthrough

Note: You must be on the main Wings of the Goddess mission Fate in Haze, and have zoned and waited one game day after completing Bonds That Never Die in order to start this quest.

Remember to grab a Flint Stone and fishing rod for this quest.

[1] Speak to Rholont in Southern San d'Oria (S) at (E-7).
[2] Speak to Daigraffeaux in Southern San d'Oria (S) at (I-11).
[3] Enter Beaucedine Glacier (S) for a cutscene.
[4] Examine the Colossal Footprint behind the tower at (I-7).  You will be asked to get 3 Key Items.
[5] Examine the Rocky Perch by the lake at (G-10) to get a Goliath Worm. You need to examine it at least 3 times, as you need at least 3 worms and may not receive the KI on first attempt.
    - You can easily fish up all 3 KI at the ocean, with 0 fishing skill.
[6] Fish for a [KI] Paladin lobster at the pond located at (G-10).
[7] Fish for a [KI] Scutum crab at the pond located at (J-7).
[8] Fish for a [KI] Lance fish at the pond located at (H-9).

Return to the Colossal Footprint at (I-7) for a cutscene to turn in your Key Items.

[9] Trade a Flint Stone to Charred Firewood located two levels above and immediately north of the (I-7) tower.
    - The Charred Firewood is north of the Campaign Arbiter.
[10] Examine the Compressed Snow that is located at (H-10) near/just south of the tower in the lowest part of the zone.
[11] Examine the Compressed Snow again to spawn a Notorious Monster named Orcish Bloodletter.
    - The Orcish Bloodletter hits very hard, AoEs, and double attacks frequently. You may also wish to clear out any nearby undead to avoid unexpected adds due to lowered HP.
        - However at ilevel 119 with trusts, it's still trivial. Don't worry about it.
[12] Examine the Compressed Snow again for a cutscene that ends the quest and your reward.
