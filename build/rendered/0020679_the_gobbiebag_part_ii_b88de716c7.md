## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Bluffnix in Muckvix's Junk Shop located in Lower Jeuno at (H-9).
[2] Trade Bluffnix a square of ram leather, a mythril ingot, a square of wool cloth, and a turquoise; or a Goblin Stew 880.
    - Ram Leather: AH ➞ Materials ➞ Leathercraft
    - Mythril Ingot: AH ➞ Materials ➞ Goldsmithing
    - Wool Cloth: AH ➞ Materials ➞ Clothcraft
    - Turquoise: AH ➞ Materials ➞ Goldsmithing
