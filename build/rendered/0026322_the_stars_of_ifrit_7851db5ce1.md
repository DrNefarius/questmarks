## Walkthrough

[1] You may work on this quest only after you have obtained an [KI] Airship pass as its completion requires a trip on the airship.
[2] If you fulfill all the prerequisites of the quest, then speak to Agapito to begin quest.
[3] During a Full Moon at night time, board the San d'Oria-Jeuno airship.
    - You can use the <code>/clock on</code> command to check the current moon phase.
[4] Search for an abnormal ??? somewhere on the ship. It will only appear during this very specific time frame.
[5] Touch the ??? to receive the [KI] Carrier pigeon letter.
[6] Return to Agapito to complete the quest.
