# Materiel Storm I (S)

The game knows this mission as: Materiel Storm I (S)   (mission/campaign #91)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Resource procurement
Description: Procure the items indicated by the kingdom quartermaster.
Orders:      Obtain list of wanted items from Quartermaster and submit items to her.
NPC Name:    Rasdinice
Zone:        Southern San d'Oria (S)
pos:         I-9
Campaign Rank: Brass Ribbon of Service §§
Time:        None
Level:       Any
Rewards:     * Increases Nation's Supplies.
            * 810 Experience Points
            * 1350 Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Solo mission
[2] Speak with the Quartermaster at (I-9), if you're facing Rasdinice, she'll be behind you across the way near the tents.
[3] She will provide different players with different types of 'supplies' to bring. The following are possible options:
    :*Wind Crystal x12
    :*Fire Crystal x12
    :*Earth Crystal x12
    :*Ice Crystal x12
    :*Light Crystal x12
    :*Dark Crystal x12
    :*Lightning Crystal x12
