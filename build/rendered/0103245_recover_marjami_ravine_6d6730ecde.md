## Walkthrough
[1] Travel to Marjami Ravine or Doh Gates and locate a Lost article.
[2] There will always be one (and only one) available Lost article in each zone. The Lost article will despawn and immediately respawn when retrieved (it can respawn in the same position).
[3] The Lost article does not show up on Wide Scan.
[4] Known lost article positions:
    - Marjami Ravine:
        - E-6, near the Apkallus next to the tree.
        - K-9
        - J-9
        - K-12
        - F/G-8, over the logs near the Stryx.
        - G9, south of bridge, east edge near H9
    - Dho Gates:
        - SW E-7
        - I-7 past reive
        - I/J-11 past reive
        - H-9 - East of the Water
        - F/G-8
        - J-9
        - J-7
