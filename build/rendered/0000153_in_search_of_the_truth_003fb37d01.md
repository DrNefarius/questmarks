## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Tressia (J-6, Third Floor: Patrol HQ)
[2] Optional - Speak to Mengrenaux (J-6, Third Floor: Patrol HQ)
[3] Optional - Speak to Chemioue (J-6, Third Floor: Patrol HQ)
[4] Speak to the following in any order to watch short cutscenes
    - Fouagine (I-9, Third Floor: under archway)
    - Noam (G-9, Second Floor: lower section)
    - Zadant (H-8, Second Floor: on the ramp down to First Floor)
    - Raminey (J-7 Third Floor: Home Point #3)
[5] Return to Tressia (J-6)
    - You will be asked to put the information in chronological order. There are two known solutions:
        - (Fouagine, Zadant, Raminey, Noam)
        - (Fouagine, Zadant, Noam, Raminey)
        - The quest seems to randomly accept one answer or the other, rerolling which one is correct with each attempt. Therefore, you can repeatedly attempt either combination and it should eventually work.
[6] You will be asked to view the data gathered by the other members of the patrol and find out the <pos> where the report was gathered
    - Reports 1 and 2 - Speak to Mengrenaux (J-6)
    - Reports 3 and 4 - Speak to Chemioue (J-6)
[7] Return to Tressia (J-6) and answer:
    - Report 1:
    - Report 2: <H-8>
    - Report 3: <G-9>
    - Report 4: <J-7>
[8] Follow the trail
    - Start by checking the ??? next to Noam (G-9, Second Floor) to receive Shaded Cruse
    - Go north and up the ramp until you find another ??? (G-7)
    - Continue upward and take the next left where there's another ??? near the open doors (G-8)
    - Continue through the hallway and check the ??? at (H-8)
    - Go left at the end of the hallway and check the ??? at the bottom right corner of (H-8)
[9] Return to Tressia (J-6, Third Floor: Patrol HQ)
[10] Go to Ondieulix (I-7, Second Floor: Near Home Point #1) for your reward
