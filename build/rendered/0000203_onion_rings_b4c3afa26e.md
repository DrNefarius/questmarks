## Walkthrough

[1] Zone after completing the previous quest.
[2] Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo for a storytelling sequence to begin this quest.
    - If you already have the Old ring, you'll skip ahead to the final cut scene.
[3] Head to Castle Oztroja and head to the door at (I-8) on the first map.
[4] Using the handles in front of the door, intentionally activate the trap door and fall down it.
[5] Check the Blank Target in this room to receive an Old ring.
[6] Return to Kohlo-Lakolo who will send you to the "House of the Hero" Windurst Walls (G-3) for your reward.
    - If you take too long to return the ring the quest will end when you return to Kohlo-Lakolo and you will not have to go to the "House of the Hero" afterwards. The quest is still considered complete.
[7] Zone and return to Kohlo-Lakolo to receive your reward.
