## Walkthrough

[1] Easily soloable mission
[2] After confirming the mission with Rasdinice, go to a San d'Orian controlled Campaign area and talk to the Gate Sentry. They will either
    - Give you the Campaign Supplies directly, or
    - Tell you that "The supply unit carrying rations and munitions to our stronghold in this area was attacked en route, and forced to abandon the cargo. You are to locate the supplies and deliver them to their final destination."
    List of Gate Sentries:
[3] East Ronfaure (S): (J-11)
[4] Vunkerl Inlet (S): (E-7)
[5] Batallia Downs (S): (F-8)
    List of ??? positions:
[6] East Ronfaure (S): (I-11) surrounded by Easy Prey orcs. (they will aggro at lvl75)
[7] Vunkerl Inlet (S): (G-6) Inside the small cave. Guarded by a loan Dire Bat (Easy Prey to lvl75)
[8] Batallia Downs (S): (G-8) on top of the hill next to a stone pillar
    Check the ??? to obtain the Key Item "Campaign Supplies".
    List of Campaign NPCs:
[9] East Ronfaure (S): (H-8)
[10] Vunkerl Inlet (S): (G-10)
[11] Batallia Downs (S): (J-8)
    Once you've arrived at the Campaign NPC, talk to him/her and you will be told to make an official report to Sir Rasdinice.
    After talking to Rasdinice, the mission is complete.
