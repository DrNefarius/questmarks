## Walkthrough

[1] Solo mission
[2] Speak with Mariette at (G-9).
[3] She will provide you with a Fiendtrapper and 5 Blank fiend plates.
[4] The Fiendtrapper will not work if you under the effect of Sneak or Invisible (Status) or too far away.
[5] Take pictures of different Quadavs. Campaign Battle mobs are valid. Picture must be taken from the front of the mob, she will not accept pictures from the back or side of the mob.
[6] Each picture you take will create a Fiend Plate with the mob's name on it and take up an inventory slot. The exposed Fiend Plates do not stack.
[7] Return to Mariette and trade her one Fiend Plate at a time.
[8] After she accepts 3 Fiend Plates the mission is complete.
[9] If you have extra Fiend Plates after completing the mission they may be used for Deep Cover II (B).
[10] Unused Blank fiend plates may be utilized for Deep Cover II (B).
[11] You must drop the Fiendtrapper to start Deep Cover II (B).
