# The Voracious Resurgence Mission 9-4

The game knows this mission as: The Prime Weapons   (mission/tvr #542)

[1] (Optional) Speak to Andreine in the Celennia Memorial Library for a hint regarding Oshasha's whereabouts.
[2] Travel to Leafallia and interact with the Odyssean Passage for a cutscene.
[3] Trade a Stygian Ash Stygian Ash to the Odyssean Passage for another cutscene. You will receive [KI] Bronze-hued tag.
    - Stygian Ash Stygian Ash drops from the Pixie family, which are easily found in Grauberg (S) around Witchfire Glen.
        - The most accessible way to get to Witchfire Glen is using your Lightsworm on the blue sparkles near Batallia Downs Survival Guide. Once in Walk of Echoes, turn around and interact with the conflux just behind you to teleport to Witchfire Glen.
        - Alternatively, either Campaign Warp to Grauberg or use the Survival Guide.
        - Recall-Pashh will also bring you near the conflux in Pashhow Marshlands (S) which you may also use teleport to Witchfire Glen, but it requires you complete late game voidwatch quests.
[4] Travel to Outer Ra'Kaznar (K-3), Upper Floor (map 1).
    - To get here, it is easy to follow the marked path on the Outer Ra'Kaznar page's maps.

    - Be careful, as there are Umbrils around the area.
[6] Return to Leafallia and interact with the Odyssean Passage for the final cutscene and your reward, completing the mission.
[7] Continue with the creation of your Prime Weapon by following the section below. You will need it to progress in the coming quests.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] At this point, you can now open up access to two new Records of Eminence objectives. See the NPC Elijah in Upper Jeuno near Home Point #1 for more information.
[2] You will need a Prime Weapon that can deal damage (NOT the Shield or Instrument!) for future missions, so make sure to create one at some point.

## After the walkthrough: Starting your Prime Weapon

[1] Speak to Oggbi in Port Bastok (E-6) Homepoint #3 in the Steaming Sheep after the mission for a cutscene.
    - You will receive [KI] Uran-Mafran's orb after the cutscene.
[2] Return to Gama-Shama in the Silver Knife, he will ask for 10k Gallimaufry to choose your Prime Weapon.
    - There is a game day wait afterwards.
    - You can purchase multiple Prime Weapons as long as you have the Gallimaufry to do so.

## After the walkthrough: A Word on Choosing Your Weapon

You will need a Prime Weapon upgraded to stage 2 to be able to enter the final mission fight of this storyline. Your choice will limit the jobs you can take to that fight!
[1] The fight is more difficult than every other fight in The Voracious Resurgence, and you will probably need the help of other players.
    If you do not intend to forge your Prime Weapon all the way to the final stage, and you have a support job you may want to use later, it is best to choose the melee weapon for that job:
[2] Lorg Mor (Level 119 III) for WHM or GEO.
[3] Opashoro (Level 119 III) for BLM, SMN, or SCH.
[4] Mpu Gandring (Level 119 III) for THF, BRD, or DNC.
[5] Caliburnus (Level 119 III) for PLD, BLU, or RDM.
    - Other job selections can be seen on the Prime Weapons page.
