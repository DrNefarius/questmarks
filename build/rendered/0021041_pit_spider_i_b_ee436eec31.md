## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus.
[2] Travel to the Beastmen Ensign at (G-12) in Grauberg.
    - One can enter solo (very easy at Item Level 119) or use a party.
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ruhotz Silvermines.
[4] A party of 3 Quadavs, 2 soldiers and 1 caravaneer, will immediately start slowly running towards the exit.
    - The Caravaneer continues to run to the exit no matter what, the two soldiers will attack your party.
    - Should the Caravaneer make it to the end of the area, which is quite large, the mission is failed.
[5] Once all three Quadavs are defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
[6] You must exit the area by using the Twilight Aureola for Hieronymus to acknowledge the mission as successful!
[7] Return and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] There seems to be no time limit to how long you can take to get to Ruhotz Silvermines.
[2] All 3 of the Quadavs are sleepable by Lullaby or the Sleep spells.
[3] Repetitive stun/sleep/bind is helpful in this battle.
[4] The Quadavs are very weak, only having around 2000 HP.
