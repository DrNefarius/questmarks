## Walkthrough

[1] Go to Gusgen Mines or Palborough Mines or Ifrit's Cauldron and mine for the [KI] sturdy metal strip.
[2] Go to East Ronfaure or Jugner Forest or Ghelsba Outpost or Yuhtunga Jungle and log for the [KI] piece of rugged tree bark.
[3] Go to Giddeus or West Sarutabaruta and harvest the [KI] savory lamb roast.
[4] After you have all three [KI] key items, examine the Inconspicuous Door in Upper Jeuno (at (H-8), across from Durable Shields) three times for three cutscenes.
    - You do not need to collect all three before the cutscenes. Each item triggers one cutscene. This method is just more efficient.
    :
[5] These [KI] key items can be obtained in any order, even before the moogle even asks for them.
[6] For maximum efficiency, be sure that your inventory is full before attempting to gather at a node, allowing repeated attempts to obtain the [KI] key item.
    - The node will persist even if the gathered item can't be received, saving you the hassle of chasing new nodes to receive the [KI] key item.
    - Obtaining the [KI] key item is truly random, and has taken 50+ attempts for unlucky characters. Tool breaks are uncommon but can happen. Bring at least 1 stack of each tool to avoid having to run back to town.

## After the walkthrough: "A Moogle Kupo d'Etat" Key

[1] Re-obtain another [KI] sturdy metal strip, [KI] piece of rugged tree bark, and [KI] savory lamb roast.
[2] Head to Port Jeuno and ask Squintrox Dryeyes (G-8) for a [KI] Blue coral key to make the exchange.
    - The key can be received once per day.
[3] Use the [KI] Blue coral key (Level 40) to open the red treasure coffer at Lower Jeuno (J-8).
    - Rewards are listed on the Add-on Scenario Rewards page.
