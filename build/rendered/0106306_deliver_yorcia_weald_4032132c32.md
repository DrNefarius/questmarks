## Walkthrough
[1] Once you accept the assignment, you will -not- be provided with any supplies. You will receive Yor. FS resource container at the Frontier Station.
    - As of Feb 18, 2014 update, Waypoints and Warp spell can be used to complete this task. Yor. FS resource container will not break.
[2] Speak to the Station Administrator to receive Yor. FS resource container.
[3] Return to the Task Delegator to deliver the supplies and receive your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes
[1] Movement speed increasing effects recommended.
