## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Exoroche (A) in Abyssea - La Theine at (H-7) near Veridical Conflux #04.
[2] Trade Exoroche (A) one of the following items for your reward.
    - Rusty Kunai for 40 Cruor.
    - Rusty Spear for 80 Cruor.
    - Rusty Zaghnal for 120 Cruor.
    - Rusty Shield for 320 Cruor.
[3] All items can be fished up with little effort from any of the four ponds. Any rod and bait seem to work, and you can hold multiple of each item. When on the line, they give the same "Something pulling at your line" message. A lure is recommended so that your bait isnt eaten.
[4] To repeat this quest, you do not need to speak to Exoroche (A) again. Simply trade him items from your inventory.
[5] You may also receive a random Evolith as an added reward when trading one of these items in to Exoroche. Danallen (talk) 15:09, 8 November 2025 (UTC)
Zoning is not required to repeat this quest.
