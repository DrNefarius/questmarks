## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Levil in the Pioneers' Coalition (E-8) for a cutscene to begin the Quest.
[2] Speak with Ploh Trishbahk at (K-9) in Eastern Adoulin for another cutscene.
    - At this point the Quest will now show in your log.
[3] Go to Escha - Ru'Aun and examine the blue ??? at (H-10) at the top of the stairs to the left of the entrance for a cutscence. You will receive [KI] Inert geomancy bell.
[4] Return to Ploh Trishbahk for another cutscene.
[5] Meet Arciela, Ygnas, and Darrcuiln in Kamihr Drifts at (H-5), and examine the Alpine Trail for a cutscene. This will take you into a zone named Mount Kamihr.
    - This is the very most northern-middle point on the map.
        - The fastest way there is take Bivouac #3, go into the northern tunnel and follow the left wall, always heading north.
    - Selecting the options "Lifestream" then "Communion" continues the conversation in a constructive way.
[6] Venture to the Crag closest to your home Nation (starting nation, not current allegiance) and click the Ergon Locus ??? located beneath the stairs leading up to the Telepoint to obtain [KI] Fistful of familiar soil and view a cutscene.
    - If you are in the middle of Dances with Luopans and have not received a [KI] Luopan, you need to turn the [KI] Fistful of familiar soil into a [KI] Luopan first, then go get another [KI] Fistful of familiar soil for the cutscene.
    - Cannot be obtained while possessing [KI] Holla crystal of gales, [KI] Dem crystal of dunes, or [KI] Mea crystal of flames from Treasures of the Earth of the same type.
        - e.g. having the Holla crystal prevents obtaining the Fistful of familiar soil from the Crag of Holla Ergon Locus ???.
[7] Enter Leafallia for a cutscene.
[8] Examine the glowing spot Drifting Feather at (H-7) for another cutscene.
[9] Examine the Drifting Feather again to enter a BCNM.
    - Your opponent will be Siren Prime.
        - The fight is Content Level 119.
        - Trusts may be used in the fight.
        - Siren absorbs Light Skillchains.
        - Siren has a move that can Charm.
[10] Upon defeating Siren, a cutscene will begin where you will be able to choose your reward.
