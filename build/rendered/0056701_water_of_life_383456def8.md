## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Miageau (A) at (L-11), Veridical Conflux #06, to begin the quest.
[2] You will receive the following four key items:
    - [KI] Vial of purification agent (blk.)
    - [KI] Vial of purification agent (brz.)
    - [KI] Vial of purification agent (slv.)
    - [KI] Vial of purification agent (gld.).
[3] Examine the 4 Murky Pond targetable locations in Abyssea - La Theine at (L-10), (K-11), and 2 at (H-7). These are the bodies of water you can see on your map.
    - With each pond examined, your key items will be replaced with:
        - [KI] Black-labeled vial
        - [KI] Bronze-labeled vial
        - [KI] Silver-labeled vial
        - [KI] Gold-labeled vial.
[4] Return to Miageau (A) to complete the quest.
Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
