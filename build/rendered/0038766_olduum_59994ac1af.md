## Walkthrough

[1] Make sure you do not leave without picking up a few Pickaxe.
[2] Speak to Dkhaaya for a cutscene and to obtain Dkhaaya's research journal.
[3] Enter Aydeewa Subterrane through the cave at (I-7) in Bhaflau Thickets.
    - It is a short trek north if exiting Whitegate at (G/H-7).
[4] Head to the room at (H-9) and begin mine in this room until you find one of three key items; Electrocell, Electropot, or Electrolocomotive.
    - This is the same room where you finish the Blue Mage Flag Quest.
        - Be aware that approaching the platform will complete this quest and warp you back to Whitegate.
[5] Return to Dkhaaya who will reward you with a Lightning Band.
[6] At this point the quest is completed, however, if you trade the Lightning Band to the Leypoint at Wajaom Woodlands (G-8) it will transform into an Olduum Ring.

== Repeating the Quest ==

If you drop your Olduum Ring, speak to Dkhaaya to repeat the quest.
