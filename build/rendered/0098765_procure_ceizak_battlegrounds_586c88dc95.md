## Walkthrough
[1] Obtain a number of Hatchets, or, preferably, Trailblazing Hatchet +1s.
    - Bring a large number. The break rate for HELM tools in Adoulin areas is very high, even with the key items from Kithvalio occasionally preventing tool breakage.
[2] Travel to Ceizak Battlegrounds or Yahse Hunting Grounds
[3] Locate Logging Points and use the Hatchet:
   Ceizak Battlegrounds: E8, F8, F10, H7, J8
[4] Repeat until you've obtained five items.
    - Note that gathering fatigue sets rather quickly, depending on various conditions such as Ionis effects.
    - Fatigue slowly decays over time, allowing one additional successful gathering attempt approximately every two minutes.
    - Fatigue also completely resets when you change zones.
[5] You do not need to keep the items you obtained during the assignment. You can safely toss or sell any HELM results and still complete the assignment.
