## Walkthrough

[1] Obtain an Ordrynite
    - Enter Pso'Xja from Beaucedine Glacier (G-9) and kill Cryptonberry Plaguer or Cryptonberry Stalker for them.
        - Outpost Warp or Survival Guide to Beaucedine Glacier -> Mount -> (G-9)
        - HP1 in Pso'Xja -> Escape -> Mount -> (G-9)
    - Do Chocobo Digging in Beaucedine Glacier
        - With Rank 10 Chocobo Digging, you can probably get 10 in a day (accepting that they are Rare, so you have to turn each in).
[2] Trade it to Raibaht to complete quest
