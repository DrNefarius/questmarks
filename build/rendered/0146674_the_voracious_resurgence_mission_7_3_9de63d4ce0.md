## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Shantotto in Windurst Walls (K-7) HP #3 to begin the mission.
    - Koru-Moru will ask for the Spectral Crimson Spectral Crimson, Spect. Goldenrod Spect. Goldenrod and Luminicloth Luminicloth.
        - Spectral Crimson Spectral Crimson is a drop from Fomor BRD, PLD, RDM in Misareaux Coast & Lufaise Meadows.
            - Misareaux Coast Near Sacrarium (E-7), the campfire at (J-11) and Lufaise Meadows at Blueblade Fell (J-6) during night (20:00 - 4:00) hours.
                - Unity Warp 125 to Lufaise Meadows brings you somewhat near Blueblade Fell.
                - Fomors share spawns with each other and may not respawn as the desired job.
        - Spect. Goldenrod Spect. Goldenrod is a drop from Haunts in Kuftal Tunnel or Srei Ap in Uleguerand Range near (K-7).
            - Take the Survival Guide to Kuftal Tunnel and head to (H-7).
        - Recommended places to farm Luminicloth Luminicloth include:
            - Fei'Yin: Revenant, Utukku, and Wekufe.
            - Abyssea - Konschtat: Qaitu.
[2] Trade the items to Shantotto for a cutscene and your rewards.
    - You will receive [KI] Memorian, the Magicked Doll after the cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: Retrieving Lost Memories

Optional Quests Background information received makes these optional cutscenes worth seeing.
[1] Speaking to the following NPCs will give a cutscene.
    - Honoi-Gomoi in Windurst Waters, second floor from the back of the Trader's Home at (E-7, South Map)
    - Dancing Wolf in Rabao (G-7)
    - Rahi Fohlatti in Rabao (G-9)
    - Ghatsad in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-7)
    - Zone into Palborough Mines from North Gustaberg
        - Survival Guide to Oldton Movalpolos and walk back outside to North Gustaberg, then head north.
[2] The two below are part of The Voracious Resurgence Mission 7-4.
    - Trion and Excenmille, The Voracious Resurgence Mission 7-4.
    - Rahal and Altennia, The Voracious Resurgence Mission 7-4.
[3] After restoring all memories, you will receive the [KI] Depleted Memorian.
    - This will only be received after you have finished The Voracious Resurgence Mission 7-4.
[4] Return to Shantotto for a cutscene, where she will take the [KI] Depleted Memorian.
