## Walkthrough

[1] Obtain 2 Darksteel Ores via the Auction House, Mog Garden, Mining, etc.
[2] Speak to Mighty Fist on the first floor of the Metalworks (HP #2) to begin the quest.
[3] Trade him the two ores to end the quest.
[4] You may immediately repeat this quest.
