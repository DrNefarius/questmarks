## Walkthrough

[1] Examine the Door: Merchant's House on the second floor, Lower Jeuno (G-11), above the chocobo stables by Home Point #1. Agree to help him out.
[2] (Optional) Speak to Shalott, Upper Jeuno (G-7) (Outside Chocobo Stables, next to Mapitoto) for a cutscene.
[3] Examine the Night Flowers down the ramp in Qufim Island (F-8) between 21:30 and 4:00 for a cutscene.
    - A Kraken (37-38) may spawn above the Night Flowers.
[4] Examine the Door: Merchant's House in Lower Jeuno for a cutscene and your rewards.
    - (checking the door again prior to completing the next quest simply says "Looks like nobody's home.")
