## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Zauko (A) at (K-9) near Conflux #5.
[2] Then interact with the Coal Casket behind him for a [KI] Torch coal.
[3] Walk to the second encampment near Conflux #6 and place the coal into the Coal Casket next to the flames.
    - The Torch coal is destroyed when you use a conflux, so you must walk to each encampment.
[4] Afterwards, walk to the third encampment near Conflux #7 and place the coal into the Coal Casket next to the flames.
[5] You may then use Conflux #7 to warp back to Conflux #5 to get back to Zauko (A) for your reward.
    - If you accidentally skip a Coal Casket and your Torch coal is destroyed, all you have to do is acquire it again then return to the missed one. You do not have to do both with the same coal.

Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
