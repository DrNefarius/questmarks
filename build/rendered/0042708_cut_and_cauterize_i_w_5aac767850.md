# Cut and Cauterize I (W)

The game knows this mission as: Cut and Cauterize I (W)   (mission/campaign #411)

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo to begin this Op.
[2] Exit to West Sarutabaruta (S) and hug the left wall to (I-9) to find the Allied Ensign. Once the party leader selects the "enter" option, you will be transported into Ghoyu's Reverie.
[3] At the start, two Student Salvemixers will pop. Speak to them to have them follow you, then proceed south to a room with 5 wounded solders.
[4] Speaking to the Salvemixers will show the following menu options:
    - Give the physician orders.
        - Commence healing, stat!
        - Specify healing methods.
            - Prescribe what type of treatment?
                - Cures and physical healing.
                - Status ailment recovery.
        - Offer moral support.
        - Rest and recover.
        - Issue no orders.
[5] When you are close to a solder who is injured, use the "Commence healing, stat!" command to order the Salvemixer to use a single Cure spell. Multiple uses will be required.
    - If a solder says "P-p-poisoned... C-c-can't b-b-breathe..." or "You must help me! I can't move my arms or legs!", use the "Prescribe what type of treatment?" option and chose "Status ailment recovery" to switch the Salvemixer to use the appropriate status spells.
        - After toggling the treatment type, order the Salvemaker to "Commence healing, stat!" to have them cast the status cure spell.
        - Since this is a toggle, be sure to use the "Prescribe what type of treatment?" option to change back to "Cures and physical healing" mode after the status ailment is cured.
    - When a solder is sufficiently cured, his/her name will turn green and will say "I owe you my life. Many thanks!".
        - This happens below 100% health. The Salvemixers will not continue to heal a sufficiently healed NPC.
    - If the Salvemixer runs out of MP, use the "rest and recover" option to restore MP before continuing.
    - If the Salvemixer says "I'm not sure I'm cut out for this...", use the "Offer moral support" option.
        - Using this option too often seems to annoy the Salvemixer.
[6] When four solders are cured, you will get the message that the operation is complete. (You can still heal the final soldier.)
    - Return to the Dawn Aureola to receive your XP and finish the Campaign Op.
    - You will not receive XP if you are too far from the Dawn Aureola when the leader examines it.
[7] Return to Emhi Tchaoryo to complete this Op.
