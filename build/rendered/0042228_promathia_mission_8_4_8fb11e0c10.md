## Walkthrough

[1] Zone into The Garden of Ru'Hmet and pass through the Cermet Portal to get to the main Garden elevator.
    - Use the Home Point you obtained earlier selecting, Home Point #1 of The Garden of Ru'Hmet.
[2] Choose to descend to zone into Empyreal Paradox.
[3] Check the Transcendental Radiance for a cutscene and then check it again to enter the "Dawn" Battleground.

## After the walkthrough: Boss Fight

This fight was initially uncapped at level 75. Level 99 players should have little to no difficulty with this mission.
[1] See final cutscenes below to receive your ring after beating Promathia.
[2] Prishe joins you for this fight.
    - Prishe can be healed if she takes damage.
    - If she's KO'd, you can cast Raise on her and she will resume fighting with full HP and no weakness.
    - Prishe will eat an Ambrosia when you start to fight.
    - Prishe uses a Daedalus Wing when you start to fight.
    - Prishe uses the Weapon Skill Auroral Uppercut.
    - Prishe can casts Divine Magic on Promathia and Cure both herself and the party.
[3] Selh'teus joins you for this fight as well.
    - Selh'teus cannot be healed by players, but has several abilities he can use to heal himself.
    - Uses the Weapon skill Luminous Lance, which does a bit of damage and stuns Promathia.
    - If Selh'teus is KO'd, the battle immediately ends in failure.
[4] If the entire party is KO'd at any point during the fight, you will be immediately removed from the battlefield.
[5] Promathia has low evasion and is immune to Stun.
[6] Promathia has two forms. Once you beat the first form, you can rest before engaging the second form.
    - After a short period of time, Prishe will engage.

## After the walkthrough: Final Cutscenes

[1] Wait until Japanese midnight to finish the following epilogue cutscenes for Chains of Promathia.
[2] You can complete these in any order.
[3] While these cutscenes are not displayed in your Mission log, they all have mission names. These names are referenced in the Goblin Footprints and Cutscene Replay Bards.
    - Louverance - A Name for the Ages:
        - Head to Southern San d'Oria (Home Point #4) and talk to Hinaree at (B-6) for a cutscene.
        - Zone into Uleguerand Range for a cutscene. (Any Home Point you may have in the zone, or Voidwatch Warp via Atmacite Refiner -> Tavnazia -> Uleguerand Range, works.)
        - Return to Southern San d'Oria and approach Count Caffaule's Manor (B-6) for a cutscene.
    - Chebukkis - A Meteoric Reunion:
        - Head to Port Windurst (Home Point #1) and talk to Chipmy-Popmy at the Fishing Guild (C-8) for a cutscene (NOT just dialgue). You may need to talk to this NPC more than once until he mentions Bibiki Bay - Purgonorgo Isle lest you take another boat ride stinking of missed cutscene shame.
        - Head to Bibiki Bay - Purgonorgo Isle via the ferry from Bibiki Bay and talk to ??? Warmachine (H-11) for a cutscene.
            - The fastest way is to take the Voidwatch Warp from any present-day Atmacite Refiner if you've obtained the Hyacinth stratum abyssite II (Tavnazia > Bibiki Bay). There's an atmacite refiner at Home Point #1 in either Tavnazian Safehold or the nearby Windurst Waters.
            - If you have a summer seasonal +1 item with Enchantment: Teleport (Purgonorgo Isle), that would take you to the same location. Those items are:
                - Custom Gilet +1 / Custom Top +1 / Magna Gilet +1 / Magna Top +1 / Wonder Maillot +1 / Wonder Top +1 / Elder Gilet +1 /Savage Top +1
        - When the cutscene ends, you will receive a colored drop that appears to be random.
            - The odds of each are unknown, but it should be one of the following: Red (STR+5), Clear (INT+5), Green (AGI+5), Yellow (VIT+5) / Purple (DEX+5), Blue (MND+5), White (CHR+5), or Black (Instant Warp). Stat buffs last 15 minutes, and all drops will give the 1 hour detrimental Medicated effect.
    - Shikarees - In the Name of Justice:
        - Zone into Mhaura for a cutscene.
    - Jabbos - Newfound Family:
        - Zone into Oldton Movalpolos (Survival Guide works) for a cutscene.
    - Tenzen - Return to the East:
        - Head to Metalworks and talk to Cid (H-8) for a cutscene.
[4] Once you've completed the previous cutscenes, head to Ru'Lude Gardens and approach the Palace for a cutscene.
[5] Head to Upper Jeuno and click on the Door: Marble Bridge for a cutscene.
[6] Click on the Marble Bridge door again to choose one of the 3 rings: , , .
    - You can continue with the cutscenes without making your decision yet.
[7] Head to Tavnazian Safehold (Home Point #3) and check the Walnut Door (K-7).
[8] Head to Lufaise Meadows and approach the Blueblade Fell (J-6) for the final cutscene.
    - Lufaise Meadows UNM 119 or 125 Warp is the fastest route.
    - The cutscene trigger is right after the first barrel on the ground in the Fell.
[9] After all cutscenes are over, you have option to start both of the quests Requiem of Sin and Storms of Fate.

## After the walkthrough: Optional

[1] Trust: Ulmia: After defeating Promathia, examine the Dilapidated Gate in Misareaux Coast (I-11) to obtain Ulmia as a Trust.
    - You can take the exit from Tavnazian Safehold (G-6) to reach it quicker.
    - This can be done prior to the final cutscenes above.
    - Further progress into the CoP epilogue quests (Storms of Fate, Shadows of the Departed, Apocalypse Nigh) can prevent this cutscene.
[2] Trust: Prishe: After finishing all the final cutscenes above, examine the Walnut Door in Tavnazian Safehold (K-7, Top floor) to obtain Prishe as a Trust.
    - Further progress into the CoP epilogue quests (Storms of Fate, Shadows of the Departed, Apocalypse Nigh can prevent this cutscene.
[3] Atma Rewards: You can now purchase the [KI] Atma of the Banisher from an Atma Fabricant in Abyssea.
[4] Diabolos cutscene: 'Waking Dreams pt. 9.
    - If Waking Dreams was already completed, you can see this cutscene from the Goblin Footprint in The Shrouded Maw (near Pso'Xja Home Point #1), by trading an item or gil to the footprint.
[5] Tango with a Tracker (level 75+) is available.
    - Requiem of Sin (level 90+) will be available after completing 'Tango with a Tracker' and waiting until the next Conquest Tally.
[6] Return to the Depths (level 75+ to solo).
[7] Waking the Beast (level 99+ to solo).
