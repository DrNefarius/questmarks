## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo to begin this Op.
[2] Travel to the Allied Ensign at I-9 in West Sarutabaruta (S) (hug the left wall as soon as you zone out of Windurst Waters (S).
    - Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ghoyu's Reverie.
[3] The Federation Infantry NPC will run toward a Trained Crab and begin attacking.
    - During this time, you will see messages that display how much attention he is giving to the fight:
        - The knight seems unfocused...
        - The knight has begun to train harder!
        - The knight is fighting with vigor!
[4] Let the Infantry fight solo until "the knight is fighting with vigor" message appears, then kill the Crab.
    - If the soldier never gains focus, you lose the mission.
[5] Once the crab is defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
    - Exit via the Dawn Aureola to receive your XP and finish the Campaign Op.
[6] Return to Emhi Tchaoryo for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] There seems to be no time limit to how long you can take to get to Ghoyu's Reverie.
[2] The Infantry will eventually die if left completely alone, but can cast Cure III on himself and can tank the Crab long enough to get the "fighting with vigor" message.
[3] The crab seems to have around 8,000-10,000 HP.
