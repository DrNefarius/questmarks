## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Kaede in Port Bastok at (J-5) - nearest to Home Point #1 (E) - in the north most house for a cutscene.
    - (if she says "I heard somebody at the warehouse saying that the people from Aht Urhgan are sneaky.", disregard it and talk to her again.)
[2] Talk to Kagetora at (F-6), inside the door to Warehouse 2.
[3] Return to Kaede's house and speak with Ensetsu at (I-5) for a cutscene.
    - The quest log entry will appear after this cutscene.
[4] Travel to (K-8) within Korroloka Tunnel, Map 5.
    - The quickest entrance is from Western Altepa Desert at (M-8), via the Survival Guide. This entrance will bring you directly to Map 5.
    - Although the Survival Guide to Zeruhn Mines is also close.

[5] Click on the ??? to spawn 3 Korroloka Leeches.
    - These leeches will link with any Thread Leeches in the vicinity.
    - If for some reason you click on the ??? and the Leeches don't pop, try relogging.
[6] Once the leeches are defeated and after the bodies despawn, click on the ??? to receive the [KI] Strangely shaped coral.
    - Multiple players may do this quest at the same time for the coral:
        - Have one member spawn it. After the Leeches are defeated and despawn, have the other players select the ??? for the coral before the spawning player.
        - Note: If the person who spawned the leeches is KO'd, do not raise them until after the Korroloka Leeches have been defeated. Otherwise, they will respawn immediately.
[7] Return to Ensetsu  in Port Bastok at (I-5).
[8] Travel to Norg and speak with Ryoma at (H-8), who will give you the [KI] Sealed dagger.
[9] Return to Ensetsu to complete the quest.
    - (Optional) Speak to Kaede afterwards for some closing dialogue.
