## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus.
[2] Travel to the Beastmen Ensign at (G-12) in Grauberg.
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ruhotz Silvermines.
[4] Two parties of Quadavs (4x Quadav Guard, 1x Quadav Carrier) will start moving slowly in different directions. The objective of this mission is to kill all 10 Quadavs before they reach the end of their route.
    - The Carrier will run off as soon as he's engaged, so you'll need to kill him quickly.
    - Should a Carrier make it to the end of the area, which is quite large, the mission is failed.
[5] Once all ten Quadavs are defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
[6] Must exit area using the Twilight Aureola for Hieronymus to acknowledge the mission as successful.
[7] Return and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.
