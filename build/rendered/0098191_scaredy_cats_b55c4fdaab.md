## Walkthrough

[1] After registering with the Pioneers' Coalition, zone and wait for a game day change before returning to the Pioneers' Coalition for a cutscene.
[2] Proceed to the eastern half of (G-8) in Ceizak Battlegrounds and click the Earthen Mound for another cutscene.
    - Located just south of Bivouac #2

[3] Head southwest to (E-10) and enter the Sih Gates.
[4] Once inside, head west to (E-6) and click on the Foreboding Presence for another cutscene.
[5] Click on it again to spawn the NM Fomor Pioneer.
    - Trusts make this fight trivial for solo players
        - The NM may cast Cure IV / Cure V on players / pets occasionally, healing them for ~600 HP.
        - Fomor Pioneer is highly evasive and has approximately 10,000 HP.

[6] Click on the Foreboding Presence one last time after defeating the NM for another cutscene and the [KI] Reive unity Key Item.
[7] Head back to the Pioneers' Coalition for another Cutscene and reward.
