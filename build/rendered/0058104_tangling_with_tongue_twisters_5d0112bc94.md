## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kupipi (A) at (H-12) to begin the quest.
[2] She will ask for you to defeat one of the 5 mentioned monsters:
    - Cuelebre, Adze, Minhocao, Chukwa, or Mictlantecuhtli
[3] If you defeat one with the quest active, you will receive a [KI] Bloodied arrow upon the first time completing the quest.
    - If you are completing this quest a second time or more, you will instead receive a [KI] Crimson bloodstone.
[4] Return to Kupipi (A) to complete the quest.
    - Evoliths with Katana Weapon Skill: Critical Hit Rate+3% have been reported, along with Double Attack+6%.
