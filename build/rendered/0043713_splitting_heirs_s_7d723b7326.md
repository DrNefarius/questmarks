## Walkthrough

Note: This special campaign ops is only available when La Vaule (S) is under the control of San d'Oria.
[1] Head to La Vaule (S) after selecting the mission.
[2] Obtain a Tigris stone (Key Item).
    - The stone drops off any of the Orcs in La Vaule (S) (excluding NMs).
    - Every member of the party currently in the zone will receive a stone when it drops.
    - However, only members with the Op flagged can enter the BCNM.
[3] The entrance to the battlefield is at the Reinforced Gateway at (F-9).
[4] Inside the battlefield, four NM's will spawn.
[5] Darkheir Grradhod (SAM) and his three helpers; Gorepledge Rozzbrezz (PLD), Malicearm Razbhobb (MNK), and Slitherword Razghogg (BLM).
    - Only Darkheir Grradhod has to be defeated to win the fight.
        - Darkheir Grradhod can use Meikyo Shisui.
        - He has a unique WS called Tornado Edge which reduces HP by 50%, MP by 50% and reduces TP to 100%.
        - He has a very high Counter rate.
        - He is resistant to Sleep spells, however his helpers are not.
        - If Slitherword Razghogg is alive, Grradhod will have heavy magic resistence.
        - If Malicearm Razbhobb is alive, Grradhod will have a heavy Attack Bonus and will weaponskill with greater frequency.
        - If Gorepledge Rozzbrezz is alive, Grradhod will have a heavy Defense Bonus.
            - His helpers are much weaker, however they will respawn shortly after they are defeated.
            - The helpers share hate with Darkheir Grradhod.
[6] Once Darkheir Grradhod is defeated, an Armoury Crate will spawn.
[7] Return to Rasdinice to complete the mission.
