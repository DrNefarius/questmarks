# Stock and Awe IV (S)

The game knows this mission as: Stock and Awe IV (S)   (mission/campaign #84)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Resource procurement
Description: Procure the supplies indicated by the kingdom quartermaster.
Orders:      Obtain shopping list from Quartermaster and submit items to her.
NPC Name:    Rasdinice
Zone:        Southern San d'Oria (S)
pos:         I-9
Campaign Rank: Brass Wings of Service §
Time:        None
Level:       Any
Rewards:     *390 Experience Points
            *1080 Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Solo mission
[2] Speak with the Quartermaster at (I-9), if you're facing Rasdinice, she'll be behind you across the way near the tents.
[3] She will provide different players with different types of 'supplies' to bring. The following are possible options:
    :*Smilodon Hide
    :*Smilodon Liver
    :*Gnole Claw
    :*Djinn Ash
    :*2 Ladybug Wings
    :*Agaricus
    :*Orc Pauldron
    :*Orc Helmet
[4] After you turn in your requested item to the Quartermaster and receive your experience points speak with Rasdinice and you will receive your Allied Notes and completion of the mission.
