## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Yazan (H-6), who asks you to exterminate Sand Bats in Valkurm Dunes.
    - Sand Bat Fangs are obtained from Sand Bats in the tunnels of Valkurm Dunes.
        - They can be bought from the Auction House (Materials ⇒ Bonecraft).
[2] Once you have one, trade it to Yazan for your reward.
