## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Qiji, who wants to give his wife, Romilda, a Brass Hairpin.
[2] Brass hairpins can be bought off auction houses, or by crafting one.
[3] Trade the hairpin to Romilda, who is standing next to Qiji.
[4] Talk to Qiji to obtain your reward.
