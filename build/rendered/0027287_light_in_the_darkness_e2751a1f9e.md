## Walkthrough

Notice: Wait one game day and re-zone after completing Fires of Discontent before starting this quest.
[1] Speak to Gentle Tiger in Bastok Markets (S) (H-6) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
    - Gentle Tiger may first remark upon a Bundle of half-inscribed scrolls, speak with him again to receive the cutscene.
[2] Speak to Pagdako at (H-9) for some dialogue.
[3] Speak to Blatherix at (F-8).  Trade him 30 Gob. Chocolate or 5,000 gil  to receive a [KI]Mine shaft key
    - Gob. Chocolate is sold by Pawkrix in Lower Jeuno (H-10).
[4] Zone in to Pashhow Marshlands (S) from Grauberg (S) for a brief cutscene.
    - Survival Guide to Grauberg (S) is quickest way.
    - Voidwatch teleport from an Atmacite Refiner in the past to Pashhow Marshlands (S) will also put you at the Grauberg (S) zone line.
[5] Examine the Corroded Door in Pashhow Marshlands (S) (F-5) to enter a battlefield.
[6] In the battlefield, you will encounter x1 Sapphire Quadav and x10 Sapphirine Quadav.
    - Trusts may be used in this fight.
    - Any job in item level 119 gear should have no issue clearing this fight.
    - It is best to defeat six of the Sapphirine Quadavs first, as defeating the Sapphire Quadav will cause the Sapphirine Quadavs to scatter around the map.
    - Defeating enough Quadav will clear the mission, returning you back to Pashhow Marshlands (S).
Notice: If for any reason your inventory is full before starting this next step below then you will not obtain the reward and you will have to rewatch the entire cutscene, choice selections included.
[7] Speak to Gentle Tiger for a cutscene. In it, you are given the opportunity to answer questions. You can choose again if you answer incorrectly, but these are the correct answers:
    - "...the ventilation shaft."
    - "...the senator did not light the lantern."
    - "...he was distracted."
[8] When the cutscene ends you will receive your reward.
