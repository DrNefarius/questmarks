## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Kieran after completing Voidwatch Ops: Border Crossing.
    - Note:Zilart Mission 14 - Ark Angels must be started before you can receive this cutscene.
[2] Speak to Gilgamesh in Norg for a cutscene.
[3] Return to Kieran for a cutscene and to receive your [KI] Ashen stratum abyssite II (the quest is flagged at this point).
[4] Engage in and complete the three Tier II Voidwatch battles in the Li'Telor region:
    - Cath Palug in The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah (E-9), (F-8), (G-10 - Survival Guide is very close by)
    - Modron in The Boyahda Tree Map 1: (G-5), (K-8) Map 2: (F-7 - Very close to 125 Unity Warp)
    - Mimic King in Ro'Maeve (E-7), (I-9), (K-7)
[5] Return to Kieran, which will automatically finish this quest, reward you with [KI] Ashen stratum abyssite III, and begin the next quest.
