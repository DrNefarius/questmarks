[TABLE]
    * After finishing all of the cutscenes after Dawn including Blueblade Fell, walk towards the Palace in Ru'Lude Gardens to begin the cutscene. * Examine the Dilapidated Gate in Misareaux Coast (F-7) for a cutscene. ** The Unity Warp npc-option "content level 122" > Misareaux Coast puts you right in front of this gate. * Head to the Unstable Displacement in the northwest section of Riverne - Site #B01 (E-7) for a cutscene. ** The Unstable Displacement will not turn green until you are standing almost directly on top of it. ** (Fastest Route) Use Home Point > Riverne - Site #B01. *** If using the Home Point go northeast to the normal Spatial Displacement in order to reach the island that has the green displacement. ** (Optional) - You can take the long way from the Dilapidated Gate in Misareaux Coast (F-7) to get there using Giant Scale along the way to reach (E-7). * Click the green Unstable Displacement again after the cutscene to enter the BC Storms of Fate with Bahamut. * Kill Bahamut for another cutscene. * Return to the Palace in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene that completes the quest. | |
