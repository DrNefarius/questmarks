## Walkthrough

[1] 8 Vanadiel days must pass after completion of His Name is Valgeir to begin this quest.
[2] Speak to Take in Mhaura (I-8) inside the Sailor's Stay.
[3] Speak to Valgeir in Selbina (I-9) inside the Shepherd's Muster.
[4] Obtain 1 Scream Fungus, and 1 Land Crab Meat then trade them to Valgeir.
[5] Wait 2 game days, return to Valgeir to receive the finished Land crab bisque.
    - 06/25/2026 - I received the KI after waiting 2 game days.
        - 07:29:16 - I traded the items.
        - 08:11:19 - He said "What now? It's not done yet!..."
        - 09:11:22 - I received the KI.
[6] Return to Take and trade her the Land crab bisque to finish the quest.
