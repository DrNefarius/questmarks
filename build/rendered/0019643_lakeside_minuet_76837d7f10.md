# Lakeside Minuet

The game knows this quest as: Lakeside Minuet   (quest/jeuno #95)

## Quest Header
Start:      Laila, Upper Jeuno (G-7)
Description: Laila has asked that you bring her a stardust pebble before she'll consider allowing you to join Troupe Brilioth.
Fame:       Jeuno
FLevel:     1
Quest Reqs: (empty)
Item Reqs:  [KI] Stardust pebble
Level:      30+
Repeatable: No
Previous:   (empty)
Next:       The Unfinished Waltz
Title:      Troupe Brilioth Dancer
Reward:     Ability to become a Dancer
Expansion:  Wings of the Goddess

## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Laila at (G-7) in Upper Jeuno to begin the quest.
    - Home Point #1 in Upper Jeuno is close by.
    - You must choose "I surely do." and then "Never!" to continue.
[2] She will then ask for a [KI] Stardust pebble.
[3] Speak with the mithran dancer Rhea Myuliah next to her.
    - If you speak to Laila and Rhea Myuliah answers, continue on to the next step.
[4] Head to Lion Springs Tavern at (K-6) in Southern San d'Oria and speak to Valderotaux at the bar to "dance" and underwhelm him.
    - Home Point #3 in Southern San d'Oria is near the Tavern.
    - You can select any of the options while on stage, the results are the same.
[5] Return to Rhea Myuliah in Upper Jeuno to discover what the pebbles are. She'll also reveal they used to lay at the bottom of Lake Mechieume in Jugner Forest twenty years ago.
[6] Travel to the past and go to (I-5) in Jugner Forest (S).
    - It is relatively close to the Batallia Downs (S) zone line. You can travel via the Survival Guide there or through the Cavernous Maw.
    - You could also travel via Recall-Jugner if you have that option available.
[7] Touch the glowing pebbles targetable location near the shore for a cutscene and to receive the [KI] Stardust pebble.
    - Note: This spot is not actually glowing, unlike other targetable locations in the game. It is simply a targetable spot in the environment. Target around at (I-5) and pay attention to your target names in order to find the spot.
[8] Speak with Laila in Upper Jeuno once more to finish the quest.
