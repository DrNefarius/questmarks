## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Faursel  (Neptune's Spire J-8) for a cutscene.
[2] He will give you three options to choose from. The third is a hidden option on the bottom. Choose the hidden option.
[3] Talk to Faursel a second time for another cutscene and choose "I want to go".
[4] Faursel will give you four ways to proceed: the Advanced path, the Intermediate path, the Beginner path, or paying 500,000 Gil.
[5] Collect the required item(s) for Faursel. See the options below for your options.
    - Tip! You can buy Coffer Keys from the Curio Moogle for 5,000 Gil if you have the [KI] "Rhapsody in Umber". If you have not progressed in Rhapsodies of Vana'diel, now it is by far the easiest choice even though it is "Advanced".
[6] Trade the requested item(s) to him. He will tell you to return tomorrow.
[7] Zone and return to Lower Jeuno the next game day. Talk to Faursel again to obtain the [KI] Boarding permit.
    - If you wish to skip obtaining the request items, you can pay him 500,000 Gil.
        - If you choose to pay the gil, you must change zones and return to Lower Jeuno the next game day. Then talk to Faursel and you will receive the [KI] Boarding permit and Map of Wajaom Woodlands and be teleported to the Leypoint in Wajaom Woodlands.
    - If you chose not to pay the gil, you are instructed to go to Mhaura and ride the ship to Al Zahbi.
        - Laughing Bison (Mhaura I-9) will tell you whether the next ship will sail for Al Zahbi or Selbina, as will Dieh Yamilsiah once you've entered the boarding area.
        - If you have joined a Unity and have (or get) 100 Unity Accolades, you may skip the boat ride by using a Unity Warp with any Unity NPC -- But you must first obtain the [KI] Boarding permit to unlock the Aht Urghan warp options.
            - 125 Wajaom Woodlands option, and then chocobo/run to Al Zhabi, and then through there to Aht Urghan Whitegate.
[8] After completing this quest approach Naja Salaheem at Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-10) to begin Aht Urhgan Mission 1: Land of Sacred Serpents.

Note: If you are on  Rhapsodies of Vanadiel Mission 2-5, simply select "Where's Tenzen?" and you will get a [KI] Boarding permit for free.

Note: The NPC Jijiroon in Nashmau will later say: "Jijiroon saaaw boat flying throoo air over Wawaaam Wooodland, and--whoosh!--out flew adventooorer! That loook fun."

== Advanced Path ==

Trade any one of the following:

[9] One coffer key from a beastman stronghold:
    - Davoi
    - Beadeaux
    - Castle Oztroja
    - Temple of Uggalepih
    - Den of Rancor
    - Quicksand Caves
    - Sea Serpent Grotto
[10] Any testimony item.
[11] Three (3) chips from Pso'Xja:
    - Carmine Chip
    - Cyan Chip
    - Gray Chip
