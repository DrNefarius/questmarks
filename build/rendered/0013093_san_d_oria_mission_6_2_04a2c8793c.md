## Walkthrough

Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
[1] You'll be instructed to go see Prince Trion. Visit him in his room at (H-7) in Chateau d'Oraguille
[2] If you're low level, gather a party or use Trusts.
[3] Head for (H-8) in King Ranperre's Tomb on Map 1.
    - The Proto-Waypoint warp to Jugner Forest is the fastest way to get to the location in King Ranperre's Tomb.
[4] When ready, check the Heavy Stone Door to spawn three (3) Skeleton NMs: Corrupted Soffeil (BLM), Corrupted Yorgos (WAR) and Corrupted Ulbrig (BLM).
    - They can be pulled one by one using Sneak.
    - All 3 must be slain.
    - The NMs are immune to Sleep and Lullaby.
    - They will use Blood Saber, so keep damage negating buffs up to reduce the HP regained.
    - Corrupted Yorgos has approximately 7250 HP.
[5] When defeated, check the Heavy Stone Door again, and enter. Check the Tombstone inside for a cutscene, at the end of which, you'll receive the [KI] Ancient San d'Orian book.
    - (Optional): Return to Prince Trion, and you will be instructed to go see the Gate Guard.
[6] Talk to any Gate Guard. A cutscene will play out saying they sent the book to scholars to decipher.
[7] You must talk to the Gate Guard once more to have them tell you that the deciphering will take some time.
[8] Zone and talk to the Guard again who will tell you to see Prince Trion.
[9] Return to Prince Trion and speak with him for a cutscene. You may need to talk to him twice.
[10] Return to the Heavy Stone Door in King Ranperre's Tomb again for another cutscene. You'll be instructed to defeat enemies, but you only need to activate the door again.
[11] Return to a Gate Guard to complete the Mission.
