## Walkthrough

Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
[1] Head to the Cathedral in Northern San d'Oria and go to the Papal Chambers, located on the third floor at (M-6).
[2] If you're low level, form a party and/or use Trusts.
    - Sneaking is recommended, bring some Silent Oil Silent Oils.
[3] Head to Bostaunieux Oubliette, which can be accessed from Chateau d'Oraguille (I-8). If you already explored the place, alternate paths are available below.
    - Head for (E-7/8) and use Sneak. Speak to Couchatorage standing by the Sewer Lid to drop down to Map 2. Be sure to grab the Survival Guide nearby if this is your first time here.
    - Follow the right wall and you'll eventually zone to West Ronfaure at (E-8). There will be a ??? in front of you.
[4] When prepared, check the ??? to spawn an Orc NM Marauder Dvogzog.
    - It is a level 67 MNK with approximately 16,000 HP, capable of using Hundred Fists.
[5] Check the ??? again to receive the [KI] Ancient San d'Orian tablet.
    - Should you fall off of the ledge, you'll need to run back through the Oubliette and fight the NM again.
[6] Return to the Cathedral and visit the Papal Chambers for the final cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: Alternate Paths

[1] Unity Warp: If you have dropped down the Sewer Lid in Bostaunieux Oubliette at least once, the Level 122 Unity Warp can teleport you directly to Bostaunieux Oubliette, right at the zone line to West Ronfaure.
[2] Proto-Waypoint: The West Ronfaure Proto-Waypoint will get you directly to your destination, provided you've already unlocked it.
