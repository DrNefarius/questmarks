## Walkthrough

[1] Approach Naja Salaheem in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-10) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
    - If you are repeating this quest, skip this step and go straight to the Imperial Whitegate in the next step.
[2] Examine Imperial Whitegate at Aht Urhgan Whitegate (L-8/9).
[3] Examine the Acid-eaten Door in Mount Zhayolm at (I-9) to receive a short cutscene.
[4] Trade 3 Slabs of Plumbago to the Acid-eaten Door to receive the [KI] lightning cell.
[5] Examine the Blank Target in Nyzul Isle Staging Point at (J-8/9) for a cutscene.
[6] Examine the Runic Seal in Alzadaal Undersea Ruins (I-8/9) to start the BCNM fight.
    - In this fight, you will be against Alexander.
    - Alexander can spawn up to three Alexander Images.
    - Trust magic may not be used in this battlefield.
    - At low HP, Alexander will use Divine Judgment dealing high damage to everyone in range.
[7] Speak to Naja Salaheem to choose your reward.
