## Walkthrough
[1] Defeat 3 Acuexs in Woh Gates. You do not need to be in EXP range as long as you are in Woh Gates when your party/alliance defeats the monsters.
[2] Wheezing Acuexes can be found at I-9.  Take #4 in Marjami and zone into Woh Gates and head Northeast.
[3] Alternatively, zone in at Kamihr Drifts Frontier Station and zone into Woh Gates. Four can be found north and south of the I-7 bridge.
