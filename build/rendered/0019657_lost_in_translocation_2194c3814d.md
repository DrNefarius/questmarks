## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Thorben in Batallia Downs (S) at (J-10).
[2] Enter The Eldieme Necropolis (S) via the Crypt Door next to Thorben.
[3] There are three pieces to the map.
    - The [KI] Right map piece is located at (I-9) (click the Sarcophagus)
    - The [KI] Middle map piece is located at (H-8) (click the Gravestone)
    - The [KI] Left map piece is located at (H-7).  This piece is given by an NPC Erik who is now present during Campaign as per the 11/26/07 patch.
[4] After you receive the final map piece; Select the Blank Target (the brazier, next to Erik at (H-7)) and select "Into the fire". This will warp you back out to Batallia Downs (S) - (J-10).
[5] Speak to Thorben again for a cutscene.
