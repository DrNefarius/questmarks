## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Erlene at The Eldieme Necropolis (S) (J-8) while on Scholar for a cutscene that begins the quest.
    - You must zone and wait until next game day after completing On Sabbatical.
[2] Zone into Southern San d'Oria (S) from East Ronfaure (S) (Survival Guide and telecrystal won't work).
[3] Return to The Eldieme Necropolis (S) and speak to Erlene.
[4] Zone into Sauromugue Champaign (S) from Rolanberry Fields (S) for cutscene.
[5] Examine the Indescript Markings targetable location in Sauromugue Champaign (S) at the south eastern corner of (J-7) for a cutscene. You will receive [KI] Ulbrecht's mortarboard.
    - The Indescript Markings disappear during  Dusty weather. They are located on the ledge in the southeastern part of the quadrant, near a ???.
[6] Return to The Eldieme Necropolis (S) and speak to Erlene for a cutscene that finishes the quest for your reward.
