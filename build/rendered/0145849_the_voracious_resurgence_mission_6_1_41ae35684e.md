[1] Bring 3 Hoptoad Hoptoad and an Eastern Ginger Eastern Ginger to save time.
[2] Speak with Reikuu (K-8) near Ethereal Ingress #6 in Reisenjima for a cutscene to begin the Mission.
[3] Head to Ethereal Ingress #10 and speak with Incantrix for another cutscene.
    - You must choose the option "No, of course not" in order to continue with the Mission.
[4] Head to (H-6) between Ethereal Ingress #3 & #5 in Reisenjima and examine Suspicious Overgrowth for a cutscene.
    - Gessho will ask for 3 Hoptoad Hoptoad and an Eastern Ginger Eastern Ginger.
        - Hoptoad Hoptoad are obtained by fishing in Reisenjima, off the bridge near Ethereal Ingress #2 with a Fly Lure Fly Lure or Lufaise Fly Lufaise Fly and any rod.
            - The bite rate may vary depending on your fishing rod and whether you have [KI] Frog Fishing.
            - With Lu Shang's F. Rod or Ebisu Fishing Rod and sufficient skill, the bite rate is nearly 100%, even without the KI.
        - Eastern Ginger Eastern Ginger can be harvested in Reisenjima (touching Grimslight requires [KI] Reaper), harvested in Bhaflau Thickets or found in treasure caskets in Reisenjima.
            - [KI] Reaper is sold by Shiftrix (F-12) for 50 Silt.
[5] Trade the items to the Suspicious Overgrowth for a cutscene.
[6] Return to Reikuu (K-8) near Ethereal Ingress #6 for the final cutscene and your reward.
