## Walkthrough

[1] You must wait until the next game day after completing The Talekeeper's Truth before you can activate this quest.
[2] Speak to Deidogg in Bastok Mines.
[3] Speak to Detzo in Bastok Mines (I-6), located in the alleyway around the corner from Deidogg.(Speak to him again if he mentions a Mythril Sallet)
[4] Obtain a Ginger Cookie and trade it to Deidogg. The quest will now be flagged.
[5] Travel to Behemoth's Dominion.
    - If you don't have the Survival Guide warp, the quickest way is via Qufim Island; follow the tunnel starting at (G-5) until you zone in.
[6] Proceed to (K-9) in Behemoth's Dominion to find a ???.  Touching it will spawn 3 NM goblins:
    - Doglix Muttsnout (Level 58 WHM)
    - Moxnix Nightgoggle (Level 58 RNG)
    - Picklix Longindex (Level 60 THF)
    - Be aware all 3 NMs will aggressively use Bomb Toss.
[7] Defeat the NMs, and zone out of Behemoth's Dominion (back into Qufim Island) to receive a cutscene and your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This quest does not get flagged in your log until after you trade the  Ginger Cookie to Deidogg.
[2] You can attempt to defeat the mobs on a non-Warrior job (as long as you have the quest).
[3] [unverified] The NMs can only be popped once per game day.  Otherwise when touching the ??? you will get the message "It is too blurry to read".
