## Walkthrough

[1] While the quest Ode to the Serpents is active, speak to Talhaal in Al Zahbi (H-6).
[2] Speak to Fari-Wari in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (K-12).
[3] In Wajaom Woodlands kill Wajaom Tigers until you get a Rusty Medal.
    - Tigers spawn near (E-8), (F-7) and (K-9).
[4] Go to the Leypoint in Wajaom Woodlands (G-8) and trade the Rusty Medal for a cutscene that completes the quest.
    - Return to the teahouse and speak to Fari-Wari again to learn Trust:Zazarg
You may only get the cutscenes in when General Zazarg is in Al Zahbi.
