## Walkthrough

[1] Mano-Amano is located at the Frontier Station. Speak to him to start the quest.
[2] Travel to (I-8) of Yorcia Weald, directly west of the Cirdas Caverns Entrance, and click on the ??? among the numbing blossoms. You will obtain the Fistful of numbing soil.
    - You can get there fast from Bivouac #2 and going north.
    - There may be an impassable bush blocking the path. Simply wait until 21:00 game time to proceed.
[3] Return to Mano-Amano for your reward.
