## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Balasiel, who asks of you a revival tree root.
[2] He is located on the upper levels on the walkway.
[3] Revival Tree Root can be bought from the auction house (Materials ➞ Alchemy 1) or dropped from Ghost type enemies.
    - It does NOT need to be obtained specifically from King Ranperre's Tomb.
[4] Trade the root to him to complete the quest and receive your reward.
