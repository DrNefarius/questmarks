## Walkthrough

[1] Head to Qufim Island (F-7) (Follow the wall NW from Home Point #1).

[2] Click on the Transcendental Radiance with a Traverser stone in your key items.

[3] Select "Proceed" to spend 10,000 Cruor to obtain a Crimson traverser stone, automatically enter Abyssea - Empyreal Paradox, and receive a cutscene.

[4] The next quest is automatically flagged upon cutscene completion.
