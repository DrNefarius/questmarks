## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Orlando to begin this quest. He is interested in gathering the items that Goblin Diggers tend to bury if left undisturbed.
[2] Trade Orlando eight (8) of any one of the items to complete this quest.
    - All of these items are purchasable on the Auction House, dug up with Chocobo Digging, or obtained through defeating monsters.
