## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Pherimociel at (G-6) in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene (Home Point #1).
[2] Head up the stairs and examine the Audience Chamber for another cutscene.
    - Optional: Pherimociel and Auchefort have new minor dialogue after this scene.
[3] Travel to (H-9) in Selbina and speak with Mathilde in the Weaver's Guild office to complete the Mission.

## After the walkthrough: Optional

[1] Note: When CoP first released, this was the last mission available. The remaining missions were added in later updates.
    - While the armada is getting ready for its attack, it's a natural stopping point to take a break and wrap up any unfinished business, and start some optional quests that didn't fit anywhere else (see below).
[2] A Question of Faith (level 60+), part of a quest chain.
[3] (THF only) The Tenshodo Showdown (level 40+), As Thick as Thieves (level 50+), and Hitting the Marquisate (level 60+)
    - These may contain information relevant to your main quest.
[4] Brygid the Stylist (level 1+), Brygid the Stylist Returns (level 52+)
    - The second quest can be triggered by wearing Artifact Armor. It requires a Constellation subligar from the Fomors in the previous CoP dungeons.
[5] Secrets of Ovens Lost (level 50+) is available after finishing Spice Gals and is also a Records of Eminence 15th Vana'versary IV objective.
[6] Elderly Pursuits (level 51+) is available after finishing 'Secrets of Ovens Lost'.
[7] Chips (level 60+) which rewards a boss-weakening item.
[8] The Naming Game (level 60+), additional completions of this quest allow for renaming after airships from other FF games.
[9] Tuning In (level 60+)
    - Tuning Out (level 60+) is available after completing 'Tuning In'.
[10] Behind the Smile (level 50+): requires you to have a HM Mannequin mannequin.
    - Knocking on Forbidden Doors (level 50+) is available after completing 'Behind the Smile'.
[11] Missionary Moblin (level 50+)
    - For the Birds (level 50+) is available after completing 'Missionary Moblin'.
    - Better the Demon You Know (level 60+) is available after completing 'For the Birds'.
