## Walkthrough

[1] After talking to the Campaign Ops NPC, you are to scour South San d'Oria (S) for a clickable point called "Suspicious Object". You must collect three of them to complete the mission.
[2] The Suspicious Object seems to spawn anywhere in South San d'Oria (S) and once someone touches it, it disappears immediately.
[3] More than one Suspicious object can appear at a time.
[4] The Suspicious Object respawns immediately in a different location. So far the point can appear at:
    :*(c-6) - Inside and to left in Mansion area.
    :*(D-7) - barely NE of Adjutant.
    :*(D-7) - top step of stairs.
    :*(D-8) - halfway up stairs to Leathercraft Guild Shop area.
    :*(E-7) - By the ??? Warmachine.
    :*(F-6) - behind Captured Orc.
    :*(F-9) - 2 paces NE of mini door.
    :*(G-9) - On the ramp in front of Westgate, or on the second floor NW of Aurfet.
    :*(H-9) - few yalms south of Quartermaster
    :*(I-9) - 10 yalms north of Rasdinice.
    :*(I-11) - ~5 yalms east of Sabiliont.
    :*(J-9) - near Voucheramme.
    :*(J-10) - at North East exit from Chocobo Stable area.

[5] After obtaining the object, you will obtain experience points and get a message that you quota has been met. After this occurs, go back to the Campaign Ops NPC and collect your Allied Notes.
