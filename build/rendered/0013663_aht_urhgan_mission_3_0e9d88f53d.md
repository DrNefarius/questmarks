## Walkthrough

[1] You must zone out and return to get the cutscenes.
    - There is an exit to Wajaom Woodlands west of the office under the stairs.
[2] Speak to Rytaal (K-10) for an explanation of Assault. This also unlocks access to it.
    - Rytaal is located in the Commissions Agency through the door in (K-9).
[3] Speak to Naja Salaheem twice for two cutscenes to finish this mission and immediately begin the next.
    - You must zone out and return to get the cutscenes.
[4] (Optional) Speak to Fubruhn (F-11) regarding Mog Locker upgrades, administration, and accessibility.

## After the walkthrough: Trust: Abquhbah

[1] If you have completed RoV Mission 2-7 - Ever Forward, you may speak to Abquhbah after this mission to receive his Trust cipher.
