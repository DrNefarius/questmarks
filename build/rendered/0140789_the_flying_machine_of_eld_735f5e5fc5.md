## Walkthrough

[1] Approach the chocobo stable in Upper Jeuno (G-7).
    - You may have to zone after completing the previous quest.
[2] Complete the The Celestial Nexus II, on any difficulty, to get the Broken flying machine Key Item.
[3] Report back to Mapitoto.
[4] Interact with the Strange Apparatus in Fei'Yin (G-6 map 1).
    - You will need to proceed to the basement (H-9, Map 1) or warp to the Fauregandi → Fei'Yin → Home Point #2.
        - Then proceed back upstairs from (E-7, Map 2) to (G/H-6, Map 1) to run through the fake wall and to the Apparatus. All apparatuses are hidden like this.
[5] Obtain a  Ve'Lugannon Coffer Key and open a coffer in Ve'Lugannon Palace to obtain the Levitation device Key Item.
    - Curio Vendor Moogles sell the Coffer Key for 5,000 gil.
    - If you are in a party, all members will receive the Key Item when one member opens a coffer.
        - Notice: If someone who's in your party but doesn't need the Key Item opens the coffer, no one will get the Key Item.
        - Advice: You can make a full party, leave 1 person at each blue/yellow gate control and send others to find the coffer. As long as someone who needs the Key Item opens the coffer, everyone in the same party (not alliance) within the zone will get it.
[6] Obtain Doctor Status from any Strange Apparatus and return to the one in Fei'Yin and examine the Apparatus.
    - You will not be able to obtain Doctor Status from the apparatus in Fei'Yin.
        - Once obtained, the status will persist after obtaining it in another zone and returning.
        - Warning: Don't accidentally trade your items to a Strange Apparatus in a zone other than Fei'Yin for this quest! You will lose them.
    - Optional: If you want to save time for the Omega whistle, talk to Boisterous Jackal in Lower Jeuno (H-7) twice times to gain the ability to enable Administrator Status in Fei'Yin.
        - This was changed at some point, as of July 2026. Speaking with Boisterous Jackal is not necessary, just interact with the apparatus again after getting the Whirring engine key item.
[7] Trade the  Green Chip and  Infinity Core to the apparatus in Fei'Yin.
    - The apparatus will then ask for 38  elemental crystal Clusters. Any combination of elements works.
[8] Trade the stacks of Clusters to obtain the Whirring engine Key Item.
[9] Return to Mapitoto to get the Levitus key Key Item.
    - Note: The Levitus inventory item exists in the game, but you are given the Key Item directly.
