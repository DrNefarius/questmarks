## Walkthrough

Talk to Berghent near the Big Bridge in Western Adoulin (J-9) to start the Quest.
[1] Talk to Masad inside the Mummers' Coalition.
[2] Talk to Dewalt inside the Couriers' Coalition.
[3] Head to Rala Waterways and talk to Chalvava at (F-11).
    - The best way there is from the Western Adoulin entrance at (I/J-12) from the Mog House.
[4] Go to Yahse Hunting Grounds and harvest a [KI] Blightberry using a Sickle. Make sure to bring multiple as failure can occur.
    - Use Yahse Hunting Grounds Waypoint #3 to be in range of a cluster of possible harvest points.
[5] Return to Berghent for your reward.
