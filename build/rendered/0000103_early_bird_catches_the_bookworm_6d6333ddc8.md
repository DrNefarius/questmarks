# Early Bird Catches the Bookworm

The game knows this quest as: Early Bird Catches the Bookworm   (quest/windurst #20)

## Quest Header
Start:      Tosuka-Porika, (north) Windurst Waters (G-8)
Description: The Library of Magic wants you to help retrieve its overdue library books. In this case, a Hume the name of Orn has checked out the book "Art for Everyone," but has failed to return it by the due date.

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Tosuka-Porika to begin the quest. You will be told to speak to Furakku-Norakku for further details. Furakku-Norakku will give you the [KI] Overdue book notification to be delivered to Orn.
    - Orn is located at F-10 in the Rarab Tail Hostelry room above the Timbre Timbers Tavern.
    - (Optional) You can speak to Furakku-Norakku again at Orn's request.
[2] You will have to go to Giddeus and talk to Quu Bokye, then trade a Silver Beastcoin. You will receive [KI] "Art For Everyone".
    - Quu Bokye is in the Treasure Room, which can be found in the underground map of Giddeus. It is the northwest room at the end of a U-turn on the map.
    - (Optional) You can speak to Orn again with the book.
[3] Speak to Furakku-Norakku with the key item to complete the quest.
