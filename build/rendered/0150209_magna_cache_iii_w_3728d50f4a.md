## Walkthrough

thumb|One of the Secret Fort Karugo-Narugo (S) Doors.
[1] After talking to the Campaign Ops NPC, speak to Yasmina (G-5) for details. She will ask you to which stronghold you want to ride:
    - West Sarutabaruta (S) - Time to Beat: (1:09 was fast enough, 1:20 wasn't.)
    - Fort Karugo-Narugo (S) - Time to Beat: (4:10 was fast enough, 4:14 wasn't.)
        - In order to progress this option, you must ride the Chocobo through Fivespires to Lamurara, C.C.. As Magna Cache missions require the nation to hold the stronghold.
        - Since the Phosphorous Ward will not open while mounted, it's required to use the small brick doors that look like Windows, such as the one at F-10.
        - Proceed inside until you reach the middle of Fivespires, and locate Lamurara, C.C. on the middle raised area/platform. Approaching them will initiate a cut-scene while mounted. DO NOT dismount under any circumstance before this cut-scene triggers. Or else the Ops Mission will fail, and no reward will be given.
    - Meriphataud Mountains (S) - Time to Beat: 7:36
    - Sauromugue Champaign (S) - Time to Beat: (10:01 was fast enough, 10:23 wasn't.)
        - To attain the time(s) needed for this, follow the mini-guide above to go through Fort Karugo-Narugo (S), take the upper level around the left side of the middle and heading for A Exit by falling down and taking the left north door from the center.
        - Take the far north exit to Meriphataud Mountains (S)
            - Go past the Watchful Pixie (or right-straight ahead leaning left from A Exit.)
            - Turn left and then immediately right, after.
            - Another Right at the next intersection, stay left, past the War Lizards.
            - Turn Immediate left before the Dragonflies, then follow the right wall avoiding as much terrain/clutter as possible to the exit.
        - In Meriphataud Mountains (S), go around the rock formation, and make sure you get to the right so you can get past the white cermet structure. After going past it, turn left and head straight for the S. Champaign Gate. Recommended that you fall down into the trench path to save time.
        - In Sauromugue Champaign (S), DO NOT go west/left around the middle mountain that sits in the middle of the zone. (This and not using the center raised platform in the Fort will lose you a lot of time.) Go to the right/north of it, staying close to it and then west toward the Stronghold through the gate. The NPC will be between the two braziers ahead.
[2] Ride your Chocobo to the stronghold. When you arrive at the Campaign Arbiter, you will automatically dismount and get a cut-scene.
[3] Report back to Emhi Tchaoryo to complete the mission.
