## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Zalsuhm (inside Muckvix's Junk Shop, Lower Jeuno H-9) with a Vigil Weapon equipped for a cutscene.
[2] With the same Vigil weapon equipped in the main hand, fight enemies that grant experience points (Incredibly Easy Prey or better) and perform Weapon Skills and Skillchains.
    - Weapon skills used before starting this quest do not count.
    - You must accumulate 250 Weapon Skill Points via the following methods:

[3] Once you have acquired enough Weapon Skill Points, your new Weapon Skill will appear under your list of Weapon Skills, but only with your Vigil Weapon still equipped.
[4] Return to Zalsuhm and trade him your Vigil Weapon.
    - He will unlock your new Weapon Skill for use with any weapon and return the Vigil Weapon to you.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] If you trade your Vigil Weapon to Zalsuhm before it is unlocked, he will give you a rough gauge on how far along you have progressed:
    - "I sense only a mote of energy present. You still have a long way to go."
    - "I can feel a marked increase in energy compared to when I first held it. Keep up the good work."
    - "The energy is threatening to burst forth. It won't be long now."
    - "You've done it! Energy is overflowing as though the floodgates have burst open!"
[2] Storing a Vigil Weapon with Ghanraam at Aht Urhgan Whitegate (K-10) resets its Weapon Skill Points.
[3] After this quest, the Vigil Weapon can still be used for the Mythic Weapon acquisition quests.
    - The Weapon Skill points will not be needed again, so it is okay to store it.
[4] For the first year and a half of implementation from 2007-2008, the Vigil Weapons were only obtainable on Floor 100.
[5] Prior to the July 2014 Version Update, the number of Weapon Skill Points required was determined by the number of floors the player cleared in Nyzul Isle Investigation.
    - At some point (likely the May 2025 Version Update that also updated other WSNM quests), the point rewards per WS were significantly increased. This quest is now much faster than it used to be.
