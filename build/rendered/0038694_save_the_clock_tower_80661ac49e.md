## Walkthrough

[1] (Optional): Speak to Collet near the clock tower (G-7) for gossip.
[2] Speak to Derrick in Lower Jeuno's Chamber of Commerce and Industry (H-7) and ask to start a petition.
[3] To complete the quest, you will need to trade the Tower Petition to 10 NPCs. Their locations are:
    - Pitantimand - Port Jeuno (H-7)
    - Teigero-Bangero - Lower Jeuno (H-10)
    - Zauko - Lower Jeuno (I-6)
    - Baudin - Upper Jeuno (G-8)
    - Collet - Upper Jeuno (G-7)
    - Constance - Upper Jeuno (G-9) (inside the Infirmary)
    - Monberaux - Upper Jeuno (G-10) (inside the Infirmary)
    - Souren - Upper Jeuno (G-9) (inside the Infirmary)
    - Zuber - Upper Jeuno (F-7) (inside the Marble Bridge Tavern)
    - Radeivepart - Ru'Lude Gardens (H-9)
[4] Once you have all 10 signatures trade the Tower Petition to Derrick to complete the quest.
