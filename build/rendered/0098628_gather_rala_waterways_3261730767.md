## Walkthrough
[1] NPC asks you to obtain 3 spools of Bloodthread.
    - The NPC specifically notes it does not matter how the player obtains it. It can be bought, traded, or farmed.
    - Pewter Diremites are plentiful in the northwest area of Rala Waterways. This can be accessed by the entrance at Western Adoulin (F-5).
    - The Diremite Stalker in the Pso'Xja tower near the Beaucedine Glacier Survival Guide have a decent drop rate. However, there aren't many of them, and their respawn rate is slow.
[2] Trade these to the NPC and then Report on the quest outcome.
