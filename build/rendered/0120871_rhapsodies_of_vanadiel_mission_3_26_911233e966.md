## Walkthrough

[1] Check the Transcendental Radiance in Empyreal Paradox and enter the battlefield "The Winds of Time".
[2] You will have to fight and defeat Metus.
    - Metus can use Promathia's TP moves, including : Bastion of Twilight, Wheel of Impregnability, Infernal Deliverance, Empty Salvation.
    - Metus can also use Osmotic Wave, Censure.
    - Metus can cast Meteor.
    - Trust NPCs can be used for this battle.
[3] After winning the battle, a cutscene will start along with the next Mission.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Upon failure, return to Reisenjima Sanctorium and click the Reisen Crystal, agree to meet the Goddess and you will return to Empyreal Paradox for a cutscene with Metus. After head into the room with the "Transendental Radiance" and there is a "Dimensional Portal" to the right of the entrance, use this to return to Reisenjima Sanctorium.
[2] Then click the "Reisenjima Crystal", agree to meet the Goddess again and you will be given the [KI] Extradimensional scar required to attempt the fight again.
    - This capability is lost when you're no longer on a RoV mission requiring access to Empyreal Paradox.
    - If you haven't reached Dawn in Chains of Promathia, you will be confined to the basement. There are no free trips to Al'Taieu.
[3] The Reisen Crystal in Reisenjima Sanctorium will also teleport you to Empyreal Paradox.
