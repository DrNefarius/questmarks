## Walkthrough

[1] Before you begin, you are required to zone if you just completed Truth, Justice, and the Onion Way!.
[2] Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo Port Windurst behind the warehouse at (G-5).
[3] Retrieve 4 Wild Onions and trade them to him.
    - In some cases, this will end the quest, showing you the cut-scene and giving you the reward.
        - The exact trigger is not known, but it seems to be related to progress in Windurst Missions and/or obtaining the [KI] Old ring, as the events of missions 2-1 and 3-1 also involve the same treasure stash.
[4] Head to House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-3) and try to enter to see a cut-scene.
[5] Return to Kohlo-Lakolo and speak with him to complete the quest and receive your reward.
