## Walkthrough

[1] Return to Aht Urhgan Whitegate and click on Imperial Whitegate (The Palace Door) for a cutscene and a [KI] Rhapsody in Crimson.
    (Optional): Locate alternate sources of darkness.
[2] You can do the following at any point during RoV Chapter 2, before Mission 2-39.
[3] Requires all Aht Urhgan Missions completed.
    - Re-enter the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins for a cutscene.
[4] Requires Promathia Mission 3-5 (Darkness Named) completed.
    - Zone into The Shrouded Maw for a cutscene.
        - Use Pso'Xja home point #1, or enter from Beaucedine Glacier (H-8) next to the Survival Guide.
[5] Requires all Promathia Missions completed.
    - Zone into The Garden of Ru'Hmet, descend to the bottom level and enter the next area for a cutscene.
        - Use The Garden of Ru'Hmet home point #1, or talk to Sueleen in the Sealion's Den for transport and enter through the palace.
