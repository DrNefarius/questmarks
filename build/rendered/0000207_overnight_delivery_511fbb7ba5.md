## Walkthrough

You must zone after completing the previous quest, Food for Thought.
[1] Speak to Kenapa-Keppa after 7:00 Vana'diel time.
    - You need to speak to him four times to officially begin the quest.
[2] Head to Mhaura and speak to Kotan-Purutan at (I-9) - near the ferry ticket area - after 18:00 Vana'diel time to obtain a Small bag.
[3] Return to Kenapa-Keppa before 6:00 to complete the quest.
