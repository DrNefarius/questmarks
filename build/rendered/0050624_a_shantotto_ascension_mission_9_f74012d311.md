## Walkthrough

thumb|Toraimarai Composite map|right|350px
In order to receive the cutscene from the Moon Spiral in Full Moon Fountain, you have to have at least 1 sap Key Item in your possession:
[1] The 8 elemental saps can be found at various locations throughout Toraimarai Canal and have the effect of weakening the Astral Flow ability of the corresponding Fomor's avatar in the battlefield awaiting you in the next mission.
    - At max level, it is possible to skip most of the mission by warping to Home Point #1, getting the [KI] Dark sap crystal near the Home Point, then backtracking to Full Moon Fountain to continue the questline.
    - If you're attempting the upcoming battle below level 99, collecting all the saps will make the fight easier.
[2] The elemental saps appear as glowing ???s at the following locations:
    - [KI] Dark sap crystal: (F-8) Map 1
    - [KI] Earth sap crystal: (J-8) or (H-7) Map 1
    - [KI] Water sap crystal: (H-7) or (J-9) Map 1
    - [KI] Fire sap crystal: (J-9) Map 1; (F-10) or (J-8) Map 2
    - [KI] Ice sap crystal: (I-8) or (H-7) Map 2
    - [KI] Light sap crystal: (G-8) Map 2 (one of two places)
    - [KI] Lightning sap crystal: (H-7) Map 1; (G-10/11) Map 2
    - [KI] Wind sap crystal: (H-9) or (J-9) Map 2
[3] Once one or more saps are collected, check the Moon Spiral in Full Moon Fountain to watch the cutscene and complete the mission.
