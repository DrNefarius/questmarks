# Windurst Mission 2-2

The game knows this mission as: A Testing Time   (mission/windurst #4)

## Mission Header
Mission Name: A Testing Time
Start:       Any Windurst Gate Guard
Description: Assist the Aurastery's School of Magic in the selection of the venue for their next skills test. They want to record the number of monsters you can hunt at a specific area, in a set number of days, to fairly standardize their skills test.
Level:       (empty)
Repeatable:  Yes
Previous:    Windurst Mission 2-1 | Lost for Words
Next:        Windurst Mission 2-3 | The Three Kingdoms
Title:       (empty)
Reward:      *Rank Points
Expansion:   windurst

## Walkthrough

[1] Accept the mission from the Gate Guard.
    - (Optional): As usual, you can speak to the gate guards for additional dialogue.
[2] Head to (L-6) in Windurst Waters and speak to Moreno-Toeno (inside the Aurastery), who gives you the [KI] "Creature Counter" magic doll to keep track of your objective.
    - (Optional): You can talk to the students on the roof and the other NPCs in the same room as Moreno-Toeno for additional dialogue.
    - You can save some time by setting your Home Point in Windurst Waters and bringing a Warp item.

## After the walkthrough: First-Time

[1] Head to Tahrongi Canyon and start killing monsters.
    - You have exactly 24 game hours (1 Earth time hour) to kill at least 30 monsters in the Canyon and return to Moreno-Toeno.
[2] Upon defeating 30 monsters, return to Moreno-Toeno.

## After the walkthrough: Repeating

[1] Head to Buburimu Peninsula and start killing monsters.
    - You have exactly 48 game hours (2 Earth time hours) to kill at least 30 monsters in the Peninsula and return to Moreno-Toeno.
[2] Upon defeating 30 monsters, return to Moreno-Toeno.

## After the walkthrough: Getting Assessed

[1] Return to Moreno-Toeno in Windurst Waters and speak to him in the final game hour of the time limit. Speaking to him any sooner will tell you how long you have left. Speaking to him later will fail the Mission.
    - This has lead to a lot of confusion over the years. If you are doing the Mission for the first time and accepted it at 06:00 game hour, he will only complete the Mission if the current time is between 05:00 and 06:00 on the next game day.
        - Despite the in-game text, it's not the whole-hour time, so if you accept at 11:30 then you can turn in at 11:30 the next gameday and do not have to wait for 12:00 (and likely will time-out if you go past 12:30).
    - Example time remaining report at 16:00 when the hour you need to report is 12:00~13:00 (20 game hours later):
    Wow...I avow it's now 16!
    So, lo, 20 hours to go.
    Sublime! You have plenty of time till the chime.
[2] You will fail this Mission if you do not return on time or defeat enough monsters. You will, however, be allowed to progress to the next Mission if you fill your Rank bar via other means.
