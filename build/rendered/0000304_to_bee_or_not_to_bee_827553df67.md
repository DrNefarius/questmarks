## Walkthrough

[1] If Zayhi-Bauhi speaks normally rather than coughing, try speaking to Kalupa-Tawalupa and Rutango-Botango in front of him to receive the quest.
[2] Speak to Raamimi to receive Honey.
[3] Trade Honey to Zayhi-Bauhi, then speak to Kalupa-Tawalupa and Rutango-Botango.
[4] Trade 4 more Honey, one at a time, to Zayhi-Bauhi.
[5] Return to Raamimi to receive 3 Mulsum.
