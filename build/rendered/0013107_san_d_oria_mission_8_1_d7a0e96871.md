# San d'Oria Mission 8-1

Mission Name: Coming of Age
Start:       Any San d'Oria Gate Guard
Description: Princess Claidie will soon celebrate her coming-of-age, and the court in d'Oraguille needs help with the ceremonies. Speak to Monarlais Halver and offer your assistance.
Level:       (empty)

## Walkthrough

Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
[1] Go to Chateau d'Oraguille, where you'll receive a cutscene upon entering.
[2] Speak to Halver for instructions.
    - (Optional): Interact with the two doors to the Princes' quarters for short cutscenes.
[3] Travel to the Quicksand Caves Map 2.
    - The cave entrance at Eastern Altepa Desert (H-10) will place you on Map 2.
        - Located just east of the Survival Guide warp to Eastern Altepa Desert.
        - Alternatively, using the Unity Warp (Level 125) for Quicksand Caves will place you at (J-5) on Map 1. You will need to walk south and then west to (F-10) to reach Map 2.
[4] You will need to drop down a hole at (E-11) to reach an isolated portion of the map.
[5] Once you drop down, travel to the Fountain of Kings at (G-14).
[6] Clear the area and check Fountain of Kings. This will spawn 2 Kraken NMs: Honor and Valor:
    - You can pull them one at a time, they will not link, but will aggro to sound.
    - Valor uses Hundred Fists.
    - Honor casts Paralyga and Silencega.
    - It is possible to land Blind and Gravity on either, but both resist Sleep.
    - Letting one depop and then defeating the other makes this a lot easier to deal with.
[7] Check the Fountain of Kings again, after the bodies disappear, for some [KI] Drops of Amnio.
[8] Return to Halver.
[9] Wait 1 Earth minute, then exit the Chateau (into Northern San d'Oria) for another cutscene.
