## Walkthrough

After confirming the mission with Emhi Tchaoryo, go to a Beastmen controlled area in the Northlands, such as Beaucedine Glacier (S) or Xarcabard (S). For Beaucedine Glacier (S), find the ??? in (H-9) down the ramp near the Campaign Arbiter and to the left when going down the ramp. In Xarcabard (S), the ??? is located at (G-10). Once interacted with, a Frost Bomb will appear and attacks immediately. Do not attack the Bomb, but do not let it despawn/deaggro/stay unclaimed either. Run to the Campaign Fort with the Frost Bomb, upon arrival, it will automatically use Hypothermal Combustion to deal damage to the fort. It does not hurt nearby players. You will receive the EXP award immediately and a message stating that "The stronghold has sustained considerable damage." Afterward, speak to Emhi Tchaoryo to complete the Campaign Op and receive Allied Notes.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] If the Frost Bomb de-aggros, the Campaign Op will fail. Failing the Campaign Op will give the message, "The bombardment failed to inflict damage on the target."
