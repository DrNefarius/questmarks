## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Romualdo, (K-9) Metalworks in the present.
    - The quest doesn't appear in your log until the next step.
[2] Go to the past, and speak to Romualdo in Batallia Downs (S), (I-7).  (On top of a hill just past the Rampart Gate.)
    - (Optional) Speak to Romualdo again for additional text.
[3] Talk to Childerich at (E-12) by the Old Cabin in Grauberg (S).
    - (Optional) Speak to Childerich again for addition text.
[4] Examine the ??? at (J-9) to the east of the Grauberg Guntower.
[5] Speak to Childerich.
    - (Optional) Speak to Childerich again for addition text.
[6] Speak to Romualdo in Batallia Downs (S) to complete the quest and receive your reward.
    - (Optional) Speak to Romualdo again for additional text.

Some post-quest cutscenes are available that award the proper title.
[7] Speak to Romualdo in present.
[8] Speak to Childerich and chose the option "Message on the Wind" to get the title.
    - You can select the other options for optional text without affecting the quest outcome.
