## Walkthrough

thumb|right|200px|Aydeewa Subterrane (Map 2) thumb|right|200px|Caedarva Mire (Map 5)
[1] Head to Aydeewa Subterrane via one of the methods below.
    - Survival Guide - Take the guide to Aydeewa Subterrane. This is the fastest option if you have it.
    - Unity Warp - Take the CL125 Wajaom Woodlands and zone into Aydeewa Subterrane right behind you. This will take you to the entrance with the Survival Guide.
    - Via Wajaom Woodlands - Head to (G-7) in Wajaom Woodlands.
[2] Make your way to the western portion of Aydeewa Subterrane (Map 5).
    - If you took the Survival Guide here, travel West on Map 2 to slide down drop point D which will take you there.
    - If you entered via the third method at (G-7) in Wajaom Woodlands, simply head South to the same spot.
[3] At (F-8/G-8) there is a blank target you need to check for a cutscene to complete the mission.
