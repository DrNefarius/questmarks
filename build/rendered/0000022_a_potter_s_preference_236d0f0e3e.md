## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Nereus (I-8) near the Proto-Waypoint to begin this quest.
[2] Go to I-7 (third map) in Gusgen Mines, near a pond, and click on the "Clay" to obtain Gusgen Clay:
    - Sneak will be useful, as there are level 85+ monsters in this area.
        - You can gain Sneak and Invisible by purchasing Circumspection from the Grounds Tome at (H-7), however only do so after opening the below door.
    - Use the Survival Guide warp then head north.
    - Run down the stairs and enter the rightmost door at (H-6). If the door is closed, use the lever to open the door; you cannot use the lever if you are Invisible.
    - Drop down the hole in the northeastern corner of (I-6) on Map 2.
    - You will find the clay near the right-most pond at (I-6/7) on Map 3.
[3] Bring the Gusgen Clay back to Nereus to complete the quest.

== Notes ==

[4] You must wait 8 hours before repeating this quest.
