## Walkthrough

[1] Easily soloable mission
[2] After confirming the mission with Rasdinice, go to a San d'Orian controlled Campaign area and talk to the Gate Sentry.
[3] List of Gate Sentries:
    East Ronfaure (S): (J-11)
[4] You will be required to escort two NPCs named "Reinforcement" to the Campaign NPC of the zone.
    Note: If you get too far away from the NPC, he will say "You're all slower than turtles in the mud! I've had enough!" and he will then proceed to run his own path towards the Campaign NPC. You can regain control of the NPC by talking to him. They may also become distracted by a perceived enemy scout and run off in a random direction, regain control by talking to them.
[5] List of Campaign NPCs:
    East Ronfaure (S): (H-8)
    Batallia Downs (S): (J-8)
    Rolanberry Fields (S): (J-7)
    Sauromugue Champaign (S): (H-6)
    Pashhow Marshlands (S): (K-6)
[6] Once you've arrived at the Campaign NPC with both Reinforcements, talk to him/her and you will be told to make an official report to Sir Rasdinice.
[7] After talking to Rasdinice, the mission is complete.
