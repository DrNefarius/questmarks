## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Lucretia the guard at Bivouac #1 in Ceizak Battlegrounds and begin the quest.
[2] Kill Fernfelling Chapulis (directly to the south of the start NPC, at H-9) until you obtain the Mantid bait key item.
[3] Use it to spawn Truculent Mantis at the Claw Mark, directly to the north of the start NPC at H-8.
    - Defeating this NM will reward Flayed mantid corpse.
[4] Return to Lucretia and exchange the Flayed mantid corpse for your reward.
