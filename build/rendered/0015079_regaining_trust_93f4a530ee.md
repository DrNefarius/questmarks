## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Luto for a cutscene that begins the quest.  She will ask you to check on your Adventuring Fellow who is in the Infirmary.
[2] Speak to Monberaux at (G-10) for a cutscene, then return to Luto for another cutscene.
[3] Head to Qufim Island and go to (E-6) to find a Giant Footprint.
    - You need to head north past Delkfutt tower and follow the tunnel to (E-6).  It is next to a cermet spine.
[4] Examining it will pop a Gigas NM Ingaevon and your Adventuring Fellow.  Defeat Ingaevon, then speak to your Adventuring Fellow.
    - The gigas can be spawned again 3 minutes after it's killed.
[5] Return to Luto in Upper Jeuno for a cutscene, then speak to Monberaux for a cutscene that completes the quest.
