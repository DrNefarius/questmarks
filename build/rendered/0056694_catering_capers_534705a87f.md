## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Regine (A) located at (E-3) near Veridical Conflux #01.
[2] She gives you 3 Hatchets and requests you log 3 Plat. Chestnut Plateau Chestnuts from the trees in Abyssea - La Theine.
    - You can bring your own Hatchets obtained by other means.
    - Logging Points exist between Conflux #01 and Conflux #02, around Puks, West/South of Conflux #07 and in the Opo-opo.
[3] Turn in the 3 Plat. Chestnut Plateau Chestnuts to Rugiette (A) located at (H-7), Veridical Conflux #04.

Zoning is not required to repeat this quest.
