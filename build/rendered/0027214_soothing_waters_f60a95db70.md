## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Fari-Wari in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (K-12) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
[2] Go to (G-9) and talk to Eunheem.
[3] Talk to Nadeey at (K-7) inside the Walahra Temple. (You may get a cutscene upon entry for The Rider Cometh.)
[4] Talk to Mihli Aliapoh in Al Zahbi (H-7), who tells you to obtain a Colorful Hair.
[5] Use the (I-7) Bhaflau Thickets entrance to Aydeewa Subterrane (not the Survival Guide entrance), and find the ??? at (H-8) after sliding down the ramp. Trade the Colorful Hair for a cutscene.
    - Kill Aydeewa Diremites until you get a Colorful Hair
    - You can find these around I-9 after you take the I-7 entrance and slide down at H-8. Circle around back up top to farm item and slide back down to the ??? at H-8.
    - Using the Voidwatch warp if you have it is a quick shortcut to getting there.
[6] Return to Fari-Wari to complete the quest.

You may only get the cutscenes when General Mihli Aliapoh is in Al Zahbi.
