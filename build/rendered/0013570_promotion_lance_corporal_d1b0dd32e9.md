# Promotion: Lance Corporal

Fame:       AU

## Walkthrough

[1] After accepting the quest, talk to Nafiwaa at the Alchemist's Guild in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (G-5).
[2] After receiving the 5 test tube key items, visit various ponds in Bhaflau Thickets and Wajaom Woodlands:

[TABLE]
    Empty test tube | Pond Location | Test tube
    Empty test tube #1 | Wajaom Woodlands (H-11) | Test tube #1
    Empty test tube #2 | Wajaom Woodlands (G-11) | Test tube #2
    Empty test tube #3 | Wajaom Woodlands (I-7) | Test tube #3
    Empty test tube #4 | Wajaom Woodlands (J-8) | Test tube #4
    Empty test tube #5 | Bhaflau Thickets (G-7) | Test tube #5
[/TABLE]

[3] Return to Nafiwaa for a cutscene and begin the mixing process.
[4] The quality of the mixture determines your imperial currency reward. The best quality possible is Luminium.
    - To obtain Luminium choose Test Tube #3 three times, Test Tube #2 once, Test Tube #4 once, and Test Tube #5 once.
[5] Wait a game day and zone at least once, and then return to Abquhbah for a cutscene.
[6] Choose Helping his fellow mercenaries to complete the quest.
