# Stock and Awe I (S)

The game knows this mission as: Stock and Awe I (S)   (mission/campaign #81)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Resource procurement
Description: Procure the supplies indicated by the kingdom quartermaster.
Orders:      Obtain shopping list from Quartermaster and submit items to her.
NPC Name:    Rasdinice
Zone:        Southern San d'Oria (S)
pos:         I-9
Campaign Rank: Bronze Ribbon of Service §
Time:        None
Level:       Any
Rewards:     * Increases Nation's Resources.
            * 90 Experience Points
            * 480 Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Solo mission
[2] Speak with the Quartermaster at (I-9), if you're facing Rasdinice, she'll be behind you across the way near the tents.
[3] She will provide different players with different types of 'supplies' to bring. The following are possible options:
    :*Arrowwood Log x1
    :*Ash Log x1
    :*Maple Log x1
[4] After you turn in your requested item to the Quartermaster and recieve your experience points speak with Rasdinice and you will recieve your Allied Notes and completion of the mission.
