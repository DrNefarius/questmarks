# Promotion: Corporal

The game knows this quest as: Promotion: Corporal   (quest/toau #93)

## Quest Header
Start:      Naja Salaheem - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (1-10)
Description: To track the movements of the Dark Rider, you have been given a quartz transmitter to place in his warhorse's hoofprint.
Fame:       AU
FLevel:     1
Quest Reqs: Complete 25 points worth of Assaults as Lance Corporal
Item Reqs:  (empty)
Level:      (empty)
Repeatable: (empty)
Previous:   Promotion: Lance Corporal
Next:       Promotion: Sergeant
Title:      Corporal
Reward:     
            - C Wildcat badge
            - Access to C Assaults
Expansion:  ToAU

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Naja to receive the Quartz transmitter.
[2] Find the Warhorse Hoofprint and click on it to place the Quartz Transmitter.
    - The Warhorse Hoofprint is visible on Widescan and found in four different zones:
        - Bhaflau Thickets
        - Caedarva Mire
        - Mount Zhayolm
        - Wajaom Woodlands
[3] Return to Naja to complete the quest.
