## Walkthrough

[1] Zone after finishing the previous quest.
[2] Speak to Honoi-Gomoi to begin the quest.
    - He is upstairs in his house, which is accessed through a back stairway.
[3] Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo, Port Windurst (G-5).
[4] Head to Windurst Walls (G-3) and check the door at the House of the Hero.
[5] Head to Toraimarai Canal and open a Coffer there to obtain the Joker card.
    - Picking the coffer as a Thief will work.
    - Various mobs in this zone drops the Toraimarai Coffer Key, or you can buy one from the Curio Vendor Moogle for 5,000 gil.
[6] If you spoke to Joker during the previous cut-scene, return to to the House of the Hero for your reward.
[7] If you spoke to Apururu, return to the Manustery in Windurst Woods (use Home Point #3).
[8] Return to Honoi-Gomoi to complete the quest.
