## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Roskin to begin the quest. He will say that he is very thirsty and could use a Frontier Soda to quench his thirst.
[2] Return to Roskin with a Frontier Soda for a cutscene.
    - Frontier Soda can be obtained from Bernegeois at (G-8) in Eastern Adoulin for 125 Gil.

[3] The next step involves talking to NPCs and checking ??? in the following zones:
    - Ceizak Battlegrounds
        - Check a ??? at (F-6). Use a Pickaxe on it to receive [KI] Royal fiat banning colonization and 1500 Experience Points.
            - Note: There are treants in this small area that aggro sound. Make sure you've got sneak up.
            - The ??? is on the line between E/F-6.
            - Bivouac #2 is closest.
    - Western Adoulin
        - Talk to Nikkhail at the Pioneers' Coalition (E-8) to receive [KI] Copy of the alliance agreement and 150 Experience Points.
    - Eastern Adoulin
        - Examine the two ???s in the Scouts' Coalition (F-9) to receive [KI] Record of the 17th Assembly and 100 Experience Points, as well as Copy of "Adoulin's Patroness" and 10 Experience Points.
            - It may be possible to complete this quest without obtaining Copy of "Adoulin's Patroness". [unverified] However, since the ???s are right next to each other, it should make no difference.
    - Optional: Yahse Hunting Grounds
        - Talk to Javolio (K-9) to receive [KI] Ulbukan navigation chart and 250 Experience Points.

[4] Return to Roskin who will ask you which documents are important.
    - Answer that [KI] Royal fiat banning colonization, [KI] Record of the 17th Assembly, and [KI] Copy of the alliance agreement are important but not [KI] Copy of "Adoulin's Patroness" or [KI] Ulbukan navigation chart.
        - Even if you did not obtain the [KI] Ulbukan navigation chart, he may still ask about it.
[5] You will then be rewarded with 500 Bayld and asked to come back another day.
[6] Speak to Roskin again after waiting 1 game day. He explains that after paying off his debts he doesn't have much of a reward for you. However, he gives you an additional 500 Bayld and says that he'll make it up to you in the future.
    - If you told him that all 5 pages were important he will only give you 2 Bayld as a reward.
