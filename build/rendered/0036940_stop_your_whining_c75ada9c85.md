## Walkthrough

[1] Travel to Norg and enter the Buccaneer's Quarters at (J-8).
[2] Talk to Washu.
[3] Washu will give you an Empty Barrel. You will need to fill it with Opo-Opo brew found in a Yhoator Jungle tree.
[4] You will need to examine a ??? that spawns randomly in Yhoator Jungle.
[5] Once you find the ??? the Empty Barrel will turn into a Barrel of Opo-Opo brew.
[6] Return to Washu to complete the quest.
