## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Rasdinice to being the Operation and proceed to the Adjutant at (D-8).
[2] Talk to Adjutant who will ask you which craft you specialize in.
[3] Once a craft has been selected, he will ask how to make two items from that craft.
    - What crystal is required? [List]
    - What is the first ingredient? [List]
    - What is the second ingredient? [List]
    - What is the third ingredient? [List]
    - What is the fourth ingredient? [List] (optional)
    - What is the fifth ingredient? [List] (optional)
    - What is the sixth ingredient? [List] (optional)
    - What is the seventh ingredient? [List] (optional)
    - What is the eighth ingredient? [List] (optional)
[4] Once you've completed your two tests, go back and talk to Rasdinice for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] It is possible that the NPC will ask for an additional material even after all materials have been selected. In this case, the 'No additional materials' option is available.
