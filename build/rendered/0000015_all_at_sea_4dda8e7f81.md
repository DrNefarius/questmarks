## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Paytah who is at the pier near the shopping piers (I-7).
[2] Fish the Ripped Cap out of the water and trade it to Paytah.
[3] Go to Baren-Moren in Windurst Waters South (H-7) and trade the Ripped Cap to him.
[4] Baren-Moren requires 4 Dhalmel Hides to fix the cap.
[5] Return the repaired cap to Paytah for your reward.
