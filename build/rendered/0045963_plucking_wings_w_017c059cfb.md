## Walkthrough

Note: This special campaign ops is only available when Castle Oztroja (S) is under the control of Windurst.
[1] Head to Castle Oztroja (S) after selecting the mission.
[2] Obtain a Xicu's Rosary (Key Item).
    - The key item drops off any of the Yagudo's in Castle Oztroja (S) (excluding NM's).
    - It can be obtained when the campaign op isn't flagged, so check that you don't have it before you begin farming it.
    - Every member of the party currently in the zone will receive a stone when it drops.
[3] Make your way to the top floor and examine the Collapsing Floor (H-7) to enter the battlefield.

## After the walkthrough: Plucking Wings

[1] Trusts can be summoned and there is no level cap for this battlefield.
[2] Inside the battlefield, you will face four Yagudo NMs.
    - Soo Luma the Ascended (SCH)
    - Jii Xai the Rimebladed (SAM)
    - Roo Beju the Eulogizer (BRD)
    - Zjaa Bao the Wrathherald (NIN)
[3] Only Soo Luma the Ascended has to be defeated to win the fight.
    - Soo Luma the Ascended will use Tabula Rasa and has access to all tier IV Black Magic as well as White Magic spells.
    - He has an apparently permanent Accession and Manifestation status, which causes all of the appropriate spells to be AoE, centered on the target.
    - Soo Luma the Ascended is immune to Sleep, but nothing else.
[4] His helpers are much weaker, with no known immunities. They will respawn shortly after they are defeated. The helpers share hate with Soo Luma the Ascended.
[5] Once Soo Luma the Ascended is defeated, an Armoury Crate will spawn.
[6] Return to Emhi Tchaoryo to complete the mission.
