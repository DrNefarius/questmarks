## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Ryoma in Norg to begin the quest.
[2] Travel to (I-7) in Yhoator Jungle.
    - Survival Guide Teleportation (Elshimo Uplands → Yhoator Jungle) is the quickest way. You can find one east of Home Point #1 in Norg.
    - Unity Warp (Level 122) will teleport you at (J-7).
[3] Once prepared, examine the ??? at (I-7) (northwest of Bloodlet Spring) to spawn 3 Sahagin NMs.
[4] These mobs are:
    - Kappa Akuso (Monk)
    - Kappa Biwa (Bard)
    - Kappa Bonze (White Mage)
        - Only one needs to be killed, so zoning to Den of Rancor and fighting one while the others despawn is a recommended strategy.
[5] After the Sahagin are defeated, examine the ??? again to receive the [KI]Old trick box. You will not get the KI until the mobs fully despawn, including the corpses.
[6] Return to Ryoma for another cutscene.
[7] Head to Rabao and speak with Leodarion at (F-7) on the west side of the lake near the windmill.
[8] Go to Kuftal Tunnel and obtain a Kuftal Coffer Key.
    - Alternatively, you can purchase one from the Curio Vendor Moogle.
    - Fastest route's are Survival Guide Teleportation (Vollbow → Kuftal Tunnel) or Unity Warp (Level 125) to Kuftal Tunnel.
[9] Find and open the Treasure Coffer to obtain the [KI]Large trick box.
[10] Return to Leodarion to complete the quest.
