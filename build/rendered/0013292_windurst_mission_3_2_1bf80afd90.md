# Windurst Mission 3-2

The game knows this mission as: Written in the Stars   (mission/windurst #11)

## Mission Header
Mission Name: Written in the Stars
Start:       Any Windurst Gate Guard
Description: On the command of Zubaba, the head lady-in-waiting, you are to journey beneath the central magic tower of the Horutoto Ruins. There, you are to report to the facility managed by Heavens Tower, which protects the talisman that seals Castle Zvahl. Render whatever assistance is necessary.
Repeatable:  Yes
Previous:    Windurst Mission 3-1 | To Each His Own Right
Next:        Windurst Mission 3-3 | A New Journey
Title:       None
Reward:      *Rank Points
            *[KI] Portal charm
Expansion:   windurst

## Walkthrough

This Mission is skippable. Should you skip it and obtain a higher rank, completing this mission will be different. See the notes at the end of the page. If you choose not to skip this mission, check the Three Mage Gate page for details on your options.
[1] Accept the mission and make your way to Heavens Tower.
[2] Pass by Kupipi and on to the second floor. Speak to Zubaba who will give you the [KI] Charm of light.
[3] Next, you will need to get past the Three Mage Gate in ruins tower at (J-7) in East Sarutabaruta (this is the same tower as the Survival Guide Inner Horutoto Ruins warp).
[4] Pass the Three Mage Gate (inside Inner Horutoto Ruins) and continue on to the small room at (G-7).
[5] Check the Gate of Light at G-7 map 4 for a cutscene.
[6] Return to Heavens Tower and speak to Zubaba to finish the mission.
    Zubaba does not give you the [KI] Portal charm. To obtain it, finish the mission and trade a Rolanberry to Kupipi. You may trade Kupipi the Rolanberry mid-mission, but she will not give you the Portal charm until you talk to her again after the mission is completed.

## After the walkthrough: Repeating or Skipping the Mission

This Mission will work differently if: you have skipped this Mission and obtained a higher Home Nation Rank after completing The Final Seal, or are simply repeating it.
[1] Accept the mission and speak to Zubaba. She will ask for you to bring her 3 Rusty Daggers. These can be purchased on the Auction House (but may be rare), or obtained from the Wendigos past the Three Mage Gate (the same skeletons that drop Test Answers.)
[2] Trade Zubaba the daggers to complete the mission. Obtaining the [KI] Portal charm is done the same way as completing the mission regularly.
