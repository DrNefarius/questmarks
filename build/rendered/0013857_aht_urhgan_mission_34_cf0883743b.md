## Walkthrough

[1] You will need another [KI] Ephramadian gold coin for this mission.
    - Check Jazaraat's Headstone at (E-10) in Caedarva Mire for another coin. (This can be easily reached from Caedarva Mire HP#1 or the western exit of Nashmau)
[2] Travel to and approach the Cutter to trigger a cutscene.
    - To access the Cutter, first travel to Dvucca Isle Staging Point and exit into the Mire.
        - Alternatively, use the Survival Guide in Caedarva Mire and head south to the Arrapago Reef zone. This is not the zone directly behind you into Arrapago Reef. Leave the tunnel into an open area with Qutrub and Lamia and head south.
        - Alternatively, purchase and use an Arrapago Ring with 100 Imperial standing accolades from Asrahd at Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-8). This places you a few yalms from the Cutter.
    - Head West to (G-9) and zone into Arrapago Reef.
    - Head North to (H-8) to find the Cutter.
[3] You will end up in Talacca Cove once the cutscene ends.
    - Don't warp away, the next mission is on Talacca Cove
