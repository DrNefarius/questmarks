## Walkthrough

[1] You cannot start this quest if you have another Weapon Skill Quest active. You must return to the person that gave said quest and quit it to start another.
[2] Speaking with Jaucribaix will grant you the key item Weapons Training Guide as well as the Tachi of Trials. You will need to break the latent on the Tachi of Trials before completing the rest of the quest.

    - Breaking the Latent

[3] Trade the Tachi of Trials back to Jaucribaix. This will give you the key item Map to the Annals of Truth. You will be instructed to travel to Kuftal Tunnel.

[4] Unity Warp(125) brings you relatively close, and at item level you will have no issues kiting everything on your way to spawn the NM.

[5] Take Silent Oil and Prism Powder with you and head to Kuftal Tunnel. When you enter the tunnel, follow the path to the first junction and head north. Continue north through the large tunnel until you come to (I-5) and then head southeast. The ??? to spawn the NM is in this tunnel at (L-7).

[6] This fight is against Kettenkaefer, a beetle. It is weak to Ice and Light attacks. Once you kill it, re-examine the ??? to receive the key item Annals of Truth.

[7] Go back and speak with Jaucribaix to receive your reward.
