## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Ryoma at (H-8) in Norg (Home Point #1) to begin the quest, then head to Port Bastok
[2] Speak to Kagetora inside Warehouse #2 at (F-6) (Port Bastok Home Point #3).
[3] Head to (I-5) in Port Bastok and speak with Ensetsu.
[4] After speaking with Ensetsu, make your way to (H-5)/(H-6) of Eastern Altepa Desert.
    - At this point you can continue on any job.
    - The shortest route to the ??? is to take the Survival Guide to Western Altepa Desert then zone to Eastern Altepa Desert
[5] Clear the area and examine the ??? once ready.
[6] Two non-sleepable Spider NMs called Tsuchigumo will spawn.
[7] Clicking the ??? again after defeating the Tsuchigumo will grant the [KI]Trick box.
    - Both NM corpses must disappear before you can obtain the [KI]Trick box.
[8] Head back to Norg and speak with Ryoma to complete the quest and receive your reward.
