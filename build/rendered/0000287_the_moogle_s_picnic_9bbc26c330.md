# The Moogle's Picnic!

The game knows this quest as: The Moogle's Picnic!   (quest/other #101)

## Quest Header
Start:      Moogle, Mog House
Description: Your moogle wants to take a shrimp lure and some Selbina butter back home. He sure would be happy if you found them for him.
Fame:       Other

Reward:     Increases Mog Safe capacity to 70.
Expansion:  (empty)

## Walkthrough

[1] Place a  Mahogany Bed in your Mog House then speak to the Moogle.
[2] Zone out of your Mog House then come back.
[3] Return and speak to the Moogle (may need to speak twice). You will receive a cutscene if you have level 5 fame in your home nation.
[4] Trade your Moogle a  Shrimp Lure and  Selbina Butter.
[5] When your Moogle returns from his trip after one earth minute, (but somehow never leaving your Mog House), he will give you a Mog Safe that holds 70 items.
