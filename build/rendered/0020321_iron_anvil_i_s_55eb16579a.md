# Iron Anvil I (S)

The game knows this mission as: Iron Anvil I (S)   (mission/campaign #131)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Supply manufacture
Description: To manufacture munitions and supplies, our nation has employed hundreds of guildworkers of various skill levels. You are to lend your trade knowledge to help improve quality and productivity. Speak with the adjutant (D-8) for details.
Orders:      (empty)
NPC Name:    Rasdinice
Zone:        Southern San d'Oria (S)
pos:         I-9
Campaign Rank: Brass Ribbon of Service §§
Time:        None
Level:       Any
Rewards:     * 140 Experience Points
            * 580 Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Rasdinice, and you'll be forwarded to Adjutant at (D-8).
[2] Talk to Adjutant, and he'll ask you for a craft to specialize in.
[3] Once you've picked a craft, he'll test you on two synths made with said craft.
    - What crystal is required? [List]
    - What is the first ingredient? [List]
    - What is the second ingredient? [List]
    - What is the third ingredient? [List]
    - What is the fourth ingredient? [List] (optional)
    - For example, the items that came up for Clothcraft were a pair of Brais, and a pair of Gaiters.
[4] Once you've completed your two tests, go back and talk to Rasdinice for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This is repeatable (as long as you have tags), making it a very quick and easy way to stock up on Allied Notes.
[2] This Ops mission is identical to Crystal Fist I (S) in nearly every aspect except the fact that there are two synth tests instead of three, and your reward is greater.
