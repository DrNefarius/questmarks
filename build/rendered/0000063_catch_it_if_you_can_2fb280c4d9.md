## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Ohruru to begin this quest.
[2] You must return to her while under the influence of one of the following status effects:
    - Bane: given by Lost Souls in Eldieme Necropolis.
        - Rewards 1,200 Gil.
    - Mute: given by Eight of Cups in Outer Horutoto Ruins from the (F-11) entrance in West Sarutabaruta.
        - Mute is given by an Additional Effect of its weapon.
        - Rewards 1,000 Gil.
    - Plague given by:
        - Bloodsuckers in Toraimarai Canal. Caused by an additional effect of their melee swings. Long duration debuff.
        - Deathmaw Orobons in Ceizak Battlegrounds (from Vile Belch, which also inflicts Silence, so if planning to use Warp to return to Ohruru quickly, either do so with an item or after using Echo Drops;
        - Devouring Mosquito in Reisenjima - (from Infected Leech, lasts 45s, so if planning to use Warp to return to Ohruru quickly) - near likely the most viable option at level cap).
        - Rewards 1,500 Gil.
[3] Many statuses will not last long, so be sure to set your Home Point at Home Point #1 close by.
[4] Once you complete the quest, she will remove the status ailment from you.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

The title of this quest in the Japanese version is "Super Doctor O", which is a reference to the manga [https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Manga/SuperDoctorK Super Doctor K], whose main character is a genius doctor who always wears a cloak.
