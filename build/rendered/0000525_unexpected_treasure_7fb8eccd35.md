## Walkthrough

[1] Purchase a Cupboard from the furniture shop in Northern San d'Oria (F-8) and put it in your mog house.
[2] Zone out and back into your mog house.
[3] Wait some time (between 10-15 real-world minutes).
[4] Talk to your Moogle. He will say he found something, and give you a [KI] Small teacup.
    - If the Moogle does not give you the item, you may not have waited long enough.
[5] Return to the furniture shop and speak to Morunaude twice.
[6] Speak to Calovour in the Cathedral (M-6).
[7] Bring Calovour a Mistletoe to complete this quest.
