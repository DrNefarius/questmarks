## Walkthrough

[1] After speaking with Emhi Tchaoryo, proceed to a Yagudo controlled Campaign area.
[2] Proceed to the fortification tower in the zone, and click the ??? at the base of the tower.
    - There are two ??? at the base of the tower, if you receive a message stating "You find nothing out of the ordinary.", check the other ???.
[3] Note that /blockaid will automatically be placed on your character as soon as you touch the ???.
[4] Your character will proceed to prepare to gather intelligence, and then actually gather intelligence, both in the healing position. No action is requied on your part once the ??? has been clicked. This can take roughly 90 seconds (hence the need for /blockaid: to prevent other PC's from interrupting your intel gathering, but to still allow beastmen to interrupt you).
[5] Upon successful completion of the intelligence gathering, you will receive 190 Experience Points.
[6] /blockaid will automatically be removed at this point.
[7] Return to Emhi Tchaoryo to complete your mission and receive Allied Notes.
