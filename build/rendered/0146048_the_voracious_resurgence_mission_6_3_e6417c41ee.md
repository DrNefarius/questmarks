## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Reikuu (K-8) near Ethereal Ingress #6 in Reisenjima for a cutscene to begin the mission.
[2] Head to (F-5) Ethereal Ingress #4 and examine the Babbling Brook for a cutscene.
[3] Click the Babbling Brook again to enter Dance of the Tengu BCNM (Zhuu Buxu) 15 Minute fight time limit.
    - Has approximately 125k HP.
    - This is an Omen fight, so you will be subject to the normal Omen queue.
    - Trusts may be used in the fight.
    - Zhuu Buxu has access to Mijin Gakure.
        - Nii Aquu will assist and must be kept alive.
[4] After the fight, you'll automatically enter a cutscene.
[5] Click the Babbling Brook again to enter Dance of the Tengu BCNM (Reikuu) 15 Minute fight.
    - Has approximately 90k HP.
    - This is an Omen fight, so you will be subject to the normal Omen queue.
    - Trusts may be used in the fight.
    - Gessho will assist and must be kept alive.
    - Both Reikuu and Gessho can summon clones, and you can stagger Reikuu with a link= if you can damage the real one.
        - Doing so will remove all of Reikuu's clones.
        - AoE abilities must target the real Reikuu for this to occur.
        - The clones make it difficult to target and face Reikuu.
    - Reikuu also has access to Mijin Gakure.
        - It is capable of doing over 2k damage even in full -dt set. Even if all party members are KOed, Gessho can win the fight still.
[6] After the fight, you'll automatically enter a cutscene.
[7] Return to Reikuu for the final cutscene and your rewards.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] At this point, you can now open up access to two new Records of Eminence objectives. See the NPC Elijah in Upper Jeuno near Home Point #1 for more information.
