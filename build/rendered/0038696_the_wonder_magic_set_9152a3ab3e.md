## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Naruru, Teigero-Bangero, or Panta-Putta located in the merchant's house in Lower Jeuno (G-10), for a cutscene.
    - There is no Fame requirement to receive the cutscene
[2] Talk to Panta-Putta. He wants you to bring him a [KI]Wonder Magic Set.
    - The [KI]Wonder Magic Set is obtained from the related quest Child's Play.
[3] Return to Panta-Putta to complete the quest.
