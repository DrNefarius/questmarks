## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Shantotto (K-7) Windurst Walls to begin the Quest.
    - The Quest Blood and Glory may prevent you from flagging the Quest, return with a Job that does not meet the prerequisites for Blood and Glory.
[2] You will have to obtain 2  Bone Chips and 1  Bomb Ash. Both items can be purchased from NPCs.
[3] Trade her the items to complete the Quest.
