## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Exoroche (S) at Southern San d'Oria (S) (K-9) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
    - (Optional) Speak to Exoroche in Southern San d'Oria (K-7).
    - (Optional) Speak to Ailbeche in Northern San d'Oria (J-9). You can ask about his father or grandfather, but only one at a time. You will need to zone if you want to ask him the other option.
[2] Speak to Landeric in Jugner Forest (S) (G-5).
    - Zone into Jugner Forest (S) from East Ronfaure (S), using the Survival Guide for the fastest route.
[3] Speak to Exoroche (S) at Southern San d'Oria (S) (K-9) to receive a Bronze Sword.
[4] Trade the Bronze Sword to Exoroche (S) (K-9) for a brief cutscene. He will return it to you.
[5] Trade the Bronze Sword to Landeric in Jugner Forest (S) (G-5) for a brief cutscene.
    - You will be given multiple options. If you chose the wrong ones the sword will be returned to you.
    - The proper answers seem to be:
        - "Ask about Exoroche."
        - "Not in the slightest!"
        - "The boy's spirit is true."
        - "Protect his family and kingdom."
[6] Speak to Exoroche (S) at Southern San d'Oria (S) (K-9) to receive Trainee's Specs..
[7] If you speak to Exoroche in Southern San d'Oria (K-7) with the Trainee's Spectacles equipped, you will see a brief extra cutscene.
