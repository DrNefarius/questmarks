## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Blandine to begin this quest.
[2] Speak to Zexu.
[3] Exit the dock, then talk to Blandine again.
[4] Speak to Celestina in the nearby Goldsmithing guild.
    - The quest will now show in your log.
[5] Trade Celestina a  Sand Charm to complete this quest.
    - Sand Charm drops off Crossbones on the Ferry - Mhaura/Selbina.
    - Crossbones appear on the ferry only when pirates attack, which is a rare occurrence.
