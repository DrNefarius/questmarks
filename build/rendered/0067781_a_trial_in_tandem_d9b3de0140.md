## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Luto Mewrilah for a cutscene that begins the quest and provides the [KI] Magian Mooglehood missive.
[2] Speak to the Magian Moogle in Ru'Lude Gardens at (H-5) (the one with the orange ball on it's head, on the left by the crates) for a cutscene and a Tandem Necklace.
[3] Trade the Tandem Necklace to the Magian Moogle (Blue) in Ru'Lude Gardens at (H-5) to start Magian Trial 4444.
[4] You must defeat 30 of any type of monster that give XP to your Adventuring Fellow while wearing the Tandem Necklace while your Fellow is present.
[5] After you defeat 30 monsters, trade the Tandem Necklace to the Magian Moogle (Blue) to receive a Copper Meed.
[6] Trade the Copper Meed to Luto Mewrilah to complete the quest.
