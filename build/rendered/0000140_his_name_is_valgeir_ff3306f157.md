## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Rycharde who gives you an Aragoneu pizza to deliver to Valgeir in Selbina.
    - Rycharde will pay your fare for the next Ferry - Mhaura/Selbina if you choose to take it.
[2] Deliver the pizza to Valgeir, Selbina (I-9).
[3] Return to Rycharde for your reward.

Note
[4] You need to wait two, possibly three game days after completing Unending Chase before this quest is offered.
