# A Furious Finale

The game knows this quest as: A Furious Finale   (quest/jeuno #99)

## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Laila as a level 66 Dancer or higher to begin this quest.
[2] Head to Grauberg (S) or North Gustaberg (S) and kill Quadav there until you get a Dancer's Testimony.
[3] Trade the Dancer's Testimony to Laila who will teleport you to Qu'Bia Arena.
[4] Trade the Testimony to the Burning Circle there to enter a battle against Laila.
    - There is a 10 minute time limit for this fight.
    - If you have the "Rhapsody in Umber" Key Item, you are able to summon Trust Magic to aid you.
[5] Bring Laila to approximately 25% HP to win the fight.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Does not need to be completed (for level limit increasing) if the player has already completed New Worlds Await or better.
