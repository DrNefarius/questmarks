## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Gumbah in Bastok Mines (HP#3) at (J-7) inside a house on the upper walkway, in the back room, for a cutscene.
[2] Speak to Tarnotik in Oldton Movalpolos (K-10) for a cutscene.
[3] To reach Tarnotik, the best options are:
    - The Proto-Waypoint warp takes you to (K-10), which is closest.
    - Survival Guide to Movalpolos.
[4] Next, travel to Mine Shaft #2716 and interact with the Shaft Entrance for a cutscene.
    - Tarnotik will warp you to Mine Shaft #2716 if you trade a Snow Lily Snow Lily to him.
    - The Home Point warp to Newton Movalpolos is closest.
    - Otherwise, Twinkbrix in Oldton Movalpolos will warp you directly to Mine Shaft #2716 if you trade 10k Gil.
        - Use the Oldton Movalpolos Survival Guide, then head to E-13 down south.
[5] After the cutscene, interact with the BCNM entrance again and select To Movalpolos! to enter the battlefield.
[6] The fight is against Gloom Phantom, Awoken Vampyr Jarl, Awoken Ariri Samariri (has access to Death), and Awoken Hildesvini.
    - Trusts may be used in the fight.
    - Jabbos will assist and can tank one mob while you work on the rest.
        - He will engage the enemy upon drawing your weapon, regardless of your distance to the enemy.
[7] After the fight, you will automatically enter a cutscene and receive your reward.
