## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Sylvie at Western Adoulin Waterfront (I-5) Waypoint #9.
[2] Venture to the crag closest to your home nation and click the Ergon Locus ??? located beneath the stairs leading up to the Telepoint to obtain a [KI] Fistful of homeland soil.
    - San d'Oria - La Theine Plateau (K-8)
    - Bastok - Konschtat Highlands
    - Windurst - Tahrongi Canyon (I-6)
[3] Return to Sylvie with the soil and trade her a Petrified Log to receive the [KI] Luopan.
[4] Proceed to one of following Ergon Locus:
    - Flourishing Island - Ceizak Battlegrounds (K-10, Frontier Station)
    - Bud of the Swarm - Ceizak Battlegrounds (I-8, Bivouac #2)
    - Immutable Boulder - Yahse Hunting Grounds (F-6, Bivouac #2)
[5] Upon arriving at one of the locations above, you will receive a message stating that "You are assaulted by an uncanny sensation."
    - Use the rest (/heal) command to begin charging your [KI] Luopan.
[6] It can take up to 8 minutes of resting to gain enough of the lifestream to warm your [KI] Luopan.
    - Once you have gathered enough energy, you will receive a message saying "You feel a mystical warmth welling up inside you!"
[7] After receiving this message, return to Sylvie to complete the quest.
- WARNING: Each time you toss the Handbell reward from this quest then talk to Sylvie for AF and Empyrean Quests, she will ask for 300k gil or 150k bayld. Don't toss it until you finish Treasures of the Earth for Empyrean Body.
    - Speaking to her again may allow you to skip paying.
