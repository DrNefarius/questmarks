## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Mamaulabion at (G-6) in Norg to activate the quest (this does not need to be done before obtaining the seven items).
[2] Obtain the following seven items by selecting them as your reward after defeating the seven avatars below in the battles listed below
    - Trial by Fire (Ifrit): Egil's Torch
    - Trial by Ice (Shiva): Rust 'B' Gone
    - Trial by Wind (Garuda): Bubbly Water
    - Trial by Earth (Titan): Desert Light
    - Trial by Lightning (Ramuh): Elder Branch
    - Trial by Water (Leviathan): Eye of Nept
    - The Moonlit Path (Fenrir): Ancients' Key
[3] Return all seven items to Mamaulabion (the items must be traded one at a time and it is not necessary to turn in all seven at once).
[4] Wait until midnight (JST), then speak with Mamaulabion once more to receive the Evoker's Ring.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This quest is only repeatable if Evoker's Ring is dropped.
