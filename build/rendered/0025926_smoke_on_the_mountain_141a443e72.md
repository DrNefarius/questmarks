## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Hungry Wolf inside the Craftmen's Eatery to start the quest. (Use Home Point #1.)
    - (Optional) Speak to Offa at Bastok Markets (F-10) (second floor, door on the left) for dinstructions on how to make the sausage.
[2] Obtain a G. Sheep Meat from the Auction House or from Ornery Sheep in South Gustaberg (or elsewhere.)
[3] Travel to South Gustaberg (K-9) to find a Goblin campfire (???).
    - To find this, look on your map for the small hill at (K-9). Proceed to the area directly south of the hill at the top of (K-10) and you'll see a ramp to go up the hill. It blends in with the surroundings, so keep an eye open for it.
[4] Trade the G. Sheep Meat to the campfire and to begin to cook it.
[5] Wait for a minute (Earth time). DO NOT ZONE OUT, or you will have to repeat these steps again!
[6] After the required wait time, check the campfire again to receive a Galkan Sausage.
    - It goes directly into your inventory, so make sure you have an open slot.
[7] Trade the Galkan Sausage to Hungry Wolf to complete the quest.
