## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Shamarhaan (F-9) in Bastok Markets - short run from HP#2.
[2] Head to Aht Urhgan Whitegate, and talk to Iruki-Waraki (K-9, top floor).
[3] Speak with Ghatsad in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-7), inside of the Automaton Workshop.
    - It's possible you will need to speak to him twice.
[4] Take the northern Port Ephramad ferry to Nashmau and go through North Gate to Caedarva Mire. Head NE/NW to the (I-6) zone to Arrapago Reef.
    - Taking the survival guide to Arrapago Reef works as well.
[5] Find the ??? at (H-10) on the first boat, hidden beneath the stairs, to receive [KI] Antique automaton.
    - THIS IS NOT THE ??? THAT YOU CHECK FOR CORSAIR, IT IS ON THE SOUTHERN BOAT OF H-10
    - Note that a Lamian Fang Key is not needed to access the ???.
[6] Speak to Ghatsad.
[7] Wait until the next Vana'diel day and speak to Ghatsad to receive your new Automaton.
[8] Speak with Iruki-Waraki (K-9, top floor) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate to complete the quest and receive your Animator. You will also be asked to choose a name for your Automaton from a list.
    - You can choose from more names if you opt to rename your Automaton.
