## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo, and you'll be forwarded to Adjutant at (F-9).
[2] Talk to Adjutant, and he'll ask you for a craft to specialize in.
[3] Once you've picked a craft, he'll test you on three synths made with said craft.
    - What crystal is used for this synth? [List]
    - What is the first ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the second ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the third ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the fourth ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the fifth ingredient in this synth [List]
[4] Once you've completed your three tests, go back and talk to Emhi Tchaoryo for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This is repeatable (as long as you have tags), making it a very quick and easy way to stock up on Allied Notes.
[2] There is a time limit for your answer, so when the Adjutant tells you which recipe he wants, look it up quick before moving on to the questions.
    - The time limit begins the line after he specifies which item he wants the recipe for, and is less than 45 seconds, possibly 30?
[3] Must have achieved "Brass Wings of Service" to access.
