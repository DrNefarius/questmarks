## Walkthrough

[1] Examine the Entrance: Coliseum at Rala Waterways (M-6) for a cutscene.
    - Upon entering the battlefield, your [KI] Ash runic board will be become a [KI] Blank ash runic board.
[2] This fight will be against Teodor.
    - He will surrender below 10% HP.
    - He does consistent AoE damage, but takes good magic (resistant to Sanguine Blade) and physical damage.
    - He puts up Dread Spikes that will kill you from your own melee swings if not dispelled.
[3] After defeating Teodor, there will be a brief cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] If you fail the battle, you must wait until the next game day and re-enter Rala Waterways.
    - Upon zoning in, your [KI] Blank ash runic board will automatically convert back into an [KI] Ash runic board, allowing you to reattempt the battle.
[2] Completing this Mission allows you to obtain [KI] Prototype sigil pearl for Vagary entry.
