# Provide: Yorcia Weald

The game knows this assignment as: Provide: Yorcia Weald   (quest/coalition #18)

## Giver
Task Delegator - Western Adoulin (G-7)
  (resolved from the Couriers' Coalition page; the assignment page names no NPC)

## Walkthrough
[1] Once you accept the assignment, the Task Delegator will provide you with a Yor. FS building mat. container to deliver to a Station Administrator in Yorcia Weald.
    - DO NOT use a Waypoint warp. Doing so will cause the Yor. FS building mat. container to break and make you unable to complete the assignment.
        - As of the [http://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/40059-Feb-18-2014-%28JST%29-Version-Update?p=493165&viewfull=1#post493165 Feb. 18 2014 Version Update], Waypoints can now be used to complete this task without risk of break. Simply warp to a Station Administrator.
[2] Head to Cirdas Caverns as quickly as you can.
[3] Travel to the middle of the map, and use a combination of "Climbing" and "Watercrafting" to access Yorcia Weald.
[4] From the entrance of Yorcia Weald, follow the path forwards a short distance and speak to the Station Administrator to complete the assignment.
[5] Return to the Task Delegator to receive your reward (you may use Waypoints again now).
