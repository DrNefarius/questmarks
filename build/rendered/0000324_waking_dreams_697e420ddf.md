## Walkthrough

Warning: Trust Magic can not be used in this BCNM.

[1] Speak to Kerutoto, Windurst Waters South (J-8) HP #3, for a cutscene that begins the quest and key item [KI] Vial of dream incense.
    - Windurst Waters Home Point #3 is just south of Kerutoto.
[2] Head to Pso'Xja Home Point #1.
----
Red!If you do not yet have the Home Point:
    - Travel to Beaucedine Glacier (H-8) and enter Pso'Xja.
        - The Beaucedine Glacier Survival Guide brings you at H-9, just south of the entrance to Pso'Xja.
    - Once inside, the correct displacement sequence is red → purple → red → purple.
        - You will lose all buffs each time you go through the displacements; this includes Trust Magic.
    - Take the elevator down.
    - Head forward (North), then left (West) at the intersection.
    - Use the Stone Gate and select Yes when asked to use the Pso'Xja pass[KI].
    - The Home Point is just past the gate on the left.
----
[3] Head down the steps into The Shrouded Maw.
[4] Select the Memento Circle and pick Waking Dreams.
[5] Defeat Diabolos Prime to receive a key item: [KI] Whisper of dreams.
    - Diabolos Prime has the following spells and abilities:
        - Drain
        - Aspir
        - Sleepga II
        - Dispelga
        - Blindga
        - Noctoshield- grants Diabolos the effect of "Phalanx."
        - Dream Shroud- Enhances magic attack and magic defense of Diabolos.
        - Camisado- Deals physical damage.
        - Nether Blast- Delivers a ranged attack that deals darkness elemental damage.
        - Ultimate Terror- Decreases attributes of players within area of effect.
        - Nightmare- Deals darkness elemental damage that puts all enemies within the area of effect to sleep and gradually reduces their HP.
        - Ruinous Omen- Astral Flow ability- Reduces the HP of enemies within the area of effect by a random percentage.
    - Tiles on the battlefield will vanish during the battle, dropping players to a Diremites-filled death.
[6] Return to Kerutoto with the whisper and she will provide several reward options including the ability to summon Diabolos.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Up to 6 party members can enter the arena.
[2] Each party member must have their own [KI] Vial of dream incense
[3] Time limit is 30 minutes
[4] You can only select 1 of the rewards Kerutoto offers at any time
[5] You may repeat this quest once per Earth day.
[6] When clearing for the first time, the event with Diabolos in The Shrouded Maw will change depending on your progress in Chains of Promathia Missions.
