# Crystal Fist III (B)

The game knows this mission as: Crystal Fist III (B)   (mission/campaign #293)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Supply manufacture
Description: To manufacture munitions and supplies, our nation has employed hundreds of young, unskilled guildworkers. You are to lend your trade knowledge to aid in the training of these initiates. Speak with adjutant (J-9) for details.
Orders:      (empty)
NPC Name:    Hieronymus

pos:         E-8

Rewards:     * 290 Experience Points
            * 880 Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus, and you'll be forwarded to Adjutant at (J-9).
[2] Talk to Adjutant, and he'll ask you for a craft to specialize in.
[3] Once you've picked a craft, he'll test you on three synths made with said craft.
    - What crystal is used for this synth? [List]
    - What is the first ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the second ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - etc
    - There is a time limit for choosing the correct ingredients.
[4] Once you've completed your three tests, go back and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This is repeatable (as long as you have tags), making it a very quick and easy way to stock up on Allied Notes.
[2] Must have achieved "Golden Star" to access.
[3] Possible alchemy recipes include: (alphabetical order)
    - artificial lens --- fire crystal, glass fiber, glass fiber
    - baking soda --- lightning crystal, rock salt, movalpolos water
    - chimera blood --- lightning crystal, lesser chigoe
    - copper bullet --- fire crystal, firesand, copper ingot
    - distilled water --- lightning crystal, tahrongi cactus
    - earth arrowheads --- earth crystal, marid tusk, copper ingot
    - fire arrowheads --- fire crystal, slime oil, grass cloth, iron ingot
    - ice arrowheads --- ice crystal, cermet chunk, copper ingot
    - lightning arrowheads --- lightning crystal, copper ingot, steel ingot
    - minnow --- fire crystal, glass fiber, copper ingot
        - this is one of two recipes
    - polyflan paper --- earth crystal, polyflan
    - prism powder --- light crystal, glass fiber, glass fiber, artificial lens
    - sleeping potion --- water crystal, chamomile, sleepshroom, poison flour
    - vermilion lacquer --- fire crystal, sulfur, mercury
    - water arrowheads --- water crystal, copper ingot, merrow scale
    - wax sword --- water crystal, beeswax, bronze sword
    - wind arrowheads --- wind crystal, copper ingot, colibri beak
