## Walkthrough

Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
[1] Speak to Naji for a cutscene.
    - You may need to speak to him several times to get the correct cutscene.
[2] Head to Norg and speak with Gilgamesh behind the Oaken Door at (K-8). Continue speaking with him until he mentions Frag Rock Frag Rocks.
    - You will receive up to 6 Frag Rock Frag Rocks per fight.
[3] Obtain 1 Adaman Ore Adaman Ore for every 6 players in your party/alliance.
    - These may be obtained from the Auction House (link= ➞ Materials ➞ Smithing)
    - If none available on Auction House, they can be mined in Ifrit's Cauldron. Be prepared for a long mining session as the drop is 'Very Rare'
[4] Proceed to Yhoator Jungle (I-5) and enter Ifrit's Cauldron.
    - Quick Travel options:
    - Ifrit's Cauldron Home Point #1, then cast Escape and zone back in.
    - Unity Warp (Level 122) → Yhoator Jungle → hug the right wall. It's a short ride.
    - Alternatively, you may use the Elshimo Uplands Outpost Warp or Survival Guide.
[5] You will have to reach a ??? which isn't too far inside. You may need use Sneak and Invisible (still useful inside the Cauldron at level 99 to avoid aggroing) to get there, as there are Bats and Bombs in the way.
[6] Once inside, head left at the first intersection, and then on the next intersection go right. The ??? is in a lava flow area around (H-7), Map 1.
[7] Buff up and prepare to fight. Trading an Adaman Ore Adaman Ore to the ??? will spawn a lvl 65 Bomb, Magma, and Salamander, a Lizard NM.
    - Barfira is recommended.
    - The Lizard is much weaker than the Bomb.
    - Sneak pulling is recommended, as you only need to defeat the Bomb, and you do not need to wait for the Lizard to depop.
    - Magma may use Self-Destruct.
[8] Upon defeating Magma, it will drop 6 Rare/Ex Frag Rock Frag Rocks.
    - If doing this fight for more than 6 people, you will need to spawn them again.
[9] When everyone has their Frag Rock, head back to Norg and trade it to Gilgamesh for a cutscene.
[10] Head back to Naji for a final cutscene and your reward.
