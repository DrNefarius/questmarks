## Walkthrough

[TABLE]
     |
[/TABLE]

[1] Speak to Dhea Prandoleh (H-10) for a cutscene, then to Velda-Galda (K-9) for another cutscene.
[2] Head to Fort Karugo-Narugo (S) and check the Stone monument at (E-7) (outside, Map 1).
[3] Check the Succulent Cactus at (F-5) for a [KI]Succulent dragon fruit
    - This is a great opportunity to unlock the Survival Guide at H/I-5 if you have yet to get it.
[4] Return to the Stone monument for another cutscene.
[5] Examine the Sunken Hollow at (G-7) for a cutscene.
[6] Examine it again to enter a BCNM in Ghoyu's Reverie.
[7] The object of this BCNM is to escort both Nhev Befrathi and Syu Befrathi to the exit.
    - Both mother and daughter can be escorted separately.
    - The daughter walks extremely slowly; however, she will run away very quickly if she gets too close to any monster inside.
    - The daughter will also run away if she's too far from the mother, regardless of whether you're close to her.
        - It's recommended to slay all enemies on the path to the next map, bring the two just at the border of the next map, then do it again for all the crabs on the path. Note that the daughter will run WILDLY around the giant shells in the room with the Yagudo NINs, it is recommended slaying them and the far goblin near the shells. The mother, however, will NOT run clear across the room to path around them.
    - The exit is located at (G-9) on map 2.  Both NPC's must be brought there. Upon success, you will be teleported out of the area.
    - If you fail the BCNM, you will need to get another [KI]Succulent dragon fruit from the Succulent Cactus at (F-5). You can not obtain another [KI]Succulent dragon fruit until the following game day.
[8] When you finish the BCNM you appear in West Sarutabaruta (S) right next to the Windurst Waters (S) zone.
[9] Zone into Windurst Waters (S) for a cutscene and your reward.
