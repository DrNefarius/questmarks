## Walkthrough

thumb|right|Nyzul Isle Staging Point
[1] Enter the Chamber of Passage and take the Runic Portal to the Nyzul Isle Staging Point.
    - See its own page for instructions on reaching it if you haven't previously.
[2] Take the southeastern transport device at (H-9) to get to the Bhaflau Remnants.
[3] Take the right (eastern) transport device to get to the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins.
[4] The cutscene will start when you zone in.
    Once the cutscene finishes, you'll recieve the [KI] Astral compass and proceed to the next mission.
