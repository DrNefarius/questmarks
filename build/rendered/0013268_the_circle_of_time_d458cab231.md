## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Mertaire in Lower Jeuno, who tells you to take the Star ring (tarnished) to an antique collector.
    - Quest wont be listed in log yet.
[2] Speak to Imasuke (E-6) in Port Jeuno.
    - You must have your current job set to Bard to receive the cutscene.
    - After speaking with him, you may change to any job.
[3] Travel to (I-10) in Xarcabard and examine the Perennial Snow at the end of the short tunnel.
    - You must now wait one Earth minute before you can retrieve the ring.
[4] Retrieve the Star ring (indecipherable) by examining the Perennial Snow.
[5] Go back to Imasuke who tells you to find someone who can translate the ring.
[6] Head to Chateau d'Oraguille and speak to Chalvatot at (F-7) to receive the Moon ring.
[7] Listen to his instructions and travel to Monastic Cavern via Davoi @ (H-11)
[8] Walk to the small circular room at (I-8) with the altar in it
    - Be careful of the True Sight Orcish Warlord that roams the area
[9] After clearing the Orcs, examine the altar to spawn Bugaboo
[10] Defeat him, then examine the altar again for a cutscene.
[11] Return and speak to Chalvatot for your reward, and to complete the quest.
