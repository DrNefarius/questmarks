## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Ilumida in Upper Jeuno at (G-8) to begin this quest.
[2] Speak to Rouliette at (H-9) in the Temple of the Goddess.
[3] Rouliette will provide you with the [KI] Holy candle once you complete the quest Candle-making.
