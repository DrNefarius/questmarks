## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus.
[2] Talk to the Gate Sentry in either Beaucedine Glacier (S) or Xarcabard (S).
[3] The Gate Sentry hands you a package and applies the effects of Silence, Amnesia, and Gravity
[4] Talk to the NPC at the stronghold and receive Experience Points.
[5] Report back to Hieronymus to complete mission and receive Allied Notes.
