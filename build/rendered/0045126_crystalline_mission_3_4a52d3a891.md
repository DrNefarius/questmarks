## Walkthrough

You need to retrieve 3 Key Items used to pop Goblin Notorious Monsters. Any characters on (or past) this mission can receive the goblin food key items as long as they are in the same party/alliance.
[1] These key items have a 10% obtainment rate. If you are in a party/alliance, 1 character at random will be chosen to receive it (if they don't have it already).
[2] NOTE: You must have the respective Goblin food Key Item when the NM is killed in order to obtain the Seedspall Key Item.

## After the walkthrough: {{KI}} [[Seedspall Roseum]]

[1] Kill Goblins in Batallia Downs to obtain the Key Item [KI] Bowl of bland Goblin salad.
    - They are scattered around the west side of the zone.
[2] Examine the ??? at (F-5) (middle) to pop Vegnix Greenthumb, and kill it to receive [KI] Seedspall Roseum.
    - The ??? is on the upper level of the area, which is connected to Beaucedine Glacier.
        - If you've unlocked the Lycopodium Warps in the past, trade a flower to the glowing spot at Batallia Downs (F-5) to teleport up.
        - The Survival Guide will bring you to (H-9) in Beaucedine Glacier. From there, navigate the area to reach the connection at (H-11).
        - You can teleport to Beaucedine Glacier via Voidwatch NPC (requires level 75 and [KI] Adventurer's Certificate). Walking there requires going through Ranguemont Pass (requires [KI] Adventurer's Certificate).

## After the walkthrough: {{KI}} [[Seedspall Caerulum]]

[1] Kill Goblins in Rolanberry Fields to obtain the Key Item [KI] Jug of greasy Goblin juice.
[2] Examine the ??? at (D-11) (NE corner) to pop Chuglix Berrypaws, and kill it to receive [KI] Seedspall Caerulum.
    - The ??? is on the upper level of the area, which is connected to Crawlers' Nest.
        - The Unity Warp 125 to Rolanberry Fields will bring you to (D-11).
        - As it shares the same area as Simurgh, it can be reached by Voidwatch NPC teleport (Jeuno -> Crawlers' Nest (entrance)).

## After the walkthrough: {{KI}} [[Seedspall Viridis]]

[1] Kill Goblins in Sauromugue Champaign to obtain the Key Item [KI] Chunk of smoked Goblin grub.
    - Some are scattered around the Survival Guide area, which is conveniently close to the ???.
[2] Examine the ??? at (J-9) (almost exactly at the I/J-9/10 intersection) to pop Dribblix Greasemaw, and kill it to receive [KI] Seedspall Viridis.
    - The Survival Guide to Sauromugue Champaign will bring you to (J-10).
    - As it shares the same area as Roc, it can be reached by Voidwatch NPC teleport (Jeuno > Garlaige Citadel (entrance)).
    Finally, examine the ??? at (G-6) in Qufim Island with the 3 Seedspall Key Items for a cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: "A Crystalline Prophecy" Key

[1] Note: Goblin Food Key Items are used to spawn the NMs for the Key Items used on the next mission. Recommend waiting to complete the next steps until you've completed Gatherer of Light (II)
    - Goblin Food Key Items are not lost when used to spawn NMs.
[2] Re-obtain a [KI] Bowl of bland Goblin salad, [KI] Jug of greasy Goblin juice, and [KI] Chunk of smoked Goblin grub, if needed.
[3] Head to Port Jeuno and speak to Squintrox Dryeyes (G-8) to receive [KI] Viridian Key.
    - You will lose your [KI] Bowl of bland Goblin salad, [KI] Jug of greasy Goblin juice, and [KI] Chunk of smoked Goblin grub.
    - The key can be received once per day.
    - Accepting the [KI] Viridian Key triggers a time limit (one real day) for the quest Gatherer of Light (II).
    - Squintrox Dryeyes will refuse to give you a [KI] Viridian Key if you already did ( Gatherer of Light (I) or Gatherer of Light (II) ) on the same day.
        - Refusal message is ""Woah, woah! Slow down there, bud. Gimme some time to sort out the stuff you just sold me, why don't ya?"
[4] Use the [KI] Viridian Key (Level 40) to open the red treasure coffer at Lower Jeuno (J-8).
    - Rewards are listed on the Add-on Scenario Rewards page.
