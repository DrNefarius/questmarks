[1] You need the Thunder Hammer Thunder Hammer you received from Mission 5-3 to complete this.
[2] Head to Map 3 of Arrapago Reef (F-7) and speak with Apkallu Guide for a cutscene to begin the Mission.
    - Teleport to Caedarva Mire's Survival Guide and then zone to the East.
[3] Proceed to (G-8) in Wajaom Woodlands to examine the Leypoint for a cutscene.
    - You may use an Olduum Ring Olduum Ring to teleport directly to the spot.
    - Alternatively, use the Wajaom Woodlands Survival Guide or Unity Warp (125).
[4] Travel to (G-9) in Aydeewa Subterrane from Wajaom Woodlands.
    - To get here, first go to (E-10) in Wajaom Woodlands, Entrance 8 on the map to enter the Aydeewa Subterrane (Map 3).
        - Voidwatch Warp to Mamook will put you close to this entrance, but it's much faster to just run there.
    - Follow the path, along the left wall the whole way, do not drop down, and take Exit 7 at (J-8).
[5] Once outside keep going and after the first ledge drop you will be in an open area.
    - If you see raptors or spiders then you went the wrong way in the Subterrane.
[6] Equip the Thunder Hammer Thunder Hammer in your main weapon slot and approach the Marolith at (G-9) for a cutscene.
    - The Marolith is not targetable. If Hydra is up, you can walk around it without aggro if you do not want to kill it.
    - You will receive [KI] Aeropearl afterwards.
[7] Return to the Apkallu Guide in Arrapago Reef (F-7) via the Caedarva Mire Survival Guide for a cutscene.
[8] Speak with Ghatsad in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-7) for another cutscene.
    - Speaking to Dkhaaya before Ghatsad will give you a mini-cutscene where he tells you he cannot fix the aeropearl and send you to Ghatsad.
[9] Head to Alzadaal Undersea Ruins and defeat the Qiqirns in the area to spawn Panaiveriyamman (WAR), an Acrolith NM.

[10] Panaiveriyamman (WAR)
    - It will appear immediately after killing a Qiqirn, so summon Trusts beforehand.
    - You may need to defeat many Qiqirns before Panaiveriyamman spawns.
        - If you're having trouble popping Panaiveriyamman equip your Thunder Hammer and get physical kills with it. (Confirmed that the Thunder Hammer is not required to pop Panaiveriyamman... Just kill till it pops, the Thunder Hammer is not required.)
        - The above comment is anecdotal and should not be taken as fact. See Discussion page.
    - Uses Mighty Strikes, but doesn't hit very hard or accurately in general.
    - Resistant to magic, especially -based spells, but is very susceptible to damage.
    - Has approximately 150k HP.
    |}
[11] You will receive the [KI] Man-made solution after the fight.
    - The Key item will drop to everyone in a party/alliance, even if you are not on the same map.
[12] Return to Ghatsad for a cutscene.
    - Note: FastCS may cause you to get stuck on a black screen during this CS. Unload FastCS or use the Print Screen button to unlock from a soft freeze.
    - You will receive [KI] Repaired aeropearl afterwards.
[13] Acquire a Lamian Fang Key Lamian Fang Key once again and head back to the ??? (H-10) Map 1 Arrapago Reef.
    - Survival guide warp to Arrapago Reef.
[14] Return to the Apkallu Guide via the Caedarva Mire Survival Guide for the final cutscene and your reward.
    - FastCS will soft-lock your client to a black screen at the end of this cutscene.
        - To resolve this, upon reaching the aforementioned black screen, hold Prt Scr (or Fn + Prt Scr if on laptop or a 60% keyboard) for 4~ seconds to unlock yourself from the soft-lock.
