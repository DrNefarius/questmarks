## Walkthrough

[1] Easily soloable mission.
[2] After confirming the mission with Emhi Tchaoryo, go to a Windurstian controlled Campaign area in the Northlands and talk to the Gate Sentry.

## After the walkthrough: List of Gate Sentries

[1] Beaucedine Glacier (S): G-6
[2] Xarcabard (S): J-9
[3] Your mission is to escort two NPCs named "Reinforcement" to the Campaign NPC of the zone.
    - If you get too far away from either NPC, she will say a line of dialogue and proceed to run either on her own back towards the Gate Sentry or ahead at random, depending on her personality.
        - This will also happen periodically even if you're right next to her.
    - You can regain control of the NPC by talking to her again.

## After the walkthrough: List of Campaign NPCs

[1] Beaucedine Glacier (S): I-8
[2] Xarcabard (S): H-8
[3] Once you've arrived at the Campaign NPC, talk to them to complete the escort quest and receive your experience points.
    - If a Campaign Battle is currently in progress, the NPC will stand there, but not despawn. You will have to wait for it to finish for the mission to be counted as a success.
[4] After returning and talking to Emhi Tchaoryo, you gain the Allied Notes and the mission is complete.
