## Walkthrough

[1] Begin quest by speaking with Naiko-Paneiko, just inside the entrance of Crawlers' Nest (S) at (L-8) (when you enter from Rolanberry Fields (S)). He will give you a [KI] Scoop-Dedicated Linkpearl.
[2] Go to (G-9) in Rolanberry Fields (S) and talk to Rakula-Motakula, who is upon a ballista up the hill, behind the long fence.
[3] When prompted, talk about the Mandragoras.
[4] He will ask you 3 questions, how many leafed mandies there are, then how many black mandies, and finally how many flowered mandies there are. The answer is  4, 4, and 5.
[5] Head back to Naiko-Paneiko for your reward.
