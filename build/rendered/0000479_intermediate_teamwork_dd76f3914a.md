## Walkthrough

[1] First, zone at (C-7) in Northern San d'Oria.
[2] You should be in a watchtower in West Ronfaure now, and Vilatroire should be up ahead after zoning in.
[3] Talk to him to activate the quest.
[4] After you form a party of two (yourself and one other) with the same race, talk to him again to complete the quest.
