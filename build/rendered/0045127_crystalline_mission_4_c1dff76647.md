## Walkthrough

[1] Prepare to battle. Trust Magic can not be used in this fight.
    - You will keep your buffs and pets.
[2] Examine the ??? at (G-6) in Qufim Island to begin a battle.
[3] Several waves of Seed Mandragoras will spawn and swarm the characters. Though 5 or 6 will come at a time, they are very weak and can be easily defeated with one melee strike or AoE magic.
[4] After killing several waves of Seed Mandragora (30 total, six waves of 5) you will receive a message that the mission has ended, and an [KI] Amber Key.
[5] Examine the ??? again for a cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: "A Crystalline Prophecy" Key

[1] Re-obtain a [KI] Seedspall Roseum, [KI] Seedspall Caerulum, and [KI] Seedspall Viridis from popping the Goblin NMs from the previous mission.
    - Requires the Goblin Food Key Items: [KI] Bowl of bland Goblin salad, [KI] Jug of greasy Goblin juice, and [KI] Chunk of smoked Goblin grub.
        - Goblin Food Key Items are not lost when used to spawn the NMs.
    - Alternatively, the 3 Seedspall Key Items can be purchased from Squintrox Dryeyes for 500g and 5 Beastmen's Seals after all Crystalline Missions are completed.
[2] Travel to Qufim Island and examine the ??? (G-6) to begin the battle with the Seed Mandragoras.
[3] When you've defeated all six waves of Seed Mandragoras you will receive an [KI] Amber Key,
    - The key can be received once per day.
[4] Use the [KI] Amber Key (Level 50) to open the red treasure coffer at Lower Jeuno (J-8).
    - Rewards are listed on the Add-on Scenario Rewards page.
