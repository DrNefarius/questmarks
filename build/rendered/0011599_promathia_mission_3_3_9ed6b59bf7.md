## After the walkthrough: Vicissitudes

[1] Zone into Northern San d'Oria for a cutscene.
[2] Head to the Cathedral and speak with Arnau at the altar (M-6).
    - (Optional) Speak to him again for some lore about the former papsque.
[3] Speak with Chasalvige, who is in the Cathedral's Manuscript room (L-6) for a cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: Descendants of a Line Lost

[1] Head to Carpenters' Landing via the (E-6) entrance in Jugner Forest.
[2] Speak with Guilloud at (H-10) to spawn the NM Overgrown Ivy.
    - This is a Morbol-type mob and can use Hundred Fists. You may need some high level help to defeat it if you are lower leveled.
[3] Speak with Guilloud again after defeating Overgrown Ivy.

## After the walkthrough: Louverance

[1] Speak with Hinaree at (B-6) in Southern San d'Oria upstairs in the Count's Manor for a cutscene to complete the San d'Oria path.

## After the walkthrough: Comedy Of Errors, (Act I)

[1] Zone into Windurst Waters for a cutscene.
[2] Speak to Ohbiru-Dohbiru at (J-9) inside the Rhinostery, located in South Windurst Waters (Map 2), Home Point #3 will take you right next to it.
[3] Speak with Yoran-Oran at (E-5) in Windurst Walls (Home Point #1).
    - Yoran-Oran offers other quests unrelated to this mission so you may need to speak with him multiple times to get the right cutscene.
[4] Speak with Kyume-Romeh at (F-10) in Windurst Waters. He is in the Tavern. Home Point #4 will take you near it.

## After the walkthrough: Comedy of Errors, (Act II)

[1] Head to Windurst Waters (South Map Home Point #3) and speak with Honoi-Gomoi at (E-7). They are upstairs behind the house to receive the [KI] Cracked Mimeo Mirror.
[2] Head back to Windurst Walls (Home Point #1) and speak with Yoran-Oran (E-5) again.
[3] Travel to (K-8) in Attohwa Chasm and look for a spot marked as "Loose Sand".
    - Fastest way to the Chasm is to use Unity Warp level 125.
    - It is recommended to start/complete the quest Middle Lands Investigation before going to Attohwa Chasm. This way, you can interact with the Geomagnetic Fount for easy access to the top of Parradamo Tor without having to climb up the mountain again. However, this won't let you skip climbing the mountain again for cases like the [KI] Mimeo Jewel which will shatter upon zoning.
[4] To reach the Loose Sand you'll have to walk around the perimeter of Parradamo Tor: Start north, proceed north-east, then south until you reach (L-8).
    - From (L-8), walk briefly west to reach (K-8) and the "Loose Sand". It is in a dead-end underneath some fancy Attohwa trees.
[5] Clicking the Loose Sand spawns an NM Antlion around level 55 named Lioumere.
    - The spawning player will be hit with Pit Ambush upon spawning.
    - Half of Lioumere's TP moves are physical while the sand named ones are , and it is weakest to and .
    - After Lioumere uses a TP move (like Sandblast or Sandtrap), it will return to the Loose Sand and recover all its HP. So it is recommended to pull it away from the Loose Sand after spawning.
    - Sleep, Bind, Stun, Gravity and a multitude of other Enfeebles will land and help prevent it from returning to the Loose Sand.
[6] Read the following four points after defeating the NM, but before clicking on the Loose Sand a second time:
    # You will receive a [KI] Mimeo Jewel after clicking the Loose Sand targetable location.
    # You will have exactly 30 Earth minutes to climb to the top of Parradamo Tor and reach the Cradle of Rebirth before the [KI] Mimeo Jewel breaks.
    # The Jewel will also IMMEDIATELY BREAK upon using a Mount, zoning, disconnecting, or using a Nexus Cape while in same area, as it counts as zoning.
    # If the Jewel breaks, you will need to return to the Loose Sand and obtain another [KI] Mimeo Jewel. You will not have to re-fight the NM.
    :*This can be quite frustrating for a lot of players because the path to the top is not clearly defined and falling off may mean 30 minutes or more of lost progress.

## After the walkthrough: Parradamo Tor

[1] After reading the points above, stand directly on top of the Loose Sand to obtain your [KI] Mimeo Jewel.
[2] Make your way up Parradamo Tor.
    - See the Parradamo Tor page for help climbing the mountain.
        - Keep your camera facing the mountain and go slow, rather than wasting time falling down and starting over.
[3] To reach the base of the mountain at (K-9), turn around from the Loose Sand and proceed south-east to then hug the right wall while proceeding north-west. The base is actually rather close after all that prior running to the Loose Sand.
    - After the base comes within view proceed south-west, behind the rock is the ramp to the path upwards.
[4] Once you reach the top with your Jewel (and sanity!) intact, check the Cradle of Rebirth to obtain 3 Key Items: [KI] Mimeo Feather, [KI] Second Mimeo Feather, and [KI] Third Mimeo Feather.
[5] Before leaving, if you have started the quest Middle Lands Investigation (part of a questline that unlocks Proto-Waypoints), go northwest of the Cradle of Rebirth (around where you finished your climb) to interact with the Geomagnetic Fount, so that you may teleport back to this peak anytime.

## After the walkthrough: Exit Stage Left

[1] (Optional) Report to Kyume-Romeh at (F-10) in Windurst Waters.
[2] Return to Yoran-Oran at (E-5) in Windurst Walls (Home Point #1) once again.
[3] Speak with Yujuju at (M-6) in Port Windurst (Home Point #3, near the Air Travel Agency)
[4] Head inside the eastern half of the Optistery in Windurst Waters (North map, Home Point #1) to speak with Tosuka-Porika at (G-8).
[5] Head back and talk to Yoran-Oran at (E-5) in Windurst Walls (Home Point #1) one final time to complete the Windurst path.
[6] (Optional) Speak to Yoran-Oran again for some additional lore about Karaha-Baruha.
