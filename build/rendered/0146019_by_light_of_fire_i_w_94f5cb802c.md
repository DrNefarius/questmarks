## Walkthrough

[1] After speaking with Emhi Tchaoryo, proceed to the Beastman Ensign at the borderline of H-8/I-8 in Fort Karugo-Narugo (S) to enter Ghoyu's Reverie.
[2] Enter the BCNM and defeat at least 9 of the 15 Yagudos.
[3] The battle is capped at 30 minutes, although you will hardly need all of it.
[4] After defeating the Yagudos, exit the BCNM via the Twilight Aureole.
[5] Only the party leader need have the Ops orders to enter the BCNM. Anyone from any nation can assist. Everyone will receive Experience Points upon completion of the BCNM. Those signed up for this Campaign Ops will receive Allied Notes upon speaking to Emhi Tchaoryo.
