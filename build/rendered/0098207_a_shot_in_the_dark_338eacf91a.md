## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Pudith (West of the Wharf to Yahse waypoint) at (F-6) in Eastern Adoulin.
[2] She'll ask for a jar of Umbril Ooze. Bring one to her to complete the quest.
    - Travel to the Yorcia Weald Frontier Station and head southwest into Cirdas Caverns where you will find a small camp of four Bloodmoon Umbril in the eastern tunnel.
    - Several spawns in Ceizak Battlegrounds, however, Appetent Umbril are limited to nighttime only.
[3] Questline is repeatable (starting from It Sets My Heart Aflutter).
[4] Questline : It Sets My Heart Aflutter -> A Good Pair of Crocs -> A Shot in the Dark
