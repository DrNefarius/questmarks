## Walkthrough

You must zone after completing Motherly Love in order to start this quest.
[1] Speak to Alisa (A) (C-11), at the outpost near Conflux #8, to begin the quest.
[2] She will return the [KI] Moon pendant to you to bury for her.
[3] Walk south through the tunnel and up by the Surveyors.
[4] Interact with the ??? at (D-11) on the concrete slab for a cutscene.
[5] Afterwards, interact with it once more for a [KI] Emerald abyssite of expertise.
[6] Return and speak with Alisa (A) to complete the quest.
