## Walkthrough

[1] You cannot start this quest if you have another Weapon Skill Quest active. You must return to the person that gave said quest and quit it to start another.
[2] Speaking with Jakoh Wahcondalo will grant you the [KI] Weapons Training Guide as well as the Dagger of Trials. You will need to break the latent on the Dagger of Trials before completing the rest of the quest.

    - Breaking the Latent

[3] Trade the Dagger of Trials back to Jakoh Wahcondalo. This will give you the [KI] Map to the Annals of Truth. You will be instructed to travel to Gustav Tunnel.
    - The quickest way to the NM is the level 128 unity warp. This will put right inside the tunnel that's leading to the NM. You will also find a Grounds Tome close to your spawn point where you can get Sneak and Invisible.
[4] Take Silent Oils and Prism Powders with you and head to Gustav Tunnel. Once inside, follow the path to (G-9) and take the western tunnel. At the fork in this tunnel, head east. In the large room, take the eastern tunnel at (I-8). The ??? to spawn the NM is at (I-9) Map 2.

== From the Unity Warp ==

[5] Head north and hug the east wall.
[6] The spawn is near the intersection of (I-9) and (J-9)

[7] This fight is against Baronial Bat. It is weak to Wind and Light attacks. Once you kill it, re-examine the ??? to receive the [KI] Annals of Truth.

[8] Go back and speak with Jakoh Wahcondalo to receive your reward.
