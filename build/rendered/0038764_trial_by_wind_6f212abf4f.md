## Walkthrough

Warning: Trust Magic CANNOT be used in this BCNM.

[1] Speak with Agado-Pugado to obtain quest. If you are positive that you have the required fame but he is not allowing you to undertake this quest, continue speaking with him until his text changes. Once you have received the [KI] Tuning fork of wind, you can now safely undertake the quest.
[2] Travel to Cape Teriggan (F-5) to enter the Cloister of Gales.
    - Preferably, home point to Cape Teriggan #1. If you don't have it yet, make sure to grab it on the way.
[3] Defeat Garuda Prime and you will receive a [KI] Whisper of gales.
[4] Return to Agado-Pugado with the whisper and she will provide several reward options including the ability to summon  Garuda (Avatar).

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Up to 6 party members can enter the arena.
[2] Each party member must have their own [KI] Tuning fork of wind.
[3] Time limit is 30 minutes.
[4] You can only select one of the rewards Agado-Pugado offers at any time.
[5] You may repeat this quest once per Earth day.
[6] You can save yourself a lot of time traveling to the Cloister by using the Mini Fork of Wind given by the quest Trial-Size Trial by Wind.  You must be on Summoner as your main job, and obtain the Mini Tuning Fork from Rahi Fohlatti, trade the fork back to Rahi Fohlatti, but enter the Protocrystal normally.  So long as you do not clear the level 20 version of the BC, you may warp to the Cloister as many times as you wish.
[7] You may also choose to retain the whisper in order to complete the requirements for this quest: The Moonlit Path.
