## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Ponono (S) at Windurst Waters (S) (K-11, North Map - Residential Area) to obtain the Key Item: Ponono's charm.
[2] Zone and speak to Ponono again.
[3] Ponono will give you three key items, you will need to go to the following bodies of water and examine them to get new key items.

[TABLE]
    Original | Location | Zone | Product
    Delicate Wool Thread | Knightwell | West Ronfaure (H-11) | Enchanted Wool Thread
    Delicate Linen Thread | Fay Spring | Grauberg (S) (F-6) | Enchanted Linen Thread
    Delicate Cotton Thread | Lake Tepokalipuka | East Sarutabaruta (F-9) | Enchanted Cotton thread
[/TABLE]

[4] Once you have all three key items, return to Ponono for a cutscene and your reward.
[5] Speaking to Ponono in Windurst Woods afterwards will lead to another cutscene.
