## Walkthrough

Utilize 15 or more Imprimaturs on completed Coalition Assignments.
[1] Depending on Fame you will get the cutscene sooner with less Imprimaturs used.
    - 15 imps utilized with I'm on a Boat / Hide and Go Peak quests completed is sufficient for all missions requiring fame.
        - It is also possible to complete missions with zero Imprimaturs spent, but the fame requirement is higher.
[2] Upon completing that, you also gain the ability to purchase Key Items that allow for participation in Wildskeeper Reives, battling against the Naakuals.
    - These can be purchased from Dimmian in Eastern Adoulin (E-6).
[3] Talk to Brenton for a cutscene to complete the mission and receive the [KI] Dinner invitation.
