## Walkthrough

[1] After just completing Hearts of Mythril, you must zone in order to flag this quest.
[2] Speak to Elki to start quest.
[3] Travel to  the top floor of Palborough Mines (Map 3).
    - Palborough Mines Home Point #1 is the fastest.
[4] Examine the Old Toolbox at the intersection of (H-7)/(H-8) to receive the Key Item Old toolbox.
[5] Return to Elki and speak to him.
[6] After the cutscene, speak to Babenn in Bastok Mines (J-6) to complete quest.
