## Walkthrough

[1] After speaking with Abeaule head over to Southern San d'Oria (G-6) and speak with Amaura.
[2] Amaura requires certain ingredients to brew the medicine, and gives you [KI] Amaura's formula which lists them:
    - 1 Insect Wing
    - 1 Malboro Vine
    - 1 Zinc Ore
[3] Gather and trade the listed items to Amaura to get the Cold medicine key item.
[4] Deliver the medicine to Abeaule for your reward.
