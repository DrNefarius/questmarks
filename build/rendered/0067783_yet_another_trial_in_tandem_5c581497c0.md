## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Luto Mewrilah for a cutscene that begins the quest and provides the [KI] Magian Mooglehood missive.
[2] Speak to the Magian Moogle in Ru'Lude Gardens at (H-5) for a cutscene and a Tandem Necklace +2.
[3] Trade the Tandem Necklace +2 to the Magian Moogle (Blue) in Ru'Lude Gardens at (H-5) to start Magian Trial 4446.
[4] Your Adventuring Fellow must Weapon Skill any type of monster that give XP to your Adventuring Fellow 20 times while you wear the Tandem Necklace +2.
    - Weapon Skills must hit in order to count.
    - Set your Adventuring Fellow to Fierce Attacker for this quest, and do not equip your Adventuring Fellow with a club, in order to avoid Moonlight and Starlight. Moonlight and Starlight do not count for Magian Trial 4446.
[5] After your Adventuring Fellow Weapon Skills 20 times, trade the Tandem Necklace +2 to the Magian Moogle (Blue) to receive a Mythril Meed.
[6] Trade the Mythril Meed to Luto Mewrilah to complete the quest.

## After the walkthrough: Notes
