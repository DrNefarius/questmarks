## Walkthrough

Category:Seekers of Adoulin Missions
[1] Proceed to the Celennia Memorial Library at (F-10) in Eastern Adoulin. Talk to Eppel-Treppel to enter the building.
[2] Read all three options in the book entitled History on the bookshelf. The section is between Yefafa and Vainrachault.
    - If you are unable to read any books on the shelves, you will first need to complete the quest Order Up from Reja Ygridhi, who is located inside the library.
        - 1000 Bayld is required to purchase a [KI] Celennia Memorial Library card from Patient Snake (Eastern Adoulin, F-9) in order to start the quest. He is located at the end of the pathway leading to the interior of the Scouts' Coalition.
[3] Return to Levil in the Pioneers' Coalition in Western Adoulin.
