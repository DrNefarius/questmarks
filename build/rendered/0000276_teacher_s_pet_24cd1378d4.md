## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Moreno-Toeno in the east Aurastery (North Map) to begin the quest. You will be requested to collect and trade in a  Bird Feather and 2Leaf Mandra Bud Two-Leaf Mandragora Bud.
    - You may need to speak with them twice.
    - Once this is done, you will be rewarded 250 gil and the quest will be complete.
[2] A  Bird Feather can be purchased from the Auction House, but the 2Leaf Mandra Bud Two-Leaf Mandragora Bud is exclusive and must be obtained from defeating Mandragora monsters.
    - Low level monsters such as Carrion Crow for the feather and Tiny Mandragora and Mandragora can be found right outside Windurst in East and West Sarutabaruta.
