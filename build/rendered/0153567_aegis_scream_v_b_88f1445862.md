## Walkthrough

[1] Participate in a Campaign battle in any Northlands allied controlled region.
[2] The mission is complete when you receive a "Objective Completed" message are told to report back to Hieronymus.
[3] If you report to Hieronymus prior to receiving the message the mission will end in failure.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This Campaign Operation is only available when either Beaucedine Glacier (S) or Xarcabard (S) is under allied control.
[2] There is presumably a minimum number of Experience Points (5000?) received from completion of campaigns to successfully complete this operation.
