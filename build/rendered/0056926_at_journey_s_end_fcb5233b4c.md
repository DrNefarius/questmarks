## Walkthrough

[1] Examine the Mithran Bivouac in Meriphataud Mountains (S) (I-8) for another Poet god's key.
[2] Head to the Collapsing Floor at the top of Castle Oztroja (S) for a cutscene.
[3] Examine the Collapsing Floor again to enter a BCNM. Your buffs will still be on.
[4] This fight is against Fenrir.
    - Fenrir will transform into Karaha-Baruha and Robel-Akbel and back throughout the fight.
    - Lehko Habhoka will assist you in this fight and must be kept alive.
[5] Return to the Mithran Bivouac in Meriphataud Mountains (S) (I-8) for a cutscene.
[6] Speak to Dhea Prandoleh (Windurst Waters (S) (H-10)) for a cutscene.
[7] Examine the Sealed Entrance at (F-10) in West Sarutabaruta (S).
[8] Return to Windurst Waters (S) to complete the quest.
    - Speak to Romaa Mihgo in Windurst Waters (S) at (G-11) to learn Trust Magic: Romaa Mihgo if you possess the [KI] Bundle of half-inscribed scrolls.

If you are working on missions then return to Adieu, Lilisette.
