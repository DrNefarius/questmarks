[1] Speak with Halver for a cutscene to begin the Mission.
[2] Speak with Rahal in the Royal Knight's Quarters for another cutscene.
[3] Head to Beaucedine Glacier.
    - The quickest way is via Survival Guide (Fauregandi).
[4] Touch the Ferric Stench (H-8) next to the East side of the tower to begin a confrontation with:
    - Orcish Atlatl (WAR)
    - Orcish Axeman (BST)
    - Orcish Praetor (WAR)
    - Harnessed Smilodon (WAR)
        - You will receive [KI] Carmine Swallows' map after the fight.
[5] Touch the Ferric Stench again for a cutscene.
[6] Touch the Point of Interest at the lowest left corner intersection of (J-6)/(I-6) for a final cutscene.
    - This is located on the lowest level, next to the Mirror Pond.
    - Fastest route is Unity warp 128 Fei'Yin and zone out into Beaucedine Glacier. Can mount here
    - You will receive Hi-Elixir Tank Hi-Elixir Tank after the cutscene.
[7] If you're heading to the next Mission, do not warp away!
