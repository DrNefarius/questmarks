## Walkthrough

[1] Head to Lower Delkfutt's Tower in Qufim Island for a cutscene and the beginning of Chapter 1.
[2] Zone into Upper Jeuno for another cutscene.
[3] Go to the Infirmary in Upper Jeuno (G-10) near Home Point #3 to speak with Monberaux.
[4] After the cutscene, you will receive the [KI] Mysterious amulet.

## After the walkthrough: Postponing

[1] If you chose to postpone the starting scenario, the mission "To Start CoP..." will appear in your mission log.
[2] Use the Tales' Beginning book at Lower Delkfutt's Tower (H-10) to start the Mission.
    - The Tales' Beginning book can only be seen visually in-game or on the map by the player(s) who set their story on hold.
