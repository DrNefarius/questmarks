## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Olbergieut to begin quest. You will receive [KI] Scripture of Wind
[2] Travel to K-8 in La Theine Plateau (its at the tail end wall of the Holla Crag) and look for an NPC named Faurbellant
[3] Speak to Faubellant and he will give you [KI] Scripture of Water
[4] Return to Olbergieut and speak with him to end quest.
