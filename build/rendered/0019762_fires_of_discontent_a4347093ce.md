## Walkthrough

Notice: Wait one game day and re-zone after completing Better Part of Valor before starting this quest.
[1] Speak to Engelhart in Bastok Markets (S) at (K-10). You are tasked with finding information about a potential Galka uprising.
[2] Speak to Pagdako in Bastok Markets (S) at (H-9)
    - Pagdako is on the way from Engelhart to the homepoint.
[3] Return to the present day Bastok Metalworks and speak with Iron Eater, inside the President's Office at (J-8). You may need to talk to him multiple times
[4] Return to the past and speak with Engelhart again.
[5] Now head to Grauberg (S) and find the ??? inside the cave at (I-6) for a cutscene.  The entrance to the cave is at (I-5), it's right on the border of H/I-5/6.
    - The Survival Guide to Grauberg (S) puts you reasonably close.
[6] After the cut-scene return to Bastok Markets (S) and speak with Engelhart again.
[7] Head towards where the Metalworks entrance would be in Bastok Markets (S) and speak with Gentle Tiger standing at the gate.
[8] Return to Engelhart and speak with him to complete the mission and receive your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] If continuing with Wings of the Goddess Missions, be sure to visit any of the Cavernous maws that you have unlocked outside Jeuno to activate the mission Cait Sith after completing this quest.
[2] Helpful Tip: If you set your homepoint to HP#1 in Bastok Markets (S) and use some form of warp, this counts as zoning.
