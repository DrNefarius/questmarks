## Walkthrough

[1] After completing Vanishing Act re-zone and wait one Earth minute.
    - The Mog House does not count as zoning.
[2] Talk to Qutiba at (K-12) for a cutscene.
[3] You may have to talk to her several times and agree to listen to a recipe before the cutscene plays.
[4] Trade Qutiba three pots of White Honey for another cutscene and your reward.
    - You may trade more White Honey if you forget the Irmik Helvasi recipe; you may get an additional food recipe also.
