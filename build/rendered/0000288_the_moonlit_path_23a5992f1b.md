## Walkthrough

Warning: Trust Magic CANNOT be used in this BCNM.

[1] Talk to Leepe-Hoppe to begin the quest. If the quest Tuning In is offered (cutscene with Shikaree Y), simply zone and talk to Leepe-Hoppe again to get the proper cutscene. Assuming you have the proper fame.
[2] Next you must battle the six avatar primes to collect their whispers.
Note:  Do not return the whispers to the NPC's who allow you access to the avatar prime battles or you will lose them.  You can get the whispers again later if you want items from the avatar prime battles.
[3] Turn the whispers into Leepe-Hoppe to receive a [KI] Moon bauble.
[4] Head to Full Moon Fountain.
    - Preferably, home point to Toraimarai Canal #1. If you don't have it yet, make sure to grab it on the way. Otherwise, the Survival Guide can be used to warp to Toraimarai Canal if you have it.
[5] With the [KI] Moon bauble key item in your possession, interact with the fountain to enter the battlefield.
[6] Defeat Fenrir Prime and you will receive the key item: [KI] Whisper of the moon.
[7] Return to Leepe-Hoppe to turn in the whisper for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Up to 6 party members can enter the arena.
[2] Each party member must have their own [KI] Moon bauble.
[3] Time limit is 30 minutes.
[4] You can only select one of the rewards Leepe-Hoppe offers at any time.
[5] You may repeat this quest once per Earth day.
[6] Once you have completed this quest you can return to Leepe-Hoppe for another [KI] Moon bauble. Reobtaining the whispers is not required.
[7] In order to start this quest, you do not need to have turned in and completed the other "Trial By" Quests, nor do you need to have unlocked the Avatars as a Summoner. Following the advice earlier in this page, you can turn in the whispers to Leepe-Hoppe first and refight the Avatars again for another set and then finish their respective quests, allowing you to complete all the avatar quests and Fenrir in one earth day.
