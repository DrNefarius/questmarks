## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Ayame (A) at (I-13).
[2] Speak to Raibaht (A) at (I-13) near the magical barrier to the north.
[3] Examine the Repair Trunks around (I-13) to get the following:
    - [KI] Rainbow pearl
    - [KI] Chipped wind cluster
    - [KI] Piece of dried ebony lumber
[4] Note that there are seven total crates around the area. Only three will give you an item. There is also one hiding behind the Maw exit.
[5] Speak to Raibaht (A).
[6] Return to Ayame (A) to complete the quest.
