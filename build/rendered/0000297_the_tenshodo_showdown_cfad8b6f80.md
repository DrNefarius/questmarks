## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Nanaa Mihgo as a Thief to begin this quest, and she will give you a [KI] Letter from the Tenshodo.
    - You must have the Tenshodo Membership to continue with this quest.
[2] Head to Lower Jeuno and talk to Harnek inside of the Tenshodo at (J-7) for a cutscene.
[3] You will be given the [KI] Tenshodo envelope.
[4] Travel to Selbina and talk to Elfriede (J-9) of the Shepherd's Muster Inn. You will receive a cutscene with Sneaking Tiger who will ask for a Quadav Stew.
[5] Quadav Stew can only be stolen from certain Quadavs in Beadeaux.
    - The stew can be stolen BEFORE you go to Selbina to save yourself some time.
[6] Head to Beadeaux and Steal from Garnet Quadav, Silver Quadav, Zircon Quadav, and/or Bronze Quadav until you obtain the Quadav Stew.
[7] Once you get the Quadav Stew, go back to Selbina and trade the stew to Elfriede (J-9).
[8] You will receive the [KI] Signed envelope.
[9] Head back to Lower Jeuno and talk to Harnek (J-7) for a final cutscene and to receive your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Having Windurst Mission 2-1 active will prevent you from starting this quest.
[2] Nanaa Mihgo is involved in several quests and missions, so you may have to talk to her multiple times to trigger the cutscene that begins this quest.
