## Walkthrough

[1] This mission is triggered by clicking any of the three Cavernous Maws outside Jeuno in the past (or any maws in the present that you have already activated by exiting through the past) after Back to the Beginning, and requires you to continue the national storylines with the following quests:
    - Bastok - Light in the Darkness, Burden of Suspicion
    - San d'Oria - Boy and the Beast, Wrath of the Griffon
    - Windurst - Knot Quite There, A Manifest Problem
[2] After finishing any pair of national storylines, enter Southern San d'Oria (S) from East Ronfaure (S). Entering via Home Point, Survival Guide or any other method won't work, you must walk in from East Ronfaure (S).
