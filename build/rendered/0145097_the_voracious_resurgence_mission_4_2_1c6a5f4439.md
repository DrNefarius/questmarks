[1] Optional: Talk to Naji, Metalworks (J-8) (HP #1) for a minor comment on addressing injuries after the prior mission.
[2] Speak with Iron Eater in the President's Office (J-8) in Metalworks (HP #1) for a cutscene.
[3] Zone into Palborough Mines from North Gustaberg for a cutscene.
    - Closest teleport is the Abyssea - Grauberg warp to the Cavernous Maw.
    - Escaping from Palborough Mines HP puts you right at the entrance.
[4] Touch the Perversion's Refuge (H-9) on Map 3 for a cutscene.
    - This is North of Palborough Mines Home Point #1.
    - If you don't have the HP you can also use Waughroon Shrine teleport from Domenic.
[5] Touch the Perversion's Refuge again to begin a confrontation with Mind-warped Scorpion.
    - Has approximately 50k HP.
    - Has an en-Drain effect on melee hits.
[6] Touch the Perversion's Refuge once more for a cutscene and a reward Iapetus .
[7] Return to Iron Eater for the final cutscene and a reward Seafood Gratin .
    - Optional: Speak with Iron Eater again for some additional dialogue.
