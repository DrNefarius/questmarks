# Provide: Marjami Ravine

The game knows this assignment as: Provide: Marjami Ravine   (quest/coalition #19)

## Giver
Task Delegator - Western Adoulin (G-7)
  (resolved from the Couriers' Coalition page; the assignment page names no NPC)

## Couriers Assignment Table
Coalition:   Couriers' Coalition
Rank:        Partner
Type:        Base Provisions
Zone:        Marjami Ravine
Objective:   Transport provisions to the site in Marjami Ravine where the frontier station is to be constructed.
IMP1:        2500
IMP2:        4500
IMP3:        6000

## Walkthrough
[1] Once you accept the assignment, the Task Delegator will provide you with a Mar. FS building mat. container to deliver to a Station Administrator in Marjami Ravine.
    - DO NOT use a Waypoint warp. Doing so will cause the Mar. FS building mat. container to break and make you unable to complete the assignment.
        - As of the [http://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/40059-Feb-18-2014-%28JST%29-Version-Update?p=493165&viewfull=1#post493165 Feb. 18 2014 Version Update], Waypoints can now be used to complete this task without risk of break. Simply warp to a Station Administrator.
[2] Zone to Ceizak Battlegrounds from Western Adoulin.
[3] Travel to Sih Gates in the southwest.
[4] Take the western exit from Sih Gates to Foret de Hennetiel using "Climbing" if you can, and head across the colonization reive towards Waypoint 4.
[5] Head southwest from Waypoint 4 to enter Doh Gates.
[6] Travel Northwest (counter-clockwise path) to exit into Marjami Ravine.
[7] From the entrance of Marjami Ravine, follow the path forwards a short distance and speak to the Station Administrator to complete the assignment.
[8] Return to the Task Delegator to receive your reward (you may use Waypoints again now).
