## Walkthrough

Note: This quest will not appear on your quest list until completion.

[1] Speak to Bki Tbujhja Lower Jeuno H-8, inside the Merry Minstrel, for a hint on where you need to go next.
[2] (Optional) Speak to Mataligeat for some extra dialogue about Lewenhart.
[3] Travel through the tunnel in Valkurm Dunes (C-6) to the "Secret Beach" and examine the Song Runes (B-7) for a last cutscene.
    - A quick way to reach the destination is to Survival Guide to Gustav Tunnel, then exit to Valkurm Dunes.
    - If you haven't been to this survival guide yet, Unity Warp (level 128) and then having a /BLM use Escape will put you right at this exit.
