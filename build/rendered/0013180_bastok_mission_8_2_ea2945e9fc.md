[2] Head for Kuftal Tunnel Map 1. Just inside from the Western Altepa Desert entrance at (H-4). At (H-8), there is a ??? you have to check.

[4] Sneak up and head down around to reach the second ??? down the cliff, directly under the first one.
[5] Checking the ??? will spawn 3 level 68 NM Ghosts: Dervo's Ghost, Gizerl's Ghost, and Gordov's Ghost.
