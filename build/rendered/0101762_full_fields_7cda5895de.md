## Walkthrough

[1] Visit either NPC outside of the Mog House entrance in Western Adoulin or Eastern Adoulin to access your Mog Garden.
[2] Upon zoning in, your Green Thumb Moogle will give you a [KI] GPS crystal, and instruct you to harvest from the nearby Garden Furrow.
[3] After harvesting from the Garden Furrow, report back to the Green Thumb Moogle for your reward.

You will automatically start the next quest after completing this one.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

Before starting this quest line, be sure to have ample inventory space (40+ spots recommended)
