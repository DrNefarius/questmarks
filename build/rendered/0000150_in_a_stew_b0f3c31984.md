## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kuoh Rhel.
    - If you just completed Chocobilious, you will need to zone out/in.
[2] Speak to Matata (K-12).
[3] Speak to Ranpi-Monpi in the northern part of Windurst Waters (D-9).
    - Trade him three Woozyshrooms to receive [KI] Ranpi-Monpi's special stew.
[4] Speak to Kuoh Rhel to complete the quest.
