## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Chapi Galepilai (A) (G-10), Abyssea - Attohwa.
[2] You will be asked to retrieve missing supply packs. There are three total.
[3] Examine at least one of the three Supply Points located around the Crevice Amoeban / Treacle Slugs:
    - [KI] Pulse martello repair pack (G-8)
    - [KI] Clone ward reinforcement pack (F-9)
    - [KI] Pack of outpost repair tools (F-9)
[4] Once you have found at least one of the packs, speak to Morlepiche (A) at Veridical Conflux #00 to complete the quest.
