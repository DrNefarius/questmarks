## Walkthrough

[1] Simply speak to your Green Thumb Moogle for a cutscene involving Chacharoon.
[2] After the cutscene speak to Chacharoon to begin raising your first monster, a baby sheep.
    - Speak with Chacharoon again.
[3] Interact with your baby sheep. Your baby sheep likes to be pet.
[4] Speak with Chacharoon again who tells you to go speak with the Green Thumb Moogle for a cutscene.
    - This ends the quest and begins the next quest, Feeding Frenzy
