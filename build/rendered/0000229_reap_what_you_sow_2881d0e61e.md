## Walkthrough

Note: if your Windurst fame is level 4 or higher, you will be assigned Let Sleeping Dogs Lie instead, and cannot start this quest until after you complete that one.

[1] Speak to Mashuu-Ajuu who will give you a bag of Herb Seeds and ask you to plant them.
    - (Optional) speak to the other students for vague tips about what can be grown from various seed types.
[2] You can plant the seeds and grow them into a Deathball or Sobbing Fungus, or you can just purchase them off the Auction House.
    - The Sobbing Fungas can also be bought at Soupox in Leafallia for 60 Gil.
[3] Trading Mashuu-Ajuu a Deathball will reward you with a Stationery Set and 700 gil.
[4] Trading Mashuu-Ajuu a Sobbing Fungus will reward you with a Stationery Set and 500 gil.

---

[5] Repeat attempts will not reward the Stationary Set.
[6] Repeat attempts require trading a Sobbing Fungus.
[7] You will not get any more Herb Seeds other than the first time.
[8] If you set your home point to Windurst and have the appropriate Rhapsodies Key Item, this will only cost 160 by using a BLM Warp. Profiting 340 Gil each turn-in by buying the Sobbing Fungus from Soupox.
