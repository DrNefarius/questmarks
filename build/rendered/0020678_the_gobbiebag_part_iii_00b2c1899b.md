## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Bluffnix in Muckvix's Junk Shop located in Lower Jeuno at (H-9).
[2] Trade Bluffnix a square of tiger leather, a gold ingot, a square of velvet cloth, and a painite; or a Goblin Stew 880.

    - Gold Ingot: AH ➞ Materials ➞ Goldsmithing
