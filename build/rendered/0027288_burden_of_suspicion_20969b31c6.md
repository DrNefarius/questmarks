## Walkthrough

Notice: Wait one game day and re-zone after completing Light in the Darkness before starting this quest.
[1] Speak to Gentle Tiger (S) in Bastok Markets (S) at (H-6) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
[2] Speak to Engelhart in Bastok Markets (S) at (K-10).
[3] From Batallia Downs (S), use the (J-10) entrance to The Eldieme Necropolis (S).
    - The quickest way is via Survival Guide to The Eldieme Necropolis (S).
    - Alternatively: via Voidwatch teleport (Jeuno I)
[4] Go to the southern room at (G-9) and fall down the center hole. Follow the path, then make a left turn at the fork and go up the stairs. At the top, enter the room to the north at (I-7). Check the Sarcophagus on the east side of the room for a cutscene.
    - There are four Orcs in this room, so either clear them out before you watch the cutscene or wait for them to turn to drop invisible.
[5] Speak to Gentle Tiger (S) for a cutscene.
[6] Speak to Blatherix at (F-8) for your reward.
    - You should keep this Elixir for use in a later mission, The Truth Lies Hid.

If you are working on missions then return to Cait Sith (Mission).
