## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kurou-Morou (A) at Conflux #2 (I-12) to being this quest.
[2] He asks for one purchasable "Goblin" item and tells you one of three locations.
    - Possible items:
        - Goblin Bread
        - Goblin Pie
        - Gob. Chocolate Goblin Chocolate
    - Possible locations:
        - "Near Abundance of Water": Northeast corner of (F-11) (Conflux #4)
        - "Dark and Cramped": (K-12)
        - "A Narrow Pathway": Southeast corner of (F-13)
[3] Trade the requested item to the Scuff Mark targetable location at the spot specified to make Ramblix (A) appear.
    - Movement speed helps with this quest, as Confluxes are not close to the areas needed.
[4] Speak to Cornelia (A) near Conflux #4 to complete the quest.
Zoning is required in order to repeat this quest.
