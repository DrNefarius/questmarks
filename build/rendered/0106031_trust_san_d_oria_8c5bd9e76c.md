## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Gondebaud in Southern San d'Oria at (L-6), he will give you the [KI]Red institute card.
[2] Head to Northern San d'Oria and speak to Excenmille at (D-9).
    - If this is not your first Trust quest that you are completing, at this point you will be finished with the quest and receive the [KI]San d'Oria Trust permit.
    - If this is your first Trust quest, you must use the Trust magic spell "Excenmille" in either West Ronfaure or East Ronfaure. After, return to Excenmille for the [KI]San d'Oria Trust permit.
