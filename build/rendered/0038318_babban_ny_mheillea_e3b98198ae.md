## Walkthrough

[1] The quest begins after speaking to Khoto Rokkorah and receiving a cutscene.
[2] Touch the Peculiar Rootprints in Rolanberry Fields (S) at H-14.
    - Using the Rolanberry Fields (S) Survival Guide is the fastest
    - Second best is to teleport to the stronghold in Pashhow Marshlands (S) and zone into Rolanberry Fields (S).
[3] Then touch another Peculiar Rootprints located in North Gustaberg (S) at E-11.
    - The Peculiar Rootprints is close to where you are teleported to by Campaign Arbiters.
[4] Afterwards, touch the third and final Peculiar Rootprints located in Meriphataud Mountains (S) at L-4, nearby to the zoneline to The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah.
    - Meriphataud Mountains (S) Survival Guide and Recall-Meriph are both good travel options.
[5] Return to Khoto Rokkorah to receive the final cutscene and reward.
