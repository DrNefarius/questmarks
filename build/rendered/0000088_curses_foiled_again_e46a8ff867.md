## Walkthrough

You must wait one game day and zone after completing the previous Quest before you can activate this Quest.
[1] Speak to Hiwon-Biwon (K-8).
[2] Speak to Shantotto (K-8).
[3] Zone out of the area and then back and speak to Shantotto (K-8).
[4] For her first question, choose either answer. For her second question, choose "No way! Not on your life!" (top option).
    - Your title will become "Tarutaru Murder Suspect."
[5] Shantotto asks you to bring her 2  Bomb Arms, 1 Revival Root Revival Tree Root, and 1  Hiwon's Hair.
    - To obtain Hiwon's Hair, speak to Hiwon-Biwon again.
[6] Trade the items to Shantotto to complete the Quest.
