## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Nbu Latteh at (J-9) to begin quest.
[2] (Optional) speak to Nbu Latteh again, as well as the guard Parnika to the south, for hints at Nbu's occupation.
[3] Travel to Konschtat Highlands and inspect the Signpost at the south edge of G-4 / north edge of G-5.
[4] You will receive a [KI] Painting of a windmill
[5] Return to Bastok Mines and speak to Roh Latteh at (H-7) to complete the quest.
