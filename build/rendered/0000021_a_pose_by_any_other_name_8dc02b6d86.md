## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Angelica (F-10)
[2] The items she asks you to bring (based upon your job) are:
    - Warrior / Paladin / Dark Knight / Dragoon / Corsair &mdash; Bronze Harness
    - Monk / Bard / Blue Mage &mdash; Robe
    - Thief / Beastmaster / Ranger / Dancer &mdash; Leather Vest
    - White Mage / Red Mage / Black Mage / Summoner / Puppetmaster &mdash;  Tunic
    - Ninja / Samurai &mdash; Kenpogi
[3] Talk to Angelica and stand still for 10 seconds.
[4] Talk to Angelica again. Now she tells you to wear your item.
[5] Talk to her the last time, and you've completed the quest.
    - You will receive Ancient Blood and [KI] Angelica's autograph.
[6] If you refuse to model and/or you take too long to get the right body armor, you get the title "Lower than the Lowest Tunnel Worm" instead of "Supermodel"
    - You can initially decline to model, get the "Lower than the Lowest Tunnel Worm" line and then model for "Super Model" without zoning.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] If you're not wearing a body piece you will be unable to start this quest.
[2] This quest is timed. If you take too long to come back dressed in the requested item, she will tell you that you are "so yesterday's style" and the quest will end without success.
    - You can re-attempt the quest by zoning out/in.
[3] +1 versions of items do not work.
