## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Enaremand (J-7) (Home Point #3) on the upper level in the Tavnazian Safehold.
[2] Head to Mhaura (Kolshushu) to talk with Fyi Chalmwoh at (G-8).
[3] Go to Carpenters' Landing where you will fight an Orc NM.
    - Zone into Carpenters' Landing from Jugner Forest at (J-8).
    - You can also take the Carpenter's Landing Survival Guide (via Norvallen) which will place you one square east of the spot.
[4] Go to (I-10), where you'll find a ??? on a red mushroom near the southwest corner.
    - When you touch it, Bullheaded Grosvez will spawn.
[5] After you've defeated Bullheaded Grosvez, touch the ??? again to receive [KI] Red oil.
[6] Go back to the Tavnazian Safehold and talk to Enaremand again for your reward.
