## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Chya Mindorah at Bivouac #1 to flag the quest. She asks you to kill a bothersome chapulli that's been eating crops in the area.
[2] Head north to the border of I-6/7. You should see a ??? between two small areas with Calfcleaving Chapuli.
[3] Kill Calfcleaving Chapuli until you get the message "You are suddenly overcome with a sense of foreboding..." (the number of chapuli killed appears to be random). You can also get the message "The smell of bloodlust hangs thick in the air!" which means you have killed enough chapulli to spawn the boss.
    - Prior to killing enough, it will read "You feel something approaching from the shadows." but nothing will happen.
[4] Click on the ??? to spawn the NM Bothersome Chapuli.
[5] After killing the NM you will receive a [KI]Chapuli horn.
[6] Return to Chya Mindorah to complete the quest.
