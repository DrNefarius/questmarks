## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to the Nomad Moogle in Ru'Lude Gardens at (H-5).
[2] Trade the Nomad Moogle 5 Kindred's Seals while having 3 Merit Points to complete the quest.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Keep in mind that besides obtaining seals from mobs, two easier ways exist to obtain various seals:
    - Spend Login Campaign points at a Greeter Moogle; each seal is only 5 points.
    - Shemo can exchange any other type of seal at a 3:1 ratio.
