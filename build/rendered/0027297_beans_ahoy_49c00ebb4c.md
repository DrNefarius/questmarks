## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Thierride in Southern San d'Oria (S) (F-8) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
[2] Trade him a Lufet Salt. After the cutscene, trade him another Lufet Salt for another cutscene.
[3] After one game day, zone and speak to him for the final cutscene and reward.
[4] Note: Once this quest has been completed you may reobtain an Angler's Cassoulet once per conquest tally by speaking to Thierride. There is no need to trade him further salts.
