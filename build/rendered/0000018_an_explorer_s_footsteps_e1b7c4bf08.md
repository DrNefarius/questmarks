# An Explorer's Footsteps

The game knows this quest as: An Explorer's Footsteps   (quest/other #19)

Start:      Abelard, Selbina (G-9)
Description: The mayor wants you to find stone monuments left by a certain adventurer and copy their writings to clay.

            - 800~10,000 Gil per Clay Tablet

                    - 2,000 Experience Points

Expansion:  (empty)

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Abelard (G-9) to receive Selbina Clay.
[2] Trade the Selbina Clay to a Stone Monument to receive a Clay Tablet.
    - If you interact with the monument, you will receive extensive and interesting Lore background of the area.
        - The inscriptions are available on the Stone Monument page.
[3] Return to Abelard and trade the Clay Tablet to him.
[4] Repeat for the remaining Stone Monuments. The quest will not "Complete" until you finish all tablets.
