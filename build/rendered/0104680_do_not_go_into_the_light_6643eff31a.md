## Walkthrough

[1] You must wait a game day after completing Flowers for Svenja before you may flag this quest.
[2] Check the door to Svenja's Manor at (I-8) in Western Adoulin (Closest to the Courier's Coalition Waypoint).
[3] Next, check the door ("to the left" as if you were exiting the manor) to the Hospital at (I-9) in Western Adoulin.
[4] Speak to Borghest at the Morimar Basalt Fields Frontier Station.
[5] Obtain a piece of Urunday Lumber, a Damascus Ingot, and a Fire Crystal.
[6] Trade the items to Chanteillie at the Inventors' Coalition to receive the Inventors' Coalition pickaxe key item.
    - Chanteillie will not take the items if she is waiting for the items for the quest Vegetable Vegetable Crisis.
[7] Head back to the Hospital to complete the quest.
