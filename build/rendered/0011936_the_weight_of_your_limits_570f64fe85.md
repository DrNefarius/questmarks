## Walkthrough

[1] You cannot start this quest if you have another Weapon Skill Quest active. You must return to the person that gave said quest and quit it to start another.
[2] Speaking with Iron Eater will grant you the key item Weapon training guide as well as the Axe of Trials. You will need to break the latent on the Axe of Trials before completing the rest of the quest.

    - Breaking the Latent

[3] Trade the Axe of Trials back to Iron Eater. This will give you the key item Map to the Annals of Truth. You will be instructed to travel to The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah.
    - To spawn the NM, use the Survival Guide to zone in The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah and head to the northwest corner of (F-6) to find the ???.
    - Alternatively, for the fastest route, use the Unity Warp to Ro'Maeve (level 125), then zone into The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah. The ??? is located at (F-5) in the southwest area.
[4] This fight is against Greenman, a treant. It is weak to Fire and Dark attacks. Once you kill it, re-examine the ??? to receive the key item Annals of Truth.
[5] Go back and speak with Iron Eater to receive your reward.
