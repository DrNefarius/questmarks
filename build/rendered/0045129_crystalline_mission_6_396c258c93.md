## Walkthrough

thumb|right|Seed Afterglow
You must find 9 Seed Afterglows in Fei'Yin: with red, orange, yellow, green, cerulean, blue, golden, silver, and white radiance.
[1] You have 30 minutes (Earth time) to gather the 9 Afterglows. If you do not collect them all in 30 minutes zone out and back in to begin the process over again.
[2] The Seed Afterglows move periodically, but stay in the same general areas, refer to the maps below.
    - NOTE: The yellow Seed Afterglow can spawn in 2 different rooms. If it's not in the big room, check the small circular room at (H/I-9).
[3] Recommended Route:
    - Starting from HP #1 (K-8) → yellow (big room) → → → → yellow (small room) → A → white → → → → B →

[4] You may only repeat this process of gathering Seed Afterglows once per Earth day.
[5] Rewards are listed on the Add-on Scenario Rewards page.
