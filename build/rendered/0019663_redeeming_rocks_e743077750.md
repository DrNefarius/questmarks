[1] Talk to Kocco Ehllek at (H-8) (lower map) to start the quest.
[2] Talk to her mother Rohn Ehlbalna at (G-9) (upper map).
[3] Return to Kocco Ehllek, who requests a Blue Gem, like the water from the land of the Mithra, which used to be found within Vunkerl Inlet (S).
[4] Retrieve a [KI] Piece of kionite from a ??? at (F-9) in Vunkerl Inlet (S) near the bridge.
[5] Return the blue stone to Kocco Ehllek.
[6] Wait until the next Vana'diel day. Speak to her again to complete the quest and receive your reward.
