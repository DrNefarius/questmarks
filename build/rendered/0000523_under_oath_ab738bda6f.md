## Walkthrough

[1] Examine the door to Prince Trion's room (Door: Prince Royal's Rm) at Chateau d'Oraguille (H-7) while you are a Paladin for a cutscene.
    - If you were a Paladin when you completed the previous quest, A Boy's Dream, you have already received this cutscene. Check your active Quests and you should see "Under Oath".

[2] The rest of this quest can be completed as any job from this point on.
[3] Speak to Vemalpeau, Southern San d'Oria (M-7), inside a house.
[4] Speak to Najjar, Southern San d'Oria (K-6) inside the Lion Springs Tavern, who will mention (Key Item) Mique's paintbrush.
[5] Speak to Ullasa, Southern San d'Oria (B-6) inside the Count's Manor, who will mention (Key Item) Mique's paintbrush.
[6] Obtain a Zvahl Coffer Key and trade it to a Treasure Coffer in Castle Zvahl Baileys to get (Key Item) Mique's paintbrush.
    - You can purchase a Zvahl Coffer Key from the Curio Vendor Moogle for 5,000 gil if you have the "Rhapsody in Umber" KI.
        - Alternatively, Zvahl Coffer Key drops off Ahriman, Abyssal Demon, Arch Demon, Blood Demon, and Doom Demon. You can also come as a Thief and pick the Coffer's lock with either Thief's Tools, Living Key, or Skeleton Key.
[7] Return to Vemalpeau.
[8] Head to the second story in Vemalpeau's house and examine the ??? on the painting. Look behind the painting and read the letter for (Key Item) Strange sheet of paper.
[9] Speak to Exoroche (K-7) in Helbort's Blades who will tell you how to read the letter.
[10] Head to Davoi and locate the Village Well at (G-9).
[11] Examine the Village Well will spawn 2 NMs:
    - Three-eyed Prozpuz (RNG)
    - One-eyed Gwajboj (PLD)
        - Drops the Well Weight you need to continue this quest.
        - Only one Well Weight drops at a time.  If you are doing this for several Paladins, you will have to wait 3 minutes after defeating them to do the fight for each of them.
[12] Trade the Well Weight to the Village Well for a cutscene and (Key Item) Knight's confession.
[13] Speak to Ailbeche, Northern San d'Oria (J-9).
[14] Travel to Jugner Forest and head for the Maiden's Spring at (E-6). When you get close to the spring, a cutscene will start.
[15] Head back to Chateau d'Oraguille and speak to Prince Trion for your reward.

== Optional component ==

[16] You may additionally talk to Ailbeche, Exoroche, and Vemalpeau for more quest-related dialogue.
    - Vemalpeau will take your Knight's confession key item.
