## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Koblakiq to begin this quest.
    - Make sure you have zoned after completing the previous quest.
[2] Head to Castle Zvahl Baileys and fight Blood Demons and Demon Generals until you obtain a Demon Pen.
[3] Trade the Demon Pen to Koblakiq.
[4] Wait 1 earth minute, then speak to Koblakiq again.
[5] Return to Castle Zvahl Baileys and check the ??? at (I-8 behind the gate where Dark Spark spawns) to spawn Marquis Andrealphus, Demon Banneret x2, Demon Secretary x2.
    - Any demons near by will link with the NM's.
    - Only Marquis Andrealphus needs to be killed to proceed. After defeating him, any additional spawned demons will immediately die, but not any linked demons.
    - Demon Bannerets and the Demon Secretarys can be slept.
    - Marquis Andrealphus has the ability to Escape whoever has hate out of the castle and back into Xarcabard.
        - He will not be able to cast Escape if there is only 1 party member left in the zone, or you are soloing him.
[6] Examine the ??? again after Marquis Andrealphus is defeated to obtain Zeelozok's earplug.
[7] Return to Koblakiq to complete this quest.
