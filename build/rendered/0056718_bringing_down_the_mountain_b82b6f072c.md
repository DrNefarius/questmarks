## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Diegai (A) at the border of (H-7)/(I-7) near Veridical Conflux #07.
[2] Travel to (J-8) and speak with Baladanzo (A). There is no quick way there, just walk from Conflux #07.
[3] Defeat Bog Body Qutrub mobs around (J-5) near Conflux #08 until they drop 10 Gunpwdr. Swathe Gunpowder Swathes

[4] Return to Baladanzo at (J-8) and trade him all 10 Gunpwdr. Swathe Gunpowder Swathe.

    - Alternatively, you may trade the items one at a time, though it is not required.
[5] You may return to Diegai (A) for an additional dialogue.
