## Walkthrough
[1] Fight Snapweed in Yorcia Weald until the Tickling Tendrils special attack is used.
[2] After being hit with Stun effect from Tickling Tendrils, the assignment will be complete.
    - A shield being equipped can block the move.
    - The move is single target, so if doing for multiple characters, each will need to be the target of the attack.
[3] There are Snapweeds located directly north of Bivouac #3.
