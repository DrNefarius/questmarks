## Walkthrough

[1] Zone in and out if you just completed Star Struck.
[2] Speak to Koru-Moru to begin the quest.
    - (Optional) Talk to the NPCs outside for some hints. Specifically: Luuh Koplehn, Bonchacha, Maan-Pokuun.
        - Maan-Pokuun suggests talking to Yoran-Oran, who recites some cryptic poetry about the past and fossils.
        - Luuh suggests asking the fisherman's guild. Mhe Quryobhi hasn't heard of the shell, and Tokaka says that Yoran-Oran used to have one.
        - After getting the hint from Tokaka, Yoran-Oran will now specifically mention the Maze of Shakhrami as a place to find marine fossils.
[3] Head to the Maze of Shakhrami.
[4] Examine the Fossil Rock at (F-8) on Map 2, to spawn the NM Ichorous Ire. It will drop a Burnite Shell ().
[5] Trade the Burnite Shell to Koru-Moru to complete the quest.
    - (Optional) Talk to Yoran-Oran again for some concluding dialogue.
