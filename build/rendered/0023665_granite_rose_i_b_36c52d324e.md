## Walkthrough

[1] Travel to the Fortilace in Rolanberry Fields (S) (D-9).
[2] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ruhotz Silvermines.
[3] A party of 3 Quadavs will be roaming the area. You must defeat them before they defeat the friendly NPC.
    - They are sleepable and have low HP.
[4] Once all three Quadavs are defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
[5] Exit via the Dawn Aureola.
    - Skipping this step will result in failure.
[6] Return and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.
