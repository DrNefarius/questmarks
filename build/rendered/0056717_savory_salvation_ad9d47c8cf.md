## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Piketo-Puketo (A) in the Western Encampment at (F-9). Veridical Conflux #03 is nearby.
    - He'll give you [KI] Vial of flower-wower fertilizer.
[2] Next travel to the large tree at (G-7). Veridical Conflux #04 is nearby.
[3] Click the Gnarled Root targetable location below in the pathway to inject the fertilizer.
[4] Lastly, click the Fragmented Nutshell up top, on the slope by the tree for [KI] Tahrongi tree nut.
[5] Return to Piketo-Puketo (A) to complete the quest.
Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
