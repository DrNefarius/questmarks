## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Ramua in Northern San d'Oria (E-3) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
    - You need to have already signed up with the Woodworking Guild for this quest to be activated. Speak to Guild Master: Cheupirudaux - Northern San d'Oria (F-3)
[2] Ramua will give you the [KI] Timber survey checklist and a Hatchet.
    - The one hatchet will probably break, so buy more hatchets before you go. Ostalie in Southern San d'Oria (E-9) is closest.
[3] Travel to Jugner Forest and log the following items:
    - Arrowwood Log
    - Ash Log
    - Yew Log
    - Willow Log
    - Walnut Log
[4] If you've never harvested before: go to the green points marked on the wiki's map, and look for yellow sparkles called "Logging Point"s. Trade your hatchet to them for a chance at an item.
[5] (Optional) turn on the Records of Eminence objective Harvesting -> Original Areas -> Jugner Forest for sparks and experience. For the first clear, it'll also give you 12 hatchets.
[6] Trade these logs to Ramua, for your reward.  She will also return the logs to you.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] For each item on the list that you log, you will get a message that data was recorded on your checklist.  It appears that you must acquire these items yourself, you cannot buy them from shops or the auction house.
