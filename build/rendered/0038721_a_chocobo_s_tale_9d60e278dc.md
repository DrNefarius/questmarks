## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Nevela to begin this quest.
[2] Go to Bastok Mines and talk to Wobke (I-9).
[3] Go to Pashhow Marshlands Outpost and examine the Outpost Gate for a cutscene.
    - The dialogue in this cutscene will imply you only have 2 days to turn in the Warding Oils, but you can take as long as you need.
[4] Obtain three Warding Oils from Emerald Quadav in Beadeaux.
[5] Trade the oils to the Outpost Gate for a cutscene.
[6] Go to Bastok Mines and speak to Wobke.
[7] Head to Batallia Downs and examine the ??? on a headstone located at (H-6) to spawn 5 NM tigers called Badshah.
[8] Kill Badshah and re-examine the ??? to obtain Silver Comet's collar.
[9] Speak to Nevela to complete the quest.
