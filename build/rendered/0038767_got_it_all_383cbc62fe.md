## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Tehf Kimasnahya in Aht Urhgan Whitegate at (F-8).
[2] Speak to Ekhu Pesshyadha in Aht Urhgan Whitegate at (H-6).
[3] Speak to Zabahf in Aht Urhgan Whitegate at (F-8).
[4] Speak to Ekhu Pesshyadha to receive the [KI] Vial of luminous water.
[5] Speak to Tehf Kimasnahya.
[6] Go to the lower level of Aht Urhgan Whitegate (J-10) to trigger a cutscene.  (Near the NPC Hajaom.)
[7] Speak to Tehf Kimasnahya.
[8] Zone and then speak to Tehf Kimasnahya again to receive your reward.
