## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus.
[2] Travel to the Beastman Ensign at (G-12) in Grauberg.
    - Once the party leader interacts with it and selects to enter, you will be transported into Ruhotz Silvermines.
[3] Two parties of Quadavs (2x Quadav Guard, 1x Quadav Carrier) will start moving slowly in different directions. The objective of this mission is to kill all 6 Quadavs before they reach the end of their route.
    - The Carriers run off shortly after engaging in combat / when reduced to low HP, so you'll need to kill them quickly.
    - Should a Carrier make it to the end of the area (which is quite large), the mission is failed.
[4] Once all six Quadavs are defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
    - You must exit area by using the Twilight Aureola for Hieronymus to acknowledge the mission as successful!
[5] Return to Hieronymus for your reward.
