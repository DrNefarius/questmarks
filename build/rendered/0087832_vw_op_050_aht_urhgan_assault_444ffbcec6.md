## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with the NPC Camille in Wajaom Woodlands at (M-7) for a cutscene.
    - Quickest way is to exit Aht Urhgan Whitegate through the Ironbound Gate at (H-10).
[2] Choose to participate in voidwatch operations to receive key item Amber stratum abyssite.
[3] Defeat the following Voidwatch notorious monsters in Aht Urhgan areas
    - Dimgruzub in Arrapago Reef (Map 1 G-8, G-10, I-10)
    - Brekekekex in Caedarva Mire (Map 1 F-11, I-9, I-11)
    - Yalungur in Mamook (Map 1 I-7, J-8, L-8)
    - Vanasarvik in Mount Zhayolm (M-6, J-8, L-8)
[4] Return to Camille to complete the quest and begin the following quest automatically.
