## Walkthrough

[1] Examine the ??? in Arrapago Reef at (H-10) Map 1 for a cutscene.
    - A Lamian Fang Key is required to reach this point.
    - Lamian Fang Keys drop from Lamiae and Draugers in the area.
    - You can also receive a Lamian Fang Key from a ??? spot in Caedarva Mire (I-7) Map 1 (southern part of the block).  You can obtain a new key from there once every game day.
[2] You no longer have to be on Corsair for the rest of this quest.
[3] You are asked to fish up a Hydrogauge.
    - The item you need must be fished up in Al Zahbi, Aht Urhgan Whitegate, Nashmau, or the Ferry - Nashmau/Al Zahbi.
    - You may use any rod and bait
    - The fishing message you will receive is the standard item message, "You feel something pulling at your line".
        - It is suggested to use any rod for large fish (ie: S.H. Fishing Rod)  and Insect Balls in Aht Urhgan Whitegate as this has a limited number of fish that may interfere with your fishing attempts.
[4] Once you've fished up a Hydrogauge, trade it to Leleroon, Nashmau (G-7).
[5] Head to (G-8) in Wajaom Woodlands and trade the Hydrogauge to the Leypoint.
    - This can be reached quickly using an Olduum Ring
    - The Survival Guide is also fairly close-by.
[6] Wait one minute, then examine the Leypoint again.
[7] Return to the ??? in Arrapago Reef  at (H-10) Map 1 for your reward.
