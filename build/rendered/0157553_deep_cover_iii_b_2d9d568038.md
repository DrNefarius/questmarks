## Walkthrough

[1] Solo mission.
[2] Speak with Mariette at (G-9) in Bastok Markets (S) to receive a Fiendtrapper and 5 Blank fiend plates.
[3] Use the Fiendtrapper to take pictures of different Quadav.
    - Campaign Battle mobs are valid.
    - Picture must be taken from the front of the mob, she will not accept pictures from the back or side of the mob.
    - The Fiendtrapper will not work if you are under the effect of Sneak or Invisible (Status) or too far away.
[4] Each picture you take will create a Fiend Plate with the mob's name on it and take up an inventory slot. The exposed Fiend Plates do not stack.
[5] Return to Mariette and trade one Fiend Plate at a time.
    - After 3 Fiend Plates are accepted, the mission is complete.
    - If you have Fiend Plates which were rejected, they might be good enough for Deep Cover I (B) or Deep Cover II (B).
