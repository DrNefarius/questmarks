## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Halver in Chateau d'Oraguille (Northern San d'Oria, HP #2) for a cutscene.
[2] Speak to Rahal in the Royal Knight's quarters to the west for a cutscene.
[3] Head to Davoi and speak with Quemaricond at either (G-7) or (H-7) for a cutscene. He walks back and forth on the path.
    - Proto-Waypoint would be the fastest way to get there.
    - Survival Guide (Norvallen) is also a fast option if you don't have the Proto-Waypoint
[4] Head to Monastic Cavern from the (H-11) entrance in Davoi.
[5] Touch the Ritual Site at the border of (H-10)/(H-9) inside the Monastic Cavern to begin a confrontation with an Orcish Bewitcher (BLM/DRK).
    - Has about 30k HP.
    - Uses Manafont as early as 90% HP.
    - You will automatically receive the [KI] Orcish battle plan upon defeating the NM.
[6] Return to Quemaricond for a cutscene to receive [KI] Quemaricond's report.
    - Escape then re-enter Davoi is the fastest path.
[7] Speak with Rahal for the final cutscene and your reward.
