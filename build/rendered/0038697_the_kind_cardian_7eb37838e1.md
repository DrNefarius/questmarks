## Walkthrough

[1] (Optional) Speak to Panta's family and Bozz again for some extra dialogue.
[2] Speak to Apururu, Windurst Woods (H-9) for a cutscene.
[3] Head to West Sarutabaruta (F-11) and enter the Outer Horutoto Ruins (Dahlia Tower).
[4] Ten of Cups spawns at (J-8), in the small room with the large locked door. Kill it until you receive a Ten of Cups item.
    - Bring someone with Treasure Hunter to improve the drop rate.
    - "Ten of X" enemies spawn in groups of 3 here, with a chance for 0, 1, or 2 enemies of each suit. Once killed, they respawn after 15 minutes. You might be here a while.
    - Alternatively, buy the card at the Auction House.
    - If you happen to get a Ten of Coins item, consider saving it for the quest The Opo-opo and I.
[5] Give the Ten of Cups item to Apururu.
[6] (Optional) return to Monberaux for some extra dialogue.
[7] Return to Panta-Putta, Lower Jeuno (G-10) to complete this quest.
