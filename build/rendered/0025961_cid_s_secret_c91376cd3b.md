## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Cid to begin the Quest.
[2] Travel to Port Bastok (Home Point #3) and visit the Steaming Sheep Restaurant.
[3] Locate Hilda at (E-6) and speak with her. You can interact with her through the floor if you stand under her (near the bar) while she's upstairs.
[4] Obtain a  Rolanberry 874 in one of two ways:
    - Via the Auction House. (Food ➞ Ingredients)
    - Defeating the Drone Crawler () in Crawler's Nest. (Survival Guide ➞ Derfland Region). This is a multi-step spawn process, see the quest Cargo for details.
[5] Trade the Rolanberry 874 to Hilda and to receive [KI] Unfinished letter.
[6] Return to Cid and speak with him to complete the Quest.
