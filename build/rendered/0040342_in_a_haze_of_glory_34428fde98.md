## Walkthrough

[1] Diordinne at (I-6) of the first map of Garlaige Citadel (S) will begin this quest.
[2] After the long cut-scene, he will give you the [KI] Number eight shelter key.
[3] Head east into the second map and go to (H-9) on the map and examine the Wooden Crates.
[4] Have the party leader examine the crates. Buffs wear upon entry.
[5] You will be transported to Ghoyu's Reverie and have 30 minutes to locate and kill the Orcish Turret.
    - On Map 15 (where you spawn) in Ghoyu's Reverie, you can find the Orcish Turret by traveling all the way to the south around H-10 walking along the western walls.
    - Be careful of the Goblin's Effluvial Grenade as the Noxious Spray will give nearby players a heavy poison effect.
    - Effluvial Flasks have approximately 150HP and do not have to be destroyed.
    - Other Orcs and Goblins do not have to be killed to win the battle though they have ~1500 HP and may sometimes get in the way.
[6] Upon defeating the Orcish Turret you will be transported out immediately and into another cutscene.
    - Note: If you fail the BC, you will need to wait a game day to re-acquire the [KI] Number eight shelter key. You must return and speak with Diordinne to begin the game day wait for the KI.
[7] Head back to Southern San d'Oria (S) and speak to Rholont (E-7) during nighttime hours (18:00 to 0:00 game time) to complete the quest and receive your reward.
