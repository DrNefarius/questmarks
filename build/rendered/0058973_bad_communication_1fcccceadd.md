## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Ferdechiond (A) (I-9) near Conflux #00 to begin the quest
[2] You will receive [KI] Espionage pearlsack.
[3] Travel to and examine the 3 ??? that Ferdechiond marks on the map.
    - They are located at (H-10), (G-8), and (E-11).
[4] Travel back to Ferdechiond to complete the quest and receive your reward.
    - The quality of the reward is based on how quickly the quest is completed.
        - 10+ minutes is the slowest reward: 700 Cruor first time completion, 300 thereafter.
        - 8~9 minutes: 1,000 Cruor first time completion, 600 thereafter.
        - 7 minutes or less: 1,500 Cruor first time completion, 800 thereafter.
        - Your time begins when the quest is accepted, and ends when the final ??? is selected rather than when you return to the NPC.
Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
