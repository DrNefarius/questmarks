## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo.
[2] Travel to the Allied Ensign (I-9) in West Sarutabaruta (S) (hug the left wall as soon as you come out).
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ghoyu's Reverie.
[4] You will come across a Soldier, who immediately runs towards a Trained Bat and begins attacking.
    - During this time, you will see messages that display how much attention he is giving to the fight:
        - The soldier seems unfocused...
        - The soldier has begun training harder!
        - The soldier is fighting with vigor!
[5] You may defeat the Trained Bat yourself once the soldier has begun fighting with vigor.
[6] Once the Trained Bat is defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
    - If the soldier never gains focus, you lose the mission.
[7] After a short period, a Dawn Aureola will appear, allowing you to exit the area.
[8] Return and talk to Emhi Tchaoryo for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] There seems to be no time limit to how long you can take to get to Ghoyu's Reverie.
[2] The soldier will die if left alone, however he can cure himself.
[3] The Trained Bat lasts a long while, and seems to shift hate very often.
