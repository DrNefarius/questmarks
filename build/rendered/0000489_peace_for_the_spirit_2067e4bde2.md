# Peace for the Spirit

The game knows this quest as: Peace for the Spirit   (quest/sandoria #86)

## Quest Header
Start:      Curilla, Chateau d'Oraguille (I-9)
Description: The spirit of Curilla's father Rainemard seems to be trying to say something.
Fame:       San d'Oria
FLevel:     1
Quest Reqs: Ability to access Chateau d'Oraguille
Item Reqs:  
            - Antique Coin
            - Nail Puller
Level:      50+ Red Mage
Repeatable: af
Previous:   Enveloped in Darkness
Next:       (empty)
Title:      Paragon of Red Mage Excellence
Reward:     Warlock's Chapeau
Expansion:  (empty)

## Walkthrough

[1] As a Red Mage, Talk to Curilla in Chateau d'Oraguille at (I-9) to start this quest.
    - Note: You don't have to be RDM for the remainder of this quest.
[2] Head to Southern San d'Oria, find Sharzalion at (K-6) in the Lion Springs Tavern and speak to him.
[3] Kill Miser Murphy in Fei'Yin for an Antique Coin.
    - He will pop at (H-8) on map1 if the quest is active.
    - 10 minute respawn time

[4] Trade the coin to the Dry Fountain at (H-8) for a cutscene.
[5] Head to Southern San d'Oria and speak to Sharzalion and then speak with Daggao who is in the bar as well.
[6] Go to Garlaige Citadel (G-8) behind the first banishing gate (map 2) click the Oaken Box in the first room to the left. This will spawn the Guardian Statue.
    - If you get the message "Something feels wrong, but nothing happens.", click off Sneak.

[7] Kill him for a Nail Puller.
[8] Trade the Nail Puller to the same Oaken box for a Cutscene.
[9] Zone into Northern San d'Oria for a Cutscene and Warlock's Chapeau.
