## Walkthrough

[1] Must zone after previous quest in order to start this quest.
[2] Speak with Iruki-Waraki in Aht Urhgan Whitegate for a cut-scene. You'll discover that he is trying to get his Automaton back, and has formed a plan.
[3] He gives you a shopping list of items needed to help his plan come to fruition.
[4] You can be on any job from here on.
    - Obtain the required items:  Sleeping Potion x1,  Chai x1
[5] Return to Iruki-Waraki and trade him the items for a cut-scene. He then asks you to arrange a meeting with the thief in Nashmau.
[6] Head to Nashmau, and seek out Dnegan for a cut-scene(@H-6 on the second floor).
[7] Warning: Do NOT select the option "Ask about the Automaton" in this cut-scene. Speaking with him will start a cutscene, during which you will be approached by Yafahb.
    - You will be given 3 chat options: Ask about the Automaton, Ask about getting around Nashmau, or invite the thief to have tea with you. Do not ask about the Automaton!
    - If you do, you will end the cutscene and be forced to wait a game day before resuming. Choose either of the other options.
[8] The next part of the cutscene takes you to Nashmau Port, and you'll see Iruki's plan in action. He has set-up a tea shop, and as you approach he asks what you would like to drink.
[9] You are then presented with 3 options. Chai, Coffee or "I'll pass, thanks".
    - Choose either drink. Regardless of your drink choice, Iruki will convince the thief to have  Chai.
        - He will, however, mix up the drinks, leaving you to guess which has the  Sleeping Potion inside, and which doesn't.
            - The cup with the "Suspicious Vapour" is the correct choice.
                - If you make the wrong choice you must wait until the next game day to try again.
                    - You do not need to trade the  Chai and  Sleeping Potion again.
                - The thief then falls asleep. Iruki then confronts the Automaton, which runs off into the depths of Caedarva Mire.
[10] The next part of this quest is long winded and leaves you stuck in a remote area, so bring either a  Warp Ring or an  Scroll of Instant Warp.
[11] The final ??? for this quest is located in Caedarva Mire, at (K-5), which is actually part of the Azouph Isle map. However, you will need to travel through Arrapago Reef to reach it.
[12] The quickest way to reef is the Survival Guide to reef.
[13] If you used the survival guide to reef, zone out then grab yourself a  Lamian Fang Key and zone into the reef from (I-6). The key can be acquired from a ??? near a pond a short way from the zone.
    - If you did not use the Survival guide, take the Azouph Isle staging point then head to I-6 for the  Lamian Fang Key and then enter reef entrance 1.
        - Beware of the green "Swamp" water near the  Lamian Fang Key as it can spawn a NM or debuff you with weight.
        - The Jnun near the  Lamian Fang Key sleeps during the day and will not aggro.
        - You can also lockpick the door as a thief with thief tools or farm the key from lamia and draugar's inside reef.
[14] For the reef you are going to need Silent Oils and Prism Powders, sub Dancer, or etc.
[15] You'll find yourself at (I-11) on the map when you first zone into reef.
[16] Apply Sneak (Status) and Invisible (Status), and run around to the Iron Gate at (J-10). Trade the  Lamian Fang Key to open it, and head inside.
    - From inside, take the north path on this map, and you'll reach (J-8) back on the first map. Open the door located there and go through.
    - Head to J-6 (Caedarva Mire 6)
        - There is a tunnel entrance at (I-6), not far north from where you are. Head there.
        - Travel East through that tunnel, until presented with a fork in the road. Take the southern route. And run that way until you zone out into Caedarva Mire.
[17] Once in Caedarva Mire Apply Invisible (Status) then head to K-6 and click the ??? behind the tree.
    - This area has Mosshorn rams and Elder Treants, so take care not to gain unwanted attention. The ??? you seek is located next to a tree a short distance from the zone.
[18] Enjoy the final cut-scene. Once it is complete you will receive your Pup. Churidars Puppetry Churidars.
