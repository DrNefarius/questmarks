## Walkthrough

[1] Care for monsters an additional 5 times in order to purchase "Sakura and the Fountain" from Zenicca or a Skipper Moogle.
[2] After purchasing the key item, enter your newly expanded Mog Garden area which features the Rearing Grounds.
    - While these are not directly rewarded by this quest and can be obtained while earlier quests in this line are still active, this key item allows for the following:
        - Access to the Rearing Grounds
        - Ability to raise two monsters at once
[3] Speak to Chacharoon in the newly open area to complete the quest and gain access to use the Trust Magic: Chacharoon.
