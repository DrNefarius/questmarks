## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Silver Owl (A) at (D-7), Veridical Conflux #03, to begin this quest.
[2] He will request that you bring him [KI] Pinch of moist Dangruf sulfur.
[3] This item is obtained from any color Pyxis chest, in any Abyssean zone.
    - Only players who have this quest flagged will obtain the Key Item.
    - Only players who actually open a Pyxis will obtain this Key Item, and it is not obtained by everybody in the party.
    - Forbidden Keys cannot be used to open the Pyxis. They must be opened by traditional means.
    - The key item has been observed to be obtained upon opening the first Pyxis. It does not appear to be very rare.
[4] Once you have the [KI] Pinch of moist Dangruf sulfur, return to Silver Owl (A) to complete the quest.
