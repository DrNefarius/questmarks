## After the walkthrough: If you have completed the [[:Category:Aht_Urhgan_Missions|Treasures of Aht Urhgan Missions]]

[1] OPTIONAL: Return to your home nation for a cutscene. You will be told to go to the Imperial Whitegate and skip to the mission Aphmau's Light.
[2] Examine the Imperial Whitegate for a cutscene.
[3] You will skip to the mission Reunited, receiving Cipher Of Nashmeira's Alter Ego II and the ability to view all Treasures of Aht Urhgan mission cutscenes.

## After the walkthrough: If you have not yet completed all of the [[:Category:Aht_Urhgan_Missions|Treasures of Aht Urhgan Missions]], but you have started Mission 12 - [[Royal Puppeteer]]

[1] Return to your home nation for a cutscene. You will be told to go to the Imperial Whitegate and also be informed where Aphmau currently is in your mission progress.
[2] After viewing the cutscene in your home nation you will skip to the mission Aphmau's Light.

## After the walkthrough: If you have not started the [[:Category:Aht_Urhgan_Missions|Treasures of Aht Urhgan Missions]]

[1] You will progress to the mission The Endless Sky, the placeholder mission to continue.
    - In order to receive the Trust Cipher, you must be past the third Treasures of Aht Urhgan Mission: President Salaheem

## After the walkthrough: Regarding the Cipher Reward

You only need to speak to Abquhbah after receiving the cutscene in your home nation to obtain it.
