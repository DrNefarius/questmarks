## Walkthrough

[1] Examine the Door:Merchant's House at (G-11) on the upper level behind Greyson in Lower Jeuno for a cutscene (HP #1).
From this point on, you can complete the quest on any job
[2] Trade a piece of Mahogany Lumber to Osker near the chocobos and Brutus at (G-7) in Upper Jeuno.
    - You will receive a Tamer's whistle, and be instructed to head to The Eldieme Necropolis.
[3] Zone into Batallia Downs from Beaucedine Glacier and enter The Eldieme Necropolis from the (F-5) entrance.
    - This entrance can be reached quickly from Batallia Downs if you have the Lycopodium (NPC) warp unlocked.
    - Voidwatch and/or Unity warps to Beaucedine Glacier are alternative methods to reach the (F-5) entrance quickly.
[4] Once inside, head to (D-4) and, if you are low level, clear the room before checking the north east sarcophagus.
[5] Examining the sarcophagus will spawn three NM's:
    - 2 Tigers named Taifun and Trombe, and a hound named Sturm.
    - Only Sturm needs to be defeated to proceed.
    - The tigers can be charmed to help you in this fight.
[6] Once defeated examine the sarcophagus for a cutscene and your reward.
