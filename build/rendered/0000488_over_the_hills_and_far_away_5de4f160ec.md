## Walkthrough

[1] First you need to to go to the second floor of the house at (G-7) in Southern San d'Oria and read the diary there until you have read up to Page 4.

[2] Speak to Antreneau, Port San d'Oria (G-7) to flag the quest.
[3] Head to Oldton Movalpolos and kill Moblin Ashman and Moblin Gurneyman until they drop a Moblin Hotrok. Alternately, you can go to Newton Movalpolos and kill Moblin Groundman.
[4] Head to Uleguerand Range and climb to the top where Jormungand spawns.
[5] At (F-9) you will find a slope leading all the way down to the entrance of the zone.
[6] Fall down the Southern Slope (the one you see as soon as you zone) and aim for the ledge on your left.
    - If you miss the ledge you will have to climb up the mountain and try again.

[8] Trade the Moblin Hotrok to the ??? to complete this quest.
