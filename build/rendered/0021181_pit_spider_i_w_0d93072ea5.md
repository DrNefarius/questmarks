## Walkthrough

[1] After speaking with Emhi Tchaoryo, proceed to the Beastman Ensign at the border line of (H-8)/(I-8) in Fort Karugo-Narugo (S) to enter Ghoyu's Reverie.
[2] Enter the BCNM and defeat 2 Yagudo Guards and a Yagudo Carrier. They are low level and easily defeated by even level 75 players.
    - The Yagudo Carrier will try to run away, so kill it first.
    - The battle is capped at 30 minutes, although you will hardly need it.
[3] After defeating the 3 Yagudo, exit the BCNM via the Twilight Aureola, then return to Emhi Tchaoryo for your reward.
[4] Only the party leader need have the Ops orders to enter the BCNM. Anyone from any nation can assist. Everyone will receive 170 Experience Points upon completion of the BCNM. Those signed up for this Campaign Ops will receive 640 Allied Notes upon speaking to Emhi Tchaoryo.
