## Walkthrough

Only one Refuel and Replenish or A Mightier Martello quest can be completed per Vana'dielian day, regardless of Abyssea area.
[1] Speak to the Machine Outfitter (Grauberg) near the entrance of zone, and choose "Assist with replenishment" to accept the quest.
[2] You will receive [KI] Vat of martello fuel.
    - You cannot zone at any point during the quest, or you lose the key item and you must restart.
[3] Find a Martello in the zone. They are usually around the various Confluxes, or you can see a list on the Martello page.
    - It is possible to replenish it even if it is already at 100% health.
[4] Click on "Prepare to replenish martello" in the menu once you interact with one, then confirm.
[5] The log will tell you a direction to stand in order to replenish the device, but the menu will tell you a different direction. Best results are achieve by following the direction in the menu.
    - Although not told to you, the [KI] Vat of martello fuel is replaced with an [KI] Empty fuel vat.
[6] Report to the Machine Outfitter (Grauberg) to complete the quest.
