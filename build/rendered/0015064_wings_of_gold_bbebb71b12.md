## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Brutus as a level 40 or higher Beastmaster.
From this point on, you can complete the quest on any job.
[2] Kill monsters on the 7th to 10th floor of Upper Delkfutt's Tower until you get a Delkfutt Chest Key
    - Alternatively, you can purchase one from the Curio Vendor Moogle.
[3] Use the key to open a chest in the zone to receive a Guiding bell.
[4] Return to Brutus for your reward.

== Note: ==
