## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Goraow (I-6) in the upper stairway of Ore Street to begin quest.
    - Close to Bastok Mines Home Point #3.
[2] Obtain a  Quadav Helm.
    - Only drops from Old Quadav in Beadeaux.
        - There are Old Quadav in other areas, but they do not drop the same items.
    - Can be bought on the  Auction House ➞ Others ➞ Beast-made.
[3] Trade a Quadav Helm to Goraow to complete the quest.
[4] Can be repeated without zoning.
