## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Black Mud to begin quest. You will obtain a Brass Canteen.
    - If you have the Proto-Waypoint warp to North Gustaberg, it will take you almost directly to the Waterfall Base. (From there, walk east and across the bridge to the north.)
[2] Otherwise, travel to Dangruf Wadi and head north from the entrance.
    - The quickest method is via Survival Guide, otherwise zone in from South Gustaberg (D-9).
    - Invisible or Prism Powders are recommended, as there are aggressive level 90 Goblins along the way.
[3] Once inside Dangruf Wadi, travel to (I-8) and go up the geyser by standing on top of it until being shot up the ledge.
[4] Head northeast to (J-3) and go up a second geyser.
[5] Continue following this path to zone out into North Gustaberg
[6] Travel east and cross the bridge.
    - Pick up the Geomagnetic Fount here if you do not have the Proto-Waypoint warp yet.
[7] Continue following the river towards the base of the waterfall.
[8] Locate the Waterfall Base and trade the Brass Canteen to it to receive Drachenfall Water.
    - There is a ??? nearby. It is for unlocking the relic weapon Apocalypse, not for this quest.
[9] Return to Bastok and trade the Drachenfall Water to Black Mud to complete the quest.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] There is a headstone for the quest An Explorer's Footsteps along the way. You may wish to complete that at the same time.
