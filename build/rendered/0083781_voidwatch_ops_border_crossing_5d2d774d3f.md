## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kieran in Norg (I-8) to receive a cutscene and the Key Item [KI] Ashen stratum abyssite.
[2] Speak with the following NPCs to begin their respective subquests:
    - Hildegard in Kazham (F-9) to begin VW Op. 054: Elshimo List.
    - Gushing Spring in Rabao (G-8) to begin VW Op. 101: Detour to Zepwell.
        - Make sure to speak to these NPCs and start both their respective subquests prior to killing the Voidwatch NMs, otherwise the kills will not count towards completing the subquests.
[3] Complete both subquests and return to Kieran to complete the quest.
