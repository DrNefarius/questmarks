## Walkthrough

[1] After talking to the Campaign Ops NPC, you are to scour South San d'Oria (S) for a clickable point called "Suspicious Object"
[2] The Suspicious Object seems to spawn anywhere in South San d'Oria (S) and once someone touches it, it disappears immediately.
[3] The Suspicious Object respawns immediately in a different location. So far the point can appear at:
    :*(F-6)
    :*(J-9) - near Voucheramme
    :*(K-8)
    :*(L-7)
    :*(M-5)
    :*(M-7)
    :*(L-9)
    :*(K-10) - near Chauxnatte and Alfred
    :*(I-11) - by a Chocobo
[4] After obtaining the object, you will obtain experience points and get a message that you quota has been met. After this occurs, go back to the Campaign Ops NPC and collect your Allied Notes.
[5] "Suspicious Object" does not show up on Wide Scan.
