## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Sobane to begin this quest.
    - You will need to zone after finishing Signed in Blood.
[2] You will need a  Attohwa Ginseng for the next part of the quest.
    - Attohwa Ginseng drops off Gallinippers, Ogreflys, and Monarch Ogreflys in Attohwa Chasm.
[3] Enter Carpenters' Landing by taking the entrance from Jugner Forest (J-8).
    - The Carpenters' Landing Survival Guide will put you right next to Anguenet.
[4] Speak to Anguenet (J-10) and trade him the  Attohwa Ginseng.  In return, he will give you a [KI] Tonberry blackboard.
[5] Purchase a ticket and board a barge headed for Central Landing via Emfea Way.
    - This barge leaves at 00:50 every game day, from the docks next to Anguenet.
[6] On the ship, you will find Riche.  Speak to him and ask him all five questions.  After you ask him all the questions, he will take the [KI] Tonberry blackboard from you.
[7] Head to Fei'Yin and kill the Shadows there until they drop Treasury Gold.
    - You only need one per party.
[8] Go to Davoi and trade the  Treasury Gold to the ??? (J-11) to spawn the NM Hematic Cyst.
[9] After the Hematic Cyst is defeated, check the ??? again for a cutscene.
[10] Return to Sobane for your reward.
