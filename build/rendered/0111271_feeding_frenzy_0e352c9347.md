## Walkthrough

[1] Chacharoon asks you to to feed your baby sheep some La Theine Cabbage.
    - You cannot continue raising your sheep until this is done.
[2] Feed (trade) the sheep the La Theine Cabbage.
[3] Leave your Mog Garden and then return to it, for a cutscene.
    - This ends the quest and begins the next quest, Cry Not, Caretaker.
