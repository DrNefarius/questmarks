## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Brutus at (G-7) in Upper Jeuno who will give you three Aquaflora Key Items.
You can be on any job from here on.
[2] Examine the pools on map 2 of Fei'Yin located at (H-8), (H-5) and lastly (F-5). They are the little green marks on the map.
    - Home Point #2 is the closest warp just outside of the Cloister of Frost
[3] When the pool located at (F-5) is inspected an NM named Dabotz's Ghost will spawn. The ghost can actually spawn at any pool but it seems (F-5) was only available after the other 2 were used.
    - Dabotz's Ghost is a Black Mage and can cast various Black Magic spells including Tornado.
    - Dabotz's Ghost uses curse often.
    - Take off Sneak if you have it on, he will spawn in the water of the pool and you can't get to him unless he aggros you.
[4] After defeating Dabotz's Ghost, check the pool at (F-5) again.
[5] Return to Brutus for a cutscene.
[6] Next head to Castle Oztroja and kill the monsters to find an Oztroja Chest Key.
    - Alternatively, you can purchase one from the Curio Vendor Moogle.
[7] Use the key to open a chest inside the castle to receive a Beast Collar.
[8] Trade the Beast Collar to the Opo-opo NPC Tebhi.
    - Tebhi likes to roam the indoor pond that is near the brass door to the magicite for city missions.
    - Tebhi will dissapear for 3 minutes after accepting the Beast Collar.
[9] Return to Brutus for your reward.
