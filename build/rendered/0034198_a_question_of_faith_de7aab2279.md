## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Ayame for a cut-scene that starts the quest.
[2] Go to Bastok Mines and talk with Virnage at (I-5) for a cut-scene. Agree to his proposal, and you will receive the [KI] Dawn talisman.
[3] Go to Oldton Movalpolos and talk with Rakorok at (K-10). This will spawn the NM Bugallug, which must be defeated. It has around 5000HP and does the standard Bugbear attacks (Earthshock, Heavy Whisk, Flying Hip Press, etc.)
[4] After defeating Bugallug, talk with Rakorok for a cut-scene where you will lose the [KI] Dawn talisman.
[5] Return to Bastok Mines and talk with Virnage to complete the quest and receive your reward.
