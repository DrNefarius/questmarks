## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Fontoumant (A) at (H-7), Veridical Conflux #04, to begin the quest.
[2] You will be given [KI] Anti-Abyssean grenade #01, [KI] Anti-Abyssean grenade #02, [KI] Anti-Abyssean grenade #03.
[3] Examine the Dark Fissure targetable locations in the canyons at (F-6), (F-7), and (H-6).  Select the option Attack the darkness!.
    - The first fissure at (H-6) is just past Fontoumant (A) amongst some Pasture Funguars.
    - The second two are in the same chasm where Ordelle's Caves would be. It is quickest just to run to it from Veridical Conflux #04.
[4] You may receive Experience Points randomly by "defeating a monster" when a bomb is tossed. This event has no bearing on completing the quest.
[5] Return to Fontoumant (A) at (H-7) to complete the quest.
Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
