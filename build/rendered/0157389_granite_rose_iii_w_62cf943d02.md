## Walkthrough

[1] After speaking with Emhi Tchaoryo, proceed to the Fortilace at L-9 in Sauromugue Champaign (S).
[2] Enter the BCNM and defeat 9 Yagudo Stormers.
    - The battle is capped at 30 minutes.
    - Buffs will remain if cast prior to entering the battlefield.
[3] After defeating the 3 Yagudo, exit the BCNM via the Dawn Aureole.
[4] Only the party leader needs the Op active for the whole party to enter the BCNM. All party members will receive Experience Points upon completion of the battle.
[5] Those signed up for this Campaign Ops will receive Allied Notes upon speaking to Emhi Tchaoryo to complete the Op.
