# Deliver: Morimar Basalt Fields

The game knows this assignment as: Deliver: Morimar Basalt Fields   (quest/coalition #22)

## Giver
Task Delegator - Western Adoulin (G-7)
  (resolved from the Couriers' Coalition page; the assignment page names no NPC)

## Walkthrough
[1] Once you accept the assignment, you will -not- be provided with any supplies. You will receive Mor. FS resource container at the Frontier Station.
    - As of Feb 18, 2014 update, Waypoints can be used to complete this task. Mor. FS resource container will not break.
[2] Zone out to Yahse Hunting Grounds from Eastern Adoulin at (G-5).
[3] Run northwest and zone to Moh Gates at (H-5) in Yahse Hunting Grounds.
[4] Run north and zone to Morimar Basalt Fields at (G-5).
[5] From the entrance of Morimar Basalt Fields, run a little ways into the first open area and the Frontier Station will be at the northern end.
[6] Speak to the Station Administrator at (K-10) to receive Mor. FS resource container.
[7] Return to the Task Delegator to deliver the supplies and receive your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes
[1] Movement speed increasing effects recommended.
