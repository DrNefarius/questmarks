## Walkthrough

{| style="max-width: 100%; height: 100%;"
|
[1] Before heading out, farm Boulder Eaters in Gustav Tunnel at (J-6) Map 2 to get 6 Fat Worm Meat Fat Worm Meat. You will need these for Dazbog.
    - The Unity Warp 128 will put you on the correct map.
        - The drop rate is around with Treasure Hunter.
        - They are Exclusive, so they cannot be purchased.
[2] Head to Map 4 of Gusgen Mines (Same Map that Juggler Hecatomb spawns on) and speak with Hedrov (H-7) for a cutscene.
    - Fastest way is to use the Survival Guide and walk towards exit A.
        - Once on the next map keep on the left and go down the tunnel at G to reach map #4.
    - Alternatively you can run through Tunnel B, then through Tunnel E and go west to Drop H when you enter Map 3. Drop down this hole and you'll be next to Hedrov.
        - Hedrov is in the middle of the room behind a shed.
[3] Trade the 6 Fat Worm Meat Fat Worm Meats to Hedrov for a cutscene and reward.
