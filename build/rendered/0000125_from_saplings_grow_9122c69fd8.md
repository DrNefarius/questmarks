## Walkthrough

[1] You cannot start this quest if you have another Weapon Skill Quest active. You must return to the person that gave said quest and quit it to start another.

[2] Speaking with Perih Vashai will grant you the key item Weapons Training Guide as well as the Bow of Trials. You will need to break the latent on the Bow of Trials before completing the rest of the quest.
    - Breaking the Latent

[3] Trade the Bow of Trials back to Perih Vashai. This will give you the key item Map to the Annals of Truth. You will be instructed to travel to Cape Teriggan.

[4] Take Silent Oil and Prism Powder with you and head to Kuftal Tunnel. Travel to (I-7) and north through the tunnel. Continue north; take no branches. At (I-4) the tunnel will loop down southward into a larger space, and you will need to curve immediately back up to the northwest. Exit Kuftal Tunnel into Cape Teriggan.
    - Faster traveling options:
        - You can also use the Home Point to the Cloister of Gales at (F-5) (which is closer to the quest spot).
        - Or the Survival Guide located at the Outpost at (G-8).
        - Or the Unity Warp to Cape Teriggan (Level 128) bring you to (H-7).

[5] Once you are at the Cape, travel to (G-7). This is where you will find the ??? to spawn the NM.

[6] This fight is against Stolas, a Bird. It is weak to Ice. Once you kill it, re-examine the ??? to receive the key item Annals of Truth.

[7] Go back and speak with Perih Vashai to receive your reward.
