# The Holy Crest

The game knows this quest as: The Holy Crest   (quest/sandoria #93)

## Quest Header
Start:      Ceraulian, Port San d'Oria (I-9)
Description: Excavate the wyvern egg that lies beneath the Maze of Shakhrami. Protect it from dragonslayers.
Fame:       San d'Oria
FLevel:     1
Quest Reqs: (empty)
Item Reqs:  
            - Pickaxe
            - Wyvern Egg (provided)
            - [KI] Dragon curse remedy (provided)
Level:      30+
Repeatable: No
Previous:   (empty)
Next:       A Craftsman's Work
Title:      Heir to the Holy Crest
Reward:     Ability to become a Dragoon.
Expansion:  RoZ

## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Arminibit and Ceraulian in Port San d'Oria (I-9) inside Cargo Room A.
    - Auction House or Mog House Home Point are both close.
[2] Speak to Novalmauge who walks between (F-8) and (G-8) in Bostaunieux Oubliette Map 1 (enter from Northern San d'Oria (I/J-6) HP#2 and then Chateau d'Oraguille (I/J-8) ) for a cutscene.
    - Note: If you have not yet completed the quest The Rumor (Drain (Scroll) Quest), you can speak to Novalmauge a second time to accept The Rumor. You no longer need to complete The Rumor before advancing the job quest.
[3] Speak to Morjean in Northern San d'Oria (L-7), within the Cathedral's Manuscript room for a cutscene.
    - The quest will show up on quests tab now.
[4] If you don't already happen to have some, buy a Pickaxe.
    - Ostalie in Southern San d'Oria (F-8) around Home Point #4 is nearby.
    - Trbl. Pickaxe does not work. It must be a regular Pickaxe.
[5] Go to the Maze of Shakhrami and trade (/trade) a Pickaxe to an Excavation Point to receive a Wyvern Egg.
    - If you come across "Fossil Rock," they are unrelated to this quest.
    - See the map on the right for Excavation Points (blue dots).
[6] Return to Morjean and speak to him with the Wyvern Egg in your inventory.
[7] Travel to Meriphataud Mountains (K-8) and look for a ??? near the base of Drogaroga's Spine. Trade the Wyvern Egg to it for a cutscene.
    - Quickest way is the Survival Guide in Castle Oztroja. Step outside and you're looking at the spine's base.
[8] Speak to Rahal in Chateau d'Oraguille (H-9).
    - He will provide you the [KI] Dragon curse remedy.
[9] Travel to Ghelsba Outpost to find the Hut Door at (F-10/G-10) to enter a BC fight. Quickest way to BC Entrance is Domenic Teleport NPC in Lower Jeuno.
    - The enemy in this BC is Wyvern Notorious Monster that is approximately level 40.
    - Only people up to this point in the quest or players who have already completed the quest may enter this BC.
    - You are unable to summon trusts in this fight.
[10] Upon defeating the Wyvern, you will receive your reward.
[11] There are more wyvern names to choose from if you pay to rename it.
