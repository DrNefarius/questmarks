## Walkthrough

[1] Zone into Tavnazian Safehold Home Point #3 for a cutscene and the beginning of Chapter 4.
[2] Go south and speak with Despachiaire at (K-10) on the top floor (behind the Walnut Door) to begin the Mission.
    - (Optional): Speak with Justinius at (J-6).
[3] Check the Dilapidated Gate at the south-west corner of (I-11) in Misareaux Coast.
    - The shortest route is via the south Misareaux Coast exit from Tavnazian Safehold (G-6 Map 2) near to the Home Point #1 and Survival Guide.
        - The Misareaux Coast Home Point and Survival Guide are much farther from the Gate.
