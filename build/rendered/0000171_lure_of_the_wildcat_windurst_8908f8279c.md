[1] Speak with Ibwam to recieve a [KI] Green Sentinel badge and begin the quest.
[2] Speak to all twenty (20) NPCs in the below chart.
[3] Return to Ibwam.  He will exchange your badge for a [KI] Green invitation card.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] After joining Salaheem's Sentinels, speak to Naja Salaheem with the invitation card, and she will reward you with Imperial currency.  The more Lure of the Wildcat quests you have completed, the greater the reward.
[2] After joining Salaheem's Sentinels, Alib-Mufalib will offer to teleport you to Aht Urhgan Whitegate.  Trade him 300 gil to teleport.
