[5] Speak to Red Ghost - Port Jeuno (G-8) who will ask you about your fellow's personality.
    - Personality determines their dialog and which Homemade Food item they make.
        - Each of these is an AoE Food with the same amount of Attack and Accuracy, with 1 additional stat. 30 min duration.
