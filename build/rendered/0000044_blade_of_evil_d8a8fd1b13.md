## Walkthrough

[1] Zone into Beadeaux from Pashhow Marshlands as a Dark Knight to receive a cutscene to start the quest.
    - The easiest way there is using the Beadeaux Survival Guide, exiting to Pashhow Marshlands, then re-entering Beadeaux.
    - You may complete the rest of this quest on a job other than Dark Knight.
[2] Farm Topaz Quadav until you acquire a Quadav Mage Blood.
    - Only one is needed, even if you're doing this with a group of multiple players.
[3] Enter Middle Delkfutt's Tower by either climbing up from Lower Delkfutt's Tower or climbing down from the Upper Delkfutt's Tower Home Point.
[4] Use the teleporter at (J-6) on the 7th floor. You will arrive at a circular chamber on the 6th floor with no exits and a ???.
[5] Trade the Quadav Mage Blood to the ??? to spawn Gerwitz's Scythe NM and two Scythe Victims.
[6] Defeat all 3 mobs and stand on the teleporter to go back upstairs.
    - You only need to defeat Gerwitz's Scythe in order to progress, but the other two mobs are weaker and it's likely faster to kill them than sleep them.
[7] Upon taking the teleport back, you will receive a cutscene and your reward.
