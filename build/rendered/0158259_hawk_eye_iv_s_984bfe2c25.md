# Hawk Eye IV (S)

The game knows this mission as: Hawk Eye IV (S)   (mission/campaign #45)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Intel gathering
Description: Gather intelligence from an enemy fortress.
Orders:      Current intel on the defensive capabilities of enemy strongholds has been deemed insufficient. You are to infiltrate one of these strongholds and gather as much data as possible.
NPC Name:    Rasdinice
Zone:        Southern San d'Oria (S)
pos:         I-9
Campaign Rank: Mythril Wings of Service §§
Time:        None
Level:       Any
Rewards:     * Experience Points
            * Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Rasdinice and sign up for this campaign operation.
[2] Go to a Campaign stronghold in a northern zone under beastmen control and examine one of the two ???s.
    - Strongholds are Beaucedine Glacier (S) (I-8) and Xarcabard (S) (H-8).
    - The other ??? is for Slaughterhouse IV (S). Examining that one yields "You find nothing out of the ordinary." if you don't have that Op active. The other one is the correct one.
[3] You will receive a Commencing intel extraction message, followed by repeated ------Gathering intel------ messages as you gather the intel.
[4] Finally, an Investigation complete! message will appear and you will receive Experience Points.
[5] Report back to Rasdinice to complete the mission and receive Allied Notes.
