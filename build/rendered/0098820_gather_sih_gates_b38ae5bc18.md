## Walkthrough
[1] NPC asks you to obtain 3 jars of Acuex Poison.
    - The NPC specifically notes it does not matter how the player obtains it. It can be bought, traded, or farmed.
    - There are several Bloated Acuex in the north part of Sih Gates map 1. This can be accessed by the entrance near Bivouac #3 in Ceizak Battlegrounds.
[2] Trade these to the NPC and then Report on the quest outcome.
