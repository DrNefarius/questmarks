## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Yoran-Oran (A) at (G-10) in Abyssea - Attohwa at the base camp.
    - Optional: Speak to Jakaka (A) in Abyssea - Attohwa (I-10) for further information on the quest.
[2] Go to the small grass area in the south-east part of (I-10) by the bones. Veridical Conflux #03 is nearby, but you may need to wait for smoke to stop at (J-9) to get through. You may be better off just running to the location from the base camp.
[3] In the grass, move around and use the /clap emote until a glowing Rockhopper targetable location appears on the ground. Interact with it to get a [KI] Rockhopper.
[4] Speak to Yoran-Oran (A) to receive [KI] Phial of counteragent.
[5] Examine the Gasponia targetable flowers in the vicinity of (F-8)/(F-9). Veridical Conflux #00 is nearby.
[6] Speak to Yoran-Oran (A) to receive your reward.
