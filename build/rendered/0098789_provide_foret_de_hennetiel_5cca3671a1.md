# Provide: Foret de Hennetiel

The game knows this assignment as: Provide: Foret de Hennetiel   (quest/coalition #16)

## Giver
Task Delegator - Western Adoulin (G-7)
  (resolved from the Couriers' Coalition page; the assignment page names no NPC)

## Couriers Assignment Table
Coalition:   Couriers' Coalition
Rank:        Probationer
Type:        Base Provisions
Zone:        Foret de Hennetiel
Objective:   Transport provisions to the site in Foret de Hennetiel where the frontier station is to be constructed.
IMP1:        1250
IMP2:        2250
IMP3:        3000

## Walkthrough
[1] Once you accept the assignment, the Task Delegator will provide you with a Hen. FS building mat. container to deliver to a Station Administrator in Foret de Hennetiel.
    - DO NOT use a Waypoint warp. Doing so will cause the Hen. FS building mat. container to break and make you unable to complete the assignment.
        - As of the [http://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/40059-Feb-18-2014-%28JST%29-Version-Update?p=493165&viewfull=1#post493165 Feb. 18 2014 Version Update], Waypoints can now be used to complete this task without risk of break. Simply warp to a Station Administrator.
[2] Zone out to Ceizak Battlegrounds from Western Adoulin at (C-9).
[3] Run southwest and zone to Sih Gates around the border of (G-11)/(H-11) in Ceizak Battlegrounds.
[4] Run south and zone to Foret de Hennetiel at (H-11).
[5] From the entrance of Foret de Hennetiel, run a little ways into the first open area and take the south path to (J-7).
[6] Speak to the Bivouac#2 Administrator at (J-10) to complete the assignment.
[7] Return to the Task Delegator to receive your reward.
