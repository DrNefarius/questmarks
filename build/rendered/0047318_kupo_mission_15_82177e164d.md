## Walkthrough

[1] Head to Tenshodo in Lower Jeuno and examine the Treasure Chest for your reward.
    - The easiest way to repeat the fight to change your augmented item is to first toss it, then purchase the [KI] Mega Bonanza Kupon from Squintrox Dryeyes in Port Jeuno for 2,000 gil and 20 Beastmen Seals.
        - This will allow you to quickly re-enter the BCNM in the Throne Room. Even with a [KI] Mega Bonanza Kupon, you will not be able to re-enter the BCNM if you still have the Angel skin key. You must first exchange it with the red chest in the Lower Jeuno Tenshodo.
    - There is a Japanese Midnight wait on immediately repeating the fight. Otherwise you will receive the message: "Mega Bonanza Kupon has no effect. You cannot enter battlefield at present. Please wait a little longer".
[2] After the fight, you will immediately receive your key rewards.
