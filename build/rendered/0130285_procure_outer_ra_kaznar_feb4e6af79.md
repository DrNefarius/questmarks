## Walkthrough
[1] Obtain a number of Pickaxes (Extras highly recommended)
[2] Travel to Outer Ra'Kaznar
[3] Locate Mining Points and use the Pickaxe
[4] Repeat until you've obtained five items.
[5] Note that gathering fatigue will set in rather quickly, depending on various conditions such as Ionis effects. Gathering fatigue will slowly decay over time, allowing one additional successful gathering attempt approximately every two minutes.
[6] You do not need to keep the physical items you obtained while doing the assignment, the "items" are abstract. You can safely toss or sell any HELM results and still complete the assignment.
