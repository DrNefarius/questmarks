## Walkthrough

Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
[1] Talk to Iron Eater in Metalworks (J-8) for a cutscene.
[2] Make your way to the Throne Room in Castle Zvahl Keep HP #1.
[3] Gather up, and enter the "Where Two Paths Converge" fight when ready.
    - Buffs will wear off once you enter, so do it inside.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Although the first phase of this fight is very easy, the second phase requires more preparation. If trying to low-man this BC, it is advisable to kill Zeid's summons, as they can deal a substantial amount of damage if allowed to skillchain.
[2] Was able to win with level 75 DRK/NIN, WHM/BLM, and BRD/WHM (2 boxed), with the DRK using Dread Spikes in phase two and saving his tp to one shot one of the two initial summons.
[3] Valaineral got killed on the 3rd or 4th step of the skillchain when ignoring the summons on a 78 WAR/SAM
[4] You can still rush down Zeid after this, but if you are attempting this at more of an era level, then you may have more success swapping to the two summons and back onto Zeid in his 2nd form.
