## Walkthrough

[1] Soun Abralah needs a piece of unburnable wood from the Firebloom Tree.
[2] Visit the Firebloom Tree Roots at Yuhtunga Jungle (H-9)
    - Survival Guide or Outpost warp and head northeast
[3] Harvest the Eastern vine, Northern vine, Western vine, and Southern vine by clicking on the trees
    - No Hatchets required
    - The Western vine is in the tunnel.
[4] Head to Ifrit's Cauldron to test the vines' fire resistance in the Flame Spouts.
    - You need to touch 3 Flame Spouts
    - Recommended to use the Survival Guide warp, then go K -> L -> H -> Ash Dragon area.
[5] Return to the appropriate Firebloom Tree Root (only one will work, and it will match your remaining Key Item) and obtain wood
    - Again, no Hatchet is required.
[6] Return to Soun Abralah for your reward.
