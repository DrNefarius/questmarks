## Walkthrough
[1] Participate in one Lair Reive in Yorcia Weald.
    - You may join the reive in the middle of the battle as long as you are present for the final evaluation.

[2] Lair Reives can be found at the following locations:
    - (F/G-7) - 2 Loyal Snapweed and 3 Furibund Rafflesia - Bivouac #3
        - Exit North run straight east and take the first left.
    - (H/I-6) - 2 Tight-lipped Flytraps and 2 Tenacious Panopts - Bivouac #1
        - Exit North until you pass 2 Saptraps and head east you'll pass 2 Snapweeds. Take a right and head East again to find some Leaflick Lapinion, and eventually find some Cheeky Opo-opo and Saptraps. It's just north from there a bit.
    - (J/K-6) - 2 Loyal Snapweed and 2 Tenacious Panopts - Frontier Station
        - Exit to West heading north through the log. Take the first right at the Snapweeds. Next, left just after the next hallow log, right at the 4 Snapweeds, and right at the 2 Corpse Flowers and 2 Snapweeds.
You'll enter a big "Room" with 4 Corpse flowers and the Reive at the back.
    - (K-9) - 2 Fervid Funguar and 2 Tight-lipped Flytraps - Frontier Station
        - Exit southeast and hold the left wall. You'll get there eventually.
    - (J-11) - 2 Fervid Funguar and 2 Furibund Rafflesia - Bivouac #2
        - Exit and head past the path on the north near the 2 Saplings and the Snapweed till you see a path that heads south near a Droughted Treant. You'll come to a T intersection with with a 3rd Treant. Head East from thereand the path will slowly point SE as you pass Saptraps and Snapweeds. You should come across a hallowed out log. Head on through and on the south end of this room you should find the Reive.
