# Brave Dawn I (S)

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Rasdinice.
[2] Travel to the Allied Ensign at East Ronfaure (S) (G-5).
    - Hug the left wall out of the gate from Southern San d'Oria (S).
    - Party of 3 or greater no longer required.
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Everbloom Hollow.
[4] You will come across a Soldier, who immediately runs towards a Trained Crab and begins attacking.
    - During this time, you will see messages that display how much attention he is giving to the fight:
        - The soldier seems unfocused...
        - The soldier has begun training harder!
        - The soldier is fighting with vigor!
[5] Once the Trained Crab is defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
    - If the soldier never gains focus, you lose the mission.
    - You can kill the crab once the soldier is fighting with vigor
[6] Exit via the Dawn Aureola.
[7] Return and talk to Rasdinice for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] There seems to be no time limit to how long you can take to get to Everbloom Hollow.
[2] The soldier will die if left alone, however can cure himself. He will reliably cure himself when falling under 70% hp.
[3] The Trained Crab lasts a long while, and seems to shift hate very often.
