## Walkthrough

Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC (one crystal is enough) to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
(Optional): As usual for Windurst Missions, the Gate Guards and their adjacent NPCs will all have optional dialogue after accepting and at end of the Mission.
[1] Head for the Optistery on the northern map of Windurst Waters (near Home Point #1). Speak to Tosuka-Porika at (G-8) (East door).
    - (Optional): The other NPCs and Tosuka-Porika inside the same room have additional dialogue.
[2] Head to (J-3) in Windurst Woods and speak to Nanaa Mihgo. She will lend you her [KI] Lapis monocle.
    - You may need to talk to her twice or more if you have a [KI] Windurst Trust permit and Aht Urhgan Lure of the Wildcat Quests started.
    - (Optional): Nanaa's cronies nearby have additional dialogue.

## After the walkthrough: Maze of Shakrami

[1] Now make your way to the Maze of Shakhrami, which is in at (K-5) in the northeast corner of Tahrongi Canyon.
    - The Survival Guide places you at the entrance of Maze of Shakhrami so long as you have it unlocked.
    - The Unity Warp to Tahrongi Canyon, Kulshushu Outpost, or Mea crag warps can otherwise save some time.
[2] Inside, head for (G-6) on Map 1, where you can transition to Map 2 (the lower map). Then, head to (H-5) on Map 2.
[3] Check each Fossil Rock until you receive the [KI] Lapis coral.
    - There are about 12 Fossil Rocks on Map 2.
    - There are Fossil Rocks at (H-5),(I-7),(H-7),(G-7),(G-8),(I-8).
    - Many mobs aggro sound, while Goblins will aggro to sight, so keep Sneak (Status) and Invisible (Status) up if you are a lower-level player. You can use Circumspection from the Grounds Tome if necessary.
[4] Make your way back to Nanaa Mihgo, who will give you the [KI] Hideout key.
    - (Optional): Nanaa's cronies have new additional dialogue.

## After the walkthrough: Inner Horutoto Ruins

[1] Head to East Sarutabaruta, and enter the Lily Tower at (J-7) to reach Inner Horutoto Ruins.
    - The Inner Horutoto Ruins Survival Guide warp places you in the right tower if you have it unlocked already.
[2] Head to the Southwest room containing an Ancient Magical Gizmo. At (G-9), there is a Cracked Wall. Open it to proceed to Inner Horutoto Ruins Map 2. Now head for (G-8). There are a few ways to get there, but ultimately that is your destination.
    - You will need Sneak and Invisible due to high level (90+) monsters in the area.
        - Silent Oil Silent Oil and Prism Powder Prism Powder are alternatives to Sneak and Invisible.
    - This also includes potential blood aggro, so be sure you are above 75% HP.
        - Without Sneak and Invisible: Beetles and Bats do not aggro here. There is a path to the door without Goblins and Skeletons, allowing you to pass safely.
[3] Check the Mahogany Door for a cutscene.
    - (Optional): Nanaa and her cronies back in Windurst Woods have new additional dialogue after this cutscene.

## After the walkthrough: Windurst

[1] Head to the House of the Hero at Windurst Walls (G-3). Check the door for a cutscene.
[2] Make your way back to Windurst Waters Home Point #1 and speak to Tosuka-Porika to complete the Mission.
    - (Optional): Some of the NPCs in the same room have new additional dialogue.
