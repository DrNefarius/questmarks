## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Erlene in The Eldieme Necropolis (S) at (J-8) to begin quest. You will be given [KI] Ulbrecht's sealed letter.
[2] Speak to Gentle Tiger in Bastok Markets (S) at (H-6) for a cutscene.
    - You may need to speak to him multiple tines if you have not completed the Bastok (S) questline - if, for instance, he mentions that he has never heard of a [KI] bundle of half-inscribed scrolls, you will need to speak to him again.
[3] Travel to the SW corner of Pashhow Marshlands (S) to touch the Indescript Markings targetable location, on the grid line between (E-11) and (E-10) for a cutscene. You will obtain [KI] Schultz's sealed letter.
    - There are level ~80 Virulent Peistes around the Indescript Markings that aggro on sight.
        - Either come with Invisible handy and wait until they turn, or defeat them with a high enough leveled job.
[4] Return to Erlene for your Klimaform Schema.
