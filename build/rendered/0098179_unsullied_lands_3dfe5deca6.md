## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Inmot-Pinmot at Bivouac #2 to accept the quest.
[2] Head west towards the beach (with a tree reive), but instead of crossing the tree, head south along the coast until you reach the secret beach at (J-11).
[3] Examine the Ergon Locus to obtain a Fistful of pristine sand.
[4] Return to Inmot-Pinmot for your reward.
