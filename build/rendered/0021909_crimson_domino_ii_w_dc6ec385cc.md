## Walkthrough

[1] Easily soloable mission.
[2] After confirming the mission with Emhi Tchaoryo, go to a Windurstian controlled Campaign area and talk to the Gate Sentry. The Gate Sentry will either tell you:
    - "These are the supplies you are to deliver to the stronghold of this area. Some of the items are very delicate and will be destroyed if you are not careful. Avoid all confrontation with the enemy." You will then receive [KI] Campaign supplies immediately.
    - or that "the supply unit carrying rations and munitions to our stronghold in this area was attacked en route, and forced to abandon the cargo. You are to locate the supplies and deliver them to their final destination."
        - This means you must examine a ??? to obtain the supplies.
    - Either way, deliver the supplies to the stronghold of the area without getting aggro.
        - You can mount to deliver the supplies.
