# Iron Anvil II (W)

The game knows this mission as: Iron Anvil II (W)   (mission/campaign #472)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Supply manufacture
Description: To manufacture munitions and supplies, our nation has employed hundreds of young, unskilled guildworkers. You are to lend your trade knowledge to aid in the training of these initiates. Speak with adjutant (F-9) for details.

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo, and you'll be forwarded to Adjutant at (F-9).
[2] Talk to Adjutant, and he'll ask you for a craft to specialize in.
[3] Once you've picked a craft, he'll test you on two synths made with said craft.
    - What crystal is used for this synth? [List]
    - What is the first ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the second ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the third ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the fourth ingredient in this synth? [List] (optional)
[4] Once you've completed your two tests, go back and talk to Emhi Tchaoryo for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This is repeatable (as long as you have tags), making it a very quick and easy way to stock up on Allied Notes.
[2] This Ops mission is identical to Crystal Fist II (W) in nearly every aspect except the fact that there are two synth tests instead of three, and your reward is greater.
[3] Goldsmithing:
    - Ardent Jadeite, Brass Cuisses, Brass Harness, Brass Tank, Mighty Sardonyx, Mythril Grip, Piercing Dagger, Sabiki Rig, Silver Belt, Thief's Tools.
