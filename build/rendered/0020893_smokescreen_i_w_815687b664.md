## Walkthrough

[1] After accepting mission, wait until a Yagudo-controlled territory is under a campaign battle.
    :*This includes all beastman controlled territories in The Aragoneu Front and The Sarutabaruta Front.
[2] Once one of those territories is under campaign, warp there and join the fray!
[3] After the campaign is over, you will immediately be rewarded with 110 experience points.
[4] Speak to Emhi Tchaoryo to receive your allied note reward and complete the campaign op.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] You will fail the mission if you do a campaign that doesn't fit the criteria (or maybe joining a fitting campaign, but not doing any damage/healing).
