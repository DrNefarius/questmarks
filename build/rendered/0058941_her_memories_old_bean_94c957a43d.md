## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Thierride in Port San d'Oria (G-7) Home Point #1 inside the Rusty Anchor Pub.  You will receive the [KI] Thierride's bean creation.
[2] Speak to Amaura in Southern San d'Oria (G-6) Home Point #4.
    - Amaura may give multiple cutscenes. The one for this quest is the one where you give her the dish to try, but while you're here you can talk to her again to start Her Memories: Of Malign Maladies (the cutscene with Lilisette in it).
[3] Speak to Thierride for the [KI] Tiny memory fragment.
