## Walkthrough

|-
| style="vertical-align: top" |
[1] (Optional): Speak to Despachiaire through the Walnut Door at (K-10) on the top floor of Tavnazian Safehold for a cutscene.
    - He may start other quests like X Marks the Spot before triggering the cutscene: it's the flashback with Ulmia.
    - (Optional): Speak to Parelbriaux by the Walnut Door at (K-7), Prishe's room, on the top floor of Tavnazian Safehold for a cutscene.
    - (Optional): Speak to Odeya at (J-10) near a water pump on the top floor of Tavnazian Safehold for a cutscene. This is down the ramp from Despachiaire's Room.
    - The two optional steps above provide some context for this mission, but they are ultimately lead ins for the X Marks the Spot quest, and are not necessary to move 5-1 forward.
[2] (Optional): Look for your companions at the aforementioned meeting place.
[3] (Optional): Speak to Chasalvige in Northern San d'Oria (L-6, Cathedral Manuscript Room) for a cutscene.
[4] (Optional): Head to Port San d'Oria and speak to Anoki (H-6, Air Travel Agency) for a cutscene.
[5] (Optional): Speak to Anoki a second time for your hint on where to go next.
[6] Zone into Port Bastok for a cutscene.
[7] Speak with Cid in the Metalworks for a cutscene.
[8] Travel to the Nue Tower at (F-7) in Beaucedine Glacier, and enter the uncapped area of Pso'Xja for a small cutscene. (Home Point will not work.)
[9] Move ahead until you reach the Stone Door at (H-8). Checking this door will spawn a Golem named Nunyunuwi.
    - This is the 1st of 2 stone doors that leads to Promyvion - Vahzl.
    - This NM has very low HP (about 3,800) but incredibly high Regen (about 100HP/tick). Unload all you have on it and it should fall easily.
[10] Check the stone door again once Nunyunuwi is defeated to continue on.
[11] In the next room, take the elevator down to the bottom (there are no Diremites at the bottom of this elevator ride).
[12] Follow the path here until you reach another Stone Door for you to check, a cutscene will play and you will enter Promyvion - Vahzl.
    - This is the 2nd of 2 Stone Doors mentioned here.
[13] You will get another cutscene upon entering.
