## After the walkthrough: Preparation

[1] Before heading out to Arrapago Reef, you will need to obtain a Hamsi Hamsi.
    - This can be purchased from Wahnid (H-11) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate, bought from the Auction House or easily fished from Aht Urghan Whitegate with zero fishing skill.
    - Wahnid is open from 1:00-18:00, closed on Lightsday.
        - The item is not automatically restocked. It is only available if previously sold to the guild.
[2] You will also need a Lamian Fang Key Lamian Fang Key. Alternatively, Thief can use Skeleton Key and or Thief Tools.
    - You can obtain the key by examining the ??? once per game day at (I-7) in Caedarva Mire Map 1 (in the vicinity of the small pond containing Jnun).
    - You will need at least 2 more keys for the upcoming steps, so if you are with multiple people/characters, each one should grab a key.
    - The Survival Guide teleport to Arrapago Reef (via Arrapago Is.) and running south will exit to Caedarva Mire (I-6).
        - Escape from Arrapago Reef will put you in Caedarva Mire (I-6) as well.
        - Alternatively, you can use the Azouph Isle Staging Point (Leujaoam Sanctum), exit Nashmau at (H-6) or use the Voidwatch warp to Caedarva Mire.
