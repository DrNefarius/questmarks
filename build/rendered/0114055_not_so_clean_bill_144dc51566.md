## Walkthrough

[1] You must wait a game day after completing Sick and Tired to flag this quest.
[2] Check the Door: Svenja's Manor in Western Adoulin (I-8).
[3] Speak with Borghest at the Morimar Basalt Fields Frontier Station.
[4] Speak with Fritha at Foret de Hennetiel Frontier Station.
[5] Head to the Foreboding Vineprints at (J-10) in Foret de Hennetiel for a cutscene.
    - This is west of Bivouac #2.
[6] Examine the Foreboding Vineprints again to spawn Pungent Patricia, a Morbol NM.
[7] After defeating the NM, check the Foreboding Vineprints again for a cutscene.
[8] Check the Door: Hospital in Western Adoulin at (I-9) to complete this quest and receive your reward.
    - NOTE: This item can be re-obtained if dropped by waiting until the next Vanadiel day and checking the Door: Svenja's Manor. After a brief cutscene you will be asked to pay 10,000 Bayld in order to reacquire this item.
