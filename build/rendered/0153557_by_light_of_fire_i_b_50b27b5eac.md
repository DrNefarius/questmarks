## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus.
[2] Travel to the Beastmen Ensign at (G-12) in Grauberg.
    - Party of 3 or greater required.
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ruhotz Silvermines.
[4] You'll start out at (J-7). Head to the intersections at (I-8). The southern path looks like it leads somewhere, but is actually a dead-end. Instead, head north where there will be a few Bronze Quadavs. Wipe them out as you see fit (Sleep to handle the inevitable links is highly reccomended) and head to the west, where you'll find stronger Iron Quadavs. Again, defeat these Quadav, and head down the stairs where you'll find some Iron Quadavs mixed in with some Darksteel Quadavs.
[5] To complete this operation, you need to meet a minimum requirement of war results, the war results tally is displayed after each kill.
    - Killing a Bronze Quadav or a Brass Quadav increments your Soldiers count.
    - Killing a Iron Quadav or a Steel Quadav increments your Enlistees count.
    - Killing a Darksteel Quadav increments your Officers count.
[6] When the completion requirements are met, "Operation Complete" message will be printed in the logs.
[7] Must exit area using the Twilight Aureola for Hieronymus to acknowledge the mission as successful.
[8] Return and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.
