## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Mishhar near HP#1 in Whitegate.
    - Requires a JP Midnight wait after Give Peace a Chance
[2] Go to the Teahouse (J-12) for another cutscene.
[3] Return to Mishhar (H-8)
[4] Enter Mamook from (D-12) in Wajaom Woodlands and click on a nearby ???
    - The ??? is right next to the Survival Guide
    - Using the Mamook Survival Guide will put you at the correct entrance
[5] Head to Jade Sepulcher and click the BCNM entrance ("Ornamental Door") for a cutscene
    - Use Aht Urghan Whitegate -> Bhaflau Thickets HP #1 will make this fast
[6] Click again to enter the BCNM: "Moment of Truth"
[7] Blacktattoo Vedool Ja (BLM)
[8] Shadowhand Kajeel Ja (NIN)
[9] Whitetattoo Rahool Ja (WHM)
    - They do not aggro if you one-shot them from a distance.
    - You are assisted by three NPCs, one paired with each Mamool.
        - These must not die.
[10] Return to Mishhar for your reward
    - You get medicine that depends on your job.
        - Vile Elixir +1 - RDM, BLU
        - Pro-Ether +1 - SMN
        - Intelligence Potion - BLM, SCH
        - Vitality Potion - PLD, RUN
        - Agility Potion - RNG, COR, NIN
        - Dexterity Potion - PUP
        - Mind Potion - WHM
        - Strength Potion - WAR, MNK, DRK, DRG
        - Charisma Potion - BST, BRD, DNC
        - Hermes Quencher - THF
        - Icarus Wing - SAM
