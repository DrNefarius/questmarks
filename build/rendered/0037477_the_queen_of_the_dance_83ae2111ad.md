## Walkthrough

[1] This mission is triggered automatically after completion of Cait Sith.
[2] Proceed to Lion Springs Tavern (K-6) in Southern San d'Oria (S) and touch the door for a cutscene.
    - if the door is locked you may have to Zone in from Southern San d'Oria (S) from East Ronfaure (S) to get a cut scene (To finish Cait Sith mission), or do Nation Quest 3 and 4.
[3] Travel to present day Upper Jeuno and speak with Turlough at (G-7) (watching the dancers). He will give you a [KI] Mayakov show ticket.
[4] Return to Lion Springs Tavern in Southern San d'Oria (S) and touch the door again to trigger a cutscene.
