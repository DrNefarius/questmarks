# Crystal Fist I (W)

The game knows this mission as: Crystal Fist I (W)   (mission/campaign #461)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Supply manufacture
Description: To manufacture munitions and supplies, our nation has employed hundreds of young, unskilled guildworkers. You are to lend your trade knowledge to aid in the training of these initiates. Speak with adjutant (F-9) for details.
Orders:      (empty)
NPC Name:    Emhi Tchaoryo

pos:         I-9
Campaign Rank: Bronze Ribbon of Service §

Level:       Any
Rewards:     * 90 Experience Points
            * 480 Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo, and you'll be forwarded to Adjutant at (F-9).
[2] Talk to Adjutant, and he'll ask you for a craft to specialize in.
[3] Once you've picked a craft, he'll test you on three synths made with said craft.
    - What crystal is used for this synth? [List]
    - What is the first ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the second ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - For example, the items that came up for clothcraft were a cotton headband, a red grass thread, and a flask of flaxseed oil.
[4] Once you've completed your three tests, go back and talk to Emhi Tchaoryo for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This is repeatable (as long as you have tags), making it a very quick and easy way to stock up on Allied Notes.
[2] Do Goldsmithing - they all use the same Wind Crystal and you can do it without even looking at the below list if you know which color the requested gemstone is IRL.
