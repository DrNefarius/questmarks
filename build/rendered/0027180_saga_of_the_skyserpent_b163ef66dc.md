## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Fari-Wari at the Shararat Teahouse in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (K-12) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
[2] Go to Wajaom Woodlands (C-8) to enter Halvung.  Go to (H-11) and examine the ??? to acquire a Lilac ribbon.
    - Fastest way is using Wajaom Woodlands Survival Guide.
[3] Speak to Fari-Wari.
[4] Wait a game day and speak to Fari-Wari to complete the quest.

You may only get the cutscenes in Aht Urhgan Whitegate when General Rughadjeen is in Al Zahbi.
