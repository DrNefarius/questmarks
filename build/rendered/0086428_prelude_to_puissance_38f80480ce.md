## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to the Nomad Moogle next to Maat to begin the quest.
[2] Retrieve a Seasoning Stone, which drops off nearly all level 90+ regular monsters outside of Abyssea and trade it to the Nomad Moogle.
    - The Seasoning Stone is also available from the Login Campaign for only 5 points.
[3] You will receive Soul gem clasp and access to the level 95 cap fight, Beyond Infinity.
        - If you are defeated, speak to the Nomad Moogle to get another clasp for 1 merit point or 5x High Kindred Seals.
    - When Atori-Tutori asks if you are ready, say 'Yes'. This does not start the BCNM fight, it merely advances the quest line.
[4] This quest is now technically completed. Continue to the next quest and win the BCNM Beyond Infinity.
