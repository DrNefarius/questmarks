## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Muzaffar to begin the quest.
[2] Bring him either 4 Eastern Pottery, 4 Northern Fur, or 4 Southern Mummy to complete the quest:
    - Eastern Pottery x4 &mdash; 2000 gil, drops off Clockwork Pods in Garlaige Citadel or Fei'Yin.
    - Northern Fur x4 &mdash; 1500 gil, drops off Giant Hunter in Qufim Island or Giant Lobber in Lower Delkfutt's Tower or Middle Delkfutt's Tower.
    - Southern Mummy x4 &mdash; 3000 gil, drops off Liches in Western Altepa Desert.

== Note: ==

[3] All the required items can be found on the Auction House as well, under Others ➞ Misc. 1.
