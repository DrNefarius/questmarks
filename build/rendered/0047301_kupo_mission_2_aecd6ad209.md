## Walkthrough

[1] Trade the following three items to your Moogle:
    - Orc. Armor Plate dropped from Orcs in Yughott Grotto.
    - Quadav Backscale dropped from Quadavs from Palborough Mines.
    - Yagudo Caulk dropped from Yagudos in Giddeus.
    NOTE: They must be traded in the mog house of your current nation of allegiance, and all at once.

## After the walkthrough: "A Moogle Kupo d'Etat" Key

[1] Collect another Orc. Armor Plate, Quadav Backscale, and Yagudo Caulk.
[2] Head to Port Jeuno and trade all three items to Squintrox Dryeyes (G-8) to receive [KI] White coral key.
    - The key can be received once per day.
[3] Use the [KI] White coral key (Level 30) to open the red treasure coffer at Lower Jeuno (J-8).
    - Rewards are listed on the Add-on Scenario Rewards page.
