## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kupipi in Heavens Tower (Windurst Walls, HP #1) for a cutscene to receive [KI] Star-crested summons. Zoning after the last mission is not required.
[2] Speak to Tosuka-Porika in Windurst Waters HP #1 in the east wing of the Optistery (G-8) (Map 1) for another cutscene.
[3] Touch the Worn Chest in the storage room of the basement area of Giddeus on the second map at (F-6).
    - Drop via E on Map 1.
    - To enter the treasure room, click on the door, not the Yagudo guard.
    - There is no preemptive cutscene. You will immediately be attacked from behind by a Yagudo Lookout (SAM).
        - Uses Meikyo Shisui around 66% HP and Yaegasumi around 33% HP.
        - The Yagudo Lookout has approximately 30k HP.
    - There is a time limit of 15 minutes.
[4] Touch the Worn Chest again after the battle for a cutscene and to get the [KI] Strange doll.
    - If you zone before touching the Worn Chest, you can return and check it to continue without fighting again.
[5] Return to Tosuka-Porika in Windurst Waters for a cutscene and your reward.
