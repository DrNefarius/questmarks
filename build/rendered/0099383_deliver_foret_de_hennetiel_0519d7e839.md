# Deliver: Foret de Hennetiel

The game knows this assignment as: Deliver: Foret de Hennetiel   (quest/coalition #21)

## Walkthrough
[1] Once you accept the assignment, you will -not- be provided with any supplies. You will receive Hen. FS resource container at the Frontier Station.
    - As of Feb 18, 2014 update, Waypoints can be used to complete this task. Hen. FS resource container will not break.
[2] Zone out to Ceizak Battlegrounds from Western Adoulin at (C-9).
[3] Run southwest and zone to Sih Gates around the border of (G-11)/(H-11) in Ceizak Battlegrounds.
[4] Run south and zone to Foret de Hennetiel at (H-11).
[5] From the entrance of Foret de Hennetiel, run a little ways into the first open area and take the south path to (J-7).
[6] Speak to the Station Administrator at (J-7) to receive Hen. FS resource container.
[7] Return to the Task Delegator to deliver the supplies and receive your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes
[1] Movement speed increasing effects recommended.
