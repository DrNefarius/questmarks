## Walkthrough

You must zone after completing Voices from Beyond before you can start this quest.
[1] Talk to Rukususu (A) (F-10)
    - South of Veridical Conflux #7, near the encampment by the waterfall. Do not drop down the river!
[2] Head to the Fay Pond at (F-5) in the Witchfire Glen area of the map via Conflux #5.
[3] You must trade  Twinkle Powder to the Fay Pond for a chance to receive the [KI] Pinch of pixie dust.
    - Bring multiples as the chance of obtaining the pixie dust is random.
[4] Return to Rukususu (A) to complete this quest.
Zoning is required in order to repeat this quest.
