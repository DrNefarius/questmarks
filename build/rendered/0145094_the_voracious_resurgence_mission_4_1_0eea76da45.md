## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Gumbah in Bastok Mines HP #1, upper level (J-7) for a cutscene to begin the Mission.
[2] Speak with Drangord (D-7) near the entrance to Zeruhn Mines for another cutscene.
[3] Enter Zeruhn Mines from Bastok Mines for a cutscene.
    - Optional: Drake Fang in Zeruhn Mines (H-6) will direct you south to stop the Quadav attack.
[4] Touch the Quadav Inquest (I-9) for a cutscene.
[5] Touch the Quadav Inquest again to begin a confrontation with:
    - Do'Bho Venomtail (BLM) - 30k HP
        - Do'Bho Venomtail uses Manafont and has a 100 HP/tic Enpoison effect on melee hits.
    - Old Quadav WAR - 25k HP
    - Brass Quadav DRK - 25k HP
    - Copper Quadav THF - 25k HP
[6] Touch the Quadav Inquest once more for a final cutscene and your reward.
