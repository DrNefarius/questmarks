## Walkthrough

[1] Accept the Mission from any Gate Guard.
    - Mokyokyo - Windurst Waters (F-5)
    - Janshura-Rashura - Port Windurst (B-5)
    - Rakoh Buuma - Windurst Woods (K-10)
    - Zokima-Rokima - Windurst Walls (H-7)
[2] Then make your way to the roof of the Rhinostery in Windurst Waters (J-8) (near Home Point #3), it is located in the southern half of the zone.
    - Speak with Leepe-Hoppe at (J-9) on the roof of the Rhinostery. He will give you the [KI] Food offering and [KI] Drink offering.
[3] Head out for Giddeus, which is located at (F-8) in West Sarutabaruta.
    - If you previously unlocked it, you can teleport directly to (G-12) with Home Point #1 and make your way north from there.
[4] Make your way to (H-7) and speak to a Yagudo NPC named Laa Mozi to hand over the [KI] Food offering.
    - You can reach this NPC by taking all left turns from the Sarutabaruta entrance.
[5] Next, head to (G-7) and speak to a Yagudo NPC called Ghoo Pakya to hand over the [KI] Drink offering.
    - Return to the Sarutabaruta entrance and take all right turns to reach this NPC.
[6] Head back to the Rhinostery. As you climb the stairs to the roof, you'll receive another cutscene.
[7] Return to the Gate Guard to complete the Mission.
