## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Naruru, Teigero-Bangero, and Panta-Putta. No one knows where the Two of Swords has gone.
[2] Speak to Bozz, Upper Jeuno (G-9)
[3] Speak to Monberaux, Upper Jeuno (G-10 in the infirmary) to get a cutscene.
    - You may have to speak to Monberaux more than once to receive the cutscene about the special patient.
[4] After the cutscene, the quest will be completed and you will receive your reward.
    - The next quest, The Kind Cardian, will be automatically started.
