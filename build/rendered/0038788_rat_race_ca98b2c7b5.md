## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kakkaroon.
[2] Speak to Nadee Periyaha Aht Urhgan Whitegate (H-9).
[3] Speak to Cacaroon Aht Urhgan Whitegate (G-11).
[4] Trade Cacaroon one Imperial Bronze Piece.
[5] Now you must complete Cook-a-roon? for a Nashmau Stew
    - Buying one from the Auction House will not work.
    - Keeping one from a previous repeat of Cook-a-roon? will not work.
[6] Trade a Nashmau Stew to Kyokyoroon back in Nashmau (H-7).
[7] Speak to Kakkaroon to complete the quest.
