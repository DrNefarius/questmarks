[1] (Optional) Aldo's Decision: At the Tenshodo HQ in Lower Jeuno, speak to Aldo for a cutscene.
[2] Zone into Lower Delkfutt's Tower from Qufim Island or via Survival Guide for a cutscene.
[3] Zone into Stellar Fulcrum for a cutscene.
    - Home Point # 1 in Upper Delkfutt's Tower puts you right at this zone. You can then use the teleporter.
[4] Having a 25px|link= Delkfutt Key or [KI] Delkfutt key will greatly speed this mission up.
[5] If you have a key, proceed to (E-8) on the first floor and go through the cermet door to the basement.
    - Then proceed to (J-8) for another cermet door.
    - In the middle of the spiral staircase at (H-8) click the ??? to be teleported to the 10th floor.
[6] If you do not have the key then you will have to climb the tower regularly.
[7] Once you reach Upper Delkfutt's Tower by any means, head for (F-8) on the first map and then through the door.
    - This area is not shown on the map.
[8] Climb the stairs to the 11th floor. You'll need Sneak and Invisible to avoid aggro from Bats and Giants. There are also dolls and pots which aggro magic.
[9] Proceed to (J-6) and go to the 12th floor.
[10] Head for (F-10) and go through the teleporter to enter the Stellar Fulcrum.
[11] Walk forward and click the Qe'lov Gate to enter the battlefield.
