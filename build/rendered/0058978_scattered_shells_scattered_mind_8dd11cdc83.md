## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Aladoverre (A) at (E-7) to activate the quest.
    - Veridical Conflux #08 is the closest teleport nearby.
[2] Your objective is to obtain a [KI] Chipped linkshell,  [KI] Cracked linkshell, AND [KI] Grimy linkshell from various ??? targetable locations throughout the zone.
[3] There are 5 areas where a ??? can spawn to obtain one of the [KI] linkshells.
    - Amongst the Helter-skelter near Conflux #8.
    - Between the Peapuks and Russet Rarabs northwest of Conflux #6.
    - Amongst the Aestutaur near Conflux #4.
    - The 4th and 5th spots jump throughout various spots in the zone, so it is best to just visit the three spots above since they never move.
        - It is important to note that only one [KI] linkshell can be obtained from each ???.
[4] Return to Aladoverre (A) once all 3 [KI] linkshells are obtained to complete the quest and receive your reward.
Zoning is required in order to repeat this quest.
