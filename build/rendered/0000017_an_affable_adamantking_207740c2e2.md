## Walkthrough

[1] Travel to Beadeaux and enter Qulun Dome at (H-7) (left after going underground the first time) for a cutscene that starts this quest.
    - If you choose not to accept this quest, you will not be given the chance to start it again until the next Conquest Tally.
[2] Speak to Peshi Yohnts, Windurst Woods (H-13) for a cutscene.
[3] Trade Peshi Yohnts a Bugard Leather, Turtle Shell and 10,000 gil.
[4] Wait until the next game day to talk to Peshi Yohnts again who will give you Quadav Parts and instructions on how to craft it into a Quadav Barbut.
    - Zoning is no longer required.
[5] To synthesize a Quadav Barbut:
[CRAFTING RECIPE]

[6] Return to Beadeaux and enter Qulun Dome at (H-7) while wearing your Quadav Barbut for a cutscene. You will receive the Orcish seeker bats key item.
[7] Check the Beastmen's Banner (F-8) while wearing the Quadav Barbut to spawn the following NM's:
    - Diamond Quadav (WHM)
        - Can use Benediction which will heal/wake any of the other NM's in range.
    - De'Pha Unscarred (WAR)
    - Hu'Rhe Marrowgorger (DRK)
    - Ka'Ghi Trovetaker (THF)
    - Mu'Zha Infernoblade (RDM)
    - No'Bhu Unyielding (PLD)
    - So'Hyu Quakemaker (BLM)
        - Only the Diamond Quadav has to be defeated to continue.  As soon as he is defeated, all the other NM's will despawn.
[8] Once Diamond Quadav is defeated, check the Beastmen's Banner again while wearing the Quadav Barbut for a cutscene.
[9] Trade your Quadav Barbut to the Beastmen's Banner to have it replaced by Da'Vhu's Barbut.
[10] Zone out of Qulun Dome and then back into the zone while wearing your new piece of equipment for a cutscene and a Gold Beastcoin.
