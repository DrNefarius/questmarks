## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Belgidiveau (A) at (F-4), the base camp (Veridical Conflux #01).
[2] Belgidiveau wishes for you to trade him a part of an Abyssean monster, such as NM pop items or a part of a Voragean.
    - Multiple items are "High Strength" and yield the greatest fame/rewards.
        - High Strength items include:  Avian Remex,  Black Whisker,  Djinn Ashes,  Fortune Wing, Gnr. Taurus Horn Gnarled Taurus Horn,  Gory Pincer, H.Q. Lim. Pincer High-Quality Limule Pincer, H.Q. Rabbit Hide High-Quality Rabbit Hide,  Mocking Beak,  Opaque Wing, Tr. Insect Wing Transparent Insect Wing
    - Many other items work. They tend to be tier 1 NM pop items that drop from ordinary monsters within Abyssea.
        - Two stackable items that work are Amb. Pseudopod Amoeban Pseudopod and  Sanguinets. Both of these drop off Stream Amoebans nearby.
            - 50 total quest completions with either of these items will max fame in this zone.
    - No Magian Trial drops work.
Zoning is not required to repeat this quest.
