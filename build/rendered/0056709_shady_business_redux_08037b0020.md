## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Talib (A) at (D-7), slightly north/east of Veridical Conflux #03. He is running around this area, so look around a bit.
[2] He will request that you provide him with 4  Limule Pincers.
    - Limule Pincers drop off of various members of the Limule family. In particular, some spawn in Abyssea - Konschtat around (E-8)/(F-8), a short walk from Talib (A).
    - They can also be purchased on the Auction House.
[3] Trade the items to Talib (A) to complete the quest.
Zoning is not required to repeat this quest.
