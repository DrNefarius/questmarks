## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Dauperiat to obtain a Suspicious envelope.  Deliver this letter to Halver.
[2] Return to Dauperiat, he will now request that you complete a second job for him.
[3] If you agree, he requests that you get the floor plans back from the Orcs.
    - Orcish Serjeants in Fort Ghelsba, Davoi, and Yughott Grotto can drop the plans. Trade the Castle Floor Plans back to Dauperiat for your reward.
    - If you chose no, you can still proceed to the next quest.
        - As of 2026, you can flag the next quest by fame alone without even flagging this one. Note that you have to turn in the Castle Floor Plans to get this quest marked as complete.
