## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Titus (A) at (G-11), at Conflux #5, to begin the quest.
[2] He will request a clump of Windurstian Tea Leaves.
    - Titus (A) synthesizes the [KI] Bottle of military ink, but this item is only used during the cutscene and is not given to you.
[3] After trading him the leaves, he provides you with a [KI] Military ink package.
[4] Deliver the package to Chumimi (A) at the Oasis near Revelation Rock, (G-7).
    - The closest conflux is #7.
[5] After delivering the package, you will complete the quest.
