# The Voracious Resurgence Mission 2-1

The game knows this mission as: Kupipi's Dilemma   (mission/tvr #138)

## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kupipi in Heavens Tower for a cutscene. Windurst Walls HP #1.
[2] Speak to the 3 Tarutaru outside her office in the lobby area of Heavens Tower, and trade them the items they request:
    - Sassa-Kotassa - Red Grass Cloth - (link= ➞ Materials ➞ Clothcraft)
    - Sheelala - Yagudo Drink - (Curio Vendor Moogle or link= ➞ Food ➞ Meals ➞ Drinks)
    - Habida-Jubida - Dream Fl. Petal - (link= ➞ Materials ➞ Alchemy 1)
        - You can obtain this flower by killing Alraune in Bibiki Bay - Purgonorgo Isle after it has used the move Dream Flower. This Mission is not required to be active for the petal to drop.
        - Drop the mob's HP to 20% and spamming Dia will speed up the process.
[3] Return to Kupipi for another cutscene. She will ask for a Wh. Rolanberry . (Alternatively, a Ri. Wh. Rolan. will also work.)

## After the walkthrough: Obtaining the Rolanberries

The Wh. Rolanberry , Ri. Wh. Rolan. and Rolanberry 854 can be purchased from the Auction House (link= ➞ Food ➞ Ingredients) if you wish to skip the NM farm, or can be farmed from the chain of force pop Crawler NMs within Crawler's Nest.
Rolanberry can be purchased from NPCs in Jeuno:
[1] Each of the types of Rolanberries can be bought and sold on the Auction House. (link= ➞ Food ➞ Ingredients)
    - It is not recommended to buy the base Rolanberries from the Auction House if you decide to do the Crawler NM climb.
[2] All types of Rolanberry stack to 12.
