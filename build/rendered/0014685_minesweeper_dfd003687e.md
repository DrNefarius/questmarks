## Walkthrough

[1] Gerbaum asks you to exterminate the pests that roam inside Zeruhn Mines.
[2] Collect 3 Zeruhn Soot either by killing the monsters in Zeruhn Mines, or by buying them off the auction house.
[3] Trade them to Gerbaum for your reward.
