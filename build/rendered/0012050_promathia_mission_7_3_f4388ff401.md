## Walkthrough

{| width=100% |
|- valign=top |
|
[1] Travel to Mine Shaft #2716 and check the Shaft Entrance for a cutscene.
    - There are several ways to reach this zone:
        - Use the Home Point (Newton Movalpolos #1) - You should have this from when you came here for Promathia Mission 5-3.
        - Trade a Snow Lily to Tarnotik at (K-10) in Oldton Movalpolos and he will warp you there.
        - Gamble with Twinkbrix at (E-13) in Oldton Movalpolos until you win the [KI] Shaft 2716 operating lever, then pay him 2000 more gil to warp you there.
        - The old fashioned (super-long) way of walking through Newton Movalpolos and operating the furnace hatches.
            - This requires at least 3 people.
[2] Head to the Metalworks and speak with Cid.
    *Wait for a new Vana'diel day and speak with Cid again to begin the next mission.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This was the last mission in the second CoP mission update.
[2] While Cid is repairing the airship, finishing Rise of the Zilart Missions is recommended to help understand the last chapters of this story.
