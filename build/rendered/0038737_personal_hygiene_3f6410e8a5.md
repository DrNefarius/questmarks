## Walkthrough

[1] After completing Even More Gullible's Travels you will have a scent of the Rafflesia flowers on you.
    - This scent will repulse all Mithra in Kazham but is required to start the quest The Opo-opo and I.
[2] Speak to Gatih Mijurabi (I-8) to begin this quest to remove the scent.
[3] The hot springs is located in Korroloka Tunnel (G-9) on map 4.
[4] Stand yourself under the waterfall for approximately 5 minutes.
    - When you are in the right spot, you will get the message that "The water in this spring is pleasant and tepid. This looks like a nice place to warm yourself up."
    - When you exit the hot spring after standing under the waterfall for the right amount of time, you will get another message informing you that the smell of the Rafflesia pollen is gone.
[5] The waterfall is guarded by several leeches that aggro to level 99 players.
[6] Return to Gatih Mijurabi to complete the quest.

== Note: ==

[7] If you need to re-apply the scent for the quest The Opo-opo and I, you can check the three Rafflesia flowers in the Yuhtunga Jungle once per game day until you get the scent back.
