## Walkthrough

Trade Harith any of the following Recollections as well as 2,000 gil to receive the listed Anima.
[1] Recollection of Pain -> Hysteroanima (negates special attacks)
[2] Recollection of Fear -> Psychoanima (negates normal attacks
[3] Recollection of Guilt -> Terroanima (causes monster to flee)

Trade Harith any of the following Recollections to receive the listed item.
[4] Recollection of Suffering -> Hamayumi
[5] Recollection of Anxiety -> Stone Gorget
[6] Recollection of Animosity -> Dia Wand
