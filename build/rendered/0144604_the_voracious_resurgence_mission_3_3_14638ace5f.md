[1] Speak with Halver for a cutscene to begin the Mission.
    - You will need to zone after the previous Mission to trigger this cutscene.
    - You will receive [KI] Missive to Altennia after the cutscene.
[2] Head to Ranguemont Pass.
    - Survival Guide (Fauregandi/Ranguemont Pass) is the fastest way to get there.
        - Otherwise zone in from East Ronfaure at (K-4).
[3] Touch the Vicious Claw Marks at (J-8) to begin a confrontation with Harnessed Smilodon (WAR).
    - Has approximately 35k HP.
    - Uses Mighty Strikes.
[4] Touch the Vicious Claw Marks again after defeating the tiger for a cutscene with Altennia.
[5] Return to Halver for a cutscene.
[6] Click the Door:Prince Royal's RM located at (H-7) Northern Door in the Central Garden for another cutscene.
[7] Head to Bostaunieux Oubliette from the Chateau.
    - Your destination is on the top floor, so don't drop down the pit at E-7/8.
[8] Touch the Frigid Confluence at (H-9) top floor to begin a confrontation with Trion.
    - Possesses very high defense. Approximately 25k HP.
    - Uses Invincible at low hp.
    - He can use it as soon as he is down to 75% hp remaining.
    - Fight ends with a short cutscene.
[9] Speak with Curilla in the Temple Knight's Quarters for a final cutscene and your reward.
    - You might need to speak to her more than once if she has other quests for you.
