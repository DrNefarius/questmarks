## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kurando to begin quest.
[2] Travel to Rolanberry Fields and proceed to (K-7).
[3] Look for the NM Silk Caterpillar. It will spawn when an airship goes by about every 14 minutes.
[4] Upon defeating the NM, you will receive a  Silkworm Egg
[5] Return to Kurando and trade him the egg to complete quest.
