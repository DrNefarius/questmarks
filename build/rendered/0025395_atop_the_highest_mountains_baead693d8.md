## Walkthrough

[TABLE]
    * Speak to Maat on a level 51 job. He will ask you for three key items guarded by monsters in Xarcabard. :*If this is your first time heading to Xarcabard you may use the Unity level 125 → Xarcabard warp to get there. :{| style="background: #E2EDF7; text-align:center; width: 100%; max-width: 350px;" class="bdrwhite thwhite R-Highlight-Blue"
    Monster | Position | Key Item
    Boreal Tiger | (I-5) | Round frigicite
    Boreal Coeurl | (J-6) | Square frigicite
    Boreal Hound | (G-10) | Triangular frigicite
[/TABLE]

[1] The Notorious Monsters stand guard in caves have fairly large aggro range and will respawn within about 5 minutes after being defeated.
    - As of the February 2012 update, the ??? spot will always be present at the far end of the cave, you will receive the key item once you touch it.
        - As a result, you do not need to defeat the Notorious Monsters. You can simply hold them while examining the spots.
            - These NMs will no longer use draw-in, and you may run away afterwards. They will not chase you for very far. Tested this as a lv 51 on 3/12/24 and the Coeurl at least did Draw-in several times. Will not draw-in if you do not engage.
[2] You also solo these Notorious Monsters now with relative ease using Trusts.
    - Otherwise you may ride through the cave on a Mount to receive the key items, and then either remount or run out.

[3] Speak to Maat once you have all three key items to complete the quest.
