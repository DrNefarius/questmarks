## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Balasiel, who will give you the Book of Tasks containing hints on how to proceed.
[2] Speak to Cahaurme (J-9) from (L-8) (In the "Tower" above the East Gate) to recieve Book of the East.
[3] Speak to Baunise (H-9) from (F-8) (In the "Tower" above the West Gate) to receive Book of the West.
[4] Go to the dead-end on the map at (E-10) in Davoi and examine the Disused Well to receive [KI] Knight's soul.
    - To get there, follow the river to the west from (J-10), and then head south after reaching (D-8).
    - Be mindful of Orcish Farkillers on the top of the ledge that can aggro even at level 99.
        - Pugils in the river will aggro to sound for characters near level 30, so take proper precautions depending on your level.
[5] Return to Balasiel to complete the quest.
