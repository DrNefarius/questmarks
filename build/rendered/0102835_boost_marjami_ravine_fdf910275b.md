## Walkthrough
[1] Travel to the frontier station in Marjami Ravine or Yorcia Weald and speak with the Station Worker.
[2] You will be presented with a list of various emotes from which you must choose one to cheer up the worker.
[3] To complete the assignment, the correct emote must be chosen while facing the Station Worker AND the direction listed below. You can utilize /point without having anything targeted to orient yourself.
    - It is far easier to line up facing the correct direction using first-person view. (V Key by default on Keyboard.) Locking onto the NPC can also help.
    - After each emote attempt, a dialogue will inform you whether the direction or emote was correct.
    - Directions are approximate; take a few steps to the left or right if the angle is incorrect.
[4] The correct emote/direction changes every 3 game hours, so it is possible to warp back and forth from the Mummers' Coalition and rapidly repeat this quest once you figure out the proper emote/direction.
