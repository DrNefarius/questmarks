## Walkthrough

thumb|right|200px|Caedarva Mire (Map 2) thumb|right|200px|Arrapago Reef (Map 5)
All members of your party (including any who have finished this mission before) need an Ephramadian gold coin to enter the battlefield during this mission. If you need another gold coin, examine Jazaraat's Headstone at Caedarva Mire (E-10).
[1] Head to the Cutter, which is located at (H-8) on [https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/File:Arrapago_Reef-map5.jpg Map 5] of Arrapago Reef. There are a few options for getting there:
    - The Arrapago Ring, purchased with 100 Imperial standing accolades, will teleport you in front of the Cutter. It will only work if you've been to the zone before but is the fastest route if true.
    - Take the Survival Guide to Caedarva Mire and run south to zone into Arrapago Reef at (G-8).
        - Note this is NOT the (G-6) zone to Arrapago Reef just east of where you teleport in from the Survival Guide
        - After zoning, head south to (H-8 on Map 5) to find the Cutter.
    - Grab an Imperial Silver Piece (or just 200 Standing), take the Runic Portal to Dvucca Isle Staging Point to Caedarva Mire (Map 2) and head West to the (F-9) entrance to Arrapago Reef. You'll have to pay the entrance fee. Once inside, head North to (H-8) to find the Cutter.
[2] Approach the Cutter for a cutscene.
[3] When you are ready, Examine the Cutter to enter a battlefield.
[4] After the fight, you will wind up in Nashmau.
    - Players who have already completed this mission will end up back at the Cutter instead.

## After the walkthrough: Battlefield

[1] Gessho will assist you in this fight. Trusts may also be summoned.
    - Gessho will wait a minute or two before he begins to fight. This gives your party a little time to buff.
    - The fight will continue even if Gessho is killed.
[2] The first phase of battle is against the Ashu Talif Crew, which consists of 2x Rangers, 2x Red Mages, and 1x Corsair.
    - The Ashu Talif Crew seem to be resistant to most forms of sleep unless Elemental Seal is used first.
    - Lullaby was easily landed in Item Level 119 equipment.
[3] Once the five Ashu Talif Crew members are defeated, another four will take their place.
[4] The Ashu Talif Captain will spawn along with the others after a moment.
[5] You must bring the Ashu Talif Captain's health to approximately 20% to win the battle.
