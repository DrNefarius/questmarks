## Walkthrough

This quest has several parts and is rather time-consuming.
[1] Speak with Gaddiux at the Inventors' Coalition.
    - Note:If you are working towards an Idris, you will need to complete or quit that weapon to begin working on Epeolatry. You will lose all materials deposited if you quit, but you will keep your Rusted handbell key item and can restart it immediately.
        - However, after completing any part after completing part 1, before trading your Lexeme Blade to Runje Desaali to begin the next part, any part of Saved by the Bell may be started and finished without having to restart either quest from the beginning.

[2] Obtain a Pickaxe, you will only need one.
    - This may be easily purchased from Tevigogo in Western Adoulin at (D-9) near the Pioneers Coalition.
    - Trailblazing Pickaxe also works.
[3] Head to (I-8) in Yorcia Weald and examine the ???.
    - From Bivouac #2 head north past the Colonization Reive and then east into the field of numbing blossoms.
        - There is more than one ??? in this location. If nothing happens, look around for the other ???.
    - There is a bush that may be blocking the path at (H-8). It opens from 21:00 to 03:00 game time.
[4] When your "Enlightened Endeavor" key item resonates, trade the Pickaxe to the ???.
    - You will obtain a Numbing blossom seed key item.
    - Your "Enlightened Endeavor" will resonate that something is hidden further down in the earth, continue digging until you obtain Broken rapier hilt key item.
    - If you are too slow you don't get the Key Item but can try again right away.
[5] Return to Gaddiux at the Inventors' Coalition.
[6] Speak with Runje Desaali at (J-11) in Eastern Adoulin. She will begin tracking materials deposited towards Epeolatry.

== Part 1 ==

[7] Trade 100 High-purity Bayld to Runje Desaali in Eastern Adoulin at (J-11).
    - This can be done cumulatively.
[8] You will receive the Vial of crimson catalyst key item.
[9] Speak with Octavien at (I-8) in Eastern Adoulin until he talks about the Vial of crimson catalyst.
[10] Travel to Rala Waterways (M-6) from Eastern Adoulin (F-7) and examine the door behind Yeggha Dolashi. Select "Quiescence" to enter a battlefield.
    - Must be on Runefencer to enter the fight.

Quiescence:
[11] There is a 15 minute time limit. Your subjob will be removed.
[12] You will fence against Zurko-Bazurko.
[13] Zurko will dual wield swords and have access to normal sword weapon skills as well as Savage Blade and Chant du Cygne.
[14] Casts Protect IV, Shell V, and Phalanx exclusively.
[15] Frequently parries which will limit your physical damage leaving Swipe and Lunge as effective options.
[16] Zurko-Bazurko will use Lunge which can do significant damage if no opposing runes are active.
    - Upon completion you will receive the key item "Quiescence"
[17] (Optional) Return to Octavien for a short dialogue directing you to the Order of Orvail's maester.
[18] Speak with Gaddiux in Western Adoulin (J-10) for a cutscene with Amchuchu and to receive your base weapon, Lexeme Blade I.
    - The quest is technically completed after this point, as the quest is moved to the completed log, however this has no bearing on finishing the weapon.

== Part 2 ==

[19] Trade your Lexeme Blade I to Runje Desaali
[20] Then trade her the following:
    - 200 Ghastly Stones
        - Note: Ghastly Stone +1s/+2s do not work for this quest. You can, however, trade them to Ornery Dhole for Obsidian Fragments. In return, you can buy NQ Ghastly Stones for 50 Obsidian Fragments each.
    - 500 High-Purity Bayld
    - 1 Gabbrath Horn
    - 1 Rockfin Fin
    - 1 Bztavian Wing
        - You can talk to Runje and peruse the option "View materials. (RUN)" to check your current turn-in progress.
[21] You will receive Superlative runic ring of deluge.
[22] Trade your Lexeme Blade I to the Runic Overflow at (K-10) in Foret de Hennetiel (Biv #2) to upgrade to Lexeme Blade II (DMG:228 PDT II -2%).

== Part 3 ==

[23] Trade your Lexeme Blade II to Runje Desaali
[24] Then trade her the following:
    - 200 Verdigris Stones
        - Note: Verdigris Stone +1s/+2s do not work for this quest. You can, however, trade them to Ornery Dhole for Obsidian Fragments. In return, you can buy NQ Verdigris Stones for 100 Obsidian Fragments each.
    - 2,500 High-Purity Bayld
    - 1 Yggdreant Root
    - 1 Waktza Rostrum
    - 1 Cehuetzi Claw
        - You can talk to Runje and peruse the option "View materials. (RUN)" to check your current turn-in progress.
[25] You will receive Superlative runic ring of luster.
[26] Trade your Lexeme Blade II to the Runic Overflow at (I-12) in Marjami Ravine (Biv #2) to upgrade to Lexeme Blade III (DMG:231 Enmity+10 PDT II -3%).

== Part 4 ==

[27] Trade your Lexeme Blade III to Runje Desaali
[28] Then trade her the following:
    - 200 Wailing Stones
        - Note: Wailing Stone +1s/+2s do not work for this quest. You can, however, trade them to Ornery Dhole for Obsidian Fragments. In return, you can buy NQ Wailing Stones for 200 Obsidian Fragments each.
    - 9,999 High-Purity Bayld
    - Pristine Yggrete Crystal
        - You can talk to Runje and peruse the option "View materials. (RUN)" to check your current turn-in progress.
[29] You will receive Superlative runic ring of vision.
[30] Trade your Lexeme Blade III to the Runic Overflow on top of the barrel in Western Adoulin at (I-10) to obtain your shiny new Epeolatry.
    - This is right beside the Delve NPC at Inventors Coalition.

== Dropped Item ==

If you have dropped any stage Lexeme Blade, you can re-aquire it at the stage that you are at by doing the following:
[31] Talk to Geosuke. You might need to talk to him multiple times.  Eventually, Runje Desaali will ask you if you accidentally dropped the weapon.
[32] Answer Yes
[33] Trade [unverified]1,000,000 Gil to Runje Desaali and she will craft you a new Lexeme Blade at the stage you were at. (Done with Lexeme Blade II, don't know if all versions cost the same amount of gil).
