## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Ratihb (J-12) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate to start this quest.
[2] Speak to Ekhu Pesshyadha (H-6). You can pick any option to proceed with this cutscene.
[3] Speak to Jijiroon (H-8) in Nashmau. You can pick any option again to proceed with this cutscene.
[4] Go up the stairs at (H-8) and zone into Caedarva Mire.
[5] Obtain a Lamian Fang Key from the ??? at (I-7) if possible, otherwise obtain it from Merrows inside Arrapago Reef.
[6] Travel to Arrapago Reef by way of the (I-6) entrance from Caedarva Mire.
[7] Go to (F-10) on Map 1 to change onto Map 2, follow the right wall straight north once on this map to find a ???.
[8] Click the ??? to see a cutscene.
[9] Click it again to spawns an Imp NM named Bukki.
[10] Defeat Bukki and check the ??? again to get the [KI] Bag of gold pieces and complete this part of the quest.
[11] Return to Aht Urhgan Whitegate and speak to Ratihb to complete this quest and receive your reward.
