## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Miageau, and he will have you run an errand for him.
[2] Trade 10 gil to Nouveil, who should be nearby Miageau, to receive a Blessed Waterskin.
[3] Head to the mouth of the river at (H-5) in East Ronfaure and find a target named the Cheval River.
[4] Trade the waterskin to it then return to Miageau, and trade the Cheval Water to him for you reward.
