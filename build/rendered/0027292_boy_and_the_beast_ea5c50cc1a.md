## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Raustigne (I-7) of Southern San d'Oria (S) to trigger a cutscene.
    - It appears that if the above cutscene isnt triggered, Rholont will not tell you anything new.
[2] Speak with Rholont (E-7) of Southern San d'Oria (S)
[3] Go to Vunkerl Inlet (S) (H-6) and touch the ??? near the bridge.
    - The quickest way there is to use the Survival Guide in the zone that you may have picked up on the way to San d'Oria your first time.
[4] You will receive a [KI]Vunkerl herb memo after the cutscene is finished.
    - On it is written the following:
        - 00:00~07:59 San d'Oria San d'Oria (Red)
        - 08:00~15:59 Bastok Bastok (Blue)
        - 16:00~23:59 Windurst Windurst (Green)
[5] Travel to Vunkerl Inlet (S) at the top left corner of (F-5) and look for a Leafy Patch. Touch it, and it will ask you which color corresponds with the current time of day as listed above.
    - Select the correct color from the list above to obtain a [KI]Vunkerl herb
        - Note: You will still receive the herb if you pick the wrong option.  However Excenmille will note that the herb is not working and will have you go fetch the right one.
[6] Bring the herb back to Vunkerl Inlet (S) (H-6) and touch the ??? near the bridge to complete quest.
