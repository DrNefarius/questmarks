## Walkthrough

[1] Sign up for "Smokescreen I (S)" under the category "Offensive Operations" with Rasdinice.
[2] To complete this operation, participate in any offense campaign battle in a territory under control of the Orcs.
[3] After the battle, return to Rasdinice to complete this Operation.
    Receiving 65 Experience Points and 68 Allied Notes from the Campaign is sufficient to complete this Campaign Op.
    If successful, you will the 110 Experience Points immediately after receiving the Exp/Notes from the Campaign.
