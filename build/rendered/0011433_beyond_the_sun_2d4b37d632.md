# Beyond the Sun

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Maat at (H-5) in Ru'Lude Gardens on each job. He will ask for a Testimony for each job and ask you to meet him at a specific BC area to fight.
[2] Defeat Maat with:
    - Bard
    - Beastmaster
    - Black Mage
    - Dark Knight
    - Dragoon
    - Monk
    - Ninja
    - Paladin
    - Ranger
    - Red Mage
    - Samurai
    - Summoner
    - Thief
    - Warrior
    - White Mage

[3] These fights are similar to the Shattering Stars fight, but there are no level caps.
    - The level required to fight Maat is at least level 66.
[4] To check which jobs that you have beaten Maat with, enter Feretory and talk to Teyrnon. Instincts associated with jobs on which you have defeated Maat will cost 5,000 Infamy instead of 10,000. However, if you have already bought an instinct at 10,000, it will not be listed again.
[5] Upon defeating Maat for the last time, speak with him to receive the cutscene and your new Maat's Cap.
