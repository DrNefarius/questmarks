## Walkthrough

[1] Obtain a Shell Shield either via Auction House or by farming it from Young Quadavs in North Gustaberg, South Gustaberg, Palborough Mines or Konschtat Highlands
[2] You will notice that only levels 7+ of the following jobs can equip the shield: WAR / RDM / PLD / BST / SAM
[3] Speak to Powhatan to start the quest. (Inside Steaming Sheep)
[4] Equip the shield.
[5] Speak to Bartolomeo in Port Bastok (F-7) (Downstairs Air Travel Agency, enter through Arrival's Exit)
[6] After the cutscene, return to Powhatan and complete quest.
