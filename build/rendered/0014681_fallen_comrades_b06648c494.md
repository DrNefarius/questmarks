## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Pavvke, who asks of you to bring back name tags.
[2] Silver Name Tags can be bought off auction houses.  Alternately, Onyx Quadav or Greater Quadav will drop them in Palborough Mines.
[3] Since the tags are rare, you can only turn one in at a time.
[4] Trade it to him for your reward.
