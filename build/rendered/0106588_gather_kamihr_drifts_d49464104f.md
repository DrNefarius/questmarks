## Walkthrough
[1] NPC asks you to obtain 3 Raaz Tusks.
    - The NPC specifically notes it does not matter how the player obtains it. It can be bought, traded, or farmed.
[2] Trade these to the NPC and then Report on the quest outcome.
[3] North of Bivouac #2 are several raazes right near the waypoint. Continuing northeast past the one-way drop, there are more at the junction that leads to Bivouac #1.
