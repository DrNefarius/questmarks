## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo.
[2] Travel to the Beastman Ensign at (I-8) (Map 1, NW corner of I-8, bordering H-8) in Fort Karugo-Narugo (S).
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ghoyu's Reverie.
    - You cannot summon Trusts inside.
[4] Two groups of three Yagudo (six total) will immediately start slowly running towards their designated ends.
    - Yagudo Carrier - occasionally runs away while fighting.
    - Yagudo Guard x2 - Links with the Yagudo Carrier, but does not run.
    - Should the Yagudo Carrier make it to the end of the area (which is smaller than the area in Pit Spider I (W)) the mission is failed.
[5] Once all six Yagudo are defeated, the objective is complete.
    - Exit at the Twilight Aureole to complete the mission. Do not warp out!
[6] Return to Emhi Tchaoryo to receive the Op rewards.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] If you run north immediately upon entering, you can catch and aggro both groups at the same time.
[2] The Yagudo are very weak and susceptible to Sleep and Gravity.
    - They have approximately 2,500 HP each.
