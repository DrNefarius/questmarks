## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Alienor in Western Adoulin (E-8) next to waypoint at Pioneers' Coalition to start the quest and receive [KI]Tray of Adoulinian delicacies.
[2] Go to Ru'Lude Gardens (G-9) and speak to Anastase to receive [KI]Small bag of Adoulinian delicacies.
[3] Anastase will ask you to check up on one of his subordinates, chosen at random:
    - Jillia - Selbina (G-10)
    - Zurko-Bazurko - Mhaura (I-8)
        - You may have to talk to him more than once for him to take the key item from you.
    - Quwi Orihbhe - Rabao (G-8)
    - Wistful Bison - Norg (G-7)
[4] Return to Anastase and speak with him again.
[5] Return to Alienor to complete quest.
[6] Questline is repeatable after every conquest tally.
