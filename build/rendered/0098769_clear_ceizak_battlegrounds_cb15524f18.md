## Walkthrough
[1] Participate in one Colonization Reive through to completion in either Ceizak Battlegrounds or Yahse Hunting Grounds.

[2] Applicable Colonization Reives can be found at the following locations:

        - (F/G-7): Floodplain Spider
        - (G-8): Lightfoot Lapinion
        - (G-9): Lavender Twitherym
        - (I-8): Bounding Chapuli
    - Yahse Hunting Grounds
        - (H-7): Temblor Beetle
        - (H-8): Lancing Wasp
        - (J-8): Lancing Wasp
        - (G-9): Quivering Twitherym
        - (I-9): Shrubshredder Chapuli
