## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Estelle (F-9) in Eastern Adoulin behind the Scout Coalition building. You will receive Margret's writ of summons.
[2] Check the ??? in the crates in the alcove next to the base of the ramp at (I-9) for a cutscene. You will receive Portrait of Melvien
    - The quest will appear in your quest log at this point.
[3] Talk to Chumli-Mojumli (K-10) and ask for Estienneux.
    - The Castle Adoulin gates waypoint is closest.
    - Select "Tell me about Melvien when he was alive" followed by "Show him the portrait of Melvien." You will receive Melvien's turn.
[4] Talk to Mastan (G-6) in Western Adoulin.
    - The Platea Triumphus waypoint is closest. Go north and then make a left before the ramp down.
    - Select "Tell me about Soufrriand's death." followed by "Show him the portrait of Melvien. Then select "I believe you.". You will receive Melvien's death?.
[5] Return to the crate at Eastern Adoulin (I-9) for a cutscene. You will receive Margret's memo.
[6] Go to Rala Waterways (H-6) and check the Storage Container for a cutscene.
    - The nearest entrance is at Western Adoulin (Big Bridge K-8).
[7] You have to answer 2 questions right in the cutscene to continue, the answers are:
[8] Arciela.
[9] Morimar.
[10] Return to the crate at Eastern Adoulin (I-9) for a cutscene.
    - Select "Show your Ring of supernal disjunction".
[11] Talk to Estelle (F-9) for your reward.
