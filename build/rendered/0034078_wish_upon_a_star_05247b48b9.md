## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Zacc (G-9) who is standing next to Enu (G-9).
[2] Speak with Malene (I-5), more popularly known as the Steam Clock/Bubbly Bernie quest giver.
[3] Return to the fountain and speak with Enu.
    - The quest will now appear in your log.
[4] Acquire a  Hatchet from the Auction House, NPC stores, or crafting.
[5] Travel to Yuhtunga Jungle and locate a Logging Point. Start logging and you will receive a  Fallen Star.
    - Yuhtunga Jungle is on Elshimo Island, accessed via airship with [KI] Kazham Airship Pass or transport to Norg via NPCs in Mhaura or Selbina (requires RoV 1-5 or later).
[6] Return to Enu and trade her the star on a clear starry night (Clear weather, with stars visible in the sky) to complete the quest.
    - Attempts during other weather conditions, including Clear weather but with overcast (clouds), will cause Enu to say "It has to be a starry night for the fallen star to work. We have to wait a little bit longer."
