## Walkthrough

[1] To begin this quest you first need a Damp Scroll.
    - The Damp Scroll can either be fished up in Sea Serpent Grotto or purchased off the Auction House.
[2] You need to have the Damp Scroll in your inventory when you speak to Shivivi to begin this quest.
[3] Take the scroll to Horlais Peak (Hot Springs side) and trade it to the Hot Springs there.
[4] This will transform the scroll into a Jubaku: Ichi (Scroll).
