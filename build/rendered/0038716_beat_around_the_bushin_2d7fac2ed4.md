## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Vola to begin this quest with Monk as your main job.
[2] Exit the Tenshodo through the Door: "Neptune's Spire" to get a cutscene with Atori-Tutori.
[3] Obtain a Wyrm Beard from Nidhogg or Early Bird Catches the Wyrm.
    - Trade the Wyrm Beard to the Door: "Neptune's Spire" for a cutscene.
[4] Speak to Degenhard, Bastok Markets (I-10).
[5] Obtain a Behemoth Tongue from King Behemoth or Horns of War.
    - Trade the Behemoth Tongue to the Door: "Neptune's Spire" for another cutscene.
[6] Speak to Maat, Ru'Lude Gardens (H-5).
    - Obtain an Adamantoise Egg from Aspidochelone or The Hills Are Alive.
    - Trade the Adamantoise Egg to the Door: "Neptune's Spire" for a cutscene.
[7] Finally, trade a Brown Belt to the same door for the final cutscene and a Black Belt.
    - The Brown Belt is lost when trading it to the door.
