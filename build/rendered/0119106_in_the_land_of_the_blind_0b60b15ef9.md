## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Behsa Alehgo in Eastern Adoulin (J-7).
[2] Enter Celennia Memorial Library for a cutscene.
[3] Go to Yorcia Weald and check the Suspicious Place in the large grove of Numbing Blossoms on the south side of (I-8) for a cutscene.
    - Either head northwest from Bivouac #2 and then zigzag north, or zigzag south from Bivouac #1.
[4] Click the Suspicious Place again to spawn Jovial Ahriman.
    - Mob has about 70,000 HP.
    - Be wary of the Numbing Blossoms nearby as they will paralyze you.
    - Uses standard Ahriman moves, along with Cruel Joke (20 tic), at 25% or so.
    - Casts Kaustra, Dread Spikes, Frazzle 3, Endark.
[5] After defeating Jovial Ahriman, check the Suspicious Place a third time for a cutscene.
[6] Head to the Pool of Clarity Ergon Locus at (G-6) (North west of Bivouac #1) and click on the Pellucid Afflusion for a final cutscene and your reward.
