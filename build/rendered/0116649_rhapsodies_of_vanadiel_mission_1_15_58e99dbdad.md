    - Players without the spell Escape can instead take a short walk to the exit.
[2] If you are starting from Norg, the quickest means to reach the ??? is renting a Chocobo from Marilleune (H-9) in Norg, near the exit to Sea Serpent Grotto and ride to the ???.
