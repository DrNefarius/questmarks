## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Glenne (A) at (L-11), Veridical Conflux #06.
[2] She will give you a [KI] Rainbow-colored linkpearl.
[3] Locate and speak to Aaveleon (A). He has a fairly large roaming pattern that includes most of Abyssea - La Theine.
    - Wide scan greatly aids in tracking him down.
    - Roaming path seems to be along the main roads, but he may also wander into the valleys.
[4] Return to Glenne (A) to complete the quest.
