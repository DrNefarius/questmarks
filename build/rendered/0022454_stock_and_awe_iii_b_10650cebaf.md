## Walkthrough

[1] Solo mission
[2] Speak with the Quartermaster at (H-9).
[3] She will ask you to travel to Grauberg (S) to harvest supplies required by the Republic. The following are possible options:
    :*Lesser Chigoe x1
    :*Red Moko Grass x1
    :*Burdock Root x1
[4] When you successfully harvest the item that she requests, you will receive a Supply order key item.
