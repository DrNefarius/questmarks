## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Glenne to receive an  Ointment.
[2] Zone to West Ronfaure and look for Aaveleon at (G-6).
[3] Trade the ointment to him, and he'll give back an Ointment Case.
[4] Return to Glenne and trade her the Ointment Case to complete the quest and to receive your reward.
