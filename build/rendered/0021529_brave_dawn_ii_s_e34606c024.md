## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Rasdinice.
[2] Travel to the Allied Ensign in East Ronfaure (S) at (G-5) (hug the left wall as soon as you come out).
    - Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Everbloom Hollow.
[3] Run forward to encounter a Soldier, who immediately runs towards a Trained Bat and begins attacking.
    - During this time, you will see messages that display how much attention he is giving to the fight:
        - The soldier seems unfocused...
        - The soldier has begun training harder!
        - The soldier is fighting with vigor!
[4] Once the Trained Bat is defeated, the Mission Objective is complete.
    - If the soldier never gains focus, you lose the mission.
[5] Return to Rasdinice for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] The soldier will eventually die if left completely alone.
    - The Soldier can also cure himself, and will do so reliably when falling under 70% HP.
[2] The Trained Bat lasts a long while, and seems to shift hate very often.
