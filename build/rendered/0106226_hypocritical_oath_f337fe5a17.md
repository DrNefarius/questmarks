## Walkthrough

[1] You must wait a game day after completing Do Not Go Into the Light before flagging this quest.
[2] Click on the door to Svenja's Manor at (I-8) in Western Adoulin.
[3] Speak to Wegellion in the back of the Scout's Coalition in Eastern Adoulin.
    - In the cutscene, Margret asks for a Frost Turnip and a Slime Oil.
        - Frost Turnip can be farmed from rabbits in Kamihr Drifts or Uleguerand Range, or can be bought from your Green Thumb Moogle if you've been working with your Mog Garden and received the White rabbit memento from Monster Rearing.
        - Similarly, Slime Oil can also be bought from the Green Thumb Moogle if you have the Slime memento from Monster Rearing.
[4] Trade the items to Wegellion for a cutscene.
[5] Return to Svenja's Manor for a cutscene, and to complete the quest.
