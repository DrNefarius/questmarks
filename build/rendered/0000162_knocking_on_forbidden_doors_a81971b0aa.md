## Walkthrough

Note: You must wait up to 1 minute after the completion of the previous quest.
[1] Talk with Enaremand (J-7).
[2] Talk to Chemioue (J-6).
    - If you haven't done Petals for Parelbriaux, and are at Promathia Mission 3-5 or later, you may get that cut-scene first.
[3] Go to Phomiuna Aqueducts, find a Wooden Ladder at C-9 on the second map, and climb the ladder for a cut-scene.
[4] Go to Misareaux Coast and select the ??? at I-6 near the end of the river for the [KI]Mire incense.
[5] Go to the base of the waterfall at F-4 in Misareaux Coast and select the ??? for a cut-scene.
[6] Select the ??? again to spawn the mannequin NM Alsha.
    - The NM can cast Silence, Paralyze, and will occasionally cast Cure III on you.
    - Once she is reduced to around 20%, and Alsha will repeatedly cast Fire II on herself until she dies.
        - If she kills herself, you will have to rezone and try to kill her again; you must deal the final blow to defeat her.
[7] Once Alsha is defeated, select the ??? again to obtain [KI]Better Humes and Mannequins.
[8] Travel to Mhaura and talk to Fyi Chalmwoh who will take the key item then allow you to pose your mannequins for 200 Gil.
