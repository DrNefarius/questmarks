## Walkthrough

[1] Travel to Sea Serpent Grotto and examine the Shady Sconce at (C-5) for a cutscene. You may need to do this twice to make sure it registers.
    - Norg is the closest teleport.
[2] Use a Sahagin Key to enter the Ornamental Door at (J-11) in Sea Serpent Grotto.
    - only one member of a party needs the Sahagin Key. It opens the door for everyone.
[3] Continue to Sea Serpent Grotto map 5, then examine the Waterfall Basin at (H-6) for a cutscene.
[4] Return to the Inconspicuous Door in Upper Jeuno (H-8) (across from Durable Shields).
