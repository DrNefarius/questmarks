## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Clautaire (I-12) Western Adoulin.
[2] Harvest the Mineral Vein in your Mog Garden to obtain Hunk of bedrock.
    - If you have already exhausted your Mineral Vein, you must wait until you are able to mine again before being able to obtain the Hunk of bedrock.
[3] Return to Clautaire for your reward.

---

[4] Note: If this is your first time entering the Mog Garden, you need to speak with the Green Thumb Moogle for a few introductory tasks, starting with Full Fields.
    - You will receive the Hunk of bedrock regardless if this is the first time you are visiting your Mog Garden
