## Walkthrough

[1] First head to Rabao and talk to Rudolfo (J-7).
[2] Talk to Zoriboh (F-6).
[3] Head to Norg and talk to Sohyon (J-8).
[4] Then talk to Washu to receive the Key Item: [KI] Washu's flask.
[5] Head to Korroloka Tunnel. You will need to fill the flask by interacting with four different Giant Clam targets.
    - The first Giant Clam can be found at F-10 on Map 1 as you zone into Korroloka Tunnel from Zeruhn Mines.
    - The second Giant Clam can be found at K-6 on Map 3.
    - The third Giant Clam can be found at G-11 on Map 5.
    - The final Giant Clam can be found at I-10 on Map 2.
    - After examining all four Giant Clams, your [KI] Washu's flask will turn into a [KI] Flask of clam water.
[6] Head to Norg and talk to Sohyon (J-8) to receive the [KI] Storeroom key.
[7] Talk to Gimb (H-9).
[8] Head to Port Bastok and talk to Kagetora (F-6).
[9] Trade 5x Eastern Gems to Patient Wheel (F-5).
    - These drop from Riparian Sahagin behind the silver door in Sea Serpent Grotto.
[10] Head to Selbina and talk to Abelard at G-9).
[11] Use the Swirling Vortex in Valkurm Dunes in the cave at (I-9) to Lufaise Meadows.
[12] You will get a cut scene when you zone into Lufaise Meadows.
[13] Head to Rabao, talk to Zoriboh (F-6).
