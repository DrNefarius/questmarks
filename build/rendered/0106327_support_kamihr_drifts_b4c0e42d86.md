# Support: Kamihr Drifts

## Walkthrough
[1] Upon accept this assignment, the Task Delegator will provide you with a Kam. FB op. materials container to deliver to any frontline bivouac in Kamihr Drifts.
    - As of Feb 18, 2014 update, Waypoints can be used to complete this task. Kam. FB op. materials container will not break.
    - If you cancel the task, you will have your Imprimatur(s) refunded. No Imprimatur(s) are lost.

[2] Bring the Kam. FB op. materials container to any Bivouac Administrator in the designated area(s) to complete the assignment.
    - Bivouac Administrator #1: (H-9)
    - Bivouac Administrator #2: (H-10)
    - Bivouac Administrator #3: (H-7)
    - Bivouac Administrator #4: (G-6)
