## Walkthrough

During this Mission, you will have to examine Seed Fragments in each zone to receive a level cap status (Level 30). While under the effects of this level cap, you will be able to collect Key Items by touching Seed Afterglows.
[1] You will not be able to be cured by people who are not under the level cap status, but you will also not lose Experience Points should you die.
    - Trust Magic is not available during this level restriction effect.
[2] PRO-TIP: Use Destrier Beret to trivialize this Mission if you obtained one. The Auto-Reraise and Movement Speed will be applied under the level cap status.
[3] It is recommended to utilize Sprinter's Shoes for their movement speed buff (18%). You will need to re-apply the buff after you examine the Seed Fragments and receive the level cap when you zone into Middle and Upper Delkfutt's Towers.
[4] Prism Powder s and Silent Oil s are recommended if you solo this, as there can be monsters that aggro by Magic around such as Dolls and Elementals.
    - Seed Fragments tend to be surrounded by monsters that aggro by Sight in most cases, so you'll be forced to drop your invisibility next to the Seed Fragment as soon as they are all facing away from you, examine it, and become invisible again as quickly as possible.
