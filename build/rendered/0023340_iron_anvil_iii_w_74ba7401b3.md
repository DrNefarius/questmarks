## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Emhi Tchaoryo, and you'll be forwarded to Adjutant at (F-9).
[2] Talk to Adjutant, and he'll ask you for a craft to specialize in.
[3] Once you've picked a craft, he'll test you on two synths made with said craft.

    - What is the first ingredient in this synth? [List]

    - What is the seventh ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the eighth ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - (NOTE) "No more materials required" is often the last item in the ingredient list.
    - (NOTE) This listing is far from complete. It's been discovered that on this (unconfirmed on prior Iron Anvils) that previous, lower level items are used as well, which could lead to an extensive and hard to manage list. It's suggested that when you get the item, have your browser window open and ready to search for the item.
[4] Once you've completed your two tests, go back and talk to Emhi Tchaoryo for your reward.
