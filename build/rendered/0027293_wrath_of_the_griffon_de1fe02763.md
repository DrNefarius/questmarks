## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Rholont (E-7) in Southern San d'Oria (S) to begin the quest.
[2] Head to (H-10) in Jugner Forest (S) (at the foot of one of the watch platforms), to find a ???.
    - Survival Guide teleport to Jugner Forest (S) is the fastest way. (If you do not have it, grab it on this mission as you will need it in another WotG mission very soon)
    - You may want to grab the Recall-Jugner KI too.
[3] After the cutscene, prepare yourself for a NM fight.
[4] Touch the ??? again to pop Cobraclaw Buchzvotch.
[5] After defeating Cobraclaw Buchzvotch, click the ??? again for another cutscene.
[6] Return to Rholont to complete the quest and obtain a [KI]Military Scrip.
    - Wyatt (L-6) in Southern San d'Oria (S) will exchange your reward for 20,147 gil.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Cobraclaw Buchzvotch soloable by 75RDM/NIN.  Was able to land Sleep, Gravity and Bind with ease.
[2] Incredibly easy fight with 75 RDM, 75 MNK/NIN.
[3] The NM is decent challenge at level 60 with Trusts.
[4] Trivial fight with 99DRG/SAM, dealt practically no damage, NM went down in seconds.

If you are working on missions then return to Cait Sith (Mission).
