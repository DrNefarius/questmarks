## Walkthrough

Note: This mission can be done easily solo. Campaign enemies at the objective do not aggro if you don't have Allied Tags.
[1] After confirming the mission with Emhi Tchaoryo, go to a Beastmen controlled area near Windurst.
[2] Interact with a ??? near the fortification usually targeted as the campaign battle objective.
    - List of ???'s:
        - West Sarutabaruta (S): (H-6)
        - Fort Karugo-Narugo (S): (H-8)
        - Meriphataud Mountains (S): (E-5)
[3] A bomb will appear and explode after some time.
[4] Interact with the ??? once again to earn the XP reward.
    - Failing to do so will cause the Op to be a failure.
        - You can confirm the objective is complete if the log says "The stronghold has sustained considerable damage.".
[5] Return to Emhi Tchaoryo to finish the mission and get your Allied Notes reward.
