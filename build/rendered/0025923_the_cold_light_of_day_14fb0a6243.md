## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Malene to begin quest.
[2] Obtain a Steam Clock via the following methods:
    - Purchase via Auction House
    - Defeat Bubbly Bernie. To spawn Bubbly Bernie, acquire a Quus (fish) either via Auction House or by fishing. Travel to the light house in South Gustaberg (M-10) and look for a ???. Trade the Quus to the ??? and Bubbly Bernie will pop in to say hi. Proceed to maul him, once defeated you may loot his corpse for a Steam Clock.
[3] Return to town and trade the clock to Malene to complete quest.
