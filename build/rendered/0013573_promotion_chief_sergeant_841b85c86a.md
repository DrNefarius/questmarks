# Promotion: Chief Sergeant

The game knows this quest as: Promotion: Chief Sergeant   (quest/toau #96)

## Quest Header
Start:      Abquhbah - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (1-10)
Description: Abquhbah has asked you to visit the weapon shop in his stead. His heavy sighs have you slightly worried...
Fame:       AU
FLevel:     1
Quest Reqs: Complete 25 points worth of Assaults as Sergeant Major
Item Reqs:  Scourshroom
Level:      (empty)
Repeatable: (empty)
Previous:   Promotion: Sergeant Major
Next:       Promotion: Second Lieutenant
Title:      Chief Sergeant
Reward:     
            - CS Wildcat badge
            - Access to CS Assaults
            - 1 Imperial Mythril Piece, and possibly 1 Imperial Gold Piece as well.
Expansion:  ToAU

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Abquhbah to initiate the quest.
[2] He will redirect you to Hagakoff at (K-10) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate
[3] Talk to Abquhbah again.
[4] Talk to Hagakoff again.
[5] Talk to Abquhbah again.
[6] Enter Aydeewa Subterrane from Wajaom Woodlands (I-10).
    - This is the same entrance next to the Aydeewa Subterrane Survival Guide.
[7] Head to the room at (I-9) in Aydeewa Subterrane.
    - Check the mushroom patch at the corner of (I-9)/(J-8) to receive a mature scourshroom.
    - Check the mushroom patch along the edge of (I-9)/(J-9) to plant the mature scourshroom.
[8] Repeat the same process but at (E-7). Ensure that you have planted mature scourshrooms in both spots.
[9] Zone and wait for the game day to change.
[10] Return to both patches of mushrooms between 04:00 and 06:00 game-time to harvest young Scourshroom.
    - You will need at least 2 young Scourshrooms to complete the quest.
[11] Return to Abquhbah to help clean the weapon.
[12] After cleaning the weapon twice, you may choose to complete the quest or continue cleaning.
    - Cleaning 5 times will add 1 Imperial Gold Piece to your reward.
