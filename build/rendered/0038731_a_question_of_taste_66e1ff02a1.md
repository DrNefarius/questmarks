## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Etteh Sulaej J-9.  You will receive the [KI] Letter to Angelica.
[2] Take the letter to Windurst Waters, and talk to Angelica at the Rarab Tail Hostelry. Then you will receive [KI] Angelica's letter and a painting [KI] "Final Fantasy" that she wants you to hang in Temple of Uggalepih.
[3] Go back to Kazham, talk to Etteh Sulaej.
[4] Go to (G-8) on the first map for the Temple of Uggalepih, and hang the picture at Stone Picture Frame (it is on the south wall of the Paintbrush Room) to spawn the Golem NM, Trompe L'Oeil .
    - Temple of Uggalepih Survival Guide warp puts you a short distance from the Stone Frame.
[5] Defeat the NM, then check the frame and you will obtain a [KI] Ripped "Final Fantasy" painting.
    - If in a party, there is a 10 minute wait between being able to respawn the NM.
[6] Return to Etteh Sulaej. As a reward, you will receive 3000 Gil.

== Repeating the Quest ==

You can repeat the quest. The only difference is when you speak to Angelica, you will instead receive the "Final Fantasy Part II" key item rather than the original. The rest of the steps are the same.
