## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Horatius inside of the Trader's Home at (I-9) in Bastok Markets, who sends you to Dangruf Wadi.
    - The monsters in the area around the ??? are level 90-95. Only the Goblins are aggressive and they can be avoided with just Invisible since they do not have pets.
        - You will lose Invisible when riding the geyser (I-8) at Dangruf Wadi, so Field Support from the Grounds Tome will fail. Refer to Invisible (Status) for other options.

[2] Inside Dangruf Wadi, head to the (I-5)/(J-5) boundary, where you'll find a ??? during sunny weather.
    - Sunny weather is the default weather with no icon. If there is a weather at the time you're attempting this quest, you will have to wait for it to clear up to find the ???.
    - You receive the  Dangruf Stone after choosing the dialog option to check the area.

[3] Head back to Horatius for your reward of 400g.
