## Walkthrough

[1] To begin this quest in your log, you need the Serpent Rumors key item.  It can be purchased from Fennella in Port Windurst (C-8) for 95,000 Guild Points with Fishing rank Adept or higher.
    - You can still obtain the other items needed for this quest before initiating it.
[2] Turn in Liks and Gugrusaurus to Zaldon to obtain Opal Silk and Saber Shoot, respectively.
    - About a ~.5% chance to get each.
[3] Speak to Irmilant in Rabao (G-7).
[4] Trade the Opal Silk and Saber Shoot to Irmilant.
[5] Return to Irmilant after conquest tally to receive your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] The Ebisu Fishing Rod can be re-obtained if dropped by mistake.
    - You'll need to speak with Irmilant who will request you trade him a Takitaro.
