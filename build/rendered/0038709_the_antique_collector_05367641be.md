## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Imasuke to begin this quest.
[2] Head to Qufim Island and kill Dancing Weapons until you obtain a Kaiser Sword.
    - Kaiser Swords can also sometimes be found at the Auction House.
[3] Trade the Kaiser Sword to Imasuke to complete this quest.
