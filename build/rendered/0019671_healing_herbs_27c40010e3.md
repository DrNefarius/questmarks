## Walkthrough

[1] Begin the quest by checking the Rhinostery door in Windurst Waters (S) at (J-8) second map. There will be cutscene dialogue between Dantie-Corantie and Maju-Naju.
    - There are two Rhinostery doors, the one on the left activates this quest.
[2] Go to the present Windurst Waters and approach the door of the Rhinostery for a cutscene. You will be given a [KI] "The Healing Herb".
[3] Go back to the past Rhinostery and check the door for another cutscene.
[4] Go to Grauberg (S) and touch the ??? located near center of (H-13) for a [KI] Fireblossom.
[5] Go back to Windurst Waters (S) and check the Rhinostery door once again for a cutscene.
[6] Wait until the next game day and check the Rhinostery door once again for a cutscene.
[7] Wait once again for the next game day. Check the Rhinostery door once more for a final cutscene and your reward, in form of a Tincture, as well as the [KI] "The Healing Herb" once again.
[8] Go back to the present Windurst Waters and check the Rhinostery door for an extra cut scene. The [KI] "The Healing Herb" will be taken from you.
