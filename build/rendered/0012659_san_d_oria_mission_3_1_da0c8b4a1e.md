## Walkthrough

[1] This Mission is Required.
    Trade enough Crystals (1+) to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission.
[2] Accept the mission Infiltrate Davoi from the Gate Guard.
[3] Speak to Prince Trion who can be reached by clicking on the Door: Prince Royal's Rm at (G/H-7) of Chateau d'Oraguille for a cutscene. It is the room on the North side of the court yard.
[4] Zone into Davoi for a cutscene.
    - Invisible is recommended for this Mission, bring Prism Powder Prism Powders or have a spell for Invisiblity.
    - Follow the right wall of Jugner Forest to the zone into Davoi at (G-12).
    - A map of Davoi can be purchased for 3,000 Gil from Elesca at I-8 of Northern San d'Oria or Violitte at G-10 of Southern San d'Oria.
[5] Speak with Quemaricond, patrolling around (H-7), when it is safe to drop Invisibility. You will obtain [KI] Royal Knights' Davoi report.
[6] Return to Prince Trion to complete the Mission.

## After the walkthrough: Repeating the Mission

[1] If you have already completed this Mission and wish to repeat it, your objective will be different:
[2] Talk to any Gate Guard and accept the Mission.
    - There are two in Southern San d'Oria, Ambrotien at (K-6) and Endracion at (F-9). There is also a gate guard in Northern San d'Oria, Grilau at (D-8).
[3] Make your way to Davoi. Zone into Jugner Forest from La Theine Plateau at (M-8). Then follow the right wall of Jugner Forest to the zone into Davoi at (G-12).
[4] When you enter Davoi, speak with Zantaviat the NPC near the tree.
[5] You will need to find 3 targetable ! marks and check them.
    - The first one is at the north side of (K-7) to obtain [KI] East Block code.
    - The second is at (L-8) to obtain [KI] South Block code.
    - The third is at the north-west corner of (J-7) to obtain [KI] North Block code.
[6] Return to Zantaviat then return to the Gate Guard.
