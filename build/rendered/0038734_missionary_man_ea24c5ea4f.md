## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Rauteinot who asks for a slab of Elshimo Marble.
    - Elshimo Marble drops off Ivory Lizard in Yuhtunga Jungle.
[2] Trade the Elshimo Marble to Rauteinot. You will receive Rauteinot's parcel.
[3] Head to Northern San d'Oria and speak to Mulaujeant at (E-5) near the Blacksmithing guild on the second floor.
[4] Wait until one Earth minute has passed and speak to Mulaujeant again to be given the [KI] Sublime statue of the Goddess.
[5] Return to Rauteinot for your reward.
