## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Edigey to begin this quest.
[2] Desert Venom drops off Doom Scorpions in Eastern Altepa Desert, Tulwar Scorpions in Western Altepa Desert, or purchased off the Auction House.
[3] Trade the Desert Venom to Edigey for your reward.
    - The first time you complete this quest you will get a Dotanuki.  Each time after you will only get gil.
