## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Nantoto at H-8 in Lower Jeuno after you have completed 100 unique Records of Eminence quests.
[2] She asks you to kill shadows in The Eldieme Necropolis.
[3] Kill 10 Formor family monsters in The Eldieme Necropolis.
    - You must first activate the Records of Eminence quest under Other -> RoE Quests -> Culling The Darkness to get credit.
[4] When you kill the last shadow you will complete the Records of Eminence quest and receive 1500 sparks and 2500 exp.
[5] Report back to Nantoto for a short cutscene to complete the quest and obtain the 5 Copper A.M.A.N Vouchers.
