## Walkthrough

[1] Travel to the Fortilace in Rolanberry Fields (S) (D-9).
[2] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Ruhotz Silvermines.
[3] 3 parties of 3 Quadavs will be roaming the area.
    - They have low HP and are vulnerable to Sleep.
[4] You must defeat them before they defeat the friendly NPCs.
[5] Exit via the Dawn Aureola.
    - Skipping this step will result in failure. Do not Warp out!
[6] Return and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.
