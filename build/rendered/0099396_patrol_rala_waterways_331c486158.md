## Walkthrough
[1] Defeat 5 Rustwater Toads and/or Spoutdrenched Toads in Rala Waterways. You do not need to be in EXP range as long as you are in Rala Waterways when your party/alliance defeats the monsters.
    - The closest entrance for the frogs is from Western Adoulin, southeast of the Mog House (The frogs are the southwest corner of Rala Waterways)
    - AOEing them will only give you credit for one kill (I used Thunderspark and killed 4, only got credit for one)
