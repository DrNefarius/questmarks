## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Yaya (H-9).
[2] Speak to Oswald (I-9) inside building by Nomad Moogles, Shepherd's Muster.
[3] Speak to Jimaida (H-9).
    - The quest is now active in your log.
[4] Speak to Zaldon (H-9).
[5] Fish up a  Fat Greedie in Selbina.
[6] Trade Zaldon the  Fat Greedie
    - You'll have to keep fishing up a single Fat Greedie and trading it to Zaldon until he gives you the Temporary Key Item: Etched ring.
[7] Return to Oswald for your reward.
