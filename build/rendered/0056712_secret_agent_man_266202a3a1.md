## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Helga (A) at (G-5), southeast of Veridical Conflux #06 to begin the quest.
[2] Speak to Naji (A) at (J-5). He will give you [KI] Naji's gauger plate.
    - To get here the quickest, use Veridical Conflux #08, then head southwest while hugging the left cliff.
        - If you pass the KS-08 Martello, you have gone too far.
    - Naji will appear as if he has Invisible (Status) cast on him. His name does show, and he is also on Widescan.
[3] Return and speak to Helga (A) to complete the quest. You will hand over [KI] Naji's gauger plate to her.
