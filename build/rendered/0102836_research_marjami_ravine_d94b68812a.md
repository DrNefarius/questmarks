## Walkthrough
[1] Fight Tulfaire in Marjami Ravine until the Molting Plumage special attack is used.
    - Note:The Trembling Tulfaire found in Lair Reives do not count towards this assignment.
    - The desired ability is AoE, but in a very narrow line. Stand behind the tank to receive credit on multiple characters.
[2] There are Tulfaires located directly southwest of Bivouac #4 and south of Bivouac #1.
[3] After being hit with Molting Plumage, the assignment will be complete.
