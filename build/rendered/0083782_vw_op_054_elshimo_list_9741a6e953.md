## Walkthrough

[1] After starting Voidwatch Ops: Border Crossing and receiving the [KI] Ashen stratum abyssite, speak to Hildegard in Kazham to begin the Quest.
[2] Engage in and complete the 3 Tier I Voidwatch battles in the Elshimo region:
    - Holy Moly - Yuhtunga Jungle - Rift Locations: (F-11), (G-6), (J-7).
    - Neith - Temple of Uggalepih - Rift Locations: Map 1: (I-9), (K-6) Map 2: (G-8).
    - Ildebrann - Ifrit's Cauldron - Rift Locations: Map 3: (F-9), Map 5: (F-9), (I-6).
        - Closest teleport is Unity Warp 125 to Map 5.
[3] Return to Hildegard to complete the Quest.
