## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to your Green Thumb Moogle to flag this quest
[2] The Green Thumb Moogle asks for a rare and beautiful shell that might wash up on your beach.
[3] Go to your Coastal Fishing Net and raise the net.
[4] You will receive a [KI] Kaleidoscopic clam.
[5] Go back and speak with your Green Thumb Moogle.
[6] You will be rewarded with  Flask of Grow-M-Good.
