## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Sharuru in Eastern Adoulin (F-8) to start the quest and receive a Waypoint scanner kit Key Item.
[2] Adjust the waypoints at the Frontier Stations in Ceizak Battlegrounds, Yahse Hunting Grounds, Foret de Hennetiel, Morimar Basalt Fields, Yorcia Weald, Marjami Ravine, and Kamihr Drifts.
[3] Attempt the waypoint in Lower Jeuno to find that it cannot be adjusted yet.
[4] Return to Sharuru.
    - Use the Lower Jeuno waypoint to travel directly to her.
[5] Head to Shipilolo outside the Inventors' Coalition (Western Adoulin (J-9)) to receive a Waypoint recalibration kit.
[6] Return to Lower Jeuno waypoint, and calibrate it.
[7] Go back to Sharuru to complete the quest.
