## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Fyi Chalmwoh in Mhaura at (G-8) to begin the quest, then speak to her again.
[2] Speak to Ramona in Selbina at (H-9) to receive [KI] Ye Olde Mannequin Catalogue.
[3] Speak to Cheupirudaux in Northern San d'Oria HP #4 at (F-3) to receive [KI] Mannequin joint diagrams.
[4] Gather all 5 Mannequin pieces and trade them to Fyi Chalmwoh. These can be also be bought off player's bazaars:
    - Mannequin Body
    - Mannequin Feet
    - Mannequin Hands
    - Mannequin Head
    - Mannequin Legs
[5] Zone and speak to Fyi Chalmwoh again to receive your reward.
