## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Gumbah in Bastok Mines HP #3 (J-7).
    - From the Home Point #3, take the stairs mid-ways on the right, go up then bust a left, cross the bridge on your right and make an immediate right, open the door and Gumbah will be in the back room. This NPC is NOT sitting next to the Home Point, instead they're in the back room of a home.
[2] Speak to Brygid in Bastok Markets HP #4 (K-9) twice.
[3] After agreeing to assist her, Brygid will instruct you to select one of a list of NPCs to cosplay for her fashion show's contest.

## After the walkthrough: NPC Outfits

You are to attempt to dress like your selected NPC exactly, ignoring any weapons and focusing on head/body/hands/legs/feet. The list includes:
[1] Azima:
    - Azima is impossible to copy. Her present-day clothing is completely unobtainable for players.
        - If attempting to copy her past style from the Shadowreign era however, she was wearing a Witch Hat Witch Hat, Marduk's Jubbah Marduk's Jubbah, Marduk's Dastanas Marduk's Dastanas, Marduk's Shalwar Marduk's Shalwar, and Sorcerer's Sabots Sorcerer's Sabots.
    - Cosplaying as Azima from the past will win you first prize, netting you an extra 2,000 Gil.
    - A /lockstyle combination of Coven Hat Coven Hat, Marduk's Jubbah Marduk's Jubbah, Mdk. Dastanas +1 Mdk. Dastanas +1, Mdk. Shalwar +1 Mdk. Shalwar +1, and Arch. Sabots +1 Arch. Sabots +1 only earned 20,202 Gil for first place.
[2] Naji:
    - Gavial Mail Gavial Mail, Breeches Breeches, and Battle Boots Battle Boots. No head or hand wear.
[3] Black Mud:
    - Wool Hat Wool Hat, Doublet Doublet, Slops Slops, and Ash Clogs Ash Clogs. No hand wear.
        - This is another easy option. Doublet and Slops can be bought from a Sparks vendor. The Wool Hat can be purchased from Chetak in Lower Jeuno (H-9).
[4] Michea:
    - Gambison Gambison, Brais Brais, Gaiters Gaiters. No head or hand wear.
[5] Nbu Latteh:
    - Bronze Harness Bronze Harness, Scale Cuisses Scale Cuisses, and Scale Greaves Scale Greaves. No head or hand coverings.
        - This is the easiest and cheapest outfit to copy. All 3 pieces of this armor are available for ~3,000 from vendors at Brunhilde Armorer in Bastok Markets HP #1 (E-10), or also available from Sparks for a very low cost.
[6] Once you have made your selection, head outside The Steaming Sheep in Port Bastok HP #3 (E-7) and speak to Ruenda while wearing your costume (make sure to either /lockstyle off or create an Equip. Set).
    - This is a long cutscene, 5+ minutes with fastCS and Enternity.
[7] After the cutscene, speak to Brygid once more to receive your reward.
