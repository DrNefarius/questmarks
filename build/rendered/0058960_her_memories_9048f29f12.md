## Walkthrough

Obtain 4 [KI] Large memory fragments from the following 6 sub-quests:
[1] Her Memories: Homecoming Queen, will give you the first [KI] Large memory fragment.
    - Her Memories: Old Bean
    - Her Memories: The Faux Pas
    - Her Memories: Grave Resolve
        - Completing these 3 quests will combine the [KI] Tiny memory fragments into the first [KI] Large memory fragment.
[2] Her Memories: Of Malign Maladies, will give you the second [KI] Large memory fragment.
        - You may want to begin this quest first, as you will be asked to wait an in-game day to complete it.
[3] Her Memories: Operation Cupid, will give you the third [KI] Large memory fragment.
    Obtain the fourth and last [KI] Large memory fragment from the Nation you are allied to in the past:
[4] San d'Oria: Her Memories: Carnelian Footfalls
[5] Bastok: Her Memories: Azure Footfalls
[6] Windurst: Her Memories: Verdure Footfalls
    Once you have 4 [KI] Large memory fragments, you will receive a message to return to Cait Sith, this mission is now completed and you will be on Forget Me Not.
