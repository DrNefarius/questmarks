## Walkthrough

thumb|One of the Secret Fort Karugo-Narugo (S) Doors.
[1] After talking to the Campaign Ops NPC, speak to Yasmina (G-5) for details. She will ask you to which stronghold you want to ride:
    - West Sarutabaruta (S) - Time to Beat: (Between 1:09 and 1:41 will be fast enough)
    - Fort Karugo-Narugo (S) - Time to Beat: (5:02 was fast enough, 5:05 was too slow.)
        - In order to progress this option, you must ride the Chocobo through Fivespires to Lamurara, C.C.. As Magna Cache missions require the nation to hold the stronghold.
        - Since the Phosphorous Ward will not open while mounted, it's required to use the small brick doors that look like Windows, such as the one at F-10.
        - Proceed inside until you reach the middle of Fivespires, and locate Lamurara, C.C. on the middle raised area/platform. Approaching them will initiate a cut-scene while mounted. DO NOT dismount under any circumstance before this cut-scene triggers. Or else the Ops Mission will fail, and no reward will be given.
    - Meriphataud Mountains (S) - Time to Beat: (8:14 was fast enough, 8:31 wasn't fast enough.)
    - Sauromugue Champaign (S) - Time to Beat: (10:27 seconds was fast enough, 13:41 wasn't fast enough)
[2] Ride your Chocobo to the stronghold. When you arrive at the Campaign Arbiter, you will automatically dismount and get a cut-scene.
[3] Report back to Emhi Tchaoryo to complete the mission.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Northern Front Strongholds will not contribute to this Campaign Op.
