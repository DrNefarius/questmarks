## Walkthrough

[1] You must zone after completing Making Headlines before starting this quest.
[2] Speak to Naiko-Paneiko to begin the quest.
[3] Head to the Ferry - Mhaura/Selbina and begin to fish for Ocean Crabs.
[4] The crabs will drop the Bronze Box you need to complete this quest.
[5] Trade the Bronze Box to Naiko-Paneiko to complete this quest.
