## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Crochepallade at (H-9).
[2] He will provide you with a Fiendtrapper and 5 Blank fiend plates.
[3] The Fiendtrapper will not work if you under the effect of Sneak or Invisible (Status) or too far away.
[4] Take pictures of different Orcs. Campaign Battle mobs are valid. Picture must be taken from the front of the mob, she will not accept pictures from the back or side of the mob.
[5] Each picture you take will create a Fiend Plate with the mob's name on it and take up an inventory slot. The exposed Fiend Plates do not stack.
[6] Return to Crochepallade and trade him one Fiend Plate at a time.
[7] After he accepts 3 Fiend Plates the mission is complete.
[8] Unused Blank Fiend Plates may be utilized for Deep Cover II (S).
