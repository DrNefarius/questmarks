## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Naja.  When presented an option, choose Balrahn Way.
[2] Go to Balrahn Way for another cutscene.  When presented an option, choose Where have you been.
[3] Obtain a Sutlac and head off to Nashmau.
[4] At (H-9) in Nashmau, another cutscene will take place.
    - Choose No, let's keep trying!
    - If you choose the wrong answer during the cutscene, you will need to wait for a new game day before repeating the cutscene.
[5] Trade a Sutlac to Totoroon.
[6] Exit East to Caedarva Mire and go to (I-10).  Check the ??? on the East side of the pond for a cutscene.
[7] Return to Naja to complete the quest.
