## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Baudin to begin this quest. (Quest never shows in Current Log)
[2] Speak to Mailloquetat (H-9) for a cutscene.
[3] Return to Baudin.
[4] Speak to Neraf-Najiruf, Ru'Lude Gardens (G-7) who will hand you a Ducal Guard's lantern.
    - You may need to speak to Neraf-Najiruf twice to receive the lantern.
[5] Enter Eldieme Necropolis from Batallia Downs I-10.
[6] You must find, and examine the four Braziers inside.  They must be examined in order or you will not be able to light the lantern.
    - First Brazier: (F-9)
    - Second Brazier: (H-7)
    - Third Brazier: (F-7)
    - Fourth Brazier: (H-9)
[7] Return to Baudin.(Obtain reward and title at this point. Quest shows as success as well)
[8] Speak to Neraf-Najiruf.
