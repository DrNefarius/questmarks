## Walkthrough

[1] Place a  Noble's Bed in your Mog House then speak to the Moogle.
[2] Zone out of your Mog House then come back.
[3] Wait one Earth minute, speak to the Moogle (may need to speak twice), you will receive a cutscene if you have level 7 fame in your home nation.
[4] Trade your Moogle a  Raptor Mantle and a  Wool Hat.
[5] When your Moogle returns from his trip after one earth minute, (but somehow never leaving your Mog House), he will give you a Mog Safe that holds 80 items.
