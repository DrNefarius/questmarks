## Walkthrough

Note
[1] You need to wait 8 real life hours after completing The Clue before this quest is offered.

[2] Speak to Rycharde who asks you to take [KI] Mhauran couscous to Valgeir in Selbina.
    - Rycharde will offer to buy you a ticket for the next Ferry - Mhaura/Selbina, however you can travel to Selbina any way you choose.
[3] Speak to Valgeir in Selbina. He will give you a  Popoto to deliver back to Rycharde.
[4] Return to Rycharde and trade him the  Popoto to complete this quest.
[5] You will receive a  Tea Set as a reward.
[6] (Optional) Speak to Valgeir in Selbina.
[7] (Optional) Speak to Rycharde one last time to conclude the questline.
