## Walkthrough

[1] Gain your mission from Hieronymus,
[2] Speak with Melina (H-5) for details. She will ask you to which stronghold you want to ride a chocobo to:
    - North Gustaberg (S)
    - Grauberg (S)
    - Pashhow Marshlands (S)
    - Rolanberry Fields (S)
[3] Ride on the chocobo to the selected stronghold. Upon arrival, the chocobo will disappear and you will receive a cutscene (whether or not you pass/fail - based on ride time).
[4] Report back to Hieronymus to complete the mission.
