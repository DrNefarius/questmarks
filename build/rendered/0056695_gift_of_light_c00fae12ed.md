## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Amaura (A) at (E-3) near Conflux #01 to begin the quest. She asks for a [KI] Sunbeam fragment.
[2] Examine the Jagged Cliff targetable location at the southeast corner of (J-6) for a mini game.
    - Conflux #07 is nearby the location.
    - You can only complete this mini game during the day, 6:00~18:00 Vana'diel time.
[3] In this mini-game, you need to retrieve a [KI] Sunbeam fragment at the bottom of the chasm by bungee jumping.  This requires pressing the "NOW" prompt at the correct time.  The optimal time seems to be when you right after your descent ends and you begin to ascend.
    - You have three tries to get the sunbeam fragment.
        - Even if you do not obtain the fragment, you can return to Amaura (A) to complete the quest, but you will only receive 60 Cruor.
[4] Once you have the [KI] Sunbeam fragment, return to Amaura (A) at (E-3) for your reward.
    - If you already have a Hi-Reraiser, you must dispose of it to complete the quest.
Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
