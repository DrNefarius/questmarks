## Walkthrough

[1] Go to the Mummers' Coalition in Western Adoulin and speak to Masad for a cutscene with Teodor.
[2] Beat him at the minigame: Boom or Bust to continue.
    - To win, you must win 3 rounds.
[3] If you lose, talk to Masad twice in a row to try again.
    ::{| class="wikitable"
    |
[4] Each round is played by either "shooting", "staying", using a trick, or folding.
[5] Shooting increases your roll in the same manner that a Corsair's Phantom Roll works.
    - Just like with Corsair's rolls, getting more than 11 total will cause you to "bust", and thus lose the round.
[6] Staying closes the round for you.
    - Either play may stay while the other player may continue.
    - When both players stay, the round ends and whoever has the highest number closest to 11 wins.
[7] Using a trick does one of three things:
    - You may only use one trick per round.
    - Peeking lets you look at one roll of the opponent.
    - Swapping lets you change one of your rolls for one of the opponent's.
    - Crooked cards forces your opponent to reroll a roll of your choice.
[8] Folding ends the round in a loss.

[1] If you possess the [KI] "Rhapsody in Azure", you may purchase the [KI] "Card Jailer Teodor" from a Curio Vendor Moogle for 1,000 Gil to skip the minigame below entirely.
    - If you choose this option, you must go back to Masad and speak with him for a final cutscene with Teodor.
