## Walkthrough

[1] Note that you do not specifically need to be allied to Bastok to start this quest.
[2] Exit Bastok Markets (S) into North Gustaberg (S) at (G-4). Upon exiting, a cutscene will play where you obtain the [KI]Clump of animal hair.
[3] Take the key item to Engelhart at the Music Shop in Bastok Markets (S) at (K-10).
[4] Head out to the waterfall in North Gustaberg (S) at (F-8).
    - This is near the survival guide.
    - Examine the ??? on the right side of the waterfall.
[5] You will be asked to retrieve an item from a Goblin NPC in Vunkerl Inlet (S).
[6] Buy a Gnole Claw from Auction House (Materials ➞ Bonecraft) to make the next step quicker.
[7] Head to Vunkerl Inlet (S) to find the goblin Leadavox at (J-6) and trade him a Gnole Claw for [KI]Xhifhut.
    - Gnoles can be found at (E-7) in Vunkerl Inlet (S).
    - Decrepit Gnoles can be found at (H-11) in Jugner Forest (S).
[8] Afterwards, head back to Bastok Markets (S) and speak to Engelhart to complete the quest.
