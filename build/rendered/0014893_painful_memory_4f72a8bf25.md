## Walkthrough

Speak with Mertaire at the Merry Minsterel Meadhouse in Lower Jeuno to begin the quest. You will receive [KI] Mertaire's bracelet.
[1] Travel to Ranguemont Pass and head to the pool at E-5.
[2] Activating the Waters of Oblivion will spawn the NM Tros.
    - One NM pop will clear for multiple people if doing in a group.
[3] Click on the Waters of Oblivion once Tros is defeated for a cutscene. At the conclusion of the cutscene you will receive your Paper Knife.

You do not need to be on Bard to activate the Waters of Oblivion, only that you have [KI] Mertaire's bracelet. Therefore, if you have a higher level job you can switch to that job and defeat Tros easily.
