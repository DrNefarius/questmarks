## Walkthrough

[1] Note that you do not specifically need to be allied to San d'Oria to start this quest.
[2] You must talk to to Louxiard in Southern San d'Oria (S) at G-7 and watch the cutscene.
[3] Leave the area then return to Louxiard for another cutscene.
    - Using Retrace counts as zoning here, and you can quickly resume your progress.
[4] Talk to Rholont at E-7 to begin the actual quest. A cutscene will play that lists people you need to trade the Plumes d'Or to:
    - Machionage (C-6)
    - Louxiard (G-7)
    - Illeuse (H-9)
    - Rongelouts N Distaud (I-9)
    - Sabiliont (by the chocobo stable at I-11)
    - Elnonde (K-9)
    - Loillie (she's up on a ledge at K-9 but you'll have to go up the stairs at L-8 to reach her)

[5] Return to Rholont to finish the quest and receive the reward.
