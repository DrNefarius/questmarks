## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Swift and accept the quest.
[2] Swift will show you a picture of an NPC, you need to memorize.
[3] Board the next airship.
[4] Several NPCs will spawn on the ship that all look very similar to the one Swift showed you.
[5] Speaking to the NPCs will give you an option to return a Dropped item to them.
[6] You only have one chance to get it right.
    - If you choose correctly, you are given 500 gil.
    - If you choose incorrectly, you fail the quest.
