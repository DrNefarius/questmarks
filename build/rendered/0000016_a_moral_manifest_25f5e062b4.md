## Walkthrough

[1] Enter Altar Room for a cutscene that starts this quest.
    - Head past the left/right switch gate with the trap door, then head to (G-7) to enter a different map. Turn right and head straight to re-enter the castle. Finally, head to (G-10).
    - If you choose not to accept this quest, you will not be given the chance to start it again until the next Conquest Tally.
[2] Speak to Ponono, Windurst Woods (G-12) for a cutscene.
[3] Trade Ponono a Velvet Cloth, Rainbow Cloth, and 10,000 gil.
[4] Wait until the next game day to talk to Ponono again who will give you Yagudo Cutting and instructions on how to craft it into a Yagudo Headgear.
    - Zoning is no longer required.
[5] Synthesize a Yagudo Headgear:
[CRAFTING RECIPE]

[6] Return Altar Room while wearing your Yagudo Headgear for a cutscene. You will receive the Vault quipus key item.
[7] Check the Stone Lid while wearing the Yagudo Headgear to spawn the following NM's:
    - Yagudo Avatar
    - Duu Masa the Onecut
    - Fee Jugu the Ramfist
    - Goo Pake the Bloodhound
    - Kee Taw the Nightingale
    - Laa Yaku the Austere
    - Poo Yozo the Babbler
    - Only the Yagudo Avatar has to be defeated to continue.  As soon as he is defeated, all the other NM's will despawn.
[8] Once Yagudo Avatar is defeated, check the Stone Lid again while having the Yagudo Headgear equipped for a cutscene.
[9] Trade your Yagudo Headgear to the Stone Lid to have it replaced by Tsoo's Headgear.
[10] Equip Tsoo's Headgear, zone out to the south of Altar Room and then back into the zone for a cutscene and a Gold Beastcoin.
