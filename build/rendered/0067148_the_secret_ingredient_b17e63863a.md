## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Nogelle (A) (K-5) at Conflux #1, who will ask you to retrieve a chunk of Badlands Salt.
    - Other types of salt do not work, it must be a Badlands Salt which is .
[2] Fish at Conflux #6 until you catch a Badlands Crab, which drop the salt.
    - The drop rate is  Uncommon, around 30%.
[3] Return to salt to Nogelle (A) for your reward.

Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
