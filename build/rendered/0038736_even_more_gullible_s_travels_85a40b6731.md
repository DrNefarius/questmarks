# Even More Gullible's Travels

The game knows this quest as: Even More Gullible's Travels   (quest/outlands #9)

## Quest Header
Start:      Magriffon, Kazham (I-7)
Description: Magriffon has spent all of his (your?) money to save a maiden in distress. Now he needs someone to save him (again) from getting kicked out of the bed & breakfast before he has completed his "top-secret" mission.
Fame:       k
FLevel:     7
Quest Reqs: (empty)
Item Reqs:  
            - 15,000 - 35,000 gil
            - Treasure map (Key Item)
Level:      (empty)
Repeatable: no
Previous:   Gullible's Travels
Next:       The Opo-opo and I
Title:      Even More Gullible's Travels
Reward:     Rafflesia Nectar x3
Expansion:  Rise of the Zilart

## Walkthrough

[1] Zone after finishing the previous quest.
[2] Return to Magriffon (I-7) who will give you Treasure map (Key Item) after you trade him more gil.
[3] Go to the three places in Yuhtunga Jungle on the Treasure map: (F-7) (H-10) and (I-12).
[4] At each spot on the map you will find a Blue Rafflesia.
    - Examining each Blue Rafflesia will give you a Rafflesia Nectar.
[5] Return to Magriffon to finish the quest.

== Notes: ==

[6] You must zone after completing Gullible's Travels before you can flag this quest.
[7] After completing this quest, all Mithra in Kazham will be repulsed by you, but Opo-opos will be attracted to you.
    - The only way to fix this is by completing Personal Hygiene, however it is recommended that you finish The Opo-opo and I quest first so you can get an Opo-opo Crown.
