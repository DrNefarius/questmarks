## Walkthrough

}|Instructions|
[1] Everything here is aggressive to sound, so make sure everyone has Sneak.
    - Using the Unity Warp 125 will locate you in Western Altepa Desert (I-7).
[2] Proceed to Western Altepa Desert and head for the tunnel at (C/D-11) south side.
    - Following this tunnel will bring you to an unmarked area of the desert.
[3] Head down the ramp to (D-12) and enter Quicksand Caves.
[4] Go to (I-9) and step on the weight pad and go through the door.
    - If the KI [KI] Loadstone was acquired, you still need to step on the weight pads.
[5] Go west to (H-7) and use another weight pad to pass through a door.
[6] In the next room, drop down the hole to a hidden area.
[7] Head to (D-4) to zone in to the Chamber of Oracles.
    }}

    - You can use Trusts in this fight.
