## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Nbu Latteh on Gold Street to start quest.
    - She will give you a Fire Crystal you can use to craft your Copper Ring if you choose.
[2] (Optional) speak to Nbu Latteh again, as well as the guard Parnika to the south, for hints at Nbu's occupation.
[3] Obtain a Copper Ring either via Auction House, Merchants or Crafting.
[4] Trade Copper Ring to Roh Latteh in Bastok Mines (H-7), she is located on the first floor house on Ore Street.
[5] Upon NPC dialogue completion, you will obtain [KI] Letter from Roh Latteh.
[6] If you 'check' the letter (as in read it from your Key Item menu) you will receive a penalty and get only half the actual reward.
[7] Return to Nbu Latteh to complete quest.
[8] This quest is repeatable for players below Bastok fame level 2. Zone out each time and you can re-do it.
