## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kopuro-Popuro to begin this quest.
[2] Trade Kopuro-Popuro a 2Leaf Mandra Bud Two-Leaf Mandragora Bud,  Insect Wing, and a  Rabbit Hide to complete the quest.
    - The 2Leaf Mandra Bud Two-Leaf Mandragora Bud is EX and can be found by killing most mandragora type mobs, including Tiny Mandragoras in East Sarutabaruta and West Sarutabaruta.
