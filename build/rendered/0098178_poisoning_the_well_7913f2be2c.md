## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Fritha. She  is located at (J-7) in Foret de Hennetiel, at the Frontier Station.
[2] After, you will want to head to J-9 and click on the River Mouth (The island of the Frontier Station). This will spawn a Notorious Monster named Cunning Craklaw.
[3] After defeating Cunning Craklaw, make sure to click the River Mouth to receive the [KI]Vial of toxic Zoldeff water.
[4] Return to Fritha to complete the quest.
