## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Izabele to begin the quest.
[2] Invite at least one other player to form a party of 2. Change to a job that's level 15 or below.
    - Trusts and Adventuring Fellows do not count toward the requirements.
    - Level Sync does work.
[3] Together with your party, travel to North Gustaberg and locate the galka Hunting Bear at F-8 (near the waterfall).
    - The Survival Guide also puts you at the Outpost, which is fairly close to him.
    - Any players under level 20 will have to walk, as mounts require level 20.
[4] Speak with Hunting Bear to complete the quest.
    - You only need to be level synced when you talk to Hunting Bear, not as you travel to him.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

Similar to When Wills Collide, Mercenary Camp, and The Kuftal Tour, this CS displays fellow party members, a very rare occurrence.
[1] It's not much of a different angle than you'd see in game.
[2] You can watch this CS again with the Goblin Footprint.

Originally this quest required 6 players and required a level 5 or below job, but was dropped to 2 players and a level 15 or below job with the [https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/47481-Jun-25-2015-%28JST%29-Version-Update June 2015 Update]
[3] This had an added effect of allowing players who did not have a level 5 or below job to complete the quest with 2008's Level Sync functionality, as with the previous level cap, you could not sync due to the minimum level for sync being level 10.
