## Walkthrough

[1] Enter Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno.
[2] Talk to Ghebi Damomohe at the counter.
[3] Select the third, blank line of dialogue. Ghebi Damomohe will inform you that you need either a Tenshodo Invite OR a [KI] Tenshodo application form.

To get the [KI] Tenshodo application form:

[4] Head to Port Bastok and into Warehouse #2 upper level.
[5] Once inside, talk to Jabbar to receive the [KI] Tenshodo application form.
[6] Return to Ghebi Damomohe to receive a Tenshodo Invite and Tenshodo Member's Card. You may now freely come and go from the Tenshodo back room.

If you'd prefer to buy a Tenshodo Invite:

[7] Search the Auction House (AH > Others > Misc.) for a Tenshodo Invite and trade it to Ghebi Damomohe to complete the quest. Please note that buying the Tenshodo Invite does NOT bypass the Jeuno Fame required for the quest.
