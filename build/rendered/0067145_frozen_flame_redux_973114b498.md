## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Guilboire (A) at (F-7) (Conflux #7) to begin this quest.
[2] He will provide you with two key items, the [KI] Snoll reflector and [KI] Experiment cheat sheet.
[3] Walk north to (G-5) and find the Impact Point location.
[4] The Notorious Monster Frost Bomb Mk-II will spawn to the west.
[5] Run up and attack it, which will raise its HP. Your goal is to raise its HP to at least 50%. The higher it is, the more Cruor reward you receive.
[6] Pull it towards the Impact Point. It will use Hypothermal Combustion regardless of its current TP. This will deal damage to the point, but not to you.
    - If you disengage, you will fail this quest. This makes Trust Magic more difficult to use if they pull hate.
[7] Examine the Impact Point to receive a [KI] Frosted snoll reflector.
    - If in a party, all players can receive the [KI] Frosted snoll reflector from the same Frost Bomb Mk-II as long as they click the Impact Point before it uses Hypothermal Combustion.
[8] Return and speak to Guilboire (A) for your reward.
    - 25 completions of this quest will max fame in this zone.
Zoning is required in order to repeat this quest.
