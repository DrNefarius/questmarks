# Iron Anvil II (B)

The game knows this mission as: Iron Anvil II (B)   (mission/campaign #302)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Supply manufacture
Description: To manufacture munitions and supplies, our nation has employed hundreds of young, unskilled guildworkers. You are to lend your trade knowledge to aid in the training of these initiates. Speak with adjutant (J-9) for details.
Orders:      (empty)
NPC Name:    Hieronymus
Zone:        Bastok Markets (S)
pos:         E-8
Campaign Rank: Sterling Star §§
Time:        None
Level:       Any
Rewards:     * 240 Experience Points
            * 780 Allied Notes

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus, and you'll be forwarded to Adjutant at (J-9).
[2] Talk to Adjutant, and he'll ask you for a craft to specialize in.
[3] Once you've picked a craft, he'll test you on two synths made with said craft.
    - What crystal is used for this synth? [List]
    - What is the first ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the second ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the third ingredient in this synth? [List]
    - What is the fourth ingredient in this synth? [List] (optional)
    - What is the fifth ingredient in this synth? [List] (optional)
[4] Once you've completed your two tests, go back and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This is repeatable (as long as you have Op Credits, and it's available), making it a very quick and easy way to stock up on Allied Notes.
[2] Reported Alchemy synths:
    - Animal Glue
    - Chocotonic
    - Poison Arrowheads
    - Ethereal Vermilion Lacquer
