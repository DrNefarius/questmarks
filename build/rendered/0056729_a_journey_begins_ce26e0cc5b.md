## Walkthrough

[1] Zone into Port Jeuno on a level 30+ job for a cutscene.
[2] Speak to Joachim in Port Jeuno (H-8) for a cutscene to receive a [KI] Traverser stone and to complete the quest.
[3] If you previously chose to postpone the cutscene, the Tales' Beginning is located next to Home Point 2 in Port Jeuno.
