## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Maudaal in E-9 of Aht Urhgan Whitegate to start this quest. You will receive a lengthy cutscene upon doing so.
[2] After, you are directed to head to the "secret entrance" to Wajaom Woodlands, G-10/H-10 of Aht Urhgan. Upon zoning, you receive another cutscene.
[3] Reach Mamook by entering the cave at D-12 on the Wajaom Woodlands map or by using the Mamook Survival Guide warp.
[4] Once you've entered, you will find a "Toad's Footprint" (J-7), click it for another cutscene.
[5] Enter the hallway in the southern part of G-7/H-7 that contain the "Toad's Footprint"s, click it for another cutscene.
[6] Check them a second time for a fight.
[7] You will spawn a Poroggo Casanova, assisted by Mikiruru‎, Mikiluru‎, Mikirulu‎, Nikilulu, and Mikilulu‎.
    - They are unable to be slept, even with Elemental Seal.
    - The helpers are ordinary frog enemies and have very weak attack power and accuracy, but the Casanova may use all normal Poroggo spells/abilities, including Sleepga, Tier IV and Ancient magic, and "Frog Song".
    - Killing the Casanova will cause all other frogs to disappear and the battle will end.
    - Mikilulu Cannot be killed but can be brought down to 1% HP
[8] After winning, check the tracks again for yet another cutscene.
    - You only have to fight it once for it to count for the rest of your party if they are at that stage in the quest.
[9] Return to Whitegate and talk to Maudaal again for the final cutscene and your reward.
