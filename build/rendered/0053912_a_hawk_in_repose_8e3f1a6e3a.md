## Walkthrough

This mission requires a 25px|link= . This can be purchased for 120 Gil at the M & P's Market in Upper Jeuno HP #1 (E), from Areebah (H-6). It is recommended to buy two as you will need one for a later quest.
[1] Examine the Weathered Gravestone in Batallia Downs at (I-10) - up the hill - for a cutscene.
    - Fastest route is using The Eldieme Necropolis Survival Guide, and walking outside to Batallia Downs.
    - If you get a message "You see the weathered gravestone." then you did not complete Another World and another quest or mission may have taken priority over it.
[2] Trade a Lilac to the Weathered Gravestone.
