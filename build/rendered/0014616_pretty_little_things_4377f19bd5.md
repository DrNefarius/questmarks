## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Zona Shodhun to start the quest.
[2] She won't come out and say it at first, but she would like a Yellow Rock.
[3] Yellow Rock can be bought off auction houses; Materials > Goldsmithing.
    - Can also be mined in Gusgen Mines or Yughott Grotto on earthsday.
[4] Trade it to her to complete the quest.
