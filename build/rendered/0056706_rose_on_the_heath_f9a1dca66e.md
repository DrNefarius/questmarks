## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Ayame (A) at (I-13) to begin the quest. You will receive the following:
    - [KI] Captain Rashid's linkpearl
    - [KI] Captain Argus's linkpearl
    - [KI] Captain Helga's linkpearl
    - [KI] Seal of the resistance.
[2] Speak to Argus (A) at (D-7) - Conflux #03, then walk north/east.
[3] Speak to Helga (A) at (G-5) - Conflux #05, then walk north through the Mesa Wivre.
[4] Speak to Rashid (A) at (F-10) - Conflux #02, then walk northwest.
[5] Return to Ayame (A) to complete the quest. She will take the [KI] Seal of the resistance back from you.
