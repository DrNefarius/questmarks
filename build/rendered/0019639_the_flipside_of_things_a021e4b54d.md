## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Rarcasmeult (J-6 2nd map and off in uncharted H-6/I-6, top right hand corner of map) and he will tell you to find the Firesand Storeroom.
[2] Return through the door from whence you came and venture South.
[3] Continue South past Loutille and then past Duchille, go through the Heavy Iron Gate. Slimes and Bats will aggro in this section, so be sure to have Sneak or Silent Oil handy.
[4] Head south and west towards the square section of this map, then continue heading towards (G-7) where you will find a Storeroom Door.
[5] Enter the Storeroom, then examine the ??? on a nearby table to receive [KI] Firepower case.
[6] Return to Rarcasmeult to obtain your reward.
