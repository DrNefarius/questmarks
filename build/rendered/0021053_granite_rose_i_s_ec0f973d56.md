# Granite Rose I (S)

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Rasdinice.
[2] Travel to the Fortilace at Batallia Downs (S) (D-6) (Far left side of the map.)
    - Survival Guide warp recommended.
[3] Once the party leader has selected to enter, you will be transported into Everbloom Hollow.
[4] A party of 3 Orc Stormers, RNG PLD WAR, will be roaming the area.
[5] Once all three orcs are defeated, the Mission Objective is complete. Exit via the Dawn Aureola.
[6] Return and talk to Rasdinice for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] There seems to be no time limit to how long you can take to get to Everbloom Hollow.
[2] All 3 of the Orc are sleepable.
[3] The orc are very weak.
    - Approximately 1800 HP each.
