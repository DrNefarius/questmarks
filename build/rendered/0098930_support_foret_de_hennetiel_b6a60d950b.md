# Support: Foret de Hennetiel

The game knows this assignment as: Support: Foret de Hennetiel   (quest/coalition #27)

## Giver
Task Delegator - Western Adoulin (G-7)
  (resolved from the Couriers' Coalition page; the assignment page names no NPC)

## Walkthrough
[1] Upon accept this assignment, the Task Delegator will provide you with a Hen. FB op. materials container to deliver to any frontline bivouac in Foret de Hennetiel.
    - As of Feb 18, 2014 update, Waypoints can now be used to complete this task. Hen. FB op. materials container will not break.
    - If you cancel the task, you will have your Imprimatur(s) refunded. No Imprimatur(s) are lost.
    - Using the Black Magic spell Warp does not seem to break the Hen. FB op. materials container.

[2] Bring the Hen. FB op. materials container to any Bivouac Administrator in the designated area(s) to complete the assignment.
    - Bivouac Administrator #1: (G-6)
    - Bivouac Administrator #2: (J-10)
    - Bivouac Administrator #3: (H-9)
    - Bivouac Administrator #4: Northwest (F-9)

[3] If you have "Watercrafting" and a Watercraft, you can easily finish this assignment and obtain the maximum reward, without having to go through any Colonization Reive, in 10-15 minutes :
    - Run out of Western Adoulin into Ceizak Battlegrounds after accepting the assignment
    - Head to (H-11) in Ceizak and zone into Sih Gates
    - Run all the way south through the cave and exit to Foret de Hennetiel at (H-11)
    - Head to the nearest Castoff Point in the south-western corner of (I-7) and go upstream 3 times in a row
    - You will end up in the (F-7/G-7) area, just head south-west to reach Bivouac #4 and give the supplies to the Administrator, at (F-8/F-9)
    - Use the Waypoint to return to the Task Delegator in Western Adoulin and you will receive 8400 Bayld and Experience Points if you used 3 imprimaturs.

## After the walkthrough: Notes
[1] Due to variable tree blockages, the same route used to go to a certain bivouac may not be available constantly.
[2] Same rewards were given when turning it into an already built bivouac.
[3] It is advised to turn in at an unbuilt bivouac if possible to help speed up the colonization rate of the area by allowing the bivouac to be built once enough supplies are delivered.
[4] Attempting to turn it in to a Bivouac Administrator with the key item broken will result in the message: "I'm terribly sorry, but we can't use broken containers of Hennetiel frontier bivouac operations materials."
[5] The Beastmaster and Ranger ability Wide Scan can pick up Bivouac Administrators.
