## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Arawn to start quest.  You will be given the [KI] Stamp sheet to collect stamps.
[2] Speak to the following 7 guards to obtain the stamps:
    - Pavel - Bastok Markets (D-11) - gate to South Gustaberg
    - Deadly Spider - Bastok Mines (G-8) - 2nd floor of the Depot, center-ish of map
    - Tall Mountain - Bastok Mines (J-7) - go down ramp and head towards Alchemist Guild, turn right at the no-entrance-allowed area
    - Romualdo - Metalworks (K-9) - southeastern Cannonry
    - Elayne - Metalworks (G-8) - at the entrance to the Temple of the Goddess
    - Ehrhard - Port Bastok (G-5) - northeast corner outside of Warehouse #1
    - Latifah - Port Bastok (J-8) - north of the drawbridge
[3] Speak to Arawn again once you have collected all the stamps to complete the quest.
