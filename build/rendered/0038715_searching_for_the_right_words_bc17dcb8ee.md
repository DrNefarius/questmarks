## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kurou-Morou after completing the prerequisite quests.
[2] Speak to Ilumida (Upper Jeuno G-8) until she gives you a cutscene requesting you obtain a [KI] Moondrop.
[3] Travel to The Boyahda Tree and examine a ??? at H-8 in 2nd map of The Boyahda Tree between 19:00 and 04:00 to spawn an Ahriman NM Agas.
    - Home Point #1 (Cloister of Storms) is close to the objective.
[4] Once defeated, examine the ??? again between the hours of 19:00 and 04:00 to get the [KI] Moondrop.
[5] Return to Upper Jeuno and talk to Ilumida for your reward.

== Agas ==

[6] Agas will not spawn during a New Moon (type /clock) as the moon must be visible to some degree.
[7] Agas uses Level 5 Petrify, which will petrify any characters with a level divisible by 5.
[8] Only one person in the party needs to spawn Agas to get the whole party their [KI] Moondrop.
[9] Casts Tier 3 single target and AoE-aga black magic spells.
[10] Agas is soloable at level 75 by a Beastmaster with the charmable mobs around the area. A couple of Summoners would also work.
    - Korrigan are more resistant to charm than the Skimmers around the tree.
[11] Also soloable by a BLU at 75 utilizing Headbutt to stop spells and abilities.
[12] Otherwise, any party or player with Trusts can take out Agas.
