## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Curilla and agree to help her to receive [KI] Curilla's bottle.
[2] Head to Horlais Peak by going through Yughott Grotto.
[3] Once in Horlais Peak, head to the Hot Springs in the springs and check it to get [KI] Curilla's bottle again. To confirm you filled it, because the name doesn't change, observe the text description of the item under "Temporary Items". It should read "The bottle from general Curilla. It is full of water from the hot spring that flows from the Horlais Peak"
[4] Return to Curilla for your reward.
