# The Voracious Resurgence Mission 10-6

The game knows this mission as: Odin's Eye   (mission/tvr #602)

## Mission Header
Mission Name: Odin's Eye
Start:       Heroes' Gambit, Xarcabard (J-9)
Description: Mission Orders: You finally meet up with Raebrimm. His soul has been severed in twain, and he appears to be suffering from partial memory loss. Collect the four pieces of his soul and head deeper in.
Level:       (empty)
Repeatable:  No
Previous:    Duke Alloces's Decision
Next:        Moglesse Oblige
Title:       Sender of Shadow
Reward:      *Cornelia's Belt
Expansion:   tvr

## Walkthrough

[1] You must change areas after completing the previous mission in order to begin this one.
    - Twinkling Presence will not be visible if you are on the previous mission.
[2] Interact with the Heroes' Gambit for a cutscene to begin the mission.
    - You will once again obtain the [KI] Despairing psyche.
[3] Enter Dynamis - Xarcabard and collect four other shards scattered around the zone.
    - The Twinkling Presence are in fixed locations.
        - NW section of (I-9) surrounded by demons in the flat area.
        - East side of (H-7), on top of the right cliff you can see when you are walking up the ramp.
        - North middle of (F-7). Above where the zone to Uleguerand Range is in Xarcabard.
        - NW corner of (G-10).
        - You receive one psyche key item at each shard: [KI] Suffering psyche, [KI] Anguished psyche, [KI] Rancorous psyche, [KI] Determined psyche.
    You will be transported out of Dynamis - Xarcabard when you begin the next cutscene.
[4] Once all shards have been collected, click the glowing targetable location Before Castle Zvahl at (D-7), where the Dynamis Lord fight is, for a cutscene.
    - You will receive [KI] Raebrimm's memento
[5] Head back to Bastok Mines and talk to Gumbah at (J-7) for a cutscene and your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] At this point, you can now open up access to two new Records of Eminence objectives. See the NPC Elijah in Upper Jeuno near Home Point #1 for more information.
