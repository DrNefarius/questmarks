## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kubhe Ijyuhla at (I-8) in the Serpentking Square to begin the Quest. The rest of the Quest involves running around Whitegate and watching cutscenes.
[2] Head to the (G/H-10) boundary and exit into Wajaom Woodlands through the Ironbound Gate. As you zone, you'll watch the cutscene.
[3] Head back to Kubhe, who started the Quest, for another cutscene.
[4] Speak with Ratihb inside the Shararat Teahouse at (J-12).
    - If the cutscene does not involve Foudeel and Wahboud, speak to him again for the correct cutscene.
[5] Next is Tehf Kimasnahya at (F-8) on the Balrahn's Way, who explains the entire thing.
[6] Finally, return to Kubhe to complete the Quest.  She rewards you with an Imp. Brz. Piece Imperial Bronze Piece.
