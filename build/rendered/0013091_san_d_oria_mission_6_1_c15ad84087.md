# San d'Oria Mission 6-1

The game knows this mission as: Leaute's Last Wishes   (mission/sandoria #16)

## Mission Header
Mission Name: Leaute's Last Wishes
Start:       Any San d'Oria Gate Guard
Description: This mission involves the late Queen Leaute. Visit Monarlais Halver in Chateau d'Oraguille to learn more.
Level:       (empty)
Repeatable:  No

Next:        San d'Oria Mission 6-2 | Ranperre's Final Rest

Reward:      *Rank Points
Expansion:   Rise of the Zilart

## Walkthrough

Trade enough Crystals to the Conquest NPC to raise your Rank bar and unlock the Mission, then accept it from the Gate Guard.
[1] Head to Chateau d'Oraguille and speak with Halver.
[2] Select the Great Hall door for a cutscene.
    - Make sure to get the cutscene where the King asks you to retrieve a Dreamrose.
[3] Speak with Halver again.
[4] Go to (G-7) in Western Altepa Desert. The Dreamrose is butted up against Revelation Rock, in the southern section of the oasis (look for the body of water on your map).
    - The Unity Warp (Level 125) will bring you close.
    - Alternatively, you could use the Survival Guide.
[5] Check the Dreamrose to spawn Sabotender Enamorado.
    - If you're low level, it is recommended that you bring enough people, pets and/or Trusts to reduce the damage done by 1000 Needles. It is possible to do it with less, but spreading the damage out will help any HP concerns.
[6] Check the Dreamrose after defeating the NM to receive the [KI] Dreamrose.
[7] Return to Halver.
[8] Go toward the Garden at (F-8) for another cutscene. At the end of the cutscene, you'll receive the [KI] Piece of paper.
