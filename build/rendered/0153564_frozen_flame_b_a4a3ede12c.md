## Walkthrough

[1] Travel to either Beaucedine Glacier (S) or Xarcabard (S) and find a ??? to spawn a Frost Bomb
    - The area must be under Beastman control.
    - Beaucedine Glacier (S): ??? is at (H-9) - down the ramp nearby the tower where the C.A. stands when the area is under Beastmen control
    - Xarcabard (S): ??? is at (G-10)
[2] Examining the ??? will spawn a Frost Bomb, which will attack shortly after. Do not attack the snoll, but be careful to not lose all enmity and render it unclaimed.
[3] Lead it to the Campaign Fortification where it will automatically use Hypothermal Combustion and damage the fort, and only the fort.
[4] After this, the experience points will be awarded along with the message: "The stronghold has sustained considerable damage."
[5] Upon failure, the message will be: "The bombardment failed to inflict damage on the target."
[6] Speak with Hieronymus to complete the Op and collect your Allied Notes.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Presumable either Beaucedine Glacier (S) or Xarcabard (S) must be under beastmen control.
[2] Be careful when spawning and maneuvering the Frost Bomb for it is possible for the snoll to spawn unclaimed, and it is possible for it to "deaggro" while being kited if you get far enough ahead of it. Despite the fact it is Impossible To Gauge, it will de-spawn like any ordinary monster if it remains unclaimed with no enmity over a player character for long enough; this will fail the Op.
