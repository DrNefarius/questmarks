## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Miene by the water, which will start a cut-scene revealing a burglary in process.
[2] Talk to Altiret, who asks you to retreive the stolen item.
[3] Talk to Miene to obtain a clue, an Eagle Button.
    - (Optional) You can attempt to trade the button to Altiret.
[4] (Optional) Several guard and bystander NPCs will have comments about the thief:
    - Port San d'Oria:
        - Eaugouint, Artinien, Brifalien in the immediate area.
        - Meuxtajean, Coribalgeant, Noquerelle, Answald near the mog house entrance.
        - Comittie, Meinemelle, Maunadolace, Sheridan, Parcarin, Solgierte near the auction house.
        - Laucimercen, Marquie in the corridors to the west.
    - Northern San d'Oria:
        - Aurege, Guilberdrier near the carpenters' guild.
    - For just the ones that give hints:
        - Port San d'Oria: Crilde, Rielle, Avandale
        - Northern San d'Oria: Pepigort, Maurinne, Gilipese, Rodaillece

[5] Head out to West Ronfaure and look for Esca inside a tower at (F-6). She will demand evidence.
[6] Trade the button to her, prompting her to admit she was the thief.
[7] After receiving the Gilt Glasses, return them to Altiret for your reward.
