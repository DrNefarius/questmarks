## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Andecia (located in the middle house on the first floor of Squire's Alley) once to trigger story text. Speak to Andecia again after the first set of text to receive the Well Water item (will be deposited into your inventory).
[2] Travel to King Ranperre's Tomb (I-10) on the first map.
[3] Trade the Well Water to the 'tombstone' to receive a Tomb Waterskin.
[4] Return to Andecia and trade her Tomb Waterskin to complete quest.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] If for some reason you lose the Well Water before completing this quest, complete the following steps:
[2] Travel to King Ranperre's Tomb (I-10) on the first map to find the 'tombstone'.
[3] Touch the tombstone and you will receive a Tomb Waterskin.
[4] Travel to Southern San d'Oria (F-6) and trade the Tomb Waterskin to the water well to receive a new Well Water. You may now complete the quest as noted in the walkthrough.
