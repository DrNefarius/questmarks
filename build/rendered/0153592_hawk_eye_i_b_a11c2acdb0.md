# Hawk Eye I (B)

The game knows this mission as: Hawk Eye I (B)   (mission/campaign #211)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Intel gathering
Description: Gather intelligence from an enemy fortress.
Orders:      Current intel on the defensive capabilities of enemy strongholds has been deemed insufficient. You are to infiltrate a Quadav stronghold in one of the areas near Bastok and gather as much data as possible.
NPC Name:    Hieronymus
Zone:        Bastok Markets (S)
pos:         E-8

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus and sign up.
[2] Head to any Quadav controlled area and make your way to the Stronghold.
[3] Examine the ??? at the Stronghold to gather the Intel.
    - Note: There are two ??? around the Stronghold - one being the one used for this mission. The "Stronghold" is just the zone's fortification, the one around which defensive and offensive Campaign battles occur.
    - Once clicked, You will receive a Commencing intel extraction message, followed by repeated ---Gathering intel--- messages periodically, until you then receive a Investigation complete! message.
[4] Report back to Hieronymus to complete the mission.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This Campaign Op becomes available when at least one of your nations areas is under Beastmen control
