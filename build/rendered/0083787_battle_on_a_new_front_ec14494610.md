## Walkthrough

[1] Engage in and complete the 6 Tier I Voidwatch battles in the 3 field areas surrounding Jeuno (present and past):
    - Cherufe - Batallia Downs (G-8), (I-9), (J-10).
    - Taweret - Batallia Downs (S). (G-8), (I-9), (J-10).
        - Fastest access is via Batallia Downs (S) Survival Guide warp which will take you very close to the (G-8) VW spawn located between the "o" and "v" of Coveffe Barrows on the map.
    - Yatagarasu - Rolanberry Fields (E-10), (F-8), (G-7).
        - Fastest Access is via Rolanberry Fields Survival Guide warp, which puts you just north of the (G-7) VW spawn located at the NW corner of the grid cell.
    - Agathos - Rolanberry Fields (S) (E-10), (F-8), (G-7).
        - Fastest Access is via Rolanberry Fields Survival Guide warp + Cavernous Maw, which puts you just north of the (G-7) VW spawn located at the NW corner of the grid cell.
    - Goji - Sauromugue Champaign (G-6), (I-6), (K-10).
    - Gugalanna - Sauromugue Champaign (S) (G-6), (I-6), (K-10).
        - Fastest access is via Sauromugue Champaign (S) Survival Guide warp which puts you in (K-10) and VW spawn is near the NE corner of the grid.
[2] Once all 6 are defeated, return to an Atmacite Refiner to have your [KI] White stratum abyssite examined and upgraded to [KI] White stratum abyssite II.
    - It is not required to return to a Refiner after each battle, only once all 6 are completed.
----
[3] Engage in and complete the 6 Tier II Voidwatch battles in the 3 dungeons entered from the areas surrounding Jeuno (present and past):
    - Gasha - The Eldieme Necropolis Map 2: (J-6), (J-10), (L-9).
    - Giltine - The Eldieme Necropolis (S) Map 2: (J-6), (J-10), (L-9).
    - Mellonia - Crawlers' Nest Map 2: (F-6), (F-8), (I-7).
    - Nympha Eunomia / Kalos Eunomia - Crawlers' Nest (S) Map 2: (F-6), (F-8), (I-7).
    - Roly-Poly - Garlaige Citadel Map 1: (G-9), (H-7) Map 2: (G-8).
    - Laidly Laurence - Garlaige Citadel (S) Map 1: (G-9), (H-7) Map 2: (G-8).
[4] Once all 6 are defeated, speak with any Voidwatch Officer, past or present, to receive a brief cutscene and [KI] Voidwatch alarum.
    - The named Officers present in Zilart/Promathia/Aht Urghan zones will not begin this cutscene.
[5] Return to the Audience Chamber in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene and to receive the [KI] White stratum abyssite III and [KI] Voidwatcher's emblem: Qufim.
    - (Optional-ish): Return to the Bulwark Gate in Sauromugue Champaign (S) at (F-6) to receive a cutscene and 30,000 Gil.
        - Note: This must be done before beginning the Quest A World in Flux if you plan on continuing this storyline.
        - Just do this step now instead of waiting. It might take longer to complete during a later Quest.
