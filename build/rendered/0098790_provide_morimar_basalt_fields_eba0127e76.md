# Provide: Morimar Basalt Fields

The game knows this assignment as: Provide: Morimar Basalt Fields   (quest/coalition #17)

## Giver
Task Delegator - Western Adoulin (G-7)
  (resolved from the Couriers' Coalition page; the assignment page names no NPC)

## Walkthrough
[1] Once you accept the assignment, the Task Delegator will provide you with a Mor. FS building mat. container to deliver to a Station Administrator in Morimar Basalt Fields.
    - DO NOT use a Waypoint warp. Doing so will cause the Mor. FS building mat. container to break and make you unable to complete the assignment.
        - As of the [http://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/40059-Feb-18-2014-%28JST%29-Version-Update?p=493165&viewfull=1#post493165 Feb. 18 2014 Version Update], Waypoints can now be used to complete this task without risk of break. Simply warp to a Station Administrator.
[2] Zone to Eastern Adoulin from Western Adoulin at (M-8).
[3] Travel to (G-5) in Eastern Adoulin and talk to Lamaron to be sent to Yahse Hunting Grounds.
[4] Travel north to (H-5) in Yahse Hunting Grounds and zone to Moh Gates.
[5] Travel north to (G-5) in Moh Gates and zone to Morimar Basalt Fields. Be advised that there appears to be a Cave Panopt in the first small room that aggros through Sneak and Invisible (Status).
[6] From the entrance of Morimar Basalt Fields, follow the path to (K-10) and speak to the Station Administrator to complete the assignment.
[7] Return to the Task Delegator to receive your reward.
