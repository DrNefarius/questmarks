## Walkthrough

[1] Start the Quest by checking the door to Svenja's Manor in Western Adoulin (I-8) (Platea Triumphus Waypoint).
[2] Talk to Eppel-Treppel in Eastern Adoulin (G-10) to enter Celennia Memorial Library for a cutscene.
[3] Harvest a [KI]Hennebloom leaf from harvest points in Foret de Hennetiel with a sickle.
    - Fill up your inventory in order to avoid losing the Harvesting Point to items harvested.
    - Normal sickles and trailblazing sickles both work.
[4] Report back to Svenja's Manor in Western Adoulin (I-8) for your reward.

## After the walkthrough: Bugs

[1] It is possible that if you have completed the quest Flowers for Svenja, NPC Grevan won't mention anything about the library ([http://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/35626-Quest-Order-Up-Glitched Bug reported] 7/12/13), thus preventing you from completing the quest Order Up.
    - As of January 2019, the said bug has been fixed. It is now possible to complete Flowers for Svenja before either starting or completing Order Up.
