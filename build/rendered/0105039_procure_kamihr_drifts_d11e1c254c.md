## Walkthrough
[1] Obtain a number of Pickaxes (Extras highly recommended)
[2] Travel to Kamihr Drifts
[3] Locate Mining Points and use the Pickaxe
[4] Repeat until you've obtained five items.
[5] Note that gathering fatigue will set in rather quickly, depending on various conditions such as Ionis effects. Gathering fatigue will slowly decay over time, allowing one additional successful gathering attempt approximately every two minutes.
[6] You do not need to keep the physical items you obtained while doing the assignment, the "items" are abstract. You can safely toss or sell any HELM results and still complete the assignment.
The only known mining points are at:

    G-9, south of Outer Ra'Kaznar,
    G-9, west of Bivouac #1
    G-11, east of Ergon Locus in cave
    H-8, west of Bivouac #3
    H-6, north of Bivouac #3, on the way to Sajj'aka
    I-7, east of Bivouac #3, right before one-way ledge drop
    F-6, west of Bivouac #4
    F-7, south-west of Bivouac #4
    J-9, south of Ergon Locus in cave
    G-7, Inside a Cave

[7] As a side note. After the Mining Point vanishes, don't leave the area right away as the Mining Point may return to the exact same place after about a minute. If it does not return, then continue searching for the next Mining Point.
