## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Chipmy-Popmy to begin this quest.
[2] Hop aboard the Manaclipper in Bibiki Bay to Bibiki Bay - Purgonorgo Isle.
[3] Head to (H-9) to find a ???.
[4] Examine the ??? to spawn 6 Peerifools.
    - These use Dream Flower often.
    - They will link with any other Mandragora nearby.

[5] Once defeated, check the ??? again to receive the [KI] Deed to Purgonorgo Isle.
[6] Return to Chipmy-Popmy. (may have to talk to him multiple times, make sure you get a cutscene)
[7] Head to Boneyard Gully by going through Attohwa Chasm.
    - You can reach Attohwa Chasm from the Maze of Shakhrami. Afterwards you will need to cross the bridge to get to the eastern side of the map. You can go back to the caves at (I-8). If you follow the tunnel, eventually you will reach the surface again at (F-7). The next tunnel entrance is at (F-6), which will take you to Boneyard Gully (you can unlock Attohwa Chasm Home Point right before you leave the Chasm).
[8] As soon as you zone into Boneyard Gully you will get a cutscene and the Map of the Attohwa Chasm (if you do not already own it).
[9] Return to Chipmy-Popmy for the 3,200 gil and 2,000 Experience Points.
[10] (Optional) Speak to Chipmy-Popmy again for some closing dialogue.
