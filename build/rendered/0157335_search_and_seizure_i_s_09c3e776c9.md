## Walkthrough

[1] Gain your mission from Rasdinice by selecting Resource Procurement from the Campaign Ops menu.
[2] He will send you to the Quartermaster located at (I-9).
[3] The Quartermaster will tell you to go to "Beastman influenced" area and attack any Beastmen Supply Divisions & Seize their cargo.
[4] Go to a Beastmen-controlled zone and seek out a Beastmen Transporter and two Guards. This can be any Beastmen controlled zone. It doesn't necessarily have to be one in your allied nation's area. The Transporter and Guards will be making a supply run.
    - Quadav Transporter and Quadav Guard can be found in:
        - Crawler's Nest (S)
        - Grauberg (S)
        - North Gustaberg (S)
        - Pashhow Marshlands (S)
        - Rolanberry Fields (S)
    - Orcish Transporter and Orcish Guard can be found in:
        - Batallia Downs (S)
        - East Ronfaure (S)
        - Jugner Forest (S)
        - The Eldieme Necropolis (S)
        - Vunkerl Inlet (S)
    - Yagudo Transporter and Yagudo Guard can be found in:
        - Fort Karugo-Narugo (S)
        - Garlaige Citadel (S)
        - Meriphataud Mountains (S)
        - Sauromugue Champaign (S)
        - West Sarutabaruta (S)
[5] Defeat the Transporter and the Guards will warp shortly after.
    - It is possible to kill the Transporter without getting aggro from the Guards, as long as they have enough distance between each other so they don't link by sound.
[6] Click the ??? where the Transporter died to get the [KI] Beastman Confederate crate.
    - Disappears after a couple minutes.
    - Even if you didn't take part in defeating the Transporter, clicking the ??? still gives you the [KI] Beastman Confederate crate.
    - If not on the quest, you can't get the key item even if you kill the Transporter.
[7] Return to the Quartermaster at (I-9).
[8] Report back to Rasdinice to complete the Mission.
[9] The Guards and Transporter can reappear at the beginning of their path as quickly as a few minutes (2 to 5 minutes) after disappearing.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

As of 2025, this Transporter can be easily 1-shotted with a geared ilvl 119 character, perhaps choose a job that can cast a nuke at range.
