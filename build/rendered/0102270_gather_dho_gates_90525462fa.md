## Walkthrough
[1] NPC asks you to obtain 3 Slug Eyes.
    - The NPC specifically notes it does not matter how the player obtains it. It can be bought, traded, or farmed.
[2] Trade these to the NPC and then Report on the quest outcome.
[3] The drop rate of slug eyes is universally bad for all slugs. It is best if you Despoil them.
    - Using and recalling the Nanaa Mihgo trust after she Despoils one (to reset the 5min Despoil cooldown) is the easiest method of completion if they are not available on the Auction House.
[4] Cleaving Slugs in Abyssea - Attohwa works decently as well, there are 11 in very close proximity.
[5] Alternatively, there are 5 slugs at the northeast of Dho Gates map 1.
