# Iron Anvil IV (B)

The game knows this mission as: Iron Anvil IV (B)   (mission/campaign #304)

## Campaign Header
Campaign Type: Supply manufacture
Description: To manufacture munitions and supplies, our nation has employed hundreds of young, unskilled guildworkers. You are to lend your trade knowledge to aid in the training of these initiates. Speak with adjutant (J-9) for details.

## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Hieronymus and select this Campaign Op
[2] Talk to Adjutant (J-9), and he'll ask you for a craft to specialize in.
    - You have to have a developed craft of at least level 61 to attempt this op. He will turn you away if you don't meet the requirement.
    - Time is limited when selecting the crystal and ingredients.
    - You will have to give him ingredients for TWO different recipes in the craft you have chosen.
    - You will fail the mission if you take too long to select the crystals and ingredients or choose the wrong ones.
    - Recipes will require up to eight ingredients. If a recipe has less than Eight ingredients, it is necessary to select the "No more ingredients are required" option after selecting the appropriate ingredients.
[3] Once you've completed your two tests, go back and talk to Hieronymus for your reward.
