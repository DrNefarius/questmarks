## Walkthrough

[1] Warp to the Western Adoulin Waterfront (Waypoint #9) and examine the Port Storage door at (J-4) for a cutscene.
[2] You are asked to bring ONE of the following items to Guilberien:
    - Eft Skin (x5)
    - Manticore Hair (x4)
    - Buffalo Horn
    - Manticore Leather
    - Buffalo Leather
[3] When you have acquired the item(s) tasked of you, trade it to the door Port Storage at (J-4) for another cutscene with Guilberien.
[4] The specific item asked for depends on the level of your bond with Arciela, with Buffalo Horn indicating the lowest level.[http://www.famitsu.com/news/201412/12067432.html Developer interview about Adoulin Missions, Famitsu.com]
