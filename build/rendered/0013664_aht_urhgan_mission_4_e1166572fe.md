## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Cacaroon (G-11) for a cutscene, across from the Mog House.
[2] Trade Cacaroon 1,000 gil or one Imperial Bronze Piece for another cutscene.
    - If you chose to pay the gil instead of giving the coin, he won't automatically take the gil from you. You have to trade it to him.
[3] Enter the Walahra Temple (K-8) for another cutscene.
[4] Head to the Shararat Teahouse (K-12) for another cutscene.
[5] Answer the following questions to complete the quest.
    #What have you learned about? Pick all 3.
    #Same as first question but with a new answer. Raillefal's secret.
    #You are really... A San d'Orian Prince.
    #Raillefal is really... Prince Trion.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

This mission begins immediately after the previous cutscene.
