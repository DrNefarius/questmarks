## Walkthrough

[1] Zone after completing Healing the Land to begin this quest.
[2] Speak to Eperdur in Northern San d'Oria (M-7) second level of the Cathedral.
[3] Travel to Fei'Yin to open a Treasure Chest.
[4] Obtain a Fei'Yin Chest Key by defeating Shadows, Underworld Bats and Ore Golems in that area.
    - If you possess the [KI] "Rhapsody in White", a key can be purchased from a Curio Vendor Moogle.
[5] Open a treasure chest using either the key or Thief's Tools to obtain the [KI] Fei'Yin magic tome.
[6] Return to Eperdur to receive your reward.
