## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Hariga-Origa in Windurst Waters (north) at (F-8).
[2] You will be given the [KI] Note from Hariga-Origa, and asked to deliver it to Ipupu.
    - Ipupu is located in West Sarutabaruta (I-8/J-8), near the entrance to Windurst Waters.
[3] She will accept the item and in return give you the [KI] Note from Ipupu to deliver to Hariga-Origa.
[4] After delivering the note, you will complete the quest and receive your rewards.
