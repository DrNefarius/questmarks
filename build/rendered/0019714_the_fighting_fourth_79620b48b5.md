## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Adelbrecht. You will turn in the [KI]Blue recommendation letter, and in exchange be given the [KI]Battle rations to deliver to Gebhardt.
    - This quest will change your Campaign allegiance. However, it has no cost or penalty as manually switching does.
    - Speaking to Adelbrecht before the conclusion of the quest will allow you to cancel it.
[2] Ascend Zegham Hill around I-8 of North Gustaberg (S) and speak to Gebhardt at I-6 for a cutscene
[3] Head to E-11 and speak with Roderich.
[4] Go to (E-7) and touch the "Barricade" for a cutscene.
[5] Return to Adelbrecht to complete the quest and become a member of the Republican Legion's Fourth Divison. If this is your first time, you will be given a pair of Sprinter's Shoes.
