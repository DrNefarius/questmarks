## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Rakuru-Rakoru in Lower Jeuno (I-6) next to the fountain.  He will ask you to defeat several Notorious Monsters (listed below) with the [KI] Data Analyzer and Logger EX in your possession.
    - Once each NM is defeated you will receive the message: "The relevant battle data has been recorded in your Data Analyzer and Logger EX."
    - Nyzul Isle variants DO NOT COUNT towards completion of this quest.
[2] Speak with Rakuru-Rakoru at any time to check your progress.
[3] Once all the NM data has been recorded return to Rakuru-Rakoru for your reward.
