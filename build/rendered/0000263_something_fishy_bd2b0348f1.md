## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Tokaka to begin this quest. Home Point #1 in Port Windurst is extremely close to her.
[2] Trade Tokaka a Bastore Sardine to complete this quest.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] You must zone every time you finish this quest before you can have it offered to you again.
[2] You can get level 9 Windurst fame if you trade 20 Stacks of fish to Tokaka.
