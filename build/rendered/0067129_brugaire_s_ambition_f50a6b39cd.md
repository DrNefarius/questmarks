## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Brugaire (A) at (L-6), northeast of Conflux #8, to begin the quest.
    - You will be given a  Prismatic Elixir.
[2] You must spawn Teekesselchen, use the Elixir on it, and defeat the NM.
    - Anyone on this quest can use an Elixir and receive credit.
[3] If you lose the Elixir, you can receive another from Brugaire (A) anytime.
[4] Return to Brugaire (A) to complete the quest.
Zoning is required in order to repeat this quest.
