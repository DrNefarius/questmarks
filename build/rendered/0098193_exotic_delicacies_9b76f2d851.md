## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Flapano (I-8) to begin the quest.
[2] He requires 1 Saffron, 2 Barnacles, and 1 Mussel to complete a dish.
    - Saffron is obtained from the synthesis of 3 Saffron Blossoms, which are obtained by harvesting in Foret de Hennetiel, using a Wind Crystal.
    - Barnacles can be obtained from the synthesis of Carrier Crab Carapace, which drops from Barnacle Crab () and Depthswalker Crabs () in Rala Waterways, and a Lightning Crystal. (Enter from Western Adoulin (K-8) by the Big Bridge).
        - Also can be fished in Western Adoulin with Sabiki Rig or Shrimp Lure.
    - Mussels can be obtained by fishing on the secret beach in Foret de Hennetiel with Robber Rig or Rogue Rig.
[3] Return with the ingredients for your reward.
