## Walkthrough

[1] Note:You must zone after finishing Girl in the Looking Glass before you can start this quest.
[2] Speak to Luto Mewrilah for a cutscene.
[3] In Port San d'Oria, head to Cargo Room B near Home Point #1 and speak to Portaure (H-9) for another cutscene that allows you to start a Burning Circle fight.
    - Note: The battlefield applies a level cap of 40: recommend bringing level appropriate gear and food.
    - The battlefield was designed to be soloable by players at level 30.
    - Trust Magic can not be used.
[4] Go to Ghelsba Outpost and interact with the Hut Door (F/G-10) to enter the battlefield.
[5] The NM Carrion Dragon will spawn. Your Adventuring Fellow will arrive to assist you once you get it to around 80%.
    - It has standard Dragon TP moves such as Thorn Song and Heavy Stomp. If you're unlucky it may use Petro Eyes and Petrify you or your fellow.
[6] Once the BC is complete you will receive a cutscene.
[7] Return to Luto to complete the quest and receive the reward.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Three people in a party may enter the BC at the same time.
[2] Players who have already completed the quest will not be able to enter the battlefield.
