## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Fari-Wari in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (K-12) for a cutscene that begins the quest.
[2] With the key item Biyaada's letter, go to Al Zahbi and speak to Gaweesh (G-8) and Talhaal (H-6).
[3] Complete the quests When the Bow Breaks and Fist of the People.
[4] Speak to Fari-Wari to complete the quest.
