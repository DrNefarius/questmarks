## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Yoran-Oran, who will request an item.  See the below chart for details:

[TABLE]
    Requested item | Reward | How to obtain
    4Leaf Mandra Bud Four-Leaf Mandragora Bud | Gil 120 gil | * Mandragora in West Sarutabaruta * Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon * Sylvestre in Buburimu Peninsula
     Cornette | Gil 200 gil | * Mandragora in West Sarutabaruta * Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon * Records of Eminence NPCs for 50 sparks * Harmodios in Bastok Markets (K-10) (when the nation is 1st or 2nd place in Conquest rankings)
     Yuhtunga Sulfur | Gil 250 gil | * Mandragora in West Sarutabaruta * Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon * Sylvestre in Buburimu Peninsula
    3Leaf Mandra Bud Three-Leaf Mandragora Bud | Gil 1,200 gil | * Yuhtunga Mandragora in Yuhtunga Jungle
     Snobby Letter | Gil 5,500 gil | * Mourioche in The Boyahda Tree
[/TABLE]

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] Maxing fame (level 9) from the minimal level requires approximately 60x  Cornettes. Other items may be weighted differently.
[2] Reaching level 8 fame requires ~50x  Cornettes.
