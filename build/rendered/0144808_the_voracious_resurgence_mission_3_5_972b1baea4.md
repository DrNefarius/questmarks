## Walkthrough

[1] Zone into Fei'Yin from Beaucedine Glacier for a cutscene.
    - The Unity Concord (Level 128) Warp to Fei'Yin is quickest. Simply turn around and rezone into Fei'Yin.
[2] Head to Fei'Yin Map 2 at (H-7).
    - If you already have the gizmo doll, use above Unity warp. If you got above cutscene before getting doll use the Fei'Yin HP #2 instead of navigating Map 1 to exit A.
[3] Trade the Doll Gizmo Doll Gizmo to the NPC Talos for a cutscene.
[4] After the cutscene, return to Beaucedine Glacier and interact with the Point of Interest next to Mirror Pond for another cutscene.
    - Subjob BLM and casting Escape puts you a short walk away from this position.
[5] Touch the Point of Interest again to begin a confrontation with Slackjawed Mukdrom (WAR)
    - Has approximately 50k HP.
    - Uses Mighty Strikes and has en-Drain on attacks.
[6] Touch the Point of Interest yet again for a cutscene.
[7] Return to Chateau d'Oraguille and click Door:Prince Royal's RM located at (H-7) for a final cutscene and your rewards.
