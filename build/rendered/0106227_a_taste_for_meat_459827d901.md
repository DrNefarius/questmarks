## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Antreneau, then Thierride. They are both located in the Rusty Anchor Pub (@ G-7 in Port San d'Oria), to begin the quest.
[2] Trade 5 Hare Meat to Thierride to complete the quest.
    - For low leveled players, both Wild Rabbit and Forest Hare can be defeated for Experience Points and have a chance to drop the Hare Meat needed for this quest.
[3] Afterwards, Antreneau will give you a Grilled Hare if you speak with him.
