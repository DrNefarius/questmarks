## Walkthrough

[1] After speaking to Hieronymus, travel to Beadeaux (S).
[2] To reach the weapon store, travel to (H-7) on the first map to reach the second map. On the second map, go to the circular room at (H-8).
    - There are two Virulent Peistes in this room. You must time dropping invisible in order to avoid getting aggro.
    - You may have to check this ??? spot twice to complete the intel process. You can also not check it if you have either sneak or invisible
[3] To reach the defensive structures, travel to (F-8) of the second map to return to the first map. On the first map, travel to the island at (G-10).
    - There are several Quadavs on the island, be careful when removing sneak to examine the ??? spot.
[4] Return to Hieronymus to finish the mission.
