## Walkthrough

[1] After speaking with Emhi Tchaoryo, proceed to the Fortilace at L-9 in Sauromugue Champaign (S).
[2] Enter the BCNM and defeat 6 Yagudo Stormers.
    - The battle is capped at 30 minutes.
    - Buffs will remain if cast prior to entering the battlefield.
[3] There are two Garrisons in this battle. One is ahead of where you start, the other is further west.
[4] 6 Yagudo Stormers will spawn from the north. 3 will apporach each Garrison and attack it.
[5] After defeating the 6 Yagudo, exit the BCNM via the Dawn Aureole.
[6] Only the party leader needs the Ops orders for the whole party to enter the BCNM. Everyone will receive Experience Points upon completion of the BCNM.
[7] Those signed up for this Campaign Ops will receive Allied Notes upon speaking to Emhi Tchaoryo to complete the Op.
