## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Ranpi-Monpi at the Culinarian's Guild (D-9).
[2] Go to Giddeus via West Sarutabaruta.
[3] Enter the caves via (F-8) or (I-8), behind where you dropped the offering for Windurst Mission 1-3.
[4] Head to the lower level of the caves, where you'll find a storage room at (G-6).
[5] Click on the altar to retrieve the [KI] Off offering.
[6] Return the key item to Ranpi-Monpi to complete the quest.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] This quest cannot be started at the same time the quest The Dawn of Delectability is active.
[2] It is recommended that you purchase a Map of Giddeus for this mission to help navigate the caves.
