## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Baha Mannohl (A) at (H-4) by Veridical Conflux #05.
[2] Teleport to Veridical Conflux #04. You need to walk into the Caoineag and find the targetable Tahrongi Cacti location at (F-6).
    - You must click it between 20:00 and 4:00 game time for [KI] Cup of Tahrongi cactus water.
[3] Return to Baha Mannohl (A) to complete the quest.
    - Attempting to begin the quest a 6th time will reward [KI] Viridian abyssite of lenity and you will not be allowed to repeat the quest.
Zoning is required to repeat this quest.
