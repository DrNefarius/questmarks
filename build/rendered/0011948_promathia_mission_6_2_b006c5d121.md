## Walkthrough

[1] Head toward the Palace in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene.
[2] Talk to Pherimociel at (G-6).
[3] Travel to (I-11) in Misareaux Coast and check the Dilapidated Gate to spawn 3 Detector-type NMs.
    - Take the lower western exit from Tavnazian Safehold.
[4] Checking the Dilapidated Gate will spawn Warder Thalia, Warder Aglaia, and Warder Euphrosyne.
    - The NM's can be slept and defeated individually although they begin to resist Sleep after a time. All three must be defeated to progress.
[5] After you defeat the Warders, check the Dilapidated Gate again for a cutscene.
