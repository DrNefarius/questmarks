## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Anguenet at (J-10) and select the 4th option (Newtpool) to begin the quest.
    - He is right next to the Survival Guide.
[2] Fish up a Muddy Siredon in the Newt Pool area of Phanauet Channel.
    - You can simply buy one from the Auction House. There is no need to actually fish one up.
[3] Trade the Muddy Siredon to Lourdaude, he will then "ask" for 100 gil.
[4] Trade him 100 gil and he will give you the Blackened Siredon reward.
