## Walkthrough

right
[1] Examine the Regal Pawprints (H-9) Beaucedine Glacier (S) for a cutscene. The target is behind the tree near the bottom of the ramp from the Campaign Arbiter.
[2] Examining the Regal Pawprints a second time will spawn five NM's: Count Halphas and 4 Dark Demons.
    - Only Count Halphas needs to be defeated to continue. Once he is dead the Dark Demons will vanish.
    - The Dark Demons can be slept with sleepga, however Count Halphas is immune to sleep.
[3] Once defeated, examine the Regal Pawprints again for a cutscene.
