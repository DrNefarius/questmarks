[1] Speak with Tateeya in the Automaton Workshop (I-7) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate for a cutscene to begin the Mission.
[2] Speak with Ghatsad for another cutscene.
    - Being in the middle of No Strings Attached may prevent you from continuing this Quest.
[3] Head to the Jade Sepulcher.
    - Fastest way is by Home Point #1 to Bhaflau Thickets Map 2.
    - Another way is to go to Mamool Ja Staging Point to Bhaflau Thickets Map 2 and head North.
[4] Examine the Ornamental Door for a cutscene.
[5] Examine the Ornamental Door again to enter the BCNM fight "Tateeya's Worries" against:
    - Drakeweaver Hageel Ja (PLD) - 60k HP
    - Riftweaver Pomaal Ja (BLU) - 60k HP
    - Fistweaver Mufaal Ja (Sahagin MNK) - 60k HP
    - Glyphweaver Sikool Ja (BLM). - 100k HP
        - Casts Stun, Thunder V, Thunder VI, Ionohelix II, Thundaja.
            - These will not do much harm to a player in MDT/Magic Evasion gear, but will deal solid damage against trusts.
    - Trusts may be used in the fight.
    - Glyphweaver Sikool Ja's weapon must be broken to win the fight, otherwise he'll remain at 1%.
        - To do this you need to weaponskill as the NM is doing a melee attack or very shortly after.
    - All foes utilize their 1-hour abilities.
    - All foes can be silenced
    - All mobs can be slept
[6] Head back to Aht Urhgan Whitegate and examine Imperial Whitegate for the final cutscene and the Thunder Hammer .
    - Do not discard the Thunder Hammer . You will need it for a later Mission.
