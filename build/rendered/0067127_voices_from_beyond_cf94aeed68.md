## Walkthrough

[1] Talk to Rukususu (A) (F-10)
    - South of Veridical Conflux #7, near the encampment by the waterfall. Do not drop down the river!
[2] Interact with a ??? location at (C-8) in Skyrend for a cutscene.
    - This is further past Azdaja's ???, at the very end of the canyon.
[3] Kill Sensenmann until you receive the  Decaying Molar, or purchase it on the Auction House.
[4] Spawn and defeat Xibalba with the molar and obtain an  Ominous Skull.
    - He can Doom even iLevel 119 characters, so be ready.
[5] Go to the the westernmost ??? in (C-8) for the short message.
    - The message is "A spectral voice echoes within your mind... Death becomes...he who trespasses...these grounds...".
    - This is required before Rukususu will accept the Ominous Skull.
[6] Trade Rukususu the  Ominous Skull to complete the quest.
