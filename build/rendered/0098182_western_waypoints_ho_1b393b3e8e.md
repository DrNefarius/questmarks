## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Alienor (E-8) outside of the Pioneers' Coalition.
[2] If you already have all the Waypoints talk to her again to complete the quest.
[3] Attune your geomagnetron to all 9 Waypoints in Western Adoulin.
    - (H-8) Platea Triumphus (COU Co.)
    - (E-8) Pioneers' Coalition
    - (G-10) Mummers' Coalition
    - (J-9) Inventors' Coalition
    - (F-10) Auction house
    - (H-11) Your Rent-a-Room
    - (L-9) Big Bridge
    - (H-4) Airship docks
    - (I-5) Adoulin Waterfront
[4] Return to Alienor for your rewards to complete the quest.
