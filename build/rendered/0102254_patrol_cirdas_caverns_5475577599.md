## Walkthrough
[1] Defeat 3 Maroliths in Cirdas Caverns.
[2] You do not need to be in EXP range as long as you are in Cirdas Caverns when your party/alliance defeats the monsters.
[3] Confirmed spawn points for Maroliths are F-5 (West of Skirmish Waypoint, no Reives), K-5 (East of Skirmish Waypoint, no Reives), K-11, and F/G-11 (2 spawns at each location).
[4] 5 minute respawn timer
[5] Maroliths will only aggro Magic, so you can have someone at the pop without worrying about aggro.
[6] To optimize time, bank imprimaturs in other coalition assignments (canceling will return them to you) until you can do 13-14 runs in a row (40+ imprimaturs) to complete Partner and Advisor rank while only needing to use the Cirdas Skirmish Waypoint while having two friends do the Maroliths.
