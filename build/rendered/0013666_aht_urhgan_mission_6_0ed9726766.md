## Walkthrough

You must wait one Earth minute after completing the previous quest to begin this quest.
[1] Approach the Palace in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene.
[2] Choose any of the answers to the questions to complete the quest.
    - Choosing "Yes", followed by "That's what I'm here for!" will reward you with 10 Imperial Bronze Pieces.
    - Choosing "What am I, a donkey?" will complete the quest but forfeit 10x Imperial Bronze Piece
