## Walkthrough

thumb|right|200px|Zeruhn Mines Map
[1] Speak to any Bastokan Gate Guard and accept the Mission.
    - Argus (NPC) - Port Bastok (L-7)
    - Cleades - Bastok Markets (D-11)
    - Rashid - Bastok Mines (H-10)
    - Malduc - Metalworks (J-8)
[2] You will have to head to the Zeruhn Mines. The mines can be entered from Bastok Mines at (D-7).
[3] Speak to Makarim in Zeruhn Mines (H-11) for the [KI] Zeruhn report.
[4] Speak to Naji in the Metalworks top floor (J-8) for a cutscene and to complete the Mission.
    - Lucius' dialog in the cutscene will change depending on whether you examine the [KI] Zeruhn report or not.
