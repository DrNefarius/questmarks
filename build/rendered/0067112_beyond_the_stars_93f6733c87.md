## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to the Nomad Moogle in Ru'Lude Gardens at (H-5).
[2] Trade 10 Kindred's Crests to the Nomad Moogle while also having 5 merit points.
    - Keep in mind that besides obtaining seals from mobs, two easier ways exist to obtain various seals:
        - Spend Login Campaign points - each seal is only 5 points.
        - Shemo can exchange any other type of seal at a 3:1 ratio.
[3] Talk to the Nomad Moogle again. You will have to play a type of "Rock, Paper, Scissors", the goal is to win 5 rounds.
    - "Raging Beast" beats "Silent Wind".
    - "Silent Wind" beats "Soaring Dragon".
    - "Soaring Dragon" beats "Raging Beast".
    - This is all random, just pick options and hope to win.
[4] Should you lose the initial fight, the number of rounds you need to win in order to win the mini-game will decrease by 1. This will continue as you continue to lose rounds.
[5] Win at the mini-game and you will receive your reward.
