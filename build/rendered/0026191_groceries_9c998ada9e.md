## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Tami in Bastok Mines at (J-8) to begin quest.
[2] She will give you the [KI] Tami's note.
    - Note: If you open and read the Key Item, you can no longer repeat the quest.

If you read the note
[3] Proceed to Zeruhn Mines and locate Zelman at (I-8).
[4] Go to (H-5) in Bastok Mines (Bat's Lair Inn) and purchase a  Meat Jerky from Griselda.
[5] Trade the jerky to Tami for your reward of a Rabbit Mantle.
    - You will now complete the quest, and it is no longer repeatable.

If you do not read the note
[6] Proceed to Zeruhn Mines and locate Zelman at (I-8).
[7] He will tell you that he is busy, and to tell Tami.
[8] Return to Tami for your reward of 10 Gil.
    - At this point, you can repeat this quest.
