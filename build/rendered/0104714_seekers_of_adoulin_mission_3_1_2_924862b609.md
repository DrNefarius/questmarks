## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Ploh Trishbahk (K-9) in Eastern Adoulin to receive a list of required items.
[2] Speak to Rigobertine in the south-east corner of (J-7) (the northern alley from the Coronal Esplanade) in Eastern Adoulin to receive the [KI] Eternal flame and hints about the other items.
    - You cannot obtain the other items without this cutscene.
[3] Go to Platea Triumphus in Western Adoulin and click the spot marked Fontis Xanira (its the water fountain at H-8/9 border) to receive the [KI] Vial of untainted holy water.
[4] Go to the Western Adoulin Waterfront (J-4) and click on the spot called Sunrise Beacon on the tower to receive the [KI] Piece of a stone wall.
[5] Speak to Erminold at (J-10) near Castle Adoulin Gates in Eastern Adoulin to obtain the [KI] Weather vane wings.
    - This will always be the last one you obtain; while the second and third can be obtained in either order, you need both to get this one.
[6] After obtaining the 4 Key Items you will be on the next Mission: The Purgation.
