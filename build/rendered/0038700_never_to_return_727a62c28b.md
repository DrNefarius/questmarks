## Walkthrough

[1] After completing Your Crystal Ball, have Kurou-Morou read your fortune on at least three different game days.
[2] Asking for your fourth reading will trigger this quest.
[3] Trade a  Horn Hairpin to Kurou-Morou to complete the quest.
    - The Hairpin may be bought from the Auction House, but is crafted only.
