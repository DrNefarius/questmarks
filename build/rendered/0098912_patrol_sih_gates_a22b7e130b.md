## Walkthrough
[1] Defeat 5 Malodorous Twitherym in Sih Gates. You do not need to be in EXP range as long as you are in Sih Gates when your party/alliance defeats the monsters.
    - The quickest way to the Twitherym is via the Frontier Station of the Foret de Hennetiel - head north into the gates and you'll immediately start running into them.
