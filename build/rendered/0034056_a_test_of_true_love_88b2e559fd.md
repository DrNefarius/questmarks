## Walkthrough

[1] Zone after completing Love and Ice
[2] Speak with Carmelo to begin quest
[3] Travel to the following locations and procure 3 necessary items from Treasure Chests:
        - Castle Zvahl Baileys or Castle Zvahl Keep Item:"Un Moment"
        - Labyrinth of Onzozo Item:"L'Ancienne"
        - Sea Serpent Grotto Item:"L'Ephemere"
[4] Return to Bastok and speak with Carmelo with all 3 key items in possesion.
[5] After the conversation, zone out, then return to him and speak with him one last time to complete quest.
