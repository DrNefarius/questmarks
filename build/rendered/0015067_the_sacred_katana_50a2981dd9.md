## Walkthrough

[1] Go to Norg and talk to Jaucribaix at (K-8) near the Captain's Chamber while on Samurai to initiate the quest.
    - You may change to a different job to complete the rest of this quest.
[2] Travel to the The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah.
    - Kill Goblin Robbers to get a Sack of Fish Bait, a common drop.
[3] Trade the Fish Bait to the ??? at (E-8) to pop Isonade, a fish NM.
    - Isonade has approximately 6,100 HP.
[4] Re-examine the ??? to obtain the Handful of crystal scales.
[5] Return to Norg and trade your Mumeito to Jaucribaix to complete the quest.
    - If you do not possess your Mumeito, Ranemaud at (I-7) can give you a new one for 30,000 gil.
