## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Kohlo-Lakolo to begin this quest.
[2] Speak to Chamama, Windurst Waters (F-10).
[3] She will make you the Invisible man sticker you need if you first bring her Shoalweed.
    - Shoalweed drops off Recluse Spiders in Kuftal Tunnel.
[4] Return to Kohlo-Lakolo for your reward.

== Final Cutscene ==

[5] If you have killed Fenrir after obtaining Windurst Rank 10, you will have the Dark Mana Orb in your Temporary Key Items.
[6] Zoning and talking to Kohlo-Lakolo again with this key item will give a final, concluding cutscene.
