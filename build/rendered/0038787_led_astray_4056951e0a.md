## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Mhasbaf in Aht Urhgan Whitegate at (J-8) to begin this quest.
[2] Walk downstairs to the pillar at (J-8) for a cutscene.
[3] Speak to Tohka Telposkha at (I-8) for a cutscene.
    - You must tell the Galka to go to Port Ephramad.
        - If you choose any other option, you must select again until you pick Port Ephramad. (You may find selecting the incorrect port entertaining.)
[4] Talk to Rubahah at (F-9) and repeat this process, and then Cacaroon at (G-11) and do the same thing.
[5] Walk to (I-11) (Down the ramp beside the ferry) for a cutscene to obtain the [KI] Letter from Bernahn.
[6] Speak to Tataroon at (G-8) in Nashmau to complete the quest and obtain your reward.
