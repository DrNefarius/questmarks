## Walkthrough

[1] Speak to Scarred Shark at (G-5) in Bastok Markets (S) for a cutscene.
    - Use the Survival Guide warp for quickest access.
[2] Trade him a Heavy Quadav Chestplate and Heavy Quadav Backplate dropped by heavily armored Quadavs in Shadowreign zones.
[3] Change zones and then speak to him again for a cutscene and to receive your reward.
