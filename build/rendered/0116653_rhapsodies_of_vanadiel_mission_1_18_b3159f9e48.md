## Walkthrough

[1] To start the next chapter, examine Gilgamesh's door in Norg multiple times, then go in and talk to Gilgamesh. This will start a cutscene with Gilgamesh, Aldo, Tenzen, Zeid, Kagero, and Prishe. Once the custscene's are completed, you will see the next chapter flagged.

## After the walkthrough: Notes

[1] You will be unable to receive this cutscene if you are currently on one of the following missions:
    - Promathia Mission 5-2: Desires of Emptiness
    - Promathia Mission 7-5: The Warrior's Path
    - Promathia Mission 8-1: Garden of Antiquity
    - Promathia Mission 8-3: When Angels Fall
    - Promathia Mission 8-4: Dawn
    - Possibly other Tenzen-related missions
[2] If you start The Warrior's Path, you must finish all remaining missions up to Dawn, including the last cutscenes in Lufaise Meadows.
    - This will include a Japanese midnight wait.
    - This is a good opportunity to look ahead in the missions as you may be missing other expansion mission requirements if you have to wait for Japanese midnight.
