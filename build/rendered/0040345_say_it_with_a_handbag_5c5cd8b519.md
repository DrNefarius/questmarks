## Walkthrough

[1] Speak with Hampu-Kampu located in the south map of Windurst Waters (S) (G-8).
    - He is standing right outside the gate currently blocking entrance to Gingerberry Grove and rest of southwest Windurst Waters (S).
[2] Interact with the Door: Acolyte hostel located in the north map at (K-4).
    - Second floor, on the right side of the left building.
[3] Return to Hampu-Kampu for another cutscene, and to receive the [KI] Torn patches of leather.
[4] Speak with Kipopo in Southern San d'Oria (present) at (D-8).
    - She is inside the room on the first floor.
[5] Trade her a Laminated Ram Leather, Sheep Leather and Silk Thread.
[6] Zone, then speak with her again. You will receive the [KI] Repaired handbag.
[7] Return to Hampu-Kampu to finish the quest and receive the Trainee's Needle.
